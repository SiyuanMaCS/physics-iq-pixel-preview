METHOD

Inspection modality

Numeric/programmatic inspection only (Track: Text). first_frame.png was never opened with an image Read/View tool and was not seen visually. All understanding came from NumPy/PIL/OpenCV array analysis: RGB statistics, row/column profiles, color-threshold masks, connected components, ASCII class maps, least-squares circle/sphere fits, and held-out reconstruction errors. inspection.png and transition_check.png are saved as audit evidence even though they were not visually read.

Scene model (from numeric inspection)

  • Gray wall (additive row+column lighting model, residual σ ≈ 4), wooden table with perspective back edge, flattened green kinetic-sand mound (x 89–182, plateau y 84–86), a blue-black inverted-U grabber with static yoke/rails and two curved claw fingers cradling the ball, tips meeting near (134,55).
  • Blue ball: circle center (136,38), r = 13, with a ~6 px soft felt/fuzz edge (coverage measured from the unoccluded top rim: α ≈ 0.94 at r12, 0.74 r13, 0.52 r14, 0.34 r15, 0.18 r16, 0.08 r17). Color is a smooth shaded sphere (fitted coefficients over the unoccluded core): per-channel ambient/diffuse/right-sheen terms with light from upper-right, plus fixed spatial grain fields. Intrinsic colors clamped to the observed envelope.

Generation (20 frames, 256×144, 4 fps)

  1. Static background retained: frame 0 is byte-identical to first_frame.png (asserted). Wall, table, pile, and yoke are never moved.
  2. Wall behind the moving head: ball disk, anti-alias halo (blue-tinted gray pixels B−R > 2), and the two source-pixel finger masks are removed and rebuilt per row by linear interpolation between the nearest strictly wall-like pixels on either side (dark tool/AA pixels skipped as anchors), with a 2.2 px distance-feathered seam and matched σ ≈ 3 grain. Held-out wall error is within ~3 levels after this revision.
  3. Release: the two fingers are rigid finger sprites rotated about pivots (117,29)/(152,29) — 25° at frame 1, 42° from frame 2 on (orientation change of the real tool parts; cv2 warpAffine, colors carried from source).
  4. Ball: procedurally shaded sphere, 2× supersampled, alpha-composited over the live canvas so its soft edge blends with wall/wood/sand correctly; frame 1 keeps the exact source ball pixels (still stationary). Free fall y = 38 + ½·25·(t−0.25)² from release to contact (frames 2–7), with mild 3-stamp vertical motion blur when per-frame speed exceeds 14 px/s.
  5. Impact (frame 8): vertical squash (0.90/1.09), embed to center y 76.3; sand layer displaced with a smooth field (2 px depressed ring at |dx| 9–13, 2.8 px raised rim at |dx| 13–19, cosine depth falloff, cv2.remap on the pile RGBA) at 70% amplitude; ten 1 px granules on ballistic arcs (g 78) frames 8–10 plus fixed rim clumps; soft shadow tightens during descent and becomes contact darkening / crater-lip shadows. Frame 9 small rebound (y 74.6, relax), frame 10 settles at y 76.2; frames 11–19 are static.

Revisions made during QA (all numeric)

  1. Ball shading initial diffuse/specular fit was ~15 levels too bright; refit on the unoccluded r<8 core using diffuse + broad upper-right sheen (interior MAE ≈ 3–5).
  2. Ball edge changed from a 1.9 px anti-alias to the measured 6 px soft-fuzz profile; this also removed a spurious dark fringe above the resting ball.
  3. Wall reconstruction: cv2.inpaint and an additive model both pulled dark tool colors into the lower ball area (errors up to −30 R); replaced with strict-anchor row interpolation after discovering loose wallish tests admitted blue-gray AA pixels as endpoints.
  4. Ball halo mask widened (r 19.5→20.5, blue-tint criterion) so faint source fringe pixels are removed rather than becoming static ghosts.
  5. Crater rim strengthened (−2.3→−2.8 px) and rim clumps made symmetric after measuring per-column pile tops; added contact-lip shadows; added intrinsic color clipping (B had reached 194 vs. source envelope ≤175).

Verification

  • frames/00.png byte-identical to first_frame.png; all 20 PNGs match the packed pixels.npz tensor (144,256,20) uint32 0xRRGGBB; independent delta decode of index.html reproduces every frame; frames 11–19 are identical.
  • Per-frame changed pixels stay inside the action corridor (head area, fall path, impact zone); static yoke rails outside the finger region are untouched (max diff 0 at y<22).
  • Diagnostics: ball color/interior match, edge-profile match, wall-fill error, crater column profile, jaw rotation clearance — measured in scripts.

Visible limitations

  • Finger/jaw ownership at r 14–16 (dark finger vs. ball AA) is ambiguous at a few pixels; resolved by color thresholds, so the open claw is an idealized rigid rotation and slightly soft at the hinges.
  • The procedural ball cannot reproduce every source grain mottle; frame 1→2 shows a small shading change where the source ball was darkened by the claw.
  • Crater amplitude, rim clumps, and ejected granules are plausible values, not measurable from a single pre-impact frame; the sand surface is a 2-D vertical displacement (no side-view granule volume).
  • 4 fps sampling makes the one-frame impact squash/smear abrupt, and the wide soft ball edge is faithful to the source but visibly blends against the table during the last frames of the fall.
