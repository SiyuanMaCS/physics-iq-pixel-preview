METHOD

Inspection modality (exact statement)

  • The only image ever seen visually was the supplied first_frame.png (shown once in the prompt as a multimodal input). No generated PNG was read with an image/view tool at any point.
  • All later inspection was numeric only, through Python with PIL, NumPy and OpenCV: channel statistics, colour-threshold connected components, per-row / per-column contours, least-squares wall colour model residuals, blob centroids, frame-difference bounding boxes, median/high-frequency noise statistics, and direct pixel-coordinate queries.
  • Evidence images (inspection.png, transition_check.png) were written for audit but not visually read by me; they were checked only for dimensions and file existence.

What was measured from frame 00

Wall gradient and wall/table seam line, sand mask/colour palette/full per-column top and bottom contours, ball disk centre (137, 35.5), radius 13, body/shadow/ highlight/seam colours and the seam arch geometry, grabber dark-blue/black colour gates and prong geometry (including the left tapered tip reaching y~50), the dark teal exposure-smudge under the ball (to y~60), and per-surface high-frequency noise magnitudes. See OBSERVATION.md.

Generation pipeline (generate.py)

  1. Static plate. Frame 00 is left untouched for output frame 00. For later frames, a mask of ball + smudge + side fringes (geometric shapes gated by a quadratic wall-model residual, wall-side only) is filled with OpenCV Telea inpainting (radius 9); the grabber footprint is inpainted jointly for a smooth fill, its genuine pixels are re-pasted from frame 00 every frame (strict colour gates plus geometric prong bands; a radius-3 static fringe is restored except within 1 px of the ball/blur mask). Inpainted patch MAE vs the fitted wall model: 4.7 (per-channel mean).
  2. Ball physics. Vertical release from cy=35.5 at g=149 px/s^2, first contact at t=0.70 s (cy=72 vs pile top 85), eased penetration to 8 px by 0.98 s, 1.5 px plastic micro-rebound, rest cy=78.5 from 1.30 s. Tiny horizontal settle offset and impact squash (sy 0.93 / sx 1.06 at maximum) return exactly to zero/one.
  3. Ball sprite (2D, recomputed every frame). AA disk with measured body colour, vertical shading, radial edge darkening, upper-left highlight, smooth value-noise felt grain, and the measured pale-teal seam arch. Motion blur is an exposure integral over ~0.10 s (9 ramped ghost sprites, 40% weight) plus a 90%-opaque sprite at the current position, so the ball stays readable while showing a vertical fall streak; contact darkening on the lower crescent after impact.
  4. Sand. Pre-impact the original sand is retained exactly (plus a deepening soft approach shadow on frames 01-02). Post-impact each column is resampled through a Gaussian crater (width 6.2 px, depth keyed to penetration, final 6.5 px) with two Gaussian raised lips at +/-9.5 px (final 3.5 px), cavity/contact darkening, deterministic grain-clump speckles in the rearranged zone, and a near-side rim painted over the embedded ball edges. Columns displaced by <0.02 px are skipped entirely, keeping undisturbed pixels byte-identical. 28 ballistic ejecta grains (1-3 px, sampled sand colours; v <= 42 px horizontal, g=200) fly at frames 03-05 and land within the pile plateau.
  5. Composite order: plate -> sand/shadow -> blur ghosts -> current ball -> front rim -> ejecta -> static grabber pixels on top. Frame 06 is computed once and copied bit-for-bit to frames 07-19.
  6. artifact_io.save writes the uint32 (144,256,20) tensor packed 0xRRGGBB, the 20 PNGs, the sheet and the player.

Numerical verification (all passing)

  • frames/00.png byte-identical to first_frame.png; frames 07-19 byte-identical to frame 06.
  • Tensor in pixels.npz equals the repacked frames for all 20 entries.
  • Strict teal blob centroids follow the intended fall: cy 35.0 (f00), 40.6, 54.4, 73.5 (impact f03), 77.7, 77.8, then fixed 76.3.
  • True grabber pixels (exact generation mask) equal frame 00 in every frame; empty wall band above the pile has zero non-wall pixels post-event.
  • Final crater contour: centre depressed to y=91-92, lips raised to y=79-80; pile base contour unchanged; differences confined to x~113-158 / y~65-125.
  • All pixels outside the event region are identical to frame 00 across all 20 frames.

Revisions made during generation

  1. Initial residual mask inpainted genuine dark arm-tip fringe as "ball" (stale speck risk) -> geometric prong bands plus strict tip colour gate.
  2. Ball-only inpainting bled arm colour across the wall (patch MAE 26) -> inpaint grabber+ball jointly and re-paste the static grabber; restored a static AA-fringe ring around the grabber.
  3. First blur scheme (full 0.25 s, normalised equal-exposure ghosts) made the ball translucent at impact -> shortened to ~0.10 s exposure and added a 90%-opaque current-position sprite.
  4. Ejecta with wide/fast trajectories landed on bare table and vanished at cutoff -> tightened launch velocities so grains land on the pile plateau.
  5. Float-noise columns caused +/-1 unit8 changes across the undisturbed pile -> skip columns with |displacement| < 0.02 px.
  6. Per-column jitter originally wiggled the whole mound -> gated to columns inside the crater.

Visible limitations

  • Crater depth/lip heights and ejecta are plausible 2D inventions; only the undeformed pile was observable.
  • Sand deformation is a column-wise vertical warp (no true 3D shading); the contact zone relies on darkening and a narrow painted front rim.
  • The grabber "release" is shown purely by the ball dropping; the prongs themselves do not open (treated as stationary, matching the prompt's emphasis on the released ball).
  • Frame 00's smear direction (tremor/horizontal-ish) differs from the physically vertical fall blur used in later frames.
  • One frame (03) straddles the 0.70 s contact instant, so impact and first depression share a 0.25 s exposure by construction.
