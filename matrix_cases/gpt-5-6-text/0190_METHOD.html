OBSERVATION.md

PixelWorld-Text observation and event plan

Numeric scene measurements

  • The source is an RGB uint8 PNG at exactly 256 x 144 pixels.
  • The camera is locked. The upper scene is a softly varying, approximately achromatic gray background; a warm brown wooden table fills the lower portion. The visible table boundary is irregular/perspectival, crossing roughly y=84 at the center and y=90--100 farther from the center.
  • The kettlebell is nearly black and centered at x=137. Its rounded weight occupies approximately x=112--162, y=29--78. The handle rises above it, with dark outer legs near x=110--123 and x=148--160 and a lighter opening between them. Its lowest dark point is near (138, 77).
  • The white styrofoam cup is directly below the weight. Its bright body occupies approximately x=119--156, y=80--97, tapering from a roughly 37 px top width toward a narrow base. The cup and weight meet at about y=78--81.
  • Representative numeric pixels: kettlebell body (128,35)=[22,20,21], background (110,50)=[158,156,157], cup center (140,84) is near neutral white (about 200), and table (128,100)=[118,76,38].
  • The image has vignette/texture and a strongly irregular dark pattern along the top boundary. That material is treated as locked background unless it falls inside the explicitly modeled central handle silhouette.
  • Expected action: 30 lb is about 13.6 kg, corresponding to about 133 N under gravity. An ordinary thin styrofoam cup cannot maintain its original wall geometry, so it first shortens, then buckles laterally, and ends as a flattened white layer while the kettlebell settles downward.

METHOD.md

Method

Construction

The sequence is made entirely from first_frame.png with deterministic local Python pixel operations. No external images, masks, models, engines, or network resources are used.

  1. The source RGB pixels are measured to locate the near-black kettlebell and the white cup. The kettlebell mask combines a source-luminance threshold with analytic body/handle envelopes. The cup uses the measured tapered silhouette from x=119--157 and y=80--98. Both masks receive a subpixel antialias blur.
  2. A clean plate is made only for the central object area using deterministic Navier--Stokes inpainting from adjacent pixels. Pixels outside the local action region stay unchanged.
  3. The kettlebell is translated vertically with cubic resampling. Its displacement grows from 0 to about 14.5 pixels during collapse. After the main collapse it has a small overshoot/rebound and quickly damps to rest.
  4. The cup is inverse-remapped between a moving top and its fixed base at y=98. Its height falls from 18 pixels to roughly 3.5 geometric pixels (about 6 antialiased visible pixels). A nonlinear width term, two lateral sine modes, and a small crease term produce asymmetric wall buckling. Low white foam lips spread outward near the end. Their color is sampled from the original cup.
  5. A subtle analytic contact shadow darkens the tabletop beneath the collapse. All other visible texture comes from the source image.

The displacement samples are explicitly listed in generate.py, one for each 0.25-second frame. Frames 17--19 hold the final state, showing that the heavy weight has come to rest instead of continuing to drift.

Artifacts

  • frames/00.png--frames/19.png: 20 RGB frames at 256 x 144.
  • pixels.npz: key pixels, shape (20, 144, 256), dtype uint32, packed as 0xRRGGBB.
  • inspection.png: labeled 5 x 4 contact sheet of every frame.
  • transition_check.png: seven enlarged action crops and their inter-keyframe difference maps.
  • index.html: offline player fixed at 250 ms per frame (4 fps), plus direct frame selection.
  • .tmp/generation_debug.npz: generation-only masks and the displacement curve.

Verification

Verification is numeric and code-based, consistent with the restriction against image viewing tools:

  • All 20 decoded PNGs have mode RGB and size 256 x 144.
  • Decoded frames/00.png is pixel-for-pixel equal to decoded first_frame.png.
  • Repacking every decoded PNG with (R << 16) | (G << 8) | B exactly equals the tensor saved in pixels.npz.
  • All changed pixels stay within the measured central action region; the surrounding locked-off scene remains unchanged.
  • Transition measurements show progressive motion through frame 16, tiny residual settling at frame 17, and identical resting frames 18--19.

No claim of visual-tool review is made; scene inspection and validation were performed through local numeric RGB analysis, masks, bounding boxes, difference statistics, and ASCII luminance summaries.
