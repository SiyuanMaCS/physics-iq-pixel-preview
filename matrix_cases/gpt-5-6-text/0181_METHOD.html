OBSERVATION.md

Numeric observation and event plan

Source measurements

  • Source: first_frame.png, 256 x 144 RGB. All measurements below came from local RGB/HSV arrays, connected components, row spans, and edge contours; the image was not opened with an image viewing tool.
  • Camera: fixed. The cool gray wall fills the upper-left; a warm brown work surface fills the lower field, with an irregular boundary descending from about y=28 at x=0 to y=66 near x=250.
  • Red support: a slightly perspective-skewed vertical block at approximately x=146..166, y=83..123. Its saturated red/orange core has 636 connected pixels.
  • Yellow balance block: a horizontal, perspective-skewed cap with saturated pixels spanning x=140..191, y=62..86. Its upper edge rises toward the right, meeting the blue block at about y=64..66. Together with the red support it reads as an L.
  • Blue wooden block: a narrow vertical quadrilateral at approximately x=174..199, y=12..65. The high-saturation blue core has 978 pixels. Its lower edge is already at the yellow block's upper edge in frame 00, so the first frame is interpreted as the contact/placement moment rather than as an earlier hover.
  • Grabber: an orange/dark gripping head crosses x=171..205, y=0..21, while its dark housing enters from the right and occupies a tapering region from x about 203 at y=46 to the full right edge, ending near y=67. It overlaps the upper/right side of the blue block and continues outside the frame.
  • Illumination is soft and photographic. The existing grain, irregular wood colors, anti-aliased edges, and source perspective should be retained.

METHOD.md

Method

I analyzed first_frame.png only as local numeric RGB/HSV arrays. Connected components, per-row color spans, contour bounding boxes, and sampled pixel values provided the measured object geometry recorded in OBSERVATION.md. I did not use an image Read/View tool and therefore make no claim of visual review.

Frame 00 is the original RGB array without modification. The scene already shows the held blue block meeting the yellow block, so the continuation treats this as the placement/contact moment. The red, yellow, and blue blocks stay fixed after contact.

For the motion, generate.py separates the orange/dark grabber with two measured polygon masks while explicitly excluding blue HSV pixels and their antialiased edge. It constructs a clean plate by cloning source pixels from an object-free wall/table strip. The clone offset follows the measured diagonal background boundary, keeping each pixel at nearly the same signed distance from that boundary and preventing color bleed from the nearby blue block. The small portion of the blue face hidden by the gripper is completed from the median color of visible blue pixels on the same scanline, with a mild measured-style gradient and deterministic grain. This completion is applied only inside the hidden intersection; the visible source block remains untouched.

The original photographic grabber pixels are premultiplied by a softly feathered alpha mask, translated upper-right along an ease-out sequence, and composited over the clean plate. No camera motion, zoom, external imagery, pretrained model, or external engine is used. Frames 01 and 00 form a short real-time contact hold; frames 02–16 show release/withdrawal; frames 17–19 hold the completed balance.

artifact_io.save writes the uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, 20 PNGs, inspection sheet, and standalone player. transition_check.png is generated as a numeric adjacent- frame difference audit: gray is unchanged and yellow/red marks changing pixels.
