ball-hits-duck · 生成方法

Generation

The sequence was generated entirely with local Python numeric pixel operations in generate.py. I did not use an image Read/View tool, an external model, a renderer, a physics engine, network data, or any other case.

  1. Pillow loads first_frame.png as a 144 x 256 x 3 uint8 RGB array. Frame 00 is overwritten with that original array immediately before saving, guaranteeing exact equality.
  2. The duck is isolated numerically from the measured action region using an RGB chroma threshold. The connected component at the measured duck center (173,63) is selected, its contour is filled so that the eye remains part of the source object, and its edge is softly antialiased.
  3. A clean tabletop plate is produced only in the duck's small local region. The duck mask is dilated to include its source contact shadow and OpenCV's local Telea inpainting reconstructs the table texture from neighboring RGB pixels.
  4. From frames 02–12, the ball follows a straight screen-space line from the measured pipe mouth toward the duck. It accelerates while clearing the opening and then rolls at almost constant speed. After contact, its center advances by progressively smaller distances until rest. The first appearance is clipped by the mouth edge.
  5. The brown tennis ball is drawn analytically from a circular coverage field and sphere normals. It has upper-left directional lighting, deterministic fine-scale nap, a soft table-contact shadow, and a curved pale seam. Seam phase is derived from distance divided by radius, so the marking rotates consistently with rolling rather than translating unchanged.
  6. Beginning at contact in frame 12, frames use the clean plate beneath the duck. The original duck pixels and alpha are affine-transformed around the measured base contact (174,73). Clockwise angles progress through -3, -14, -31, -55 degrees, then rebound and damp to a -48 degree resting pose while the pivot shifts slightly right and down. A soft grounded shadow lengthens with the tip angle.
  7. The couch, pipe, table outside the local action corridor, lighting, and camera remain unchanged. The source is never globally warped or recolored.

Timing

  • 20 frames

  • 5-second nominal sequence
  • Ball first visible: frame 02

  • Settled endpoint: frames 18–19

Diagnostics and review

  • inspection.png is the required sampled contact sheet created by artifact_io.save.
  • transition_check.png contains frames 10–13, amplified consecutive-frame RGB differences, and nearest-neighbor enlargements of the pipe-to-duck corridor.
  • Review was numeric only, in accordance with the request. I measured connected components, bounding boxes, per-frame changed-pixel counts, RGB delta magnitudes, object centroids, and file/tensor equality. I do not claim visual review.

Encoding

artifact_io.save packs every RGB pixel as uint32 0xRRGGBB, transposes the result to (144, 256, 20), writes pixels.npz, emits frames/00.png through frames/19.png, and embeds the same packed data into the standalone index.html player.
