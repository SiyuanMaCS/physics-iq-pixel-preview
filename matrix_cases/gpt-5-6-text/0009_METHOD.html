+def alpha_over(dst, rgb, alpha):
+    """Composite an RGB float/uint8 layer using scalar float alpha."""
+    a = np.clip(alpha, 0.0, 1.0)[..., None]
+    return dst.astype(np.float32) * (1.0 - a) + rgb.astype(np.float32) * a
+
+
+def soft_ellipse_alpha(cx, cy, rx, ry, opacity=1.0):
+    yy, xx = np.mgrid[0:H, 0:W].astype(np.float32)
+    d = ((xx - cx) / rx) ** 2 + ((yy - cy) / ry) ** 2
+    # One-pixel smooth boundary, including the very flat contact shadows.
+    feather = max(0.18, 1.7 / min(rx, max(ry, 0.5)))
+    return np.clip((1.0 - d) / feather, 0.0, 1.0) * opacity
+
+
+def add_shadow(frame, cx, cy, rx, ry, opacity):
+    a = soft_ellipse_alpha(cx, cy, rx, ry, opacity)
+    shadow_rgb = np.zeros((H, W, 3), np.float32)
+    shadow_rgb[:] = (42, 35, 30)
+    return alpha_over(frame, shadow_rgb, a)
+
+
+def ball_layer(cx, cy, radius, phase, emergence_clip=None):
+    """Return a shaded fuzzy brown tennis ball and its antialiased alpha."""
+    yy, xx = np.mgrid[0:H, 0:W].astype(np.float32)
+    u = (xx + 0.5 - cx) / radius
+    v = (yy + 0.5 - cy) / radius
+    d2 = u * u + v * v
+    # Coverage ramps over about one source pixel at the circular edge.
+    d = np.sqrt(np.maximum(d2, 1e-8))
+    alpha = np.clip((1.0 - d) * radius + 0.55, 0.0, 1.0)
+    if emergence_clip is not None:
+        alpha *= np.clip((xx + 0.5 - emergence_clip) * 1.5, 0.0, 1.0)
+
+    inside = d2 < 1.0
+    z = np.sqrt(np.maximum(0.0, 1.0 - d2))
+    # Directional upper-left illumination plus a restrained warm ambient term.
+    ndotl = np.maximum(0.0, -0.42 * u - 0.58 * v + 0.69 * z)
+    intensity = 0.48 + 0.48 * ndotl
+    base = np.array([132.0, 72.0, 39.0], np.float32)
+    rgb = base[None, None, :] * intensity[..., None]
+
+    # Stable fine-scale variation reads as tennis-ball nap at this resolution.
+    fuzz = 1.0 + 0.045 * np.sin(xx * 2.17 + yy * 3.11 + phase * 0.37)
+    rgb *= fuzz[..., None]
+
+    # A rotating curved seam: rotate local coordinates, then use an S curve.
+    c, s = np.cos(phase), np.sin(phase)
+    p = c * u + s * v
+    q = -s * u + c * v
+    curve = p - 0.31 * np.sin(np.pi * q)
+    seam = inside & (np.abs(curve) < 0.075) & (np.abs(q) < 0.88)
+    seam_edge = inside & (np.abs(curve) >= 0.075) & (np.abs(curve) < 0.13) & (np.abs(q) < 0.9)

OBSERVATION.md

Numeric scene observation and event plan

Source and measured layout

  • first_frame.png is an RGB image of exactly 256 x 144 pixels. All observations below come from local NumPy/Pillow numeric RGB inspection; no image viewer or image-reading model was used.
  • The camera is locked. The image is a close, shallow-angle view across a light beige/gray coffee-table top. The tabletop fills most of the middle and lower frame, becoming brighter from about y=40 through y=115.
  • A mustard-yellow couch occupies the upper background, especially from roughly x=75 to x=255 and y=0 to y=40. It is darkened at the far right edge by the scene lighting/vignette.
  • A black pipe enters diagonally from the upper-left. Its broad dark body occupies much of x=0..40, y=0..45, while its small circular/elliptical mouth is centered near (50,47), with a measured dark component spanning about x=44..57, y=40..53. It points down and right across the tabletop.
  • The small yellow rubber duck is centered near (173,63). Its high-chroma yellow component spans x=161..186, y=52..74 (319 yellow pixels; 403 pixels under the brighter threshold because of nearby antialiased tones). Its base/contact is near y=73. The beak projects slightly to the right around y=59..62.
  • The unobstructed table corridor from the pipe mouth to the duck is approximately (56,49) to (158,65). Perspective suggests a straight roll whose screen-space center descends slightly, from about y=49 to y=66.
  • Dark frame-edge regions and the objects already present in frame 00 are treated as locked background. Frame 00 will be copied byte-for-byte from the source RGB pixels.

Motion and physics design

  • The ball is a small brown tennis ball, radius about 6 pixels, with a shaded spherical body, contact shadow, and a curved pale seam. The seam rotates between frames to make rolling legible rather than looking like sliding.
  • The center follows one straight tabletop line from the pipe mouth toward the duck. Its screen-space speed ramps up as the ball clears the pipe, is nearly constant across the table, then transfers momentum at impact.
  • At contact, the original upright duck is removed with a numerically generated clean background plate, then transformed about its lower contact point. It compresses/tips clockwise, slides a few pixels right, rebounds slightly, and settles. A soft contact shadow follows it.
  • The pipe, couch, table, lighting, and camera remain fixed for all 20 frames.

METHOD.md

Method

Generation

The sequence was generated entirely with local Python numeric pixel operations in generate.py. I did not use an image Read/View tool, an external model, a renderer, a physics engine, network data, or any other case.

  1. Pillow loads first_frame.png as a 144 x 256 x 3 uint8 RGB array. Frame 00 is overwritten with that original array immediately before saving, guaranteeing exact equality.
  2. The duck is isolated numerically from the measured action region using an RGB chroma threshold. The connected component at the measured duck center (173,63) is selected, its contour is filled so that the eye remains part of the source object, and its edge is softly antialiased.
  3. A clean tabletop plate is produced only in the duck's small local region. The duck mask is dilated to include its source contact shadow and OpenCV's local Telea inpainting reconstructs the table texture from neighboring RGB pixels.
  4. From frames 02–12, the ball follows a straight screen-space line from the measured pipe mouth toward the duck. It accelerates while clearing the opening and then rolls at almost constant speed. After contact, its center advances by progressively smaller distances until rest. The first appearance is clipped by the mouth edge.
  5. The brown tennis ball is drawn analytically from a circular coverage field and sphere normals. It has upper-left directional lighting, deterministic fine-scale nap, a soft table-contact shadow, and a curved pale seam. Seam phase is derived from distance divided by radius, so the marking rotates consistently with rolling rather than translating unchanged.
  6. Beginning at contact in frame 12, frames use the clean plate beneath the duck. The original duck pixels and alpha are affine-transformed around the measured base contact (174,73). Clockwise angles progress through -3, -14, -31, -55 degrees, then rebound and damp to a -48 degree resting pose while the pivot shifts slightly right and down. A soft grounded shadow lengthens with the tip angle.
  7. The couch, pipe, table outside the local action corridor, lighting, and camera remain unchanged. The source is never globally warped or recolored.

Diagnostics and review

  • inspection.png is the required sampled contact sheet created by artifact_io.save.
  • transition_check.png contains frames 10–13, amplified consecutive-frame RGB differences, and nearest-neighbor enlargements of the pipe-to-duck corridor.
  • Review was numeric only, in accordance with the request. I measured connected components, bounding boxes, per-frame changed-pixel counts, RGB delta magnitudes, object centroids, and file/tensor equality. I do not claim visual review.

Encoding

artifact_io.save packs every RGB pixel as uint32 0xRRGGBB, transposes the result to (144, 256, 20), writes pixels.npz, emits frames/00.png through frames/19.png, and embeds the same packed data into the standalone index.html player.
