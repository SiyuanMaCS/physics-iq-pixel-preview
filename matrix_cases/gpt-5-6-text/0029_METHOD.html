+def clean_table(source):
+    """Remove the three source balls with a row-wise table-plane interpolation."""
+    yy, xx = np.mgrid[0:H, 0:W]
+    erase = np.zeros((H, W), np.float32)

+        disk = 1.0 - smoothstep(9.2, 12.3, np.hypot(xx - cx, yy - 54.0))
+        shadow = 1.0 - smoothstep(1.0, 1.35, np.sqrt(((xx - (cx + 1.0)) / 12.5) ** 2 + ((yy - 61.0) / 4.4) ** 2))
+        erase = np.maximum(erase, np.maximum(disk, shadow))
+
+    # Reconstruct each affected scan line from clean samples on both sides.
+    fill = source.astype(np.float32).copy()
+    left_band = source[:, 78:88].astype(np.float32).mean(axis=1)

OBSERVATION.md

Numeric source observation

first_frame.png was inspected only as a 144×256 RGB array. No image viewing tool was used.

Measured scene

  • Source is RGB, 256×144. Channel means are approximately (104.50, 99.78, 85.57).
  • The locked camera sees a light, warm-gray coffee-table plane covering most of the frame. Its RGB values rise from roughly (113,108,99) near y=40 to (154,148,142) near y=105. The front edge falls into a dark strip around y=128–143.
  • A dark/black structure occupies the far-left side, while the prompt identifies the pipe and establishes its rightward firing direction. It remains static throughout.
  • The isolated gray ball is centered at approximately (103,54), with visible support from x=94–114 and y=44–61. Its center is cool gray-blue, about (91,108,132), with a dark lower rim and a soft contact shadow.
  • The brown ball is centered at approximately (149,54), supported by x=140–158 and y=45–61. Its median body is very dark warm brown; representative body pixels range from (50,29,22) to (95,78,65).
  • The orange ball is centered at approximately (164,54), supported by x=155–173 and y=45–62. Its lit body ranges around (201,124,73) to (231,152,102). It touches the brown ball.
  • All balls have an apparent radius of about 9 px. Their contact line is nearly horizontal, so the intended dynamics are left-to-right along y≈54.
  • The scene illumination is soft and fixed; object shadows are compact, offset slightly down/right. Background, camera, table, and pipe do not move.

Construction intent

Frames after 00 will use a numerically inpainted clean table patch beneath the three source balls. Ball sprites will be reconstructed from the source pixels with feathered circular alpha, analytic sphere shading, retained local texture, rotating seam marks, and matching compact shadows. Positions follow a one-dimensional, damped impulse model designed around the measured centers and radii.

METHOD.md

Method

I treated first_frame.png only as a local numeric RGB array; I did not use an image Read/View tool or an external model.

The source geometry was measured from color and edge statistics. Frame 00 is the decoded source RGB array with no edits. For later frames, I reconstructed the small table area beneath the original balls by interpolating clean RGB samples along each affected scan line, adding back a small amount of source table grain, and feathering the replacement over the three measured ball/shadow regions.

Each ball uses a 25×25 source-derived texture. The unobstructed gray ball comes directly from its measured source crop. Because the brown and orange balls overlap in frame 00, each hidden side is completed by mirroring pixels from that ball's visible side. Circular analytic alpha, mild fixed-direction sphere shading, a restrained curved felt seam, and a compact blurred contact shadow preserve the source lighting while allowing clean translation and rotation. Texture/seam rotation is proportional to horizontal travel.

Motion follows a manually parameterized one-dimensional damped collision. The gray ball moves right at 4 px/frame until contact at frame 07. The gray ball then rebounds by 3 px, the brown ball shifts 4 px, and the already-touching orange ball receives most of the transmitted impulse, rolling from x=164 to x=201 with decreasing frame-to-frame increments. A very small one-frame horizontal squash marks each compression stage. The table, pipe, lighting, and camera remain fixed.

transition_check.png contains numeric-rendered collision frames and amplified per-pixel transition deltas. It is a generated diagnostic, not a claim of visual review. generate.py finally verifies frame-00 equality, every PNG against the in-memory RGB frames, and pixels.npz against the required uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor.
