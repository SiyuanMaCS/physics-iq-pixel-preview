OBSERVATION.md

Numeric observation and event plan

Inspection method

I inspected first_frame.png only with local Python/Pillow/NumPy/OpenCV numeric RGB operations. I did not use an image Read/View tool. Measurements came from RGB extrema and means, threshold masks, scan-line runs, Canny connected components, and Hough line coordinates.

Frame measurements

  • Source is RGB, 256 x 144. Channel extrema are R 16–255, G 12–255, B 7–255.
  • The scene is locked. The lower support/tray is a dark, low-saturation region; perspective edges numerically run through approximately (78,77)–(122,143) on the left and continue across the lower frame.
  • The saturated red reservoir is concentrated in the right-center. A strict bright-red mask (R>150, G<100, B<100) has 4,490 pixels and bounds (82,0)–(209,97), with its strongest contiguous vertical body at about x=163–209.
  • The dispenser's lower/right body has a strong edge near x=163 from about y=66–95 and another near x=206–209 from about y=52–74; its red body rounds away near y=98–104.
  • Immediately left of that red body, Canny components at (108,57,51,15) and (130,52,36,11) locate the outlet/tap hardware. The outlet terminus is treated as approximately (143,62).
  • The receiving glass is the dark transparent cavity directly below that outlet, approximately x=119–160, y=72–105. Its visible content area is modeled as a subtly tapering polygon: a wider top opening and a narrower base. Existing luminance detail will be retained through the liquid tint so the glass stays transparent.
  • The untouched backdrop and tray are preserved from the source. Only the outlet-to-glass corridor and the glass interior are animated.

METHOD.md

Method

This continuation was generated deterministically from first_frame.png with local Python, NumPy, Pillow, and OpenCV pixel operations. No image-viewing tool, external model, external engine, network resource, reference image, or mask was used.

OBSERVATION.md records the numeric RGB/edge measurements made before coding and the intended 20-frame event sequence. Frame 00 is a byte-for-byte pixel copy of the source image. Frames 01–19 retain that source as the fixed background and modify only the measured outlet-to-glass corridor and the receiving glass interior.

The red stream is an analytic, slightly sinuous subpixel-width field with a darker cylindrical edge and a narrow specular core. It starts as a bead, becomes a broken stream, then becomes continuous. The receiving glass is represented by a tapering interior mask. Its fill level rises monotonically with smooth-step timing; the fill is an alpha tint derived from the original pixel luminance so photographed texture and transparency remain present. A narrow ellipse creates the meniscus, and tiny bounded highlights model impact ripples. The stream terminates at the current liquid surface.

artifact_io.save packs RGB values as uint32 0xRRGGBB, transposes them to the required (144,256,20) tensor, writes all PNG frames, creates inspection.png, and embeds the delta-coded animation in index.html. generate.py separately creates transition_check.png with selected frames and amplified consecutive-frame differences. The script then reloads pixels.npz and every PNG, checks exact equality against the generated uint8 tensor, confirms frame 00 equals the source, and bounds consecutive changed-pixel counts to catch unintended whole-frame edits.
