+class BallSprite:
+    def __init__(self, center, radius_xy, half_size):
+        self.center = np.asarray(center, dtype=np.float32)
+        self.radius = np.asarray(radius_xy, dtype=np.float32)
+        self.half = int(half_size)
+        cx, cy = map(int, center)

+        yy, xx = np.mgrid[0:n, 0:n].astype(np.float32)
+        # A narrow feather retains photographed antialiasing without carrying a square of table.
+        dist = np.sqrt(((xx-self.half) / self.radius[0]) ** 2 +
+                       ((yy-self.half) / self.radius[1]) ** 2)
+        self.alpha = np.clip((1.08 - dist) / 0.16, 0.0, 1.0).astype(np.float32)

+    def rotated(self, degrees):

+        matrix = cv2.getRotationMatrix2D((self.half, self.half), degrees, 1.0)

OBSERVATION.md

RGB observation and event plan

Measured scene

  • Source is an RGB PNG at exactly 256 x 144. The camera is locked and the scene is a single perspective view of a light grey-beige coffee-table surface.
  • The black pipe occupies the far-left edge, approximately x=0..25, y=34..68; its visible mouth is centered near (12, 52).
  • The grey tennis ball is already outside the pipe in frame 00. Its colored/edge support is approximately x=117..136, y=37..56, with center near (126, 47) and apparent radius about 9 px.
  • The brown ball is centered near (170, 41), radius about 8 px, with support about x=161..178, y=33..49.
  • The orange ball is centered near (184, 40), radius about 8 px, with support about x=176..193, y=31..49. It touches the brown ball.
  • The pipe mouth, grey ball, and brown ball follow a perspective trajectory that rises slightly to the right: about y = 47 - 0.13 * (x - 126).
  • The tabletop is softly textured and spatially varying. Vacated ball footprints therefore need local reconstruction rather than flat-color fills.
  • All observations above came from numeric RGB statistics, connected color regions, gradients, and circle estimates. No image viewing tool was used.

The intended physics is an approximately equal-mass, damped collision chain: the incoming grey ball strikes the brown ball, which is touching the orange ball, so most visible outgoing motion appears in the orange ball while the grey and brown balls move only slightly after impact.

METHOD.md

Method

The animation is built only from first_frame.png, prompt.txt, and local numeric RGB processing. No image Read/View tool, network resource, reference case, external model, mask, or rendering engine was used.

Reconstruction

  1. I measured the source using RGB distributions, connected color regions, local gradients, and numeric circle estimates. This located the visible pipe mouth and the grey, brown, and orange ball centers.
  2. Frame 00 is the decoded source RGB array without any modification. Its saved PNG is pixel-identical (and in this run byte-identical) to first_frame.png.
  3. The grey and orange source-ball appearances are extracted as small photographic sprites with narrow feathered elliptical alpha masks. Their texture is rotated in proportion to distance traveled, so their motion reads as rolling rather than sliding.
  4. Only the original grey- and orange-ball footprints are reconstructed, using OpenCV's local TELEA inpainting over small masks on the smoothly textured tabletop. The pipe, table, brown ball, lighting, camera, and every pixel outside the ball-motion corridor remain fixed.
  5. Compact blurred contact shadows are recomputed under the moving balls. Their position follows the same shallow perspective trajectory measured between the pipe, grey ball, and brown target.

Motion and physics

  • The grey ball moves from (126,46) to contact near (153,43), with a one-pixel compression advance on frame 09 and a two-pixel damped rebound on frame 10.
  • The brown ball is already touching the orange ball. The collision is treated as a damped, approximately equal-mass three-ball impulse chain: the brown ball visibly remains near its equilibrium location while the outgoing orange ball receives most of the transmitted motion.
  • The orange ball separates on frame 10, then rolls right/up along the tabletop perspective line and decelerates toward (223,36) by frame 19.
  • The scene is sampled as 20 fixed-camera frames at 4 fps. No camera motion, cuts, or unrelated scene changes are introduced.

Output and verification

artifact_io.save writes the required 20 PNGs, the standalone player, the inspection contact sheet, and the uint32 packed tensor. generate.py additionally writes a 20-panel amplified temporal-difference diagnostic in transition_check.png.

The generator verifies:

  • shape (20, 144, 256, 3) and uint8 RGB type;
  • exact RGB equality of frame 00 and the source;
  • exact agreement between every PNG and the corresponding generated frame;
  • exact agreement between the PNG RGB values and pixels.npz stored as 0xRRGGBB with tensor shape (144, 256, 20);
  • confinement of all temporal changes to the measured ball corridor.

inspection.png and transition_check.png were generated as diagnostics, but they were not visually reviewed because the task prohibits image viewing tools. Validation was performed numerically.
