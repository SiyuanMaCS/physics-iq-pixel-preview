OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Numeric observations from first_frame.png

  • Source is RGB, 256×144 pixels. The shot is locked off; all animation must preserve this framing.
  • The tabletop/background is warm brown and gray. The cradle occupies approximately x=47–158, y=8–130.
  • A blue-black grabber enters from the left. Its strongest blue components occupy x=26–82, y=72–95, with smaller connected dark/blue pixels extending toward the left edge.
  • The five reflective balls follow a shallow perspective diagonal. Measured/estimated equilibrium centers are (88,97), (100,99), (112,101), (124,103), (136,105); the visible chrome body radius is about 6–7 px, with reflections and surrounding dark edges extending slightly farther.
  • In the source, the left pair is pulled to about (72,95) and (84,97), approximately a 13° left displacement. Their chrome highlights are around x=67–86, y=88–91.
  • The three resting-ball highlight groups are around x=107–115/y=95–97, x=118–126/y=97–99, and x=130–137/y=99–102.
  • Suspension pivots are modeled along (88,25) through (136,33), with an apparent pendulum length of about 72 px. This matches the observed diagonal perspective and pulled-pair displacement.
  • The cradle frame/base and everything outside the moving strings, spheres, and grabber remain fixed.

20-frame plan at 4 fps

  1. Frame 00: exact source frame; grabber holds the left two balls at maximum amplitude.
  2. Frame 01: grabber opens/retracts left; released pair accelerates down and right.
  3. Frame 02: released pair approaches the resting cluster; grabber is almost clear.
  4. Frame 03: first impact has occurred; the two rightmost balls begin their outward swing.
  5. Frames 04–05: right pair rises toward its maximum amplitude.
  6. Frames 06–07: right pair returns toward the cluster.
  7. Frame 08: return impact transfers momentum back to the left pair.
  8. Frames 09–10: left pair swings outward with reduced amplitude.
  9. Frames 11–12: left pair returns and makes the next contact.
  10. Frames 13–15: right pair performs a smaller damped excursion and returns.
  11. Frames 16–19: a final smaller left excursion begins and settles toward the cluster.

Only two outer balls move together on each excursion; the center ball remains near equilibrium. Excursion amplitude decays on each collision to communicate realistic losses while keeping the two-ball transfer readable at 4 fps.

METHOD.md

Method

I used only local code and numeric RGB inspection of first_frame.png; I did not use an image viewing or image-reading tool.

The first frame is copied byte-for-byte. For later frames, generate.py builds a clean photographic plate by masking the measured original sphere positions, their suspension-thread paths, and blue/black grabber pixels, then locally inpainting those limited areas with OpenCV Telea inpainting. The rest of the source frame remains unchanged.

The grabber is a feathered sprite extracted from the source RGB pixels. It opens slightly through a local remap and translates left over frames 01–04. Five anti-aliased analytic chrome spheres are composited at measured centers. Their shading combines a dark rim, broad cool upper reflection, dark horizon band, warm tabletop reflection, and compact specular glints. Thin steel suspension wires connect measured pivots to the moving spheres.

Motion follows a pendulum-arc model with a 72 px apparent length. The released left pair starts near −13°, reaches the resting balls after about 0.62 s, and transfers its motion to the two right balls. Subsequent 1.20 s excursions alternate between the outer pairs with an 0.82 damping factor per transfer. The center ball stays essentially at rest, and a subpixel contact compression is added near collisions.

artifact_io.save creates the packed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, PNG sequence, inspection sheet, and standalone player. The generator then reloads all PNGs and pixels.npz and verifies them exactly against the in-memory RGB tensor. transition_check.png shows frames 00–03 above and 3× amplified per-transition RGB differences below.
