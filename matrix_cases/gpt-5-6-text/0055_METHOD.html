duck-and-dominos · 生成方法

What was inspected

I inspected first_frame.png only through local Python RGB arrays and numeric outputs. I used row/column statistics, threshold masks, connected-component bounding boxes, sampled RGB values, and text/ASCII category maps. I did not use an image Read/View tool and do not claim visual-tool review.

The numeric measurements identified the saturated-yellow duck at x=122..166, y=35..85; eight alternating black/white dominoes arranged along the tabletop perspective line; and a brown diagonal striker arm pivoting on the dark upper-right platform.

How frames were made

generate.py uses Pillow, NumPy, and OpenCV locally:

  1. It loads the source as a 256 x 144 RGB uint8 array.
  2. The eight domino silhouettes are measured polygons. Their pixel appearance is sampled directly from the source, including the existing lighting and small texture variations.
  3. The duck is segmented with numeric yellow/orange foreground seeds, a measured enclosing polygon, and local GrabCut. This retains its source pixels, including the small dark facial details.
  4. For each animation frame, only objects that have begun moving are removed from a fresh copy of the original photograph. OpenCV TELEA inpainting fills the narrow vacated regions from nearby tabletop grain and illumination. Static objects remain as original photographed pixels, avoiding reconstruction flicker.
  5. Dominoes rotate leftward about their measured lower contact points with smoothstep angular motion and short damped landing rebounds. The schedule propagates from the small upper-right pieces toward the duck, then continues through the larger foreground-left pieces.
  6. The duck rocks through +5.5 degrees, -3.2 degrees, and smaller damped returns. This provides a physically readable transfer between the domino groups while leaving the duck upright at the end.
  7. The striker is redrawn as an antialiased, shaded brown arm around its measured pivot near (194, 29.5). It accelerates clockwise through 72 degrees, makes the initiating contact, and remains lowered.
  8. Transformed objects receive soft alpha edges and contact shadows whose offset and softness increase as a domino nears the table.
  9. Frame 00 is copied directly from first_frame.png; frame 01 is an intentional brief hold. Frames 18 and 19 are identical to show a fully settled final state at real-time 4 fps playback.

Output and verification

artifact_io.save writes the 20 PNGs, the uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, the inspection contact sheet, and the standalone HTML player. generate.py also writes transition_check.png, containing numeric consecutive-frame heat maps and changed-pixel counts.

The script then independently:

  • asserts shape (20, 144, 256, 3) and uint8 RGB frames;
  • asserts frame 00 is exactly equal to the decoded source RGB array;
  • repacks every RGB pixel as uint32 0xRRGGBB and checks exact equality with pixels.npz shape (144, 256, 20);
  • reloads every saved PNG and checks exact pixel equality with the generated frame tensor;
  • confirms through numeric motion auditing that all changed pixels remain inside the measured action area, while the locked background remains unchanged.

Run with:

python generate.py
