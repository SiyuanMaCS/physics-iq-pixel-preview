OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Numeric observations

  • Source is an RGB uint8 PNG, 256 x 144 pixels.
  • The camera is locked. A pale neutral wall fills the upper field and a warm brown tabletop begins near y=52; no camera motion should be introduced.
  • The Newton's-cradle frame occupies approximately x=65..179, y=5..136. Its top rail is near y=14..18, uprights are near x=68 and x=175, and the dark base is near x=65..179, y=110..136.
  • The resting metal-ball row is near y=96..103. Four touching balls have approximate centers x=112, 125, 138, and 151 with radius about 6 px. The fifth ball is displaced to the left near x=87, y=94 and is partly occluded by the holding setup.
  • Suspension threads are only 1-2 pixels wide. The displaced ball's thread trends from the upper attachment near x=81..83 toward x=87..89 at the ball. The right-side threads and right upright overlap around x=163..175.
  • A blue/black grabber enters from the left. Its blue body is concentrated in x=0..61, y=67..97, with darker gripping/occluding parts continuing toward the displaced ball. Numeric blue clusters are around RGB (5,122,205), (16,99,165), and (21,72,113).
  • The source has warm, low-resolution photographic texture and hard-to-soft 1-pixel edges. New elements must be antialiased and locally grain-matched rather than rendered as flat vector shapes.

20-frame event plan (4 fps)

  • 00: Exact source frame; grabber still holds the left ball at maximum amplitude.
  • 01: Grabber jaws relax and begin moving left; the ball is newly free and has only begun its inward swing.
  • 02: Released ball accelerates down and right toward the resting row; grabber withdraws farther left.
  • 03: First near-center impact/compression; all five balls appear almost touching, with only a tiny collision spacing cue.
  • 04: The far-right ball departs up/right while the four left balls remain nearly still; grabber is almost out of frame.
  • 05: Right ball reaches the first high point (largest outgoing arc).
  • 06: Right ball starts returning toward the row.
  • 07: Right ball approaches center rapidly.
  • 08: Return impact; the right ball rejoins and the far-left ball starts the reciprocal swing.
  • 09: Left ball climbs outward.
  • 10: Left ball reaches a smaller high point because of damping.
  • 11: Left ball returns inward.
  • 12: Second center impact; energy transfers back to the rightmost ball.
  • 13: Right ball rises on a smaller arc.
  • 14: Right ball reaches its second, damped high point.
  • 15: Right ball returns.
  • 16: Third center impact transfers motion left again.
  • 17: Left ball rises on a small residual arc.
  • 18: Left ball turns and returns.
  • 19: Balls are close to the resting row with a subtle residual separation/oscillation; tool is fully gone.

The pendulum paths will be computed as circular arcs about fixed suspension pivots. Contact transfers the moving endpoint to the opposite endpoint, and arc amplitude decays on each exchange. Threads will be redrawn from the fixed pivots to the moving ball centers in every altered frame.

METHOD.md

Method

I inspected first_frame.png only through local Python numeric RGB analysis: image statistics, row/column edge projections, thresholded connected components, Hough-circle candidates, RGB cluster centers, small text/ASCII luminance maps, and template correlations. I did not use an image viewing tool.

Frame 00 is the unmodified RGB source array. For frames 01–19, the fixed camera plate is retained. The blue/black grabber is isolated using its blue-channel excess plus connected dark gripping pixels; OpenCV Telea inpainting builds the small revealed background plate, while the original photographic tool pixels are feathered and translated left over frames 01–04.

The ball motion is analytic. A 78-pixel pendulum length and fixed y=19 pivots generate circular trajectories with x = rest_x + L sin(theta) and y = pivot_y + L cos(theta). The released left endpoint enters the four-ball row, transfers motion to the far-right endpoint, then alternating endpoint swings decay in amplitude at each collision. The four untouched source balls and the cradle frame remain original pixels. The moving ball is a feathered circular sprite sampled from a real chrome ball in the source, retaining the scene's photographic reflections and grain. A thin antialiased suspension filament is redrawn behind each moving ball. Only the original right endpoint is locally replaced with matched tabletop texture while that endpoint is away from rest.

pixels.npz contains pixels, a (144, 256, 20) uint32 tensor packed as 0xRRGGBB with time on the final axis. Every PNG is decoded and checked against this tensor after writing. inspection.png is a labeled 5×4 contact sheet. transition_check.png contains selected frames, amplified previous-frame differences, and enlarged motion-region crops for transition diagnostics.
