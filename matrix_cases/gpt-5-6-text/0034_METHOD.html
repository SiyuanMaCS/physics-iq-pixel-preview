Summary

This continuation was generated deterministically with local Python pixel operations. No external model, engine, network resource, other case, or subagent was used. I did not visually open the source or outputs; source understanding and quality control used numeric RGB inspection only.

Scene reconstruction

  1. first_frame.png is loaded as an RGB uint8 array. Frame 00 is copied directly from that array.
  2. Six block silhouettes were measured from RGB/HSV runs and edge coordinates. Their real source pixels are retained as texture, with 4x antialiased polygon masks.
  3. A clean plate beneath the blocks is made with narrow, local OpenCV TELEA inpainting. Each block's original footprint is exposed only when that block begins moving, so stationary source areas remain unchanged.
  4. The wooden arm is an antialiased shaded capsule with a dark edge, warm wooden face, fine highlight, tiny fixed grain marks, and a metal axle cap. It rotates clockwise around (204,54) and stops with a small recoil after contacting the purple block.

Motion and compositing

  • Each block rotates rigidly about its measured bottom contact point with an accelerating angular curve of approximately 9, 48, 83, and 87 degrees.
  • Starts are staggered every two frames from purple to blue, green, pink, red, and yellow. This makes the collision chain travel right-to-left.
  • Soft table shadows are made from offset, blurred versions of the transformed alpha masks.
  • The arm and shadows are composited behind upright blocks. Moving blocks are ordered so a falling block can overlap the neighbor it has just struck.
  • The camera/background never moves; the upper-left reference region remains byte-identical in all 20 frames.

Outputs and verification

artifact_io.save writes the RGB PNG sequence, packed uint32 tensor, inspection sheet, and standalone HTML player. generate.py also creates a 19-panel transition heatmap. The generator then checks:

  • shape (20, 144, 256, 3) and uint8 RGB frames;
  • exact pixel equality between frame 00 and first_frame.png;
  • source SHA-256 326aa04e4e8021ee5e98c589cc9f521b06d97ce8baa6fbd830073b7067404ef2;
  • all 20 PNG arrays against the generated frames;
  • pixels.npz shape (144, 256, 20), uint32 type, 0xRRGGBB packing, and exact equality with every PNG.
