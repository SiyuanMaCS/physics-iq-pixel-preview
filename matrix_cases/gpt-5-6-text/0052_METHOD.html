OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Numeric source measurements

  • Source: first_frame.png, RGB uint8, exactly 256 x 144 pixels.
  • The camera is locked. The upper background is a soft neutral gray; a warm brown tabletop fills the lower half, with a slanted horizon around y=62-76.
  • The Newton's-cradle frame occupies approximately x=84-205, y=7-143. Its upper metal rails run from about (91, 8) to (203, 14); dark upright/support lines are strongest near x=85-88, x=106-110, x=163-168, and x=197-204.
  • Five small steel balls form the working group around x=110-174 and y=91-113. Their apparent diameter is approximately 11-13 pixels. The closely spaced resting positions are treated as centers x=116, 128, 140, 152, 164 at y=103, with paired suspension strings terminating at each center.
  • Two left balls are initially displaced together toward the left amplitude and are partly occluded by the tool. Their trajectory is reconstructed as a rigid adjacent pair on equal-length pendulums.
  • The grabber/tool is blue-black and enters diagonally from the lower-left. Strong blue pixels form one connected region from x=0-106, y=94-143. Its narrow working end reaches the displaced pair; the broad handle leaves down-left after release.
  • The table, background, frame, and camera remain fixed. Only the grabber jaws/handle, the two released balls, their strings, and the corresponding two-ball response on the opposite side move.

All observations above were obtained from local numeric RGB/HSV values, connected components, edge/line detection, and text-only pixel summaries. No image viewer or image-reading tool was used.

METHOD.md

Method

Construction

  1. first_frame.png is decoded locally as an RGB uint8 array. Frame 00 is returned directly from that array without drawing, filtering, resampling, or color conversion.
  2. Scene geometry was measured numerically from RGB/HSV values, connected components, Canny edges, and Hough line evidence. These measurements locate the cradle rails, five ball positions, the displaced pair, and the blue-black tool. No image viewer, image Read/View tool, external model, or external reference was used.
  3. The largest saturated blue connected component in the lower-left is used to isolate the tool. Its closed convex silhouette includes the tool's dark inserts. The photographed RGB texture and a softly antialiased alpha mask are shifted down-left over frames 01-05; two narrow dark-blue jaws open during withdrawal.
  4. A local clean plate is made only beneath the dynamic tool, original balls, and suspension paths with OpenCV Telea inpainting. Unaffected pixels retain the source photograph. The narrow near-side steel upright hidden by the tool is reconstructed behind the motion.
  5. The five ball resting centers follow the photographed perspective. Each ball is attached to paired suspension wires and moves along a fixed-length pendulum arc. The input pair approaches the center together, the impulse transfers to the right pair, the returning impulse transfers back to the left pair, and a smaller final rightward swing demonstrates damping. Contact occurs exactly at frames 04, 10, and 16.
  6. Steel balls are rendered from analytic sphere normals: dark Fresnel-like rims, a neutral sky reflection, a compact lamp highlight, a cool edge reflection, and a warm lower reflection from the table. The balls use subpixel antialiasing and retain fixed adjacent spacing as pairs.
  7. artifact_io.save writes the packed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, all 20 PNGs, the inspection sheet, and the standalone player. transition_check.png contains five representative before/after transitions and 4x absolute RGB deltas.

Timing

  • 20 frames at 4 fps, 256 x 144 RGB.
  • Frame 00: exact held source.
  • Frames 01-04: jaw opening, tool withdrawal, and incoming-pair descent to impact.
  • Frames 05-10: first right-pair swing and return impact.
  • Frames 11-16: damped left-pair swing and second impact.
  • Frames 17-19: smaller second right-pair swing, still in progress at the end.

Verification

generate.py asserts all of the following after writing:

  • frame array shape is 20 x 144 x 256 x 3 and dtype is uint8;
  • frame 00 is exactly equal to the decoded source array;
  • every saved PNG exactly equals its in-memory frame;
  • every PNG, packed as uint32 0xRRGGBB, exactly equals the corresponding plane in pixels.npz;
  • every adjacent transition contains meaningful changed pixels.

The final pass also counted compact neutral-bright connected components around the mechanism to confirm that reconstructed frames contain five, rather than an accidental residual sixth, steel-ball highlights. Review was numeric and text-only; no claim of image-tool visual review is made.
