magnet-wrench · 生成方法

This sequence was created with local Python pixel operations only.

generate.py loads the 256 x 144 RGB source and identifies the wrench as the largest connected, low-saturation mid/light-gray component in its numerically measured platform neighborhood. It expands that component by only a one-pixel collar and removes the original tool/contact area from a clean plate with OpenCV Telea inpainting. The wrench texture is represented as the signed RGB difference between the untouched source and the clean plate. Adding that residual back at zero degrees reconstructs the source exactly; rotating it carries the original highlights, dark edges, small gaps, and contact shading together.

Frames 01–19 rotate that source-derived residual clockwise around the measured wrench centroid (130.34, 68.46). The cumulative rotation follows a smoothstep curve from 0 to 280 degrees, providing a physically plausible smooth start and stop at the fixed 4 fps sampling rate. No camera motion or unrelated scene changes are introduced. Frame 00 bypasses all compositing and is an exact copy of first_frame.png.

The script writes all 20 PNGs, packs them into a uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor of shape (20, 144, 256) in pixels.npz, and writes two diagnostics. inspection.png is a labeled 5 x 4 contact sheet; transition_check.png contains the source plus amplified consecutive-frame differences. index.html plays the PNG sequence at 4 fps.

Validation is performed by decoding every written PNG and comparing it exactly with its corresponding packed tensor plane. The generator also asserts exact frame-00 identity and asserts that all animated changes remain inside a conservative local wrench region. Analysis and validation were numeric; no image read/view tool was used, so no claim of visual review is made.
