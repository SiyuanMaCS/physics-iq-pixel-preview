OBSERVATION.md

Numeric scene observation

All measurements below were obtained from local RGB arrays; no image viewing tool was used.

Source and layout

  • Source: first_frame.png, RGB uint8, 256 x 144.
  • Locked camera. A curved wooden tabletop fills the lower field; a cool neutral wall fills the upper field.
  • The yellow duck is the single connected yellow region under the threshold R>155, G>120, B<70: 1,192 pixels, bounding box (105,46)-(144,88), centroid approximately (123.1,68.1).
  • The actuator is the large near-black platform at image right, approximately (209,58)-(255,106).
  • The narrow brown arm lies across approximately (181,73)-(220,82), pivoting near (219,73) and initially pointing down-left toward the first domino.
  • Alternating upright dominoes run right-to-left at approximate base pivots:
    • black: (178,103), source bounds about (172,78)-(183,104)
    • white: (166,100), source bounds about (162,74)-(172,100)
    • black: (157,96), source bounds about (152,71)-(161,96)
    • white: (147,92), source bounds about (143,67)-(152,92)
    • center pieces are substantially occluded by the foreground duck
    • black: (104,75), source bounds about (101,56)-(108,76)
    • white: (99,72), source bounds about (95,52)-(104,72)
    • black: (93,70), source bounds about (88,50)-(99,71)
    • white: (86,67), source bounds about (82,48)-(92,68)
  • The platform's wooden arm initially touches the upper-right area of the rightmost black domino. The causal direction is therefore from right to left.
  • Dark/neutral structures reaching the upper-left frame boundary are left unchanged: they are not required to move during the five-second causal demonstration and their exact silhouette is safest preserved.

Motion and compositing plan

  • Frame 00 is copied byte-for-byte at the RGB pixel level.
  • Build a clean tabletop plate only beneath the moving arm and domino masks, using deterministic local texture synthesis from neighboring tabletop pixels plus a light inpaint pass.
  • Preserve the platform, duck, table, wall, exposure, grain, and framing.
  • Rotate the arm clockwise about (219,73), using a foreground mask derived from its brown stripe and soft antialiased compositing.
  • Rotate each visible domino around its measured bottom pivot. Pieces fall leftward in causal order. A damped settling angle and subtle contact shadow make the motion readable at 4 fps.
  • For the center gap, show a short delay as the chain passes behind the opaque duck; do not deform or translate the duck.

METHOD.md

Method

The animation is a deterministic 2D pixel reconstruction made entirely from first_frame.png. No image viewing tool, external model, renderer, reference image, mask, or network service was used.

  1. The source PNG is loaded as a 256 x 144 RGB uint8 array.
  2. The duck, actuator arm, and eight visible domino sections are located with numeric RGB thresholds and measured polygon coordinates. The duck silhouette is retained as a foreground occlusion mask.
  3. Each domino is cut from the source with a small polygonal alpha mask. Its motion is an affine rotation around the measured lower contact point, with a smooth two-frame acceleration and deceleration to a roughly 80-degree resting angle.
  4. The wooden arm is extracted from its diagonal brown stripe and rotated clockwise around the right-platform pivot. It begins moving before the contacted domino.
  5. Only pixels exposed by a moving object are replaced. Their clean tabletop/platform values are reconstructed once with OpenCV's deterministic Telea inpainting from immediately neighboring source pixels. Upright pieces and the rest of the source remain pixel-identical until they move.
  6. Transformed cutouts are composited with antialiased alpha and small blurred down-right contact shadows. The original duck pixels are finally restored over the moving layers so the central transfer remains correctly occluded.
  7. artifact_io.save writes the uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, PNG frames, inspection sheet, and standalone HTML player. transition_check.png contains representative output frames and 4x-amplified consecutive RGB deltas.

Frame 00 is explicitly copied from the source and verified for exact equality. The saved PNGs are also checked against the packed tensor after generation.
