OBSERVATION.md

Observation and event plan

Numeric scene measurements

  • Source: first_frame.png, RGB, 256 × 144 pixels.
  • Camera/background: locked frame. The upper field is a cool, nearly neutral background (typical block means about RGB 170–210); the woven brown support enters diagonally around y=55–75 and fills the lower half.
  • Dispenser: the principal saturated red reservoir is approximately x=125–195, y=35–69. Its glass/highlight structure continues around that region, with a narrow darker/red assembly at x=117–139. The most plausible lower outlet region is around x=123–132, y=61–69.
  • Receiving glass: centered near x=128. The glass bowl is about x=100–157 and y=76–141. Existing bright/dark red liquid forms one connected component with bounding box x=102–156, y=88–141; the broad stable liquid body begins at about y=94, so the glass is approximately half full. Its tapered body narrows toward the bottom.
  • Clear fall gap: roughly y=69–91 near x=125–132. This is the smallest and least disruptive region in which to show the transfer.
  • Lighting: soft and warm from above/left, with substantial photographed texture and specular glass highlights. Added liquid should remain dark crimson, semi-transparent at edges, and retain local background texture.

All measurements above came from local RGB arrays, connected components, row/column color statistics, and gradient inspection. No image viewing tool was used.

METHOD.md

Method

Actual generation method

The animation is a deterministic 2D continuation built only from first_frame.png and local mathematical/pixel operations in generate.py.

  1. The source PNG is converted to an RGB uint8 array. Frame 00 is an unmodified copy.
  2. The receiving glass is modeled with a tapered analytic interior mask. Its liquid level rises from about y=94 to y=83. Pixels for the new liquid are sampled from the photographed red liquid already below the original surface, shifted/warped upward, gently color-adjusted, and alpha-composited with feathered glass edges. This retains the source's natural grain and reflections.
  3. A narrow crimson stream is drawn from the measured outlet area near (129, 64). Supersampled paths and ellipses create the bead, elongation, connected flow, pinch-off, and final droplet with antialiased edges, a darker body, and a weak warm specular edge.
  4. The upper reservoir level falls about five pixels as the lower glass gains roughly eleven rows over a narrower cross-section. Only pixels classified as existing red liquid inside the measured reservoir bounds are changed. Cleared pixels are filled from nearby photographed glass/background samples with a residual red cast.
  5. The receiving meniscus uses a small sinusoidal displacement while flow is active. Its amplitude damps after shutoff, leaving the apparatus and all non-liquid scene structure stationary.
  6. artifact_io.save writes the RGB PNG sequence, packs every pixel as uint32 0xRRGGBB with tensor shape (144, 256, 20), and builds the standalone HTML player and inspection sheet. generate.py also writes a before/after/amplified-difference transition sheet.

Verification performed

  • Confirmed output shape: 20 frames × 144 × 256 × 3, RGB uint8.
  • Confirmed frames/00.png is exactly equal to first_frame.png pixel-for-pixel.
  • Repacked the generated RGB frames to 0xRRGGBB and confirmed exact equality with every value in pixels.npz.
  • Reloaded all 20 saved PNGs and confirmed exact equality with the in-memory frame tensors.
  • Measured every edit against frame 00. Changes remain within x=103–194 and y=37–94 (apart from the intentionally smaller stream/glint bounds), so camera, basket, vessel outlines, and background remain fixed.
  • Measured the receiving red-surface proxy at y=92 initially, y=89 at frame 08, y=85 at frame 11, and y=83 from frame 14 onward.

Inspection was numeric only: RGB statistics, masks, connected components, edge measurements, change bounds, and tensor comparisons. No image viewing/reading tool or external model was used.
