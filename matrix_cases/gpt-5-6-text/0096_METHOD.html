magnet-domino · 生成方法

Generation

The sequence was generated entirely with local Python pixel operations using Pillow, NumPy, and OpenCV. No image viewer, external reference, network service, AI model, mask, or rendering engine was used.

  1. first_frame.png was loaded as an RGB uint8 array. Frame 00 is a direct array copy of this input.
  2. The domino was isolated inside a measured five-point support polygon. A numeric color test retained the cool/neutral plastic and rejected both the warm tabletop and the purple magnet. Small components were removed and the result was morphologically closed. The final hard domino mask contains 682 pixels.
  3. A clean plate was made only under the source domino. OpenCV Navier–Stokes inpainting reconstructed the surrounding tabletop, while the portion belonging to the black platform was rebuilt from a least-squares color plane fitted to known platform pixels plus deterministic, low-amplitude correlated texture. Purple magnet pixels were explicitly protected from the removal mask.
  4. The photographed domino pixels were premultiplied by a soft subpixel alpha and rotated around (101,48). Premultiplied bicubic affine warping avoids black resampling halos.
  5. The visual rotation is clockwise: OpenCV receives the negative of the scheduled angle. The schedule is 104 * smoothstep(frame/19) degrees, providing a natural ease-in and ease-out over the 19 transitions.
  6. A tight, blurred, down-right contact shadow follows the transformed alpha. It is clipped to the measured platform top and has a maximum opacity of 24%. The magnet, platform outside the repair, tabletop, wall, and camera remain locked.

Output

artifact_io.save packs each RGB pixel as uint32 0xRRGGBB, transposes the tensor to (144,256,20), and writes pixels.npz. It also writes the 20 RGB PNGs, the inspection contact sheet, and the standalone HTML player. generate.py separately creates transition_check.png, whose rows contain selected adjacent frames and their 5× amplified absolute pixel delta.

Numeric validation

  • Array shape and dtype: (20,144,256,3), uint8.
  • Frame 00 exactly equals first_frame.png as an RGB array.
  • All changed pixels across frames lie inside x=74..130, y=21..74.
  • Pixels at the magnet center (60,60) and distant tabletop (200,120) remain unchanged, and the complete area outside the local action box is bit-exact across all 20 frames.
  • All 19 adjacent frame pairs are distinct. Their changed-pixel counts range from 823 to 1,109 and follow the easing profile.
  • A numeric cool-object PCA check measures the visible long-axis orientation progressing from about 11.5° to the equivalent of 120.4° in image coordinates, confirming clockwise screen-space rotation.
  • Every saved PNG was loaded back and compared with the corresponding frame array.
  • The saved uint32 tensor was loaded back and compared exactly with the 0xRRGGBB values computed from the frames.

The inspection images are provided for downstream review; no claim of visual review is made because image viewing tools were prohibited for this task.
