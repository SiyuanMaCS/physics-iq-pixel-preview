liquid-on-duck · 生成方法

Overview

The animation was generated locally and deterministically from first_frame.png with Python, NumPy, Pillow, and OpenCV pixel operations. No external images, masks, models, engines, network resources, or image viewing tools were used.

The source frame is the immutable background for every rendered frame. Frame 00 is copied directly from the decoded RGB source. For frames 01–19, changes are restricted to the liquid-action area around the outlet, duck, and tray immediately below it; the camera, background, glass body, basket, tray rim, and all other scene pixels remain locked.

Scene extraction

  • The yellow duck was isolated inside a measured x=102..157, y=48..106 region using its distinctive HSV hue, saturation, and value range.
  • Small gaps in that component were closed morphologically, the largest connected component was selected, and only the final edge was softly antialiased.
  • The liquid origin, duck crown, and tray contact region were positioned from numeric color-component bounds and coordinate samples recorded in OBSERVATION.md.

Liquid simulation and rendering

  • The falling liquid uses supersampled analytic masks (4x resolution, then Lanczos downsampling) for smooth subpixel stream edges.
  • Frames 01–02 form and elongate a bead. Frames 03–16 use a slightly pulsing, mildly curved continuous stream. Frames 17–18 replace it with separated falling droplets; frame 19 has no newly drawn stream.
  • The coating on the duck is built from a growing crown impact field plus three gravity-driven, slightly meandering rivulets. The coating is clipped to the numerically extracted duck silhouette.
  • The liquid color is not a flat fill. A translucent red tint is calculated from each underlying pixel's luminance so the photographic duck and tray textures continue to show through. A darker stream edge and narrow warm highlights suggest thickness and wet gloss.
  • The tray puddle grows from the duck's base using an irregular analytic ellipse. It is clipped away from the duck and limited to the tray area. A lower rim, specular arc, and short-lived expanding ripples provide depth and impact feedback.
  • Tiny crown splashes pulse during the main pour. All random-looking variation is analytic and deterministic.

Artifact production

artifact_io.save writes:

  • pixels.npz as a (144, 256, 20) uint32 tensor packed as 0xRRGGBB
  • frames/00.png through frames/19.png
  • inspection.png
  • the standalone index.html player

generate.py additionally writes transition_check.png, containing selected adjacent frame pairs and numerically amplified RGB differences.

Verification performed

  • Confirmed the rendered array has 20 RGB uint8 frames at 256 x 144.
  • Confirmed frame 00 is pixel-identical to first_frame.png.
  • Reloaded every saved PNG and checked it against the in-memory frame.
  • Repacked every PNG to uint32 0xRRGGBB and checked it against the corresponding plane in pixels.npz.
  • Confirmed every adjacent pair is distinct.
  • Confirmed all animation changes stay inside a conservative x=85..185, y=38..120 action region.
  • Numerically inspected red-pixel growth in the stream, duck, and puddle regions and printed per-transition change counts/bounds.

The supplied inspection sheets were generated for artifact review, but no claim of visual review is made because the task prohibited image Read/View tools.
