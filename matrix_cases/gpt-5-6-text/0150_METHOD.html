+def smoothstep(edge0, edge1, x):
+    """Hermite interpolation, also supporting reversed edges."""
+    q = np.clip((x - edge0) / (edge1 - edge0), 0.0, 1.0)
+    return q * q * (3.0 - 2.0 * q)

+def blend_region(frame, x0, y0, rgb, alpha):
+    """Alpha blend a small float RGB/alpha patch into uint8 frame."""
+    h, w = alpha.shape
+    x1, y1 = x0 + w, y0 + h
+    sx0, sy0 = max(0, -x0), max(0, -y0)
+    sx1, sy1 = w - max(0, x1 - W), h - max(0, y1 - H)
+    dx0, dy0 = max(0, x0), max(0, y0)
+    dx1, dy1 = min(W, x1), min(H, y1)
+    if dx0 >= dx1 or dy0 >= dy1:
+        return
+    a = alpha[sy0:sy1, sx0:sx1, None]
+    src = rgb[sy0:sy1, sx0:sx1]
+    dst = frame[dy0:dy1, dx0:dx1].astype(np.float32)
+    frame[dy0:dy1, dx0:dx1] = np.clip(dst * (1.0 - a) + src * a, 0, 255).astype(np.uint8)

OBSERVATION.md

Numeric observation and event plan

Source measurements

  • first_frame.png is RGB, 256 x 144, uint8. All later work uses this decoded pixel array; no image viewer or image-reading UI was used.
  • The image is a locked camera view. The lower tabletop occupies roughly y=58 through y=143. Its local RGB is warm gray/brown, generally about (118..140, 108..133, 100..129), and its existing grain must remain static.
  • A dark tube enters from the upper-left. Thresholded connected components below grayscale 70 join from x=0 through x=114 and y=0 through y=56. Its approximately circular warm-dark outlet is centered near (104, 48), spanning about x=96..114 and y=39..57.
  • The pale lampshade is the cool bright component around x=110..163 and y=0..36, with RGB near (166,176,201). Its narrower lower support continues toward the tabletop. It remains stationary and behind the moving ball.
  • The outlet bottom and tabletop boundary establish a rolling contact line near y=58. A ball radius of about 8.5 pixels therefore places its center near y=49.5.
  • The source is subdued and slightly noisy, so the inserted ball should use modest warm-brown values, a soft one-pixel antialiased edge, fine deterministic texture, and a small soft contact shadow rather than clean vector-flat colors.

Motion and physics

  • Frame 00 is copied byte-for-byte from first_frame.png.
  • The dark circular source feature at (104,48) serves as the visible tube interior as the ball clears it.
  • A brown tennis ball of radius 8.5 px translates rightward at 8.25 px/frame (33 px/s at 4 fps), on a straight, nearly horizontal track.
  • The center starts at x=104.0 in frame 00, advances from x=112.25 in frame 01 to x=260.75 in frame 19, and ends mostly beyond the right boundary. Center y changes only from 49.1 to 49.8 to follow the subtly receding tabletop line.
  • Rolling angle is -(x - 104) / 8.5 radians, so the seam and surface flecks rotate consistently without sliding. The contact shadow translates with the ball and remains on the table.

METHOD.md

Method

The sequence is a deterministic local pixel composite generated from first_frame.png. No external images, masks, models, engines, or network resources are used, and the source image was inspected numerically rather than with an image viewer.

generate.py first preserves the supplied RGB image as frame 00 without modification. For frames 01–19 it copies that same source background, subtly resolves the source's warm-dark circular outlet into a stable tube interior, and analytically composites a ball plus its contact shadow. Re-copying the original for every frame keeps the camera, lamp, tube, table grain, and compression texture perfectly locked.

The ball is an 8.5-pixel analytic sphere. Lambert-style illumination, a restrained specular term, warm table bounce, deterministic multi-frequency felt variation, a curved tan tennis seam, and a one-pixel coverage ramp produce its brown textured appearance. Its texture and seam are mapped in material coordinates and rotated by angle = -distance / radius, tying spin to rightward translation without slipping. A soft elliptical Gaussian shadow follows the contact point.

Motion is constant at 8.25 pixels per frame, or 33 pixels per second at 4 fps. The center advances from the tube outlet at x=104 to x=260.75 while maintaining contact with the measured tabletop line. The last frame leaves only the trailing part of the ball visible at the right edge.

artifact_io.save encodes every frame into the required uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor and produces the PNG frames, inspection sheet, and standalone HTML player. generate.py additionally produces transition_check.png, then decodes every tensor plane and asserts exact equality against every saved PNG as well as exact equality between frame 00 and first_frame.png.
