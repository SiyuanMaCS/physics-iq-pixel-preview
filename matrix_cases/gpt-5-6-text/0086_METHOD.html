liquid-on-duck · 生成方法

I used the supplied photograph as a completely fixed plate. Frame 00 is an exact copy of first_frame.png; no reconstruction, generative model, external engine, network source, or reference image was used.

The source was inspected only through local numeric RGB/HSV samples, thresholded connected components, per-row bounds, and Sobel edge magnitudes. That inspection identified the central yellow duck at x=131..176, y=50..97, the tray below it, and—most importantly—the already-visible narrow liquid stream at x=157..159, y=21..50. No image Read/View tool was used, so no claim of interactive visual review is made.

generate.py performs the continuation with deterministic NumPy, Pillow, and SciPy pixel operations:

  1. It isolates the largest central yellow component to obtain a soft duck surface mask while keeping the photographed silhouette fixed.
  2. It reinforces the existing stream with a subpixel sinusoidal centerline, a small width pulse, and a descending specular glint. The outlet position remains fixed.
  3. It grows a red crown impact and an antialiased curved rivulet down the duck's screen-right contour. Two narrow late branches and moving broken highlights make downward flow legible while preserving the eye and beak.
  4. From frame 09 onward, it grows a shallow perspective ellipse on the tray, clipped away from the duck, plus a few small nearby droplets. The pool is composited behind the duck-side rivulet.
  5. Liquid color inherits luminance from the underlying photograph but is mapped to saturated red, with a dark wet meniscus and pale pink-red glints. This preserves the source lighting and makes the coating read as glossy translucent liquid rather than flat paint.

All vector masks are rasterized at 4x resolution and Lanczos-downsampled for photographic antialiasing. Only liquid-related pixels change; camera, wall, glass dispenser, woven basket, baking tray, and rubber duck geometry are fixed.

The script saves all deliverables with artifact_io.save, creates a boosted consecutive-difference contact sheet as transition_check.png, then verifies:

  • 20 RGB uint8 frames at 256x144;
  • exact frame-00 equality with the source pixel array;
  • exact equality of every PNG with the packed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor in pixels.npz;
  • non-empty pixel changes at every transition.
