OBSERVATION.md

Source-frame observations

Inspection was performed with local Python only, using numeric RGB/HSV values, thresholded component bounds, row runs, and Sobel magnitudes. No image viewer or image-reading UI was used.

  • Canvas: 256 x 144 RGB. The camera is locked off.
  • Background: cool pale wall across the upper corners; tan/brown woven basket texture occupies the middle background.
  • Beverage dispenser: the red liquid reservoir is cropped by the top of frame. Its strong red regions span approximately x=114..190, y=0..20, interrupted by blue/white glass reflections around x=150..161. The dispenser is resting on the basket.
  • Existing outlet/flow alignment: a narrow coral-red vertical feature is already present around x=157..159 from y=21 to y=50. Numeric RGB samples along it are about (240,110,80) over the basket, and it terminates exactly at the duck's crown. This is treated as the dispenser's active liquid stream.
  • Duck: the principal connected yellow component is x=131..176, y=50..97. The head is roughly x=140..169, y=50..72; its crown is centered near (157, 51). The wider body is approximately x=131..177, y=70..98. The small orange-red beak/detail is around x=148..161, y=72..77. The animation keeps those facial details readable.
  • Baking tray: dark charcoal/steel, beginning near y=57 behind the duck and filling most of the lower frame. Its rim and perspective edges remain fixed. The duck/tray contact is around x=149..163, y=97..100.
  • The intended fluid path therefore runs from the fixed outlet at (158, 21), down to the crown at (158, 50), over the duck's screen-right head/body contour, and into a shallow pool on the tray at the duck's base.

Twenty-frame event plan (4 fps, 5 seconds)

  • 00 (0.00 s): exact source frame, byte-for-byte pixel equality.
  • 01-02: established narrow stream subtly pulses; first red wet specks appear on the crown.
  • 03-05: crown splash grows and a glossy rivulet starts down the duck's screen-right side; a couple of tiny droplets separate near the impact.
  • 06-08: uninterrupted pour; rivulet reaches the shoulder/body and branches slightly while preserving the eye and beak.
  • 09-11: liquid reaches the tray, creating a small dark-red contact pool and a bright wet edge at the base.
  • 12-15: steady stream continues; wet red coverage on the right flank expands and the shallow pool spreads laterally in tray perspective.
  • 16-18: strongest visible pour, with a brief crown splash pulse, glints moving down the stream, and a few nearby tray droplets.
  • 19 (5.00 s): stable end state: dispenser still pouring, duck visibly coated by a glossy red rivulet, and a modest red pool collected on the otherwise empty baking tray.

Only the stream, liquid coating/splashes, and resulting tray pool move or grow. All camera, dispenser, basket, tray, and duck geometry remains registered to frame 00.

METHOD.md

Method

I used the supplied photograph as a completely fixed plate. Frame 00 is an exact copy of first_frame.png; no reconstruction, generative model, external engine, network source, or reference image was used.

The source was inspected only through local numeric RGB/HSV samples, thresholded connected components, per-row bounds, and Sobel edge magnitudes. That inspection identified the central yellow duck at x=131..176, y=50..97, the tray below it, and—most importantly—the already-visible narrow liquid stream at x=157..159, y=21..50. No image Read/View tool was used, so no claim of interactive visual review is made.

generate.py performs the continuation with deterministic NumPy, Pillow, and SciPy pixel operations:

  1. It isolates the largest central yellow component to obtain a soft duck surface mask while keeping the photographed silhouette fixed.
  2. It reinforces the existing stream with a subpixel sinusoidal centerline, a small width pulse, and a descending specular glint. The outlet position remains fixed.
  3. It grows a red crown impact and an antialiased curved rivulet down the duck's screen-right contour. Two narrow late branches and moving broken highlights make downward flow legible while preserving the eye and beak.
  4. From frame 09 onward, it grows a shallow perspective ellipse on the tray, clipped away from the duck, plus a few small nearby droplets. The pool is composited behind the duck-side rivulet.
  5. Liquid color inherits luminance from the underlying photograph but is mapped to saturated red, with a dark wet meniscus and pale pink-red glints. This preserves the source lighting and makes the coating read as glossy translucent liquid rather than flat paint.

All vector masks are rasterized at 4x resolution and Lanczos-downsampled for photographic antialiasing. Only liquid-related pixels change; camera, wall, glass dispenser, woven basket, baking tray, and rubber duck geometry are fixed.

The script saves all deliverables with artifact_io.save, creates a boosted consecutive-difference contact sheet as transition_check.png, then verifies:

  • 20 RGB uint8 frames at 256x144;
  • exact frame-00 equality with the source pixel array;
  • exact equality of every PNG with the packed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor in pixels.npz;
  • non-empty pixel changes at every transition.
