OBSERVATION.md

Numeric scene observation

Inspection method: local Pillow/NumPy/OpenCV numeric RGB analysis only. No image-viewing tool was used. The source is an RGB uint8 image at 256×144. Mean RGB is approximately (124.8, 110.4, 103.0); the scene is a warm, subdued locked-off close-up.

Measured layout

  • The glass cutting board fills the frame. Its stable diagonal/rim edges are strongest near the far left and far right borders; these areas must not move.
  • The tangerine half is centered near (127, 64). Its warm, high-saturation body spans approximately x=98–155 and y=34–94. Strict orange pixels form two main connected components: a left lobe with bounding box x=99–125, y=39–88 and a right lobe with bounding box x=125–154, y=35–85.
  • The knife/cut corridor is a narrow, mostly vertical reflective blue-gray/dark band. Above the fruit its center trends from about x=131 at y=0 to x=128 at y=35; through the fruit it continues toward about x=123 at y=88. Strong near-vertical edges confirm this orientation.
  • Orange highlights peak around RGB (228, 134, 51); darker peel/flesh texture is near (180, 96, 43) and (127, 99, 84). The board near the subject is mainly neutral/warm gray-brown around RGB (108–135, 96–126, 90–125).
  • Camera, board, illumination, and frame crop remain fixed. Motion is restricted to the existing knife and tangerine, plus their soft contact shadows.

METHOD.md

Method

This sequence was generated locally from first_frame.png with deterministic Python pixel operations. No external model, engine, network source, reference image, mask, score, or image-viewing tool was used.

Construction

  1. Pillow, NumPy, and OpenCV numeric RGB analysis measured the source palette, strong edges, orange connected components, and the knife corridor.
  2. A skewed analytic citrus silhouette was fitted around the measured tangerine bounds. Source chroma slightly tightens this mask while a high analytic floor retains dark peel/flesh detail. The silhouette is divided at the measured slanted knife centerline into left and right source-textured pieces.
  3. The narrow knife mask follows the measured near-vertical reflective band. Its motion texture retains source luminance and shading but is partially neutralized to a cool metallic tint so fruit-colored reflections do not move upward as false orange metal during withdrawal.
  4. Covered board pixels are reconstructed locally with Telea inpainting and bilateral smoothing. This clean plate is used only where moving subject masks vacate source pixels; all untouched source pixels remain unchanged.
  5. Frames 01–11 animate a eased 23-pixel knife downstroke with slight fruit compression and subpixel opposing lobe shifts. Frames 12–19 withdraw the knife while the pieces separate by about 11 pixels in total and rotate by about ±3 degrees. Soft contact shadows, a narrow cut shadow, subdued juicy inner rims, and very restrained motion smear are derived with local analytic masks.
  6. Frame 00 is assigned directly from the decoded source array with no processing. artifact_io.save writes the packed uint32 tensor, PNG sequence, inspection sheet, and standalone player. A separate transition sheet contains selected frames and 5× amplified absolute adjacent-frame differences.

Validation

  • Array shape and type: (20, 144, 256, 3), uint8, RGB.
  • frames/00.png is byte-for-pixel identical to the decoded first_frame.png RGB array.
  • Every saved PNG was decoded and compared to its in-memory frame.
  • Every PNG was repacked as 0xRRGGBB and compared to the corresponding pixels.npz uint32 tensor plane.
  • Motion remains local to the measured subject area; the union of changed pixels has bounding box (92, 0)–(173, 125).
  • Numeric component tracking shows the two main strict-orange centroids separating from approximately x=112/141 in the source to x=107/148 in the end pose.
  • Temporal changed-pixel counts were checked for every adjacent pair; all 19 transitions contain motion with no whole-frame camera change.

No claim of visual review is made; inspection was limited to numeric RGB statistics, connected components, edge/line measurements, low-resolution categorical color maps, and transition metrics.
