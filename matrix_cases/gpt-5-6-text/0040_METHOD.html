OBSERVATION.md

Numeric scene observation

first_frame.png was inspected only through local RGB arrays, channel statistics, threshold masks, scanlines, and edge/Hough measurements. No image-viewing tool was used.

  • Source is RGB, 256 x 144. The overall mean RGB is approximately (117, 101, 88).
  • Most of the left/background area is warm brown. The right side contains a cool, glass-like quadrilateral. Its strongest measured edges include (174,15)-(255,56) and (185,106)-(242,49), with a lower edge continuing toward (207,143).
  • The saturated orange subject is concentrated at x=144..190, y=45..91; including its dim rind/shadow, the working fruit footprint is approximately x=140..198, y=42..104. Its visual center is near (168,72).
  • A strong narrow diagonal structure crosses the frame and fruit. Parallel edge evidence includes (127,3)-(187,80), (117,25)-(157,70), and (170,101)-(197,140). The inferred knife/cut direction is therefore approximately the vector (0.62,0.78), with centerline x = 115 + 0.75*y. This passes through the orange footprint near its center.
  • Lighting and camera remain fixed. Existing RGB texture is the primary source for all moving pixels; synthetic pixels are limited to locally sampled fill, contact shadow, moist cut edges, and tiny juice highlights.

20-frame event plan (4 fps, 5 seconds)

  • Frame 00: exact untouched source frame.
  • Frames 01-03: knife settles into contact; subpixel/one-pixel diagonal rocking and a compact contact shadow begin, without moving the board or camera.
  • Frames 04-08: first sawing stroke. Knife texture travels a few pixels along its own axis; the cut grows progressively through the upper half of the tangerine.
  • Frames 09-13: return stroke and firm press. The incision reaches the lower rind; a narrow dark seam plus pale moist pith edges become visible immediately beside the blade.
  • Frames 14-16: breakthrough. The complete cut is established, the two fruit sides relax about 1-2 pixels apart normal to the cutting axis, and a small localized board shadow/juice glint appears at the exit point.
  • Frames 17-19: the knife eases back slightly while the separated cut remains. The final frame rests on the completed slice with no camera motion.

Motion is deliberately small because the knife already intersects the fruit in the source frame; the main readable change is the growing incision and slight separation, not wholesale object translation.

METHOD.md

Method

The animation is a deterministic local pixel construction made from first_frame.png. I did not use an image viewing tool, external model, external engine, network resource, reference case, mask, or score.

  1. I measured RGB statistics, warm/cool threshold bounds, scanline runs, gradients, and Hough line segments. These measurements locate the tangerine near (168,72), the diagonal knife, and the glass-board perspective edges.
  2. The original knife pixels are isolated with a soft analytic band centered on the measured diagonal. Their vacated pixels are reconstructed with OpenCV Telea inpainting using only neighboring pixels from the source frame.
  3. The knife source texture is translated by small, hand-timed displacements along its own axis to create a restrained sawing/pressing motion. A weak moving specular streak and contact-shadow rim clarify motion without redrawing the object.
  4. A fruit mask is derived from source RGB chroma inside the measured footprint, morphologically closed, and softly feathered. As the cut completes, the two sides translate by at most 2.35 pixels in opposite directions normal to the cut. Vacated slivers are reconstructed from source-local board pixels.
  5. The incision grows along the measured cutting edge. Its dark center and pale, moist pith borders are analytic blends with the underlying source colors. A tiny exit-point glint is added only after breakthrough.
  6. artifact_io.save packs each RGB pixel as uint32 0xRRGGBB, writes all 20 PNGs, creates the inspection sheet, and embeds lossless delta packets in the standalone player. generate.py then reloads every PNG and pixels.npz and asserts exact agreement. Frame 00 is asserted byte-for-byte equal to the decoded source RGB.

inspection.png is the standard eight-frame contact sheet. transition_check.png contains six temporal checkpoints; each panel has its changed-pixel count and a 12-pixel-high amplified-difference strip at its foot. These files were generated for inspection, but no claim of image-tool visual review is made.
