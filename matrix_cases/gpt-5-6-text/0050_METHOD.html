+def rigid_layer(rgb, alpha_u8, pivot, angle_deg):
+    """Rotate a premultiplied source layer around an image-space pivot."""
+    alpha = alpha_u8.astype(np.float32) / 255.0

+    matrix = cv2.getRotationMatrix2D(tuple(map(float, pivot)), float(angle_deg), 1.0)

OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Numeric inspection

  • Source: first_frame.png, RGB uint8, 256x144. Pixel-channel sums are [4421500, 4327788, 3625093]; frame 00 will be copied without recompression changes to the RGB values.
  • Camera: fixed, wide, eye-level/low-oblique view. The pale blue-gray background occupies roughly y=0..31. The warm wooden tabletop begins near y=31 and fills the lower image. No camera motion is planned.
  • Upright dominoes share a floor line near y=76. Numeric color/component masks locate the left row at approximately x=56 (black), 75 (white), 94 (black), and 117 (white). The row next to the mechanism is at approximately x=159 (black), 175 (white), and 185 (black). The empty interval between rows is about x=121..156, much wider than a domino can bridge when falling.
  • The right mechanism is a very dark platform/wheel silhouette over roughly x=188..250, y=36..62. A narrow wood-toned arm runs diagonally from about (186,56) to its attachment near (216,38). Its free end is adjacent to the upper/right side of the rightmost black domino.
  • Domino masks to animate are tightly bounded by x=156..191, y=49..77. Their approximate floor pivots, right-to-left, are (186.5,75.5), (177.5,75.5), and (159.5,75.5). The four dominoes left of the gap and the platform stay fixed.
  • Lighting is soft and subdued, with short contact shadows. Extracted source pixels will be transformed so the original material texture and shading are retained. Vacated pixels will be locally inpainted from the same source frame.

METHOD.md

Method

The sequence is a local 2D pixel animation derived only from first_frame.png and the task text. No image viewing tool, external model, external asset, or rendering engine was used.

Construction

Numeric RGB thresholds and connected components were used to measure the table line, the two domino rows, the platform, and the diagonal wooden arm. The three dominoes next to the mechanism and the arm are extracted from the source using supersampled polygon alpha masks. This preserves their original texture, lighting, edge antialiasing, and perspective. Vacated pixels are filled once with OpenCV's local Telea inpainting using only neighboring pixels from the source.

The arm follows an eased 39-degree clockwise image-space rotation around its attachment near (216.3,38.7). Dominoes use staggered eased rigid rotations about their measured table-contact pivots: the right black piece starts at frame 01, the white piece at frame 04, and the left black piece at frame 08. Their final angles are 80.5, 82, and 86.5 degrees, compensating for each silhouette's slightly different initial lean. The row beyond the measured gap is never transformed, so the reaction physically stops there. Soft shadows come from blurred, translated copies of each transformed alpha silhouette. The camera and all unmentioned scene pixels remain fixed.

The provided output-only artifact_io.save helper serializes the finished RGB frames into the required height-width-time tensor, PNG sequence, inspection sheet, and embedded-data standalone HTML player. It does not generate pixels.
