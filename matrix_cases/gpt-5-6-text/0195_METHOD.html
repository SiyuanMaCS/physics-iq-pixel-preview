OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Numeric RGB observations

  • Source is an RGB uint8 image at 256×144. The camera is locked and the lighting is subdued, neutral gray.
  • A dark 30-lb kettlebell occupies approximately x=43–94, y=7–70. Its narrow top suspension/placement line is centered at x=69 and continues to the top edge. The handle has a bright interior opening; the dark rounded body spans about x=51–90, y=35–69.
  • A saturated green sheet occupies approximately x=143–186, y=25–78. A dark narrow suspension/placement line is centered around x=169 above the sheet, with a small dark attachment overlapping the sheet's top. Green segmentation (G > R+20, G > B+15) yields 1,946 pixels in x=143–186, y=25–78.
  • The left gray pillow is beneath the kettlebell, with its upper surface beginning around y=66–72 and its broad textured body extending to roughly x=5–131, y=128. The kettlebell's base is just at/above that surface.
  • The right gray pillow is beneath the paper, with an upper surface around y=78–82 and a broad body extending roughly x=105–245 into the lower edge. The paper's bottom edge is just at/above that surface.
  • The lower edge contains darker bed/floor material and pillow shadows. These remain fixed except for localized pillow deformation.

20-frame real-time event plan (4 fps, 5 seconds)

  • Frame 00 (0.00 s): preserve first_frame.png byte-for-pixel in RGB.
  • Frames 01–03 (0.25–0.75 s): both objects begin descending at the same instant with smooth acceleration; fixed camera and background.
  • Frames 04–07 (1.00–1.75 s): both arrive at their respective pillow surfaces simultaneously. The attachment lines remain connected upward as the objects are guided down.
  • Frames 08–11 (2.00–2.75 s): the heavy kettlebell makes a deep, broad indentation and sinks several more pixels; its pillow bulges slightly at the sides. The paper produces only a shallow local dimple and remains visibly uncrushed.
  • Frames 12–15 (3.00–3.75 s): damped rebound: the kettlebell and left pillow rise a small amount, then settle; the paper/right pillow show a much smaller response.
  • Frames 16–19 (4.00–4.75 s): both objects rest on the pillows. Residual motion decays to zero, leaving the left pillow substantially compressed and the right pillow almost unchanged.

The intended physical contrast is mass-dependent pillow deformation: the 30-lb kettlebell strongly compresses its pillow, while the lightweight paper barely does, even though placement begins and reaches contact simultaneously.

METHOD.md

Method

Source inspection

I did not use an image Read/View tool or an external model. I loaded first_frame.png locally with Pillow and inspected its RGB tensor through dimensions, thresholds, row runs, connected components, per-tile means, sample coordinates, bounding boxes, centroids, and text-only luminance/hue maps. Those measurements are recorded in OBSERVATION.md.

Frame construction

generate.py deterministically creates all 20 frames at 256×144 RGB and 4 fps:

  1. Frame 00 is a direct copy of the source RGB array.
  2. The kettlebell matte is measured from low-luminance pixels inside a conservative row-wise silhouette. The handle opening remains transparent. The paper matte comes from the convex hull of the uniquely saturated green connected region, including its dark top attachment.
  3. OpenCV TELEA inpainting produces a stationary local background plate underneath the two initial foreground locations. No external pixels or assets are used.
  4. Both original-RGB foregrounds are vertically translated with cubic subpixel resampling on one shared smooth placement timeline. Their narrow dark placement lines stay anchored at the top and are extended using RGB cross-sections sampled from the source image.
  5. Pillow deformation is an analytic inverse-mapped displacement field. It shifts the upper material downward and slightly outward, decaying spatially into each pillow. The left field reaches an 8-pixel transient indentation and settles near 6.2 pixels; the paper side reaches only 0.95 pixels transiently and settles near 0.63 pixels. Localized Gaussian contact shadows reinforce contact without changing the remote locked background.
  6. A short, damped overshoot/rebound follows contact. The last two transitions are an intentional full hold after motion has decayed.

The paper is kept upright by its visible placement line during the demonstration, matching the initial setup; it descends simultaneously with the kettlebell but barely deforms its pillow.

Output and verification

The supplied artifact_io.save helper writes pixels.npz, the 20 PNGs, inspection.png, and the standalone index.html. transition_check.png contains six labeled before/after/amplified-delta triplets around motion onset, approach, impact, peak compression, rebound, and settling.

The generator then reloads every PNG and the NPZ and asserts:

  • frame array shape is (20, 144, 256, 3) with uint8 RGB;
  • every PNG equals its generated frame exactly;
  • pixels.npz is uint32, shape (144, 256, 20), packed as 0xRRGGBB;
  • every packed tensor pixel equals every PNG pixel;
  • frame 00 equals first_frame.png pixel-for-pixel.
