METHOD.md

Method

Actual generation method

The animation is a deterministic local Python/Numpy/Pillow pixel synthesis. No image-generation model, external engine, network source, mask, other case, or subagent was used. I did not use an image Read/View tool and therefore do not claim a visual review; source interpretation and revision were based on numeric RGB statistics, connected components, color masks, changed-pixel bounds, and exact array comparisons.

  1. first_frame.png is loaded as the immutable RGB background. Frame 00 is a direct array copy of it.
  2. Frames 01--07 use a 4x-supersampled wavy polygon for a nearly edge-on ivory sheet descending from above. A smoothstep timing curve prevents mechanical constant-speed starts and stops.
  3. Frames 08--16 interpolate that shallow sheet into a draped polygon. The supported crown stays above the numerically measured teapot silhouette; gravity lowers the two unsupported sides and the lower hem to the table.
  4. Frames 17--19 add a small damped vertical overshoot and rebound before the cloth comes to rest.
  5. The silk pixels use analytic satin shading: two broad directional lustre waves, narrow alternating highlight/shadow folds, crown illumination, outer-slope darkening, a lower-hem shadow, and seeded smooth/fine texture. A blurred, down/right cast shadow anchors the fabric to the photographed scene. Supersampled masks and Lanczos reduction antialias the cloth edge.
  6. The source stays exactly static; only the generated silk and its local cast shadow are composited.

Revision and verification

The first generated contour passed shape/tensor checks but a numeric occlusion test found that 249 pixels in the measured upper red side profile could remain unchanged. The final shoulder curve was broadened and rerendered. In the final result, zero of the 1,630 measured blue teapot pixels and zero of the 1,558 measured central red profile pixels remain unchanged at frame 19.

generate.py asserts all of the following on every run:

  • tensor shape (20, 144, 256, 3) and uint8 RGB type;
  • frame 00 equality to the source array and the expected source SHA-256;
  • pixels.npz shape (144, 256, 20) and uint32 type;
  • exact reconstruction of all RGB channels from packed 0xRRGGBB values;
  • exact equality of every saved PNG to its corresponding generated frame;
  • a nonzero pixel transition between every adjacent pair of frames.

inspection.png contains eight representative full frames. transition_check.png contains the exact source followed by 19 amplified frame-to-frame RGB-difference panels with changed-pixel counts.
