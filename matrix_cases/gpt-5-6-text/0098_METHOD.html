+def feathered_rect(h: int, w: int, x0: int, y0: int, x1: int, y1: int,
+                   feather_x: int = 3, feather_y: int = 3) -> np.ndarray:
+    """A rectangle with a short linear feather on its inner border."""
+    yy, xx = np.mgrid[:h, :w]
+    inside = (xx >= x0) & (xx < x1) & (yy >= y0) & (yy < y1)
+    dx = np.minimum(xx - x0 + 1, x1 - xx)
+    dy = np.minimum(yy - y0 + 1, y1 - yy)
+    wx = np.clip(dx / max(feather_x, 1), 0.0, 1.0)
+    wy = np.clip(dy / max(feather_y, 1), 0.0, 1.0)

+    r = np.median(src[:, right[0]:right[1]], axis=1)[:, None, :]

METHOD.md

Method

This sequence was generated entirely with deterministic local Python pixel operations. No image read/view tool, external model, external engine, network resource, mask, score, or other case was used.

Scene analysis

first_frame.png was inspected numerically with Pillow/NumPy. I measured RGB statistics, HSV-like saturation and hue masks, row/column edge gradients, coordinate samples, and connected spatial extents. Those measurements identified the wall/table boundary, the tennis ball and face, the cord, and the approximately 45×42 px clear acrylic box. The measurements and animation timing are recorded in OBSERVATION.md.

Frame synthesis

  1. Frame 00 is a direct RGB array copy of first_frame.png.
  2. For subsequent frames, only the initially occupied apparatus region is reconstructed. The smooth wall behind the cord and box is estimated by horizontal interpolation between clean samples immediately to the left and right, with a small amount of low-frequency local sensor texture retained. The table and smiley ball are never inpainted or moved.
  3. The original apparatus is represented as its signed RGB difference from that reconstructed wall. This is an additive transparency/refraction matte, not an opaque rectangular crop. When translated over the tennis ball, the ball remains present and receives the original acrylic box's subtle darkening, edge contrast, and refraction.
  4. The rigid acrylic-box matte is translated downward with bilinear subpixel interpolation according to an eased 32 px trajectory. A lateral correction of at most 0.31 px supplies a restrained suspension response and decays to zero.
  5. The upper cord has its own difference matte. It is inverse-warped vertically so its top remains fixed at the top border while its lower end follows the box. Its minute lateral displacement increases gradually from the fixed end to the moving end.
  6. During the last few pixels of descent, a very low-opacity analytic contact shadow appears beneath the lower acrylic rim. The motion includes a 0.25 px soft settling overshoot and then holds exactly still for frames 16–19.
  7. Results are rounded and clipped to uint8 RGB. artifact_io.save writes the 20 PNG frames, packed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, inspection contact sheet, and standalone HTML player. transition_check.png contains enlarged central crops and black-to-amber numeric temporal-difference maps for selected transitions.

Verification

The generator asserts the (20, 144, 256, 3) uint8 shape, exact frame-00 source equality, nonempty animation, and exact stability of the final hold. A separate post-generation check verifies that every PNG is RGB 256×144 and that every decoded PNG pixel exactly equals its corresponding value in the packed (144, 256, 20) uint32 tensor.

inspection.png and transition_check.png were generated as required artifacts, but they were not visually reviewed because the task explicitly limited access to local code and numeric RGB inspection.
