magnet-transparent-peakaboo · 生成方法

Overview

The animation is a deterministic local Python pixel synthesis. It uses only first_frame.png and the scene description. The source image is never redrawn wholesale: the wall, wooden table, tennis ball, smiley face, lighting, grain, and fixed camera pixels come directly from the supplied frame.

No image viewing tool, network service, external reference, external AI model, or external rendering engine was used. Scene measurements were obtained from RGB arrays, threshold counts, sampled pixel coordinates, and numeric gradient scans.

Construction

  1. first_frame.png is loaded as a 256×144 RGB uint8 array.
  2. A narrow mask follows the measured hanging fitting and acrylic outline: the top perimeter, four perspective verticals, and two lower side edges.
  3. OpenCV's local Telea pixel inpainting removes those masked edge/fitting pixels to make a clean plate. The mask deliberately excludes the tennis ball and keeps the photographed upper cord anchor.
  4. For each moving frame, the clear box is displaced downward according to the schedule in OBSERVATION.md. Its real source appearance is transferred as a background-relative RGB residual (source - clean plate) rather than as an opaque rectangular crop. This preserves the photographic edge texture while allowing it to cross wall, wood, and ball naturally.
  5. Four very low-opacity polygonal faces add clear-acrylic refraction: a one-pixel lateral sample, cool neutral tint, and subtle specular edge threads. The ball remains plainly visible through the front face.
  6. A narrow shaded orange cord is extended vertically between the unchanged photographed upper anchor and the moving fitting. The table and ball do not move.
  7. Motion follows integer downward offsets 0, 0, 1, 3, 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 25, 27, 29, 30, 31, 30, 30, 30, 30. This gives a gentle start, controlled descent, one-pixel settling overshoot, rebound, and final hold at a constant 4 fps.

Output and checks

  • artifact_io.save writes the uint32 packed 0xRRGGBB tensor, all 20 PNG frames, the inspection contact sheet, and standalone HTML player.
  • frames/00.png is explicitly assigned from the source array. Pixel equality against first_frame.png is asserted.
  • Every PNG is decoded back to RGB and asserted equal to the corresponding plane reconstructed from pixels.npz.
  • The stored tensor is asserted to have shape (144, 256, 20) and dtype uint32.
  • A numeric localization audit confirms final changes are confined to the hanging apparatus region (approximately x=118..176, y=19..105); distant wall, table, and camera regions remain unchanged.
  • transition_check.png contains four diagnostic rows. Each row shows a pre-transition frame, a five-times-amplified absolute RGB difference map, and the following frame.
