+def extract_layer(source, region_mask, alpha_floor=7.0, alpha_full=25.0):
+    """Return an inpainted background and a soft RGBA extraction.
+
+    The mask is modestly dilated for background reconstruction.  Alpha is
+    measured from the RGB difference between the real pixels and that local
+    reconstruction.  Small morphological closing retains dark/neutral painted
+    details inside the otherwise colorful object.
+    """
+    kernel = cv2.getStructuringElement(cv2.MORPH_ELLIPSE, (5, 5))
+    fill_mask = cv2.dilate(region_mask, kernel, iterations=1)
+    background = cv2.inpaint(source, fill_mask, 5, cv2.INPAINT_TELEA)
+
+    difference = np.max(
+        np.abs(source.astype(np.int16) - background.astype(np.int16)), axis=2
+    ).astype(np.float32)
+    alpha = np.clip((difference - alpha_floor) / (alpha_full - alpha_floor), 0, 1)
+    alpha *= region_mask.astype(np.float32) / 255.0
+
+    # Close only the confident binary support, then use it to strengthen—not
+    # replace—the measured soft alpha.  This preserves handle/background holes.
+    support = ((alpha > 0.30).astype(np.uint8) * 255)
+    support = cv2.morphologyEx(
+        support,
+        cv2.MORPH_CLOSE,
+        cv2.getStructuringElement(cv2.MORPH_ELLIPSE, (3, 3)),
+    )
+    support = cv2.GaussianBlur(support, (3, 3), 0.55).astype(np.float32) / 255.0
+    alpha = np.maximum(alpha, support * 0.88)
+    alpha = cv2.GaussianBlur(alpha, (3, 3), 0.42)
+    alpha *= region_mask.astype(np.float32) / 255.0
+    return background, np.dstack([source.astype(np.float32), alpha * 255.0])

OBSERVATION.md

Numeric scene observation and event plan

Source measurements

  • first_frame.png is RGB, 256 x 144. All measurements below came from local Pillow/NumPy RGB values, threshold maps, scanlines, and connected components; no image viewer or image-reading tool was used.
  • The shot is locked. The upper background is mostly neutral gray. A strong vertical boundary occurs at x=135/136. The tabletop occupies the lower portion and is warm brown; its apparent boundary slopes from roughly y=82 on the right toward y=120 on the left.
  • The physical teapot is the small, strongly colored object at approximately x=169..216, y=54..84. Its main body is around x=177..204, with asymmetric protrusions on both sides and a small top/lid. Blue, red, near-black, and brown details make rotation readable.
  • Its stationary black platform is approximately x=176..203, y=87..97. This is kept fixed while the pot turns.
  • The mirror image is larger and lower, approximately x=94..133, y=62..103. Its reflected black platform is approximately x=97..141, y=106..128 and is also kept fixed.
  • The rightmost background is near black, beginning near x=211 at the top and around x=220 near y=84. Source boundaries and tabletop/background texture must remain stationary.
  • Frequent neutral source values include (157,157,159), (150,150,152), and (128,128,130); the extreme background includes values near (2,0,1). The full frame mean is approximately (110.58,101.37,96.02).

Motion design

The teapot completes one steady clockwise revolution in five seconds. Twenty samples are spaced 18 degrees apart. The body retains its volume while surface color/details travel around it; the side protrusions foreshorten near quarter turns and exchange sides at the half turn. The mirror uses the opposite apparent horizontal phase, as a real mirror reverses the observed rotation. Both black platforms, mirror boundary, tabletop, camera, lighting, and background remain locked.

METHOD.md

Method

I used only local numeric RGB processing of first_frame.png; I did not use an image viewer, an image Read/View tool, external models, external engines, masks, references, or network access.

generate.py loads the source with Pillow and constructs two measured polygonal regions: the physical teapot and its mirror image. OpenCV local inpainting supplies a static background estimate beneath each pot. The extraction alpha is calculated from per-pixel RGB difference between the source and that local estimate, with a small morphological close and subpixel feather to retain neutral painted detail while keeping holes transparent.

For frames 01–19, each extracted pot is turned with an image-based pseudo-3D projection. Its central volume stays full-sized while its surface texture shifts and morphs continuously toward a horizontally reversed back-facing view at 180 degrees. Side appendages are projected horizontally by cos(angle), so they foreshorten at 90/270 degrees and exchange sides after 180 degrees. The reflection receives the negative phase to reproduce mirror-reversed apparent motion. Both black platforms and the rest of the source remain stationary. Frame 00 bypasses all processing and is a direct array copy of the source.

The angular samples are 360 * frame / 20 degrees: 18 degrees per frame at 4 fps, giving one steady revolution per five-second playback loop. artifact_io.save writes the required uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, PNG sequence, inspection sheet, and player. The script additionally creates transition_check.png, then reloads every PNG and pixels.npz to assert exact equality with the generated uint8 RGB tensor and exact equality of frame 00 with first_frame.png.
