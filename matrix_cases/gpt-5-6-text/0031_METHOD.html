balls-collide · 生成方法

The sequence is a deterministic local pixel synthesis implemented in generate.py.

  1. first_frame.png was read as a 256 x 144 RGB array. Scene measurements came from numeric RGB thresholds, connected-component bounds, row spans, sample values, and array statistics. No image Read/View tool, external model, network source, or external engine was used.
  2. The measured blue and yellow balls are approximately 19 px across, centered at (120.1,71.3) and (185.5,40.5). Soft circular alpha mattes extract each photographed ball as a premultiplied RGB sprite.
  3. A stable tabletop plate is reconstructed once by masking the two original balls plus their small contact-shadow areas, then applying OpenCV's local Telea inpainting with a 3 px radius. This only uses neighboring pixels from the supplied frame. All pipe and edge structures remain source-fixed.
  4. The center axis is the normalized measured vector from blue to yellow. Both ball centers move at exactly 4.5241 px/frame in opposite directions. The speed is chosen so the measured 72.3 px starting separation becomes an 18.0 px contact separation on frame 06.
  5. The impact uses the one-dimensional equal-mass elastic solution. The balls exchange velocities; with equal and opposite incoming speeds, each colored ball reverses direction without a speed mismatch. Frame 06 adds a restrained 5% axial squash and 4% transverse expansion. Fixed-direction soft contact shadows keep the balls grounded.
  6. Rolling is conveyed by inverse-mapped rotation of the source ball texture. Angular displacement is translational displacement divided by the 9 px apparent radius. Angular velocity reverses after the collision together with linear velocity.
  7. Frame 00 is the untouched source array. Frames 01-19 use the stable reconstructed plate and source-derived moving sprites. artifact_io.save writes all PNGs, the 0xRRGGBB uint32 tensor, inspection sheet, and standalone player. transition_check.png contains selected before/after frames and 4x-amplified numeric RGB differences; it was generated diagnostically and was not visually reviewed.
  8. The generator reloads pixels.npz and every saved PNG, repacks the frame arrays to uint32, and asserts exact equality. It also asserts exact array equality between frame 00 and first_frame.png.

Run with:

python3 generate.py
