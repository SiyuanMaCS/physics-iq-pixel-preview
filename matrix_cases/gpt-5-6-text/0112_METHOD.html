+def polygon_for_oriented_ellipse(center, along, radius_along, radius_cross, n=24):

+        p = center + along * (math.cos(a) * radius_along) + cross * (math.sin(a) * radius_cross)

OBSERVATION.md

PixelWorld-Text observation and event plan

Numeric RGB observations

  • Source: first_frame.png, RGB uint8, exactly 256 x 144.
  • Camera: static and level. No camera movement is planned.
  • Background: cool gray wall in the upper field. The wooden tabletop fills the lower field and its rear edge rises toward frame-right because of perspective.
  • Black balloon: dominant dark connected component at approximately x=79..150, y=29..104. Its widest visible section is about x=79..150 around y=64..79; its lower contour narrows onto the table near y=104. It remains stationary.
  • Black platform: second dark connected component at approximately x=174..230, y=66..93, sitting to the right of the balloon. Its body remains stationary.
  • Match/pivot: a narrow light diagonal crosses the platform from the left pivot around (174,81) to the initial head/flame end around (194,72), giving an initial screen-space angle near -24 degrees and a working radius near 22 pixels. The pale match pixels and reddish head sit over the platform.
  • Contact geometry: after a clockwise sweep to about 180 degrees, the match head reaches x about 152 and a 4-6 pixel flame envelope reaches the balloon boundary around x=146..149, y=77..84.
  • Existing lighting is soft and diffuse. New flame light will therefore be local and restrained: a compact amber halo, orange outer flame, pale-yellow core, and a faint warm reflection only at contact.

METHOD.md

Method

The sequence was produced entirely with local Python pixel operations. I did not use an image Read/View tool and did not claim visual review.

  1. I inspected first_frame.png numerically as a 256 x 144 RGB array: luminance/color thresholds, connected components, row/column statistics, bounding boxes, dark-pixel runs, and sampled RGB values. This located the balloon, platform, tabletop, pivot, and source match.
  2. Frame 00 is the unmodified source array. For frames 01-19, OpenCV Telea inpainting removes only the original match's narrow diagonal footprint. This produces a fixed clean plate; no camera, balloon, table, or platform motion is synthesized.
  3. The match is reconstructed procedurally at 4x resolution and downsampled with Lanczos filtering. Its layers are a contact shadow, dark wooden edge, tan shaft, narrow highlight, red-brown charred head, and a fixed mounting pin at (174,81).
  4. The match angle follows a deterministic clockwise schedule from about -24 degrees to about 181 degrees. It eases into contact with the balloon, overshoots by three degrees, then settles while remaining in contact.
  5. The flame uses a compact, deterministic per-frame flicker: orange outer plume, yellow interior, pale core, and a local analytic Gaussian amber halo. At contact, the same restrained halo supplies a warm reflection on the nearby black balloon surface.
  6. artifact_io.save writes all PNGs, the uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, inspection contact sheet, and standalone HTML player. generate.py additionally creates transition_check.png from amplified adjacent-frame RGB differences and verifies every PNG pixel against pixels.npz.

The balloon is deliberately not burst: the requested event is the lit match rotating clockwise and touching it, and the final frames hold that contact without adding an unrequested later action.
