OBSERVATION.md

Numerical scene observation

Inspection method: local Pillow/NumPy/SciPy code only. I did not use an image read/view tool. Measurements came from RGB statistics, HSV-like masks, connected components, row/column profiles, and printed RGB samples.

Measured layout

  • Source: RGB, 256 x 144.
  • Camera: fixed. The background is a muted gray-green vertical surface over a brown tabletop in the lower portion.
  • Vessel: approximately x=124..174. Its transparent sides continue upward through much of the frame.
  • Green water-level band: the dominant green connected component spans x=124..173, y=77..87 (368 classified pixels). It is most solid at y=80..85.
  • Blue liquid: the dominant cyan/blue connected component spans x=125..173, y=86..133 (1905 classified pixels), with smaller refracted blue regions down to y=141.
  • Paper: irregular, crumpled, light neutral region between about x=136..166 and y=2..51. Its brightest connected portions lie at x=143..165, y=2..50, with darker folds inside the same silhouette.
  • Grabber/tool cues: blue-black narrow elements enter from the top around x=129..138 and x=156..171. A dark vertical component at x=168..171 runs down to y=78, beside the paper and inner vessel wall.
  • The water surface is therefore treated as y=87/88, and the paper's initial bottom is near y=51.

20-frame event plan (4 fps)

  • 00: exact locked source frame.
  • 01: held pose; only an extremely small anticipatory paper/tool relaxation.
  • 02: grabber opens subtly; the paper separates but has barely moved.
  • 03: paper begins falling, about 2 px downward with a slight clockwise wobble.
  • 04: gravity accelerates the paper downward.
  • 05: faster fall; mild counter-wobble.
  • 06: paper approaches the marked liquid surface.
  • 07: lower edge reaches the liquid; paper starts compressing vertically.
  • 08: contact/entry, with a small local blue-white crown splash.
  • 09: paper partly enters the blue liquid; splash droplets rise briefly.
  • 10: peak short-lived ripples; wet paper is visibly more compact and blue-green tinted.
  • 11: droplets descend; the paper rocks at the surface.
  • 12: ripples expand and weaken.
  • 13: wet paper settles slightly lower.
  • 14: residual surface oscillation, smaller amplitude.
  • 15: paper darkens/tints as it absorbs water.
  • 16: nearly settled, with a small buoyant bob.
  • 17: final weak counter-bob.
  • 18: ripples nearly gone.
  • 19: damp crumpled paper resting partly submerged near the center of the blue liquid.

The action stays entirely inside the measured vessel footprint, and all unchanged scene pixels remain sourced from frame 00.

METHOD.md

Method

I generated the sequence entirely with local deterministic Python pixel operations. I did not use an image read/view tool, external model, network resource, reference case, mask, or rendering engine.

Source analysis

Pillow loaded first_frame.png as RGB. NumPy/SciPy measurements (color ranges, hue/saturation-like thresholds, connected components, axis profiles, and printed RGB samples) located the vessel, green level band, blue liquid, crumpled light paper, and blue-black grabber elements. Those measurements are recorded in OBSERVATION.md.

Animation construction

  • Frame 00 is a direct array copy of first_frame.png; frame 01 deliberately holds the same pose for a short anticipatory beat.
  • The light neutral paper region is isolated inside the measured x=136..166, y=1..52 region. Thresholded bright neutral facets are morphologically connected, hole-filled, and combined with an irregular measured outline. The final alpha edge is softly feathered.
  • OpenCV Telea inpainting reconstructs the narrow clean plate revealed behind the released paper. The reconstruction is used only inside a feathered mask.
  • The original paper RGB texture and folds are transformed with deterministic affine matrices. Its trajectory accelerates under gravity, wobbles by a few degrees, then compresses vertically on contact with the liquid.
  • Two restrained blue-black pad shapes spread outward near the original grip to communicate the grabber opening.
  • From frame 08 onward, the paper receives a gradually increasing blue-green absorption tint. Its lower folds are partly occluded with the measured liquid color to create partial submersion.
  • Contact adds a localized, one-pixel-scale crown, a few short-lived droplets, and two fading sinusoidal/elliptical ripples, all clipped to x=124..173 around the measured surface at y=88.
  • The camera and every pixel outside the localized interaction remain locked to the source frame.

Output and verification

artifact_io.save packs the uint8 RGB frames into the required uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, writes the 20 PNGs, builds inspection.png, and embeds the delta-coded sequence in index.html. generate.py additionally creates transition_check.png, pairing selected frames with 5x-amplified consecutive-frame absolute differences.

The generator asserts all of the following after writing:

  • tensor shape is (144, 256, 20) and dtype is uint32;
  • every decoded PNG exactly equals its in-memory uint8 RGB frame;
  • every packed tensor value exactly equals the corresponding PNG RGB value;
  • frames/00.png exactly equals first_frame.png pixel-for-pixel.

Review and revision were numerical only: per-frame changed-pixel counts, difference magnitudes, bounding boxes, weighted change centroids, and bright-paper counts across the falling/contact zones were inspected to confirm the planned release, acceleration, impact, and settling cadence.
