OBSERVATION.md

Numeric observation of first_frame.png

Inspection method: local Python/Pillow/NumPy only. I inspected RGB statistics, thresholded connected components, scanline samples, and text-rendered luminance/edge maps. I did not open the image with an image viewer or image-reading tool.

Frame and scene measurements

  • Source is RGB, 256×144. Lighting is low-key and warm, with a light wooden tabletop filling the frame and becoming brighter toward the lower foreground.
  • The shot is fixed. Long, nearly horizontal table texture and unchanged background/object silhouettes provide the registration reference.
  • The black pipe is cropped by the left edge. Its darkest visible mouth/side occupies roughly x=0–24, y=40–69 and points into the open tabletop toward the right.
  • The gray tennis ball is already out of the pipe in frame 00. Its colored core is approximately x=111–128, y=44–60, centered near (120, 52), with a diameter of about 17 px. A soft contact shadow extends down-left into about x=106–128, y=55–62. The ball has a cool bluish-gray highlight on its upper/right-facing surface and a dark lower-left rim.
  • The 30 lb kettlebell occupies the right side. Its dark handle rises through approximately x=175–216, y=0–54; the much larger gray body continues through the right foreground. The ball's first unobstructed collision boundary is near x=175 at ball height.
  • The rubber duck is the compact yellow connected region around x=211–235, y=29–49. It remains separate from the moving ball and sits with the heavy bell setup.
  • The clean ball travel corridor is approximately y=44–63. The measured gap from the frame-00 ball center to the kettlebell contact center is about 46–48 px after accounting for the ball radius.

20-frame event plan (4 fps)

The ball travels in a straight rightward line, visibly rotates by changing its surface highlight/seam phase, briefly squashes at contact, then rebounds left with restitution and rolling friction. The kettlebell is 30 lb, so it does not translate; only a tiny one-frame highlight/shadow tremor is warranted. The duck remains still. Camera, pipe, table, exposure, and background remain locked.

The original ball region will be removed from a clean plate by local, boundary-driven interpolation that follows the tabletop's smooth illumination. METHOD.md

Method

I produced the continuation entirely with local deterministic pixel operations. No image viewer, image Read/View tool, network source, external model, or rendering engine was used.

  1. I loaded first_frame.png as an RGB NumPy array and measured the pipe, ball, kettlebell, duck, tabletop gradient, and collision corridor using numeric RGB thresholds, connected components, scanline values, and terminal text maps.
  2. Frame 00 is assigned directly from that source array and never passes through cleaning, drawing, or color adjustment. After the output helper writes the sequence, the supplied PNG is copied over frames/00.png, making that artifact byte-identical as well as pixel-identical to first_frame.png.
  3. For frames 01–19, I made a clean table plate by masking the original 17 px ball plus its small contact shadow and applying OpenCV's local Telea inpainting. The mask is restricted to the tabletop patch around the original ball.
  4. I rendered the moving ball analytically at subpixel coordinates. An ellipsoidal normal field supplies spherical diffuse light and a soft specular highlight; deterministic sinusoidal micro-variation supplies felt texture; a curved pale seam rotates according to integrated travel distance. A one-pixel antialias ramp blends the edge into the photographed table.
  5. A blurred elliptical shadow remains registered to the tabletop. Its softness and opacity vary with the small rebound lift. Fast frames receive a very faint two-sample exposure smear.
  6. The approach is straight and swift. At contact the ball becomes narrower and slightly taller against the kettlebell, then reverses direction. Signed velocity integrates the visible rotation. Damped center positions bring it to rest for the last three frames. The 30 lb kettlebell and duck remain stationary, consistent with the large mass ratio.
  7. artifact_io.save writes the uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, PNG sequence, inspection sheet, and standalone player. The generator additionally writes an impact/transition diagnostic sheet and then reloads every PNG to assert exact equality with every pixels.npz time slice. It separately asserts that frame 00 equals the supplied source RGB tensor.
