Actual generation method

This sequence is a deterministic local 2D pixel animation derived from first_frame.png. It does not use an external model, renderer, reference image, mask, or engine. The source image was inspected numerically with Pillow, NumPy, connected color regions, RGB profiles, and Sobel-gradient coordinates; it was not opened with an image viewing tool.

generate.py loads the 256 x 144 RGB source and uses it unchanged as the background for every frame. Frame 00 is a direct copy. The only synthesized pixels are in the measured outlet/receiving-glass region around x=130..177 and y=90..129.

The animation consists of:

  • A narrow falling stream formed from a supersampled analytic polygon. Its center and width have small deterministic sinusoidal variations, and a thin highlight moves on its front face to suggest cylindrical refraction.
  • A tapered receiving-glass interior mask whose rising top follows the 20-frame schedule in OBSERVATION.md.
  • Translucent red tinting derived from the original per-pixel luminance. This keeps the source tray shading and weak glass reflections visible through the added liquid instead of replacing them with a flat color.
  • A subpixel wavy meniscus, an impact dimple, a restrained moving ripple, a brief initial splash, and a few tiny deterministic bubble highlights.
  • Four-times-resolution mask rasterization followed by Lanczos downsampling for antialiased boundaries.

The reservoir, dispenser glass, baking tray, background, lighting, and camera remain locked. No global color grading or frame motion is applied.

Output and validation

The shared artifact_io.save helper packs RGB pixels as uint32 0xRRGGBB, transposes them to the required (144, 256, 20) tensor, writes pixels.npz, exports the 20 PNGs, creates inspection.png, and embeds lossless frame deltas in index.html.

generate.py also creates transition_check.png, containing four representative before/after/difference transitions. Its verification pass checks:

  • tensor shape (144, 256, 20) and dtype uint32;
  • frame array shape (20, 144, 256, 3) and dtype uint8;
  • exact pixel equality between frame 00 and first_frame.png;
  • exact equality of every decoded PNG with the generated RGB tensor;
  • exact equality of the unpacked 0xRRGGBB archive with all 20 frames;
  • nonempty changes for every animated frame and confinement of all changes to the action region;
  • byte-identical PNG hashes for frames/00.png and first_frame.png.

To reproduce all generated artifacts, run:

python3 generate.py
