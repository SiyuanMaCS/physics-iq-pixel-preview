OBSERVATION.md

Source observation and event plan

All observations below come from local numeric RGB inspection of first_frame.png; no image viewing tool was used.

Measured scene

  • Source is RGB uint8 at exactly 256 x 144. Channel means are approximately (65.51, 62.71, 53.24), confirming a predominantly dark, warm setup.
  • The lit wall/background occupies roughly x=106..224, y=40..106. Its brightest neutral area is centered around x=150..180, y=55..95, with RGB values commonly in the 170--210 range.
  • A strong, nearly vertical dark shadow band interrupts the lit wall at about x=118..135, y=54..97. The center of this band drops to mean luminance 20--55 while adjacent lit wall is commonly 170--205. This is the source large cast shadow.
  • The yellow mug is measured at approximately x=122..157, y=95..129. Saturated yellow pixels form the body and lip; a bright vertical reflection is near x=137..143. Its numeric saturated-yellow component has centroid near (139, 113).
  • The blue rectangular block is immediately right of the mug at approximately x=154..179, y=97..133. Its upper/illuminated pixels are pale cyan and its lower face becomes deep blue around y=126..132.
  • The black turntable/table area fills much of the lower image and is partially cropped by the bottom edge. The useful rotation pivot is estimated at (109, 126). Because the camera views the disk obliquely, circular motion is modeled as an ellipse with a vertical-to-horizontal ratio near 0.34.
  • Lighting is fixed: a warm directional spotlight produces the bright wall patch and projects enlarged, softened object silhouettes upward onto that wall. The camera and every background pixel outside the moving setup remain locked.

Motion model

  • Duration: 20 frames at 4 fps (5 seconds), constant real-time sampling.
  • Total platform phase advance: 78 degrees clockwise. The mug and block keep fixed offsets in turntable coordinates and remain upright while their screen positions follow the projected ellipse.
  • The mug's cylindrical body stays vertically oriented; its handle cue and horizontal shading shift with yaw. The block remains an upright cuboid, with its visible top/front/side proportions changing continuously with yaw.
  • The large wall shadow moves consistently with the rotating objects, is enlarged upward from the objects, and remains soft because the source spotlight is not a point light.
  • Frame 00 is copied byte-for-byte in pixel values from the source before any animation operation.

METHOD.md

Generation method

The sequence was produced entirely with local Python numeric pixel operations using NumPy, Pillow, and OpenCV. No network resource, external model, external engine, image viewer, or image Read/View tool was used.

Source analysis

first_frame.png was loaded as a 256 x 144 RGB uint8 array. I inspected channel statistics, thresholded color populations, per-row/per-column numeric samples, and coarse text-printed cluster maps. Those measurements locate the mug, blue block, lit wall, dark turntable, and original wall-shadow band. The measurements and frame-by-frame plan were recorded in OBSERVATION.md before generate.py was written.

Pixel construction

  1. Frame 00 is a direct array copy of first_frame.png; no resampling or recompression affects its RGB values.
  2. Tight antialiased masks isolate the source mug and blue block. Their real source pixels supply the object texture in later frames.
  3. The pixels uncovered behind the two source objects are reconstructed locally with OpenCV Telea inpainting. The measured original wall-shadow band is repaired with a feathered horizontal interpolation from adjacent illuminated wall pixels.
  4. The turntable surface is modeled as an obliquely viewed ellipse. Its dim source texture is inverse-mapped around the ellipse at a constant clockwise phase, with a soft rim and a luminance gate so the surrounding set does not rotate.
  5. Object anchors follow the same circular turntable coordinates projected with a 0.30 vertical foreshortening factor. The total phase change is 78 degrees over 19 equal frame intervals. The mug remains upright. The rectangular block remains upright while horizontal scale and shear vary smoothly to indicate changing yaw.
  6. The source object layers are bicubically transformed and alpha composited in projected depth order. Small blurred ellipses create contact shadows on the platform.
  7. The wall shadow is redrawn analytically from enlarged mug and block silhouettes at 4x resolution, reduced with area filtering, blurred, and multiplicatively composited only inside the measured lit-wall window. Its displacement is tied to each object's turntable displacement. The spotlight and camera never move.

Output and verification

artifact_io.save writes the uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, the 20 PNG frames, the inspection contact sheet, and the standalone HTML player. transition_check.png contains five representative adjacent-frame overlays above 4x-amplified absolute differences.

The generator then reloads every PNG and pixels.npz, asserting:

  • shape (20, 144, 256, 3) and dtype uint8 for the generated frame array;
  • exact RGB equality between frame 00 and first_frame.png;
  • tensor shape (144, 256, 20) and dtype uint32;
  • exact equality of every PNG with the corresponding packed tensor slice; and
  • nonzero motion in every consecutive transition.
