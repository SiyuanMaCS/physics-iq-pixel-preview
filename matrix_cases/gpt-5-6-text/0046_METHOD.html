Actual generation method

I generated the continuation entirely with deterministic local Python pixel operations. I did not use an image viewer, external model, external asset, network resource, mask, score, reference case, or rendering engine.

  1. first_frame.png was loaded as an RGB NumPy array. Its layout was measured through numeric RGB clusters, connected components, threshold masks, sampled rows/cells, and edge-energy statistics. Those observations and the animation schedule were written to OBSERVATION.md before generate.py was created.
  2. Source-measured polygons isolate the white domino and the two visible blue/black upper grabber regions. Their soft alpha edges are source-derived; there is no replacement artwork for those objects.
  3. A small local OpenCV TELEA inpaint reconstructs pixels exposed behind those cutouts. It draws only from adjacent pixels in this frame: warm wood in the upper area and dark liquid near the mug opening.
  4. The domino follows an accelerating vertical displacement sequence (0, 0, 1, 3, 7, 13, 21, 31, 44, 58... pixels) with less than one degree of rotation. A depth mask darkens and then occludes it across the measured liquid line, so it enters the mug instead of passing graphically over its front.
  5. The source grabber cutouts translate upward after release. They remain aligned at the start and clear the frame as the domino becomes submerged.
  6. The liquid response is analytic: paired light/dark perspective-elliptical wave bands expand from the impact point and decay exponentially. A short five-frame antialiased crown and a few droplets provide the impact cue. Water effects are clipped to a color-and-geometry mask of the existing dark liquid.
  7. The camera, wood, mug, and all unrelated pixels remain fixed. No global zoom, pan, color shift, or relighting is applied.

Timing

The output contains 20 frames at the requested 256×144 resolution. At 4 fps this is 5 seconds. Frame 00 is assigned directly from the decoded source array after all frame construction to guarantee exact RGB equality. Frame 01 is a deliberate brief hold; frames 02–09 contain release, accelerating fall, impact, and submersion; frames 10–19 show withdrawal and damped settling ripples.

Output and verification

artifact_io.save writes pixels.npz, the 20 PNG frames, inspection.png, and the standalone index.html player. generate.py separately writes transition_check.png, a two-row sheet of key transition frames.

The generator then reloads every PNG and the compressed tensor and verifies:

  • frame array shape is (20, 144, 256, 3) and dtype is uint8;
  • tensor shape is (144, 256, 20) and dtype is uint32;
  • every tensor value uses 0xRRGGBB packing and equals its corresponding PNG;
  • frames/00.png is numerically identical to first_frame.png.

The final numeric motion audit also confirmed that changes remain confined to the right-side action area (x >= 120). The tracked bright-domino centroid descends from about (167.8, 42.4) while its visible bright area shrinks from 835 pixels to 151 pixels near entry, then disappears beneath the liquid. I did not perform or claim visual review.
