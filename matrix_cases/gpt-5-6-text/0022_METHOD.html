ball-rolls-off · 生成方法

I inspected first_frame.png only as a local 256×144 RGB array. I used numeric channel summaries, luminance rows, thresholded connected components, gradient coordinates, and selected RGB coordinate dumps to measure the pipe and table. I did not use an image read/view tool, an external model, or any external assets.

generate.py keeps the source array unchanged as locked-off scenery. Frame 00 is the source RGB exactly and its original PNG file is copied to frames/00.png. Frames 01–19 add only:

  1. a compact two-scale analytic contact shadow;
  2. a 4×4 supersampled grey sphere with Lambert-style upper-left lighting and a small specular term;
  3. deterministic object-space felt variation;
  4. a pale, grooved curved tennis seam whose clockwise rotation is traveled distance divided by ball radius.

The ball advances 12 pixels per frame along a constant y=91.5 center line, maintaining contact near y=98.8. Its initial pixels are clipped at the measured pipe exit (x=29), creating foreground occlusion while it emerges. The final frame places the center at x=243, so the ball has traversed the whole table and is partly beyond the right boundary.

The renderer uses no physics or graphics engine. It is deterministic NumPy/Pillow pixel synthesis followed by the provided artifact_io.save output helper. transition_check.png contains key frames and 5× amplified consecutive-frame RGB differences. Validation reloads every PNG, checks it against the generated uint8 frames, independently repacks RGB as uint32 0xRRGGBB, and verifies exact equality with the (144,256,20) tensor in pixels.npz.

No claim of image-tool visual review is made; quality control is based on numeric RGB inspection and programmatic invariants.
