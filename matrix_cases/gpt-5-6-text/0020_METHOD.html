OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Numeric observations

  • Source: first_frame.png, RGB, 256×144. All observations below come from local pixel arrays, HSV masks, connected components, Canny edges, and line fitting; the image was not opened with an image viewer.
  • The camera is locked. The lower portion is a light, low-saturation wooden tabletop; the apparatus and background remain static.
  • The tennis-ball component is centered at about (86.5, 51.5). Its high-chroma yellow/green core has bounding box x=77–96, y=44–59 (20×16, 251 core pixels). The complete antialiased silhouette is approximately an ellipse of radius 10×9 pixels.
  • A strong ramp edge is the fitted line from approximately (156, 74) to (236, 45), angle −19.9°. The low entrance lip is near x=150–156, y=66–75.
  • The blue support is the dominant blue connected component, x=217–253, y=40–72 (765 pixels), underneath/behind the raised right side of the ramp.
  • The pipe/apparatus occupies the left side and the ball is already emerging toward the right in frame 00. The ball therefore first moves down-right toward the low ramp lip, then follows the incline up-right.
  • Only the ball, its tiny contact shadow, and the pixels it initially occludes should change. The source frame must be copied byte-for-byte at frame 00.

METHOD.md

Method

The continuation is a local pixel animation derived only from first_frame.png and the text prompt.

  1. The source was decoded as a 256×144 RGB array. HSV thresholding was restricted to the measured ball neighborhood, and the largest connected yellow-green component supplied the ball center and contour. The filled contour includes the pale tennis-ball seam; a one-pixel dilation and subpixel Gaussian edge produce a compact antialiased alpha mask.
  2. The source ball and its small contact shadow were removed once with a tightly bounded elliptical dilation and OpenCV TELEA inpainting. This reconstructs the static apparatus from immediately neighboring pixels. Every generated frame after frame 00 begins from that same reconstructed background, so the locked camera and all static surfaces remain stable.
  3. The ball follows the measured 20-point path recorded in OBSERVATION.md. It first moves down-right from the pipe to the low ramp lip, then follows the approximately −20° ramp edge up-right. Horizontal steps shrink near the raised end to model uphill deceleration.
  4. The extracted RGB sprite is premultiplied by its alpha before affine rotation, preventing dark or colored halos. Cumulative path length divided by the measured 9.1-pixel radius determines clockwise rotation, so texture motion agrees with rolling distance. A small analytic Gaussian contact shadow is aligned to the ramp after contact.
  5. Frame 00 is never reconstructed or composited: it is an exact array copy of first_frame.png. The shared artifact_io.save helper writes the PNG sequence, packed uint32 tensor, inspection sheet, and standalone delta-encoded HTML player. generate.py additionally writes transition_check.png and then reloads every PNG and pixels.npz to assert shape, type, exact frame-00 identity, and per-pixel tensor equality.

No image Read/View tool, external model, external engine, network source, or visual-review claim was used. Scene interpretation and revision were based on numeric RGB/HSV statistics, connected components, edge coordinates, fitted line geometry, and output consistency checks.
