cut-orange · 生成方法

This sequence was generated deterministically from first_frame.png with local Python pixel operations. No image viewing tool, external model, external engine, reference image, mask, network source, or other case was used.

The source was inspected numerically through RGB/HSV samples, connected components, and straight-edge measurements. Those measurements locate the tangerine at about (118, 88) and the existing knife axis from the upper-right toward the fruit. The animation therefore continues that exact axis down-left.

generate.py builds three local layers:

  1. A locked background plate is filled behind the original knife and fruit masks using deterministic Telea inpainting from neighboring board/table pixels.
  2. The source tangerine texture is divided along the measured diagonal x+y=204. The knife follows an eased translation parallel to that diagonal. As it passes, a narrow brown cut seam is revealed.
  3. Near the end of the stroke, the two pieces move about three pixels in opposite directions perpendicular to the cut. Narrow warm-orange edge highlights suggest newly exposed flesh, and a faint contact shadow seats the moving blade in the fruit.

All other scene pixels remain on the fixed plate. Motion paths are analytic and temporally eased; no framewise noise is used. Frame 00 is copied directly from the source and checked for exact equality. artifact_io.save serializes the 20 RGB frames, PNGs, player, inspection sheet, and packed uint32 tensor. The generator then reloads pixels.npz and verifies exact equality with every PNG-bound frame. transition_check.png contains enlarged action crops and amplified successive selected-frame differences for audit; it is generated from pixels rather than visually reviewed here.
