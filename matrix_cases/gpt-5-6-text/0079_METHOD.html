+def warp_about_contact(layer, alpha, old_contact, new_contact, xscale=1.0, shear=0.0):
+    ox, oy = old_contact
+    nx, ny = new_contact
+    matrix = np.array(
+        [[xscale, shear, nx - xscale * ox - shear * oy], [0.0, 1.0, ny - oy]],

+def projected_rotation(point, degrees, center=(188.0, 130.0), y_compression=0.35):

Construction intent

Use only local pixel operations on first_frame.png: polygonal alpha extraction for the two objects, local inpainting/interpolation for the wall and platform revealed behind them, projected planar rotation for contact points, affine sprite resampling, analytic soft shadows, subtle rotating platform markers, and exact restoration of source pixels for frame 00.

METHOD.md

Method

The animation is a local pixel construction based only on first_frame.png and prompt.txt; no external images, models, masks, engines, or references were used.

generate.py first isolates the photographed yellow mug and blue block with measured polygonal alpha mattes. It locally reconstructs the small wall/turntable region hidden by the original objects and their initial shadow using OpenCV's deterministic Telea inpainting. The original scene outside that local mask remains fixed.

For frames 01–19, each object's contact point is rotated at constant angular speed around the estimated turntable center. Rotation is performed in an uncompressed platform plane and then projected back with a 0.35 vertical foreshortening factor, producing a clockwise elliptical orbit. The photographed sprites remain upright, with only mild horizontal scale/shear changes to suggest their changing yaw. Their painter order follows projected depth.

The moving wall shadows are analytic projections of the same alpha mattes: enlarged, displaced from the fixed light direction, softly blurred, clipped to the illuminated wall, and composited as dark translucent silhouettes. Small blurred contact shadows keep the objects grounded. Three extremely low-contrast rotating scuffs supply an additional motion cue on the otherwise black platform.

Frame 00 is restored directly from the source array and asserted bit-exact. artifact_io.save writes the 20 RGB PNGs, packed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, inspection sheet, and standalone HTML player. The script additionally writes an amplified consecutive-difference contact sheet (transition_check.png) and verifies every saved PNG and every tensor value against the in-memory frames.
