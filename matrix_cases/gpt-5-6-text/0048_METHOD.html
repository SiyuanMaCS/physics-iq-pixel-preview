OBSERVATION.md

Pixel observations and event plan

Source measurements

  • The source is exactly 256×144 RGB. The shot is locked off; all continuations keep the original camera, framing, illumination, background, wooden surface, and mug position fixed.
  • A warm brown wooden environment fills most of the frame. The tabletop occupies the lower part of the image and the mug is centered near x=120.
  • The white/cool ceramic domino is a narrow, slightly clockwise-slanted object. Its bright core is approximately x=95..118, y=11..54; its visible polygon is approximately (96,13), (112,11), (119,47), (110,56), (103,52). The main bright connected component contains 515 pixels and has mean RGB about (212,233,253).
  • The blue-black grabber enters from the top around x=89..116. Its darker blue contact/shadow pixels continue beside the domino. The continuation will keep its top stem fixed and show short blue-black jaws opening after release, then retracting slightly upward.
  • The mug/rim occupies approximately x=87..153, y=44..135. The dark liquid is the red-black elliptical area at about x=95..147, y=58..87, centered near (120,73). The near-black outer rim and blue-black mug provide strong foreground occlusion below the liquid surface.
  • Nothing besides the domino, grabber jaws, liquid response, and their tiny local shadows/reflections should move. No camera motion, cuts, zoom, text, or new objects are introduced.

METHOD.md

Method

The continuation is generated entirely with deterministic local Python pixel operations. I did not use image viewing tools, external references, masks, models, engines, or network access.

generate.py reads the RGB source and preserves it verbatim as frame 00. I measured the ceramic domino, the grabber contact, the dark liquid ellipse, and the mug's front occlusion boundary numerically from the source pixels. A tight hand-measured polygon extracts the original ceramic texture. OpenCV's local Telea inpainting reconstructs only the small source area hidden by the initial domino, sampling the surrounding wood and mug pixels.

For frames 01–08, the extracted domino is moved with a gravity-like accelerating vertical trajectory and a small increasing clockwise rotation. Pixels below the measured liquid surface are darkened/warmed, and the lower portion is clipped by a curved front-rim occlusion mask. The blue-black grabber jaws open at release and withdraw slightly. From impact onward, antialiased dark-red elliptical rings and two tiny droplets are clipped to the existing liquid region; their radius grows and their opacity decays through frame 17. The rest of every frame remains static.

Outputs are encoded by artifact_io.py. The generator also creates an amplified adjacent-frame difference sheet (transition_check.png) and verifies every saved PNG against the packed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor in pixels.npz, including exact equality between frames/00.png and first_frame.png.
