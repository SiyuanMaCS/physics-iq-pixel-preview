The sequence is a local, deterministic 2D pixel synthesis from first_frame.png. No network source, other case, mask, external model, external engine, or subagent was used.

  1. The source is loaded as an RGB uint8 array. Frame 00 is copied directly and bypasses every synthesis operation.
  2. The bright connected porcelain region is measured in the known central object box. Morphological closing fills dark painted/self-shadowed details inside its outer contour, and a small Gaussian edge produces an antialiased foreground matte.
  3. Pixels hidden by the source statue are reconstructed locally with OpenCV Telea inpainting. This reconstruction is used only when a rotating view reveals formerly occluded wall/platform pixels.
  4. Statue yaw is approximated analytically. A cosine-controlled horizontal foreshortening produces broad front/rear views and narrow side views; the source and an axis-mirrored reverse proxy blend continuously through a full cycle. Internal ceramic tone features drift with the turn, and a restrained fixed-world light gain keeps the spotlight direction coherent. The angular step is exactly 18 degrees per frame.
  5. The black platform texture is remapped in normalized elliptical coordinates around (147,132). An inverse angular map advances its surface texture clockwise while a feathered mask holds the platform outline and surrounding scene fixed.
  6. The unoccluded wall spotlight is estimated scanline-by-scanline with cubic fits to visible samples on both sides of the shadow. Dividing the source by this smooth light field gives a measured RGB shadow-attenuation layer. That layer is cyclically mirrored, narrowed at side views, broadened at front/rear views, and slightly skewed with angle before multiplication back onto the fixed spotlight field.
  7. The static wall, room, and camera never translate. The 20 samples cover 0° through 342°, leaving one ordinary 18° step from frame 19 back to frame 00 for seamless loop playback.
  8. artifact_io.save packs RGB into uint32 0xRRGGBB pixels with tensor layout 144×256×20, writes all PNG frames, the inspection contact sheet, and the standalone player. A separate transition sheet shows four target frames and amplified absolute deltas, including the loop boundary.

Validation is numeric: the script asserts exact equality of frame 00 to first_frame.png, decodes pixels.npz, and checks every decoded tensor frame against every saved PNG. Source analysis and validation used numeric RGB arrays, thresholds, gradients, connected components, and textual summaries only. No image Read/View tool or visual-review claim was used.
