OBSERVATION.md

Observation and event plan

Numeric inspection of frame 00

  • Source is RGB, 256 x 144. It was inspected only by local pixel statistics, sampled RGB grids, saturation/value classifications, and edge/mask measurements; no image viewing tool was used.
  • The upper background is a warm neutral gray with a left-to-right illumination gradient. The lower surface is brown wood beginning around y=84--96 (the boundary is partly hidden by the subject). The far right edge is strongly vignetted/dark.
  • The teapot is centered above the platform. Its measured occupied area is approximately x=73--180, y=16--101. It has a dark blue main body, a small yellow/olive lid and knob near x=122--139, y=16--34, a red loop handle on the left (approximately x=73--110, y=32--80), and a red spout/projection to the right (approximately x=158--180, y=34--79). Specular and painted details are retained from the photograph.
  • The stationary black platform is approximately x=100--164, y=100--128, with an irregular/elliptical top and near-black front. The teapot visually meets its top near y=99--104.
  • Background and table remain fixed. There is no camera motion, zoom, cut, added text, or added object.

20-frame event plan (4 fps, 5 seconds)

  • Frame 00 (0.00 s source sample): exact byte-for-byte pixel image of first_frame.png; teapot at its initial orientation.
  • Frames 01--03: clockwise yaw begins gently, reaching about 3 degrees. The pot remains seated at the same center and height.
  • Frames 04--07: steady slow clockwise rotation to about 12 degrees. The left handle and right spout begin to foreshorten toward the body.
  • Frames 08--11: rotation continues through about 20 degrees. Cylindrical/body surface detail drifts consistently around the curved body while the platform stays locked.
  • Frames 12--15: rotation continues through about 29 degrees. Side appendages compress horizontally and migrate slightly with perspective; the lid remains centered on the body axis.
  • Frames 16--19: the turn eases to about 36 degrees by the last frame. Contact with the platform, scale, lighting, and the locked camera remain stable.

The motion is deliberately small and smooth because the prompt asks for slow real-time rotation rather than a full revolution.

METHOD.md

Method

The source frame was inspected numerically with local Python: dimensions and RGB statistics, sampled coordinate grids, color/saturation classifications, connected-component bounds, and mask row extents. No image read/view tool, external model, network source, reference case, or external rendering engine was used.

generate.py performs the following deterministic local pixel process:

  1. It loads first_frame.png as RGB and preserves that array verbatim as frame 00.
  2. OpenCV GrabCut, initialized by a measured rectangle around the object, separates the teapot from the neutral background. Connected-component constraints retain the photographed pot and its tiny top highlight while excluding the black platform.
  3. A narrowly dilated copy of the mask is locally inpainted to reconstruct only the background pixels that can be exposed by the turn. The original table and platform outside the footprint are unchanged.
  4. The teapot is treated as three overlapping photographic layers: rear/left handle, central vessel/lid, and front/right spout. Across an eased 36-degree clockwise yaw, the handle and spout foreshorten toward their attachment points. The handle rises/darkens slightly as it recedes; the spout lowers/brightens slightly as it advances. The vessel keeps its base position, with a small curved surface-detail drift and moving side-light cue.
  5. Layers are resampled in premultiplied RGB/alpha with bicubic interpolation and composited over the reconstructed patch. Each pose is delta-calibrated against the same renderer at zero degrees and that motion-only delta is applied to the exact source. This prevents a segmentation/feathering pop at the first transition. The camera, platform, table, and outer background remain locked.
  6. artifact_io.save writes the uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, 20 PNG frames, inspection sheet, and standalone player. transition_check.png contains frames 00/10/19, amplified adjacent-frame differences, and a platform-contact strip. The script then decodes every PNG and verifies it pixel-for-pixel against the generated arrays and pixels.npz.

The animation contains 20 frames at 256 x 144. Playback is 4 fps for 5 seconds, and only the photographed teapot rotates slowly clockwise on the stationary black platform.
