magnet-domino · 生成方法

What was generated

The animation is a locked 256 x 144 RGB shot containing 20 frames at 4 fps. Frame 00 is an exact pixel copy of first_frame.png. The white domino rotates clockwise by 132 degrees over frames 01-19 and drifts 1.5 pixels left toward the magnet. The rest of the scene remains fixed.

Actual pixel method

  1. I read first_frame.png locally with Pillow as an RGB uint8 array. I did not use an image Read/View tool or visually inspect the source or generated sheets.
  2. Numeric color/component analysis isolated the domino as a 257-pixel connected blue-white component with bounding box x=128..155, y=40..52. Its measured matte centroid is (141.627, 45.825).
  3. I took the convex hull of that component and Gaussian-softened only its edge. A 588-pixel dilated area under the original domino was repaired using OpenCV Navier-Stokes inpainting so that the platform/table boundary continues behind the moving piece.
  4. I premultiplied the original domino RGB by its alpha matte, then transformed both with bicubic interpolation. The motion uses explicit per-frame angles of 0, 2, 5, 9, 14, 20, 28, 37, 47, 58, 70, 82, 94, 105, 114, 121, 126, 129, 131, and 132 degrees clockwise.
  5. The affine transform is conjugated through a 0.72 vertical table-plane scale. This approximates perspective foreshortening: at the middle of the turn the domino becomes steeply oriented without becoming implausibly tall in the image.
  6. A small blurred alpha-derived shadow, offset about 1.15 pixels right and 1.55 pixels down, is darkened into the reconstructed plate before the premultiplied domino is composited. The shadow direction stays fixed because the camera and lighting are fixed.
  7. artifact_io.save packed each RGB frame into the required uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, wrote all PNGs, and produced the standalone player and inspection montage. generate.py additionally made transition_check.png, which contains numeric-motion crops and amplified consecutive-frame delta maps.

Numeric verification

  • Array shape and type: (20, 144, 256, 3), uint8.
  • Packed tensor shape and type: (144, 256, 20), uint32.
  • Frame 00 RGB SHA-256: 8c1e908a7bf26a4be4be0a118e1e1b49e27413af9fb237d533868ef310d585d4, identical to the decoded source RGB hash.
  • Every saved PNG was decoded and compared pixel-for-pixel with its corresponding unpacked tensor slice.
  • Every consecutive pair changes. Changed-pixel counts are: 489, 341, 375, 392, 379, 399, 405, 402, 415, 414, 411, 414, 408, 399, 394, 375, 338, 334, 265.
  • All transition changes are confined to the small domino neighborhood; numeric bounding checks put them within approximately x=124..159, y=35..58.
  • Connected-component/PCA tracking confirms a continuous clockwise orientation path: the domino's undirected principal image-axis changes from about 174.6 degrees initially (equivalent to -5.4 degrees), through 42.8, 57.3, 77.9, 102.7, and 118.3 degrees, to 141.9 degrees in the final frame.

Reproduction

Run python generate.py in this directory. It deterministically rewrites pixels.npz, frames/00.png through frames/19.png, inspection.png, transition_check.png, and index.html, then performs all pixel/tensor assertions described above.
