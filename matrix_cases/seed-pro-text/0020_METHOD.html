What happens

Locked-off camera on the provided scene. The yellow tennis ball rolls out of the black pipe (left) onto the low end of the cardboard ramp, climbs the ramp toward the blue block (right, high end), slows to a stop without reaching the block, then rolls back downhill. All pixels were produced with local numpy/PIL math only. No external models, engines, or image tools were used; the first frame was inspected purely via numeric RGB analysis (see OBSERVATION.md).

Static background (ball removal from frame 00)

  1. Ball mask in frame 00: disc r < 6 px about center (86.5, 51.5), unioned with a chroma gate (green/yellow felt) out to r < 8.8 px.
  2. Masked pixels are seeded from the boundary-ring mean, then filled by 600 Jacobi diffusion iterations (4-neighbour average) to reproduce the smooth local ramp shading.
  3. Resulting patch std (~8–9) matches nearby clean ramp patches (~6–12). This one reconstructed image is the background for frames 01–19.

  • Fuzzy fringe from r = 7 to ~9.9 px: deterministic hashed fibres in ball-local rotated coordinates (so fuzz rotates with the ball), dense near the body and sparse/background-tinted outward, slightly sparser on top; fibre colour blends from lit albedo to olive (121,145,49) to background. Radial RGB profile was iteratively matched to the frame-00 ball ring means (within ~1–9 levels per channel).
  • Soft elliptical contact shadow darkened onto the ramp beneath the ball, offset down-right by ~1 px.
