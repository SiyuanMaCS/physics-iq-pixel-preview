METHOD — case_0029

Task: locked-off single shot, 20 RGB frames, 256×144, 4 fps. A grey tennis ball rolls right out of the black pipe at the left and strikes the brown ball of a touching brown/orange pair. Frame 00 is required to equal first_frame.png exactly. Everything below is local Python pixel/numeric work (NumPy, OpenCV, SciPy); no external models, engines or reference frames are used.

1. Measurements (numeric RGB inspection only)

  • Black pipe opening cut by the left edge: near-black bbox x 0–18, y 36–58.
  • Three spheres resting on the light coffee table, radius R ≈ 7.1 px: grey (103.0, 53.0), brown (149.5, 53.5), orange (163.5, 53.5). Brown/orange are touching (centre spacing 14 px); grey is 46 px left of brown.
  • Soft contact shadows fall to the left and slightly below the balls (light from upper right), with a mild blue-ish contact tint measured as per-channel darkening ≈ (22 %, 14 %, 6 %).

2. Clean table background (build_background)

Only the swept corridor (x 74–198, y 37–73) is ever altered; every pixel outside it stays byte-identical to frame 0 in all 20 frames (asserted).

Inside the corridor:

  1. A per-row table tone is linearly interpolated between medians of clean strips left (x 40–90) and right (x 182–234) of the objects.
  2. Real, spatially-correlated table grain (high-pass residual, σ ≈ 3.2) is remapped up from a clean front band of the same table and added to the tone.
  3. The ~4-px boundary band is finished with a small-radius Telea inpaint so the seam is invisible. The black pipe, back edge and right-side dark furniture lie outside the corridor and are never touched.

3. Ball appearance model

Each ball patch (19×19 sampled with cv2.remap) is decomposed by a single joint bounded least-squares fit (scipy.optimize.least_squares, TRF) over all three balls with a shared light vector and shared half-vector:

RGB = A·(ambient + diffuse·max(n·l,0)) + K·exp(−shin·(1−n·h)),

with n the analytic sphere normal of the pixel (screen-locked shading), plus per-ball albedo A, ambient/diffuse, specular colour K and shininess.

Residual to the measured patch is split in two:

  • fixed layer — masked Gaussian-smoothed residual (broad sheen/AO/fuzz) and compact bright specular glints (residual above threshold, softened): these stay locked in screen coordinates because illumination doesn't rotate;
  • rotating layer — the remaining high-frequency felt detail (mean |x| ≈ 3–7 levels), faded to zero near the rim. It is inverse-rotated per frame with θ = travel/R (no-slip roll; clockwise for rightward travel — sign unit-tested on a synthetic top-dot marker).

The disk alpha is an antialiased radius-7.1 circle. Patches are warped to continuous (x, y) positions with warpAffine (sub-pixel translation).

4. Motion (simulate, 1-D, 128 substeps/frame)

  • grey initial speed 4.6 px/frame (18.4 px/s), rolling friction 0.12 px/f² before impact, 0.20 after;
  • pairwise equal-mass impulses with restitution 0.30 (grey→brown) and 0.00 (brown→orange, so the initially touching pair never separates), plus positional de-overlap to the 14-px contact distance;
  • grey settles at x ≈ 139.2 touching brown at 153.2, orange at 167.2; all velocities reach zero by frame 15 and frames 15–19 are identical.
  • spin θ accumulates from arc length (v/R) for every moving ball.

5. Compositing (compose, incremental)

Starting from frame 0, each frame erases only the previous footprint (disk + shadow) of balls that have moved ≥ 0.15 px since their last paint, never touching pixels currently inside any ball disk; fills from the clean background; lays down fresh per-channel soft shadows (clipped outside disks); then repaints the balls right-to-left. This keeps pre-impact brown/orange and the entire rest of the scene byte-identical to frame 0, prevents shadow accumulation, and avoids erased-edge halos.

6. Outputs and verification

generate.py writes frames/00.png–19.png, pixels.npz (uint32 tensor 0xRRGGBB, shape 144×256×20, via artifact_io.save), inspection.png (8 key frames), transition_check.png (frames 05–10 and amplified inter-frame differences), and index.html (standalone player).

  • brown/orange region byte-identical to frame 0 through f07;
  • template (NCC) ball tracking agrees with simulated centres within 1 px;
