METHOD — case_0028

Fully local, deterministic pixel synthesis. Inputs used: first_frame.png (256×144 RGB) and prompt.txt only. All scene measurements came from numeric RGB inspection with numpy/PIL (no image viewing tool was used at any stage, so no visual-review claim is made); scipy is used for gaussian filtering and a KD-tree. The pipeline is in generate.py; intermediate calibration experiments lived in .tmp/.

1. Geometry measured from frame 0 (pixel units)

  • Black pipe mouth: centre (170.3, 40.9), outer ellipse 7.0×7.25, inner ellipse 5.45×5.75.
  • Brown ball (front): centre (182.3, 40.05), radii 6.6×7.0.
  • Orange ball (behind-right): centre (185.7, 39.85), radii 6.1×6.6.
  • The balls overlap on screen (centres 3.4 px apart) and are seen almost along their contact axis, so only a thin crescent of the orange ball is visible in frame 0.
  • Empirical limb alpha (deconvolved against the inpainted background along the exposed rims): alpha ≈ 1 for normalised radius q < 0.83, ≈ 0.7 at q = 0.88, ≈ 0.03 at q = 0.93, 0 at q ≥ 1.03. Model: a = clip((0.895 − q)/0.075 + 0.5, 0, 1).

2. Table background (normalized-convolution fill)

The mask of pixels occupied by the resting balls is the alpha union of the two discs. Only warm ball pixels are filled (R−G ≥ 11); dark desaturated pixels of the pipe rim/crevice (mean < 115 and R−G < 13) are kept as static scene. The mask is softly feathered (gaussian σ 0.5). Each channel is filled by normalized convolution with a gaussian kernel σ 3.2 (blur(frame·K)/blur(K)), and one fixed correlated noise field (white noise blurred σ 0.9, σ 2.6) is added so revealed table pixels match the frame-0 grain and never flicker. Outside the feathered hole the background is the frame-0 pixels themselves, so the pipe and its static shadow are untouched.

3. Ball shade fields (fitted to frame 0)

Coordinates are ball-normalised: u = (x−cx)/rx, v = (y−cy)/ry, q = √(u²+v²).

  • Brown: a 21-term basis in (u, v) — constants, linear, quadratic and cubic monomials, the view normal nz = √(1−u²−v²), n·u / n·v terms, and three clipped "terminator" ramps (left dark terminator, saturated right arc, lower arc) — is fitted to frame-0 ball pixels with iteratively reweighted least squares (3 iterations, outlier threshold 24/channel). Sample region q < 0.92, u > −0.95 (the robust step rejects pipe-rim outliers on the occluded left edge). 120 of 127 samples kept, fit RMS ≈ (6.2, 4.7, 6.8).
  • Orange: its visible crescent (q_orange < 0.82 and q_brown > 1.02, 14 pixels) is fit by a robust quadratic (1, u, v, u², v², uv), RMS ≈ (2.9, 3.9, 8.1). The hidden hemisphere is a smooth blend (s = clip((u+0.22)/0.55)) toward a dark warm ambient (96, 60, 44); a small additive recentering (mean crescent residual ≈ (5.7, 3.9, 2.8)) is applied. The hemisphere choice is unverifiable from frame 0 and is a physical plausibility choice (warm bounce in room light).
  • Both fields are radially clamped at the limb: for q ≥ 0.80 the value is blended (smoothstep, full by q = 0.93–0.95) toward the field sampled along the same ray at q = 0.80, which prevents polynomial extrapolation blow-ups. 4. Surface detail and the contact seam

Residuals fade toward the limb.
