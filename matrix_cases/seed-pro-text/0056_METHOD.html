Actual method (honest account, local Python pixel ops only)

  1. Numeric inspection, no image viewing tools. Geometry and colours were measured from first_frame.png with NumPy/PIL only: per-row/column RGB sampling, threshold components, and ASCII luminance maps. Measurements are in OBSERVATION.md.
  2. Background. Rather than resynthesise the whole table (which changed untouched pixels), the generator keeps the original photo and inpaints only the plank polygon and its soft table contact shadow (OpenCV Telea, radius 3, 1 px dilated mask). Everything outside the moving parts is therefore pixel-identical to frame 0. A separate smoothed per-row robust table-colour field is built solely to threshold sprites out from the wood.
  3. Sprites. Six dominoes and the duck are cut from frame 0 as RGBA:
    • domino masks = solid interior rectangle union colour/distance edge detection (black: luminance < 85 or colour distance > 42; white: low-saturation luminance band or distance > 30), hole-filled, largest connected component kept, table-coloured outer fringe trimmed, 0.7 px Gaussian alpha feather;
    • duck mask = yellow plus orange (beak/feet) threshold, dilated 3 px and extended by colour-distance fringe so the brown shaded rim is included, cropped to the measured bbox, 0.8 px feather. Eyes/beak/feet come from the original pixels.
  4. Plank. Rebuilt as a rotating polygon (dark tapered side face (22,17,14), lit grey top face (76,74,82), dark rounded tip) pivoting at the measured platform joint (206,57), length 27 px, with a blurred dark quad cast shadow kept off the wall (alpha zero above y40). Screen-space angles follow 229 deg (native pose) -> 214 -> 197 -> 181 -> 168 (contact) -> 156 deg (rest) in image coordinates.
  5. Animation physics (analytic, hand-timed). Each domino rotates about its bottom-left corner from 0 deg (upright) to 90 deg (lying flat toward the left) via PIL bicubic rotation over 2-3 sampled frames; the duck rotates up to 12 deg about its foot base and translates 6 px left. Per-frame angles are in the schedule tables in generate.py and the timeline in OBSERVATION.md; contact frames line up with the measured gaps (stick-K3 at f04, W3-duck f06, duck-K2 f08, then one block roughly every 0.5 s).
  6. Compositing. For each frame only the regions of actors that actually moved are erased (native mask union rotated mask union a soft contact ellipse) and restored from the inpainted background; new layers are alpha-composited in pile order (K3, W3, duck, K2, W2, K1, W1, plank). Unmoved actors are never redrawn, so static dominoes keep their exact original pixels. Small soft elliptical shadows are added under falling / fallen pieces.
  7. Output. Frames go through artifact_io.save, which writes the 20 PNGs, the uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor pixels.npz (H x W x 20), the 8-frame inspection.png, and the self-contained index.html player. transition_check.png is a 4x5 labelled contact sheet of all 20 frames.
