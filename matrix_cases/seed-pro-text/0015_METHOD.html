ball-in-basket · 生成方法

What happens

The orange inflatable basketball is released from rest at the mouth of the black plastic crate, falls ~54 px into the crate under gravity, strikes the crate floor, bounces twice with restitution 0.40, and is at rest inside the crate from 2.50 s (frame 10) onward. The camera is locked; nothing except the ball changes. Generated entirely with local Python (NumPy/PIL/SciPy), no external models or assets.

Measurements from first_frame.png (numeric RGB inspection only)

  • Ball silhouette: least-squares circle fit to the orange edges (robust refit, residual σ=0.69 px) → centre (108.7, 37.9), radius 29.1 px.
  • Crate: back rim band y≈59–67, open dark interior y≈60–101 with sparse molded ribs (light grey components up to luminance ~150), solid front panel y≈102–134; crate x≈56–153. Wooden table beneath.
  • Ball appearance: core orange ≈ (190,73,2), limb darkening toward (140–150,55–60,5–30), a near-vertical dark seam at x≈107 y10–44 slanting to x≈109 by y58, soft specular blob at ≈(99,39) peaking at (253,240,233), 1 px silhouette anti-aliasing.
  • Wall: smooth low-saturation grey vignette; a local quadratic surface (normalised coords about the ball) fits clean wall pixels to RMSE ≈6/channel with fine grain σ≈2.6.

Static background

One backdrop is built from frame 0; all non-ball pixels stay byte-identical. Inside the ball footprint (d < R+0.5):

  • rows y≤58: filled with the fitted wall surface plus correlated grain (Gaussian σ=0.9 px rescaled to σ=2.6, so horizontal gradients match the surrounding wall texture: mean |ΔR| ≈1.8 inside vs ≈0.9 outside);
  • row 59: a soft wall→rim blend at the measured bar edge (y≈59.6);
  • rows 60–67: near-black rim fill from row-wise medians of real rim pixels on each side (left (6,6,4) → right ~(16,16,16)), with matched noise;
  • the 2.5 px anti-aliased annulus is dissolved into the fill by coverage.

Ball sprite

A canonical 256×144 sprite map keyed to the measured circle:

  • every real ball pixel visible above the rim in frame 0 (disk rows y≤58) is harvested verbatim, preserving the measured seam and specular highlight;
  • the 1 px silhouette band is coloured from pixels 1.4 px radially inward;
  • the bottom cap hidden by the rim in frame 0 uses an analytic shading model: a 10-term linear model in sphere normals (1, nx, ny, nx², ny², nx·ny, fresnel s, s², s·nx, s·ny, s²·ny) fitted by least squares to the harvested orange pixels excluding the seam column (fit RMSE ≈6/10/16 for R/G/B);
  • the measured seam is extended ~7 px into the cap along its slant with a Gaussian profile and cosine fade so it dies out inside the crate.

Physics and per-frame rendering

  • Vertical 1-D motion from rest: g=100 px/s², floor at centre y=92, coefficient of restitution e=0.40. Impacts at t=1.040, 1.873, 2.205 s; settled after 2.33 s. Horizontal centre stays at x=108.7; no spin.
  • Squash-and-stretch about the fixed contact point only in frames whose 0.08 s shutter window touches an impact: vertical scale 0.95 (f04), 0.99 (f08), 0.98 (f09), with 35% compensating horizontal spread.
  • Each frame averages 9 sub-samples evenly spaced across a 0.08 s shutter, giving motion blur proportional to instantaneous velocity (up to ~8 px of smear near the first impact).
  • Sprites are bilinearly sampled, coverage-alpha anti-aliased, and alpha-composited over the static backdrop. Coverage is forced to zero for y≥102 so the crate's solid front panel always occludes the lower ball; rows y≥102 are never modified in any frame.
  • Frame 00 is assigned directly from first_frame.png and verified byte-identical.

Outputs and verification

  • 20 frames at 256×144 RGB, frames/00.png…19.png, plus packed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor pixels.npz (shape 144×256×20) via artifact_io.py; every PNG was checked numerically against its tensor slice.
  • index.html standalone delta player; inspection.png (8 keyframes); transition_check.png (f00→01, 02→03, 03→04, 04→05, 07→08, 08→09 pairs and ×3 amplified |difference| maps).
  • Numeric audits: frame 00 exact; ball orange centroid stays at x≈109 while its y follows the fall/bounce/settle curve (cy 35.9 → ~84 at rest); changed-pixel counts decay with the bounces (3072, 3021, 867, 444, 752, 650, 325, 212) and are exactly zero from f10→f11 onward; rows y≥106 identical in every frame. Quality was assessed through numeric RGB/ASCII inspection only (no image viewing tool used).
