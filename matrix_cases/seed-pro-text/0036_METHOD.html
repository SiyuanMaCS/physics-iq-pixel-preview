METHOD.md

METHOD — case_0036

All pixels were produced by generate.py (local Python only: numpy, scipy, PIL). No network, external references, masks, models, engines, or subagents were used. first_frame.png was inspected exclusively through numeric RGB analysis and ASCII renderings derived from the RGB array; no image viewing tool was used on it.

1. Geometry (measured numerically, image coords, origin top-left)

  • Hub/pivot of the rotating stick: P = (208, 57); static hub disc radius ~8.5 px (never erased or repainted).
  • Stick frame-0 unit direction ang0 = (−0.925, +0.390) (8 o'clock, screen sense), assembly length 60 px. It is a 3–6 px light-wood body with a black holder neck near the hub and a short dark cast-shadow wedge on the +perp side while it crosses the platform edge.
  • Six cuboids, far → near: Y (x49–67), R (64–85), K/rose (85–101), G (103–119), B (123–137), P/purple (136–155). Each block is rotated about a measured left-bottom ground-contact pivot (Y(55,92), R(68,95), K(88,95), G(107,102), B(125,106), P(137,109)).
  • The stick sweeps screen-clockwise from 8 o'clock toward 9–10 o'clock (tip rises while moving slightly left), shoving the purple block; blocks then topple toward −x into the next cuboid.

2. Object masks

Blocks. Per-block hue rules restricted to tight rectangular zones extract the saturated face cores (connected components, holes filled). Each core is then grown (grow_block, 8-neighbour flood, ≤40 rounds) through a colour gate that accepts only "block material" — strong warm or cool tint, bright highlight, or dark shade (r−b ≥ 85, r−b ≤ −5, max > 165, max < 100, green/blue dominance, or r−g > 42). Plain warm-grey wooden table fails the gate, so open table is never claimed. A final 2 px dilation inside the zone and hole-fill attaches shaded edges/AA. The union footprint is clipped to x44–159, y44–112; the 1 px contact rim between/around blocks is assigned to the nearest block by an EDT over block names so every erased pixel is owned by some sprite.

Stick. A geometric corridor in (along, perp) hub-centred coordinates bounds the assembly with asymmetric, piecewise-linear half-widths _HP/_HN (wide +perp bulge only between along 27–38 where the stick crosses the platform edge; the static black platform face and table beyond are outside it). Pixels inside the corridor are claimed only when they differ from a converged clean plate: warm wood/dark shade in the ±5.8 px body band, warm penumbra / genuinely dark core in the halo (the far +perp halo past along 27 is capped at perp 7 — it is static black platform edge, not moving shadow), the black holder neck (along < 22, near-neutral dark), and a −perp shaded-edge band across the platform lip declared geometrically so plate-matching black pixels cannot disconnect the mask. Purple pixels at the tip are excluded. Mask and plate are iterated to a fixed point (15 passes max): the plate defines ownership, ownership defines the erase region that rebuilds the plate.

3. Clean background plate

build_plate reconstructs every erased pixel:

  • A global quadratic colour field in (x, y) is fit once on surviving table pixels (least squares), and a matched grain field (filtered Gaussian noise scaled to the per-channel fit residual, seeds 36036 / 73017) is frozen, so iteration cannot drift background colours.
  • Grey platform-top and near-black front-face pixels are surface- classified independently of the erase and filled from the nearest surviving pixel of the same surface (EDT) plus small fixed noise.
  • Table holes are filled by a 110-iteration masked 3×3 Laplacian diffusion, seeded by the quadratic field and pinned at surviving table pixels (a local membrane, so 1–6 px stick-shadow holes take the actual nearby table colour), followed by a 45/55 blur blend inside erased areas only.

4. Rigid rotation and compositing

Each owned region is cut once from the original frame as hard-alpha RGBA (block sprites from grown masks; the stick from the converged ownership mask, including wood, neck, and its small shadow wedge).

rot_layer_paste rotates with a full-canvas inverse map and scipy bilinear sampling (map_coordinates, no prefiltering), giving exact sub-pixel pivot placement (integer-rounded PIL layer origins produced per-frame jitter). In screen coordinates (y down) visual clockwise by θ has forward matrix [[c,−s],[s,c]]; the output→source inverse is [[c,s],[−s,c]]. Hard alpha preserves exact identity at zero angle and the edge pasted back at rest is unchanged. The stick additionally uses a min_out_y=46 alpha gate so no part of the assembly can rotate above the physical scene region (defence against any residual shadow-wedge pixel). Blocks are pasted with angle −φ (visual CCW: tops move to −x) about their left-bottom pivots.

Compositing order per generated frame: clean plate → ground-contact shadows (a soft dark ellipse under each block that has started falling, width growing with sin φ) → rotated stick assembly → block sprites far→near (near cuboids occlude far ones). The hub disc is part of the plate and is never touched.

5. Timeline (t = frame index, 0.25 s/frame)

  • Frame 0 = untouched first_frame.png (byte-identical).
  • Stick: θ(t) = 26° · clamp(t/13)^1.7 (ease-in from rest, holds 26° after frame 13). It contacts the purple right face at θ≈2–3° (t≈3–4).
  • Block lean φ(t) = φmax · smoothstep(clamp((t − t0)/5.2)), with start frames P=4, B=6, G=8, K=10, R=12, Y=14 and φmax = 78, 75, 70, 66, 62, 58° respectively. Smoothstep gives torque-like acceleration into and deceleration out of each fall, and the 2-frame offsets stagger the cascade down the row.

8. Honest approximations

This is rigid cut-and-rotate compositing from the single still, not a physics simulation: blocks rotate about fixed pivots with prescribed smoothstep profiles and no collision-driven translation; a soft ellipse stands in for the moving contact shadow; sprite edges gain bilinear-AA colour while rotating; and the stick's small cast-shadow wedge moves rigidly with the assembly (or stays with the platform where it was judged static). All static geometry, colours, grain, and lighting otherwise come from the original image.
