METHOD.md

METHOD — case_0044

Task: "Two blue-black grabber tools pull the piece of green paper from opposite corners, causing it to tear." 20 frames, 256×144, 4 fps. Frame 00 is an exact copy of first_frame.png. Everything below was produced with local Python/NumPy/SciPy pixel operations on first_frame.png only; no external models, engines, network, or subagents were used. The first frame was never opened with an image viewing tool — all inspection was numeric RGB analysis (per-channel thresholds, per-row spans, class maps rendered as ASCII).

1. Segmentation of frame 0

Thresholds were chosen from channel histograms and verified with ASCII class maps:

  • Green paper: G > 70 & G−R > 22 & G−B > 22. The per-row filled hull (between leftmost and rightmost green pixel, minimum 4 px/row) gives the sheet outline x61–202, y9–99; hull & ~green is the frame-0 triangular tear opening.
  • Grabbers: near-black max(R,G,B) < 78 plus blue accent B>90 & B−R>25 & B−G>20 & R<120, connected-component labelled and split into left/right groups at x=120. The right group includes its long dark jaw prong lying over the paper. A 1 px halo around each tool is claimed into the tool mask when its colour is closer to the tool than to the local table estimate (handles anti-aliased edges).
  • Backdrop: B>150 & B−R>30 (top blue band).
  • Table: everything else; local colour ≈ (110, 92, 64), grain σ ≈ 7.

The existing crack geometry: mouth on edge A–D at (138,25), tapering to tip T = (133,42), with per-row centreline taken from the hole and continued straight (±2 px seeded jitter) down to Qv = (133,53), where the visible crack line runs under the right tool's dark prong. Green pixels are split into piece L (left of the centreline; gripped corner D + bottom corner C) and piece R (right; gripped corner B + top corner A).

2. Edge fringe

Strict colour masks exclude 1–2 px anti-aliased blends at object borders (e.g. (86,106,74) at the sheet edge, bluish pixels around the tool bodies). If left in place while the objects move, these read as lingering green/blue outlines. A distance transform finds every non-solid, non-backdrop pixel within 2 px of a strict-green region with a greenish tint (G−R>8, G−B>10); it is assigned to the nearer piece and translated with that piece's displacement field. Blueish fringe within 2.5 px of a tool mask (B−R>12, B−G>8) is folded directly into that tool's mask.

3. Table fill for vacated pixels

All pixels belonging to moving content (strict paper, fringe, tools) define movable; frame-0 table pixels elsewhere are free. A local table colour field is built per channel by gaussian-normalised averaging (σ=10 px) of free pixels, plus one fixed grain field (spatially correlated σ=1.3 noise plus fine noise, amplitude set from the robust σ of free-table residuals), seeded once (np.random.default_rng(44)). Each frame starts from frame 0 and only movable pixels that are not covered by transformed content are replaced with this fill. Pre-existing soft shadows therefore stay baked on the table and no moving shadow is fabricated.

4. Tear dynamics

A crack-front arc coordinate u runs 0 at the mouth, through uT at tip T, to 1 at Qv. crack_proj(x,y) maps each paper pixel to its arc coordinate and perpendicular distance d from the crack line.

Tear progress E[t]: 0 for t0–3, then 0.06/0.18/0.36/0.56/0.74/0.89/1.00 across t4–10 while the front runs T→Qv, then 1.04…1.20 across t11–15 as a "release" tail; the final image holds for t15–19.

Per-pixel horizontal displacement piece_dx:

  • ahead of the front (age = u_front − u ≤ 0): 0, untouched paper;
  • behind the front, eased by a smoothstep over 0.10 of arc length onto the target displacement;
  • target = k·rigid + (1−k)·σ·opening, where k = clamp(d/26,0,1) tapers from rigid motion far from the crack to pure local crack-face opening at the crack faces (opening amplitudes 8 px left / 9 px right, σ=∓1); local opening acts only on the existing-flap segment (u < uT);
  • after the front completes, a release factor ramps k → 1 so by t15 every paper pixel moves rigidly with its grabber (final offsets −9 px left, +11 px right).

Grabber rigid translations are piecewise-linear keyframes (left 0/−1/−7/−9, right 0/+1/+8/+11 over t0→15), matching the paper's rigid term. Three single-pixel green "bridging fibres" are drawn at the running front while 0 < f < 0.95. Pieces are drawn per horizontal run with nearest-source sampling so 1 px stretches/holes in integer motion stay filled. Tools are pasted last, translated with their grips.

5. Output and verification

artifact_io.save writes pixels.npz (uint32 0xRRGGBB, shape (144,256,20)), frames/00.png–19.png, inspection.png, and index.html. generate.py additionally writes transition_check.png (frames 00/09/19 plus an amplified |f19−f00| difference panel).

Numeric verification (.tmp/verify.py):

  • tensor is uint32, shape (144,256,20), all values ≤ 0xFFFFFF;
  • every frames/NN.png, repacked as (R<<16)|(G<<8)|B, equals pixels.npz[:, :, NN] — all 20 match;
  • frame 00 equals first_frame.png exactly;
  • changed-pixel counts vs frame 0: 319 (t1, tension-only), rising as the front runs (≈5.1k–6.8k during t4–10) to 9,909 at t15, then static;
  • strict-green pixel counts stay ≈ 5408–5532 across all frames (paper is never erased, only relocated);
  • per-row span checks and ASCII class maps confirm: crack faces separate symmetrically during the run, a table-coloured gap opens between the two pieces, and the tools translate with their gripped corners.

Known simplifications

  • Motion is horizontal per-pixel only (the locked-off tabletop framing makes in-plane horizontal separation dominate); there is no out-of-plane curl.
  • The crack path below T is a seeded near-straight line rather than a full fracture simulation; tearing below y53 is occluded by the right tool's prong and is expressed through the piece separation instead.
  • Soft contact shadows stay fixed on the table; vacated tool/paper interiors are filled with a stationary local table texture rather than a physically tracked one.
