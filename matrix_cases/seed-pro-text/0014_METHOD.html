METHOD.md

METHOD — case_0014

Prompt: locked-off shot; black plastic crate on a wooden table; orange inflatable basketball released over the crate. 20 frames, 256×144, 4 fps.

Everything was produced with local Python pixel operations (numpy + Pillow) from first_frame.png alone. No external models, engines, network access, or image-viewing tools were used; the first frame was inspected exclusively through numeric RGB analysis.

1. Measurements from frame 00

  • Ball silhouette (orange chroma key) fitted to a circle by least squares on row edge points: center (132.13, 37.61), radius 25.5 px, radial fit residual ≈ 0.45 px.
  • Crate front face measured from the image: mouth trapezoid from the back edge (y≈53.6, x 110–154) to the front edge (y≈57.6, x 97.5–172.5); front rim band y58–63; wide slot y64–67; rail y68–71; small grid holes y72–79; rail y80; diamond lattice y81–90; rail y91–95; solid base panel y96–119.
  • Back wall is a smooth warm-gray gradient with camera grain and vignette; the wooden table occupies the lower scene and is never touched by the simulation.

2. Static background plate

The ball occupies the mouth region in frame 00, so a clean plate was reconstructed:

  1. Degree-3 polynomial (10 basis terms in normalized x, y) least-squares fit to ~16.5 k wall samples (excluding ball, crate, wood, vignette), RMS residual ≈ 6 levels.
  2. Harmonic (Laplace) fill of the vacated disk with Dirichlet boundary taken from real wall pixels (900 Jacobi iterations on a 48×59 local grid), seeded with the polynomial surface, then mild σ≈3 grain. Result: boundary neighbor contrast ~2.4 levels, i.e. no visible circular seam.
  3. Inside the mouth trapezoid the fill is near-black with σ≈1.6 noise matching the dark interior; orange-fringed rim AA pixels are replaced with dark plastic, true rim pixels are kept.

Only the ball footprint (dilated to R+3.2 px for its soft AA fringe) is painted; every other pixel equals frame 00.

3. Ball sprite

Per-pixel coverage α from the fitted disk (α = clip((R+0.35−d)·1.15)). Interior sprite colors come straight from frame 00 (orange body, dark curved seams, highlight); the AA ring is un-composited against the reconstructed plate (C = (I − (1−α)B)/α) and clipped. The sprite is re-sampled bilinearly at sub-pixel positions.

4. Crate coverage

A plastic-coverage map P of the front face is keyed from plate luminance (L≤32 → plastic, L≥58 → opening), with warm brown reflections forced open and the structural bands (rim, rails, solid base y96–119) forced solid. The mouth is an explicit trapezoid mask between the back and front rim edges.

5. Simulation and compositing

  • Vertical motion only (ball is centered in the opening): g = 92 px/s² from rest, inner floor at cy = 88 (ball bottom y≈113.5), restitution e = 0.38, sub-stepped at 1 ms. First impact between frames 04 and 05, small rebound (apex frame 06), second contact near frame 08, settled (|Δy|<1 px) from frame 09 on.
  • Mild centre-anchored, area-preserving squash (sy down to 0.93 at the frame-05 impact).
  • For every frame the moved sprite is composited in three depth layers:
    1. above the back rim edge — normal sprite over the wall;
    2. inside the mouth trapezoid — sprite with interior depth shading (0.90 at the rim to 0.60 deep plus a small cool ambient term);
    3. behind the front face — shaded sprite visible only where the plastic coverage is open (slot, grid holes, lattice), opaque plastic painted back over it; the solid base hides the ball below y96.

Frame 00 is the supplied first_frame.png, byte-for-byte (frames/00.png copied, tensor verified against it). Frames 09–19 are pixel-identical, showing the ball resting inside the crate.

6. Verification

  • frames/00.png equals first_frame.png exactly; all 20 PNGs unpack to the uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor in pixels.npz (asserted in generate.py).
  • index.html delta stream decoded back and matched against all 20 PNGs.
  • Changed-pixel bounding box stays inside x104–159 / y10–90; wall, table, and crate structure outside the ball path are unchanged from frame 00.
  • inspection.png shows representative frames 1/3/6/9/12/15/18/20; transition_check.png shows per-frame absolute difference maps (localized to the moving ball).
