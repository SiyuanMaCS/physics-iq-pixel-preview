METHOD.md

METHOD — case_0041

What was generated

20 RGB frames, 256×144, 4 fps (5 s): a knife blade finishing a vertical slice through a tangerine half resting on a glass cutting board. Everything was produced by generate.py from two inputs only — first_frame.png and prompt.txt — using local Python pixel operations (NumPy + Pillow). No network, external models/engines, other cases, masks, or subagents were used. first_frame.png was inspected only through numeric RGB analysis; no image viewing tool was used on it.

1. Scene measurement (OBSERVATION.md)

Numeric inspection (per-pixel dumps, saturation/hue masks, row/column profiles) established:

  • Warm wood table; translucent glass board from y≈8 down.
  • Tangerine dome ≈ ellipse center (128, 62.5), radii (30, 28.5), solid bottom y≈90–93.
  • Blade dark-edge center path d(y) = 131 − 0.083·y, already ~80–85 % through the fruit at frame 0 (tip y≈83), with a bright steel face right of the edge and a bluish reflection streak on the glass at x≈121–123.
  • Per-row flesh extents: left flesh ends ≈ d(y)−3, right flesh begins ≈ d(y)+6.

2. Base frame and masks

first_frame.png is loaded as float64 RGB and kept immutable as f0. A fruit mask is built only inside a small window (x 90–170, y 33–94) from photometric tests: saturated orange (R−G≥9, R−B≥17, R≥126, saturation ≥26) or bright peel (max ≥168, low saturation), excluding bluish pixels (blade/reflection). Frame 00 is a byte-exact copy of f0.

3. Animation drivers (smooth, eased; t = frame index)

  • Held start: frames t=0 and t=1 return the base frame unmodified (0 changed pixels, verified).
  • Slice progress p = smoothstep(0.5, 14.5, t)
  • Parting per side sep = 2.6·smoothstep(1, 14, t) px
  • Blade tip row tip = 83 + 12·p (83 → 95)
  • Steel glint row glint_y = 50 + 18·p
  • Glass reflection end refl_end = 102 + 11·p
  • Juice fade juice = smoothstep(13, 17, t)

4. Per-row fruit rendering (y = 34–93)

For each row relative to the moving tip (q = tip − y):

  1. Rows more than 1 px below the tip are left exactly as in frame 0 (intact fruit).
  2. The opening tapers to zero within 3 px of the knife point and within ~7 px of the blade entry: se = sep · tip_taper · entry.
  3. Gap fill. Between d−2−se and d+5+se a 1-D color profile is painted. Two hand-authored RGB stop palettes interpolate across the gap — STOPS_CLOSED (dark slit + steel face, matching frame 0) and STOPS_OPEN (dark-orange cut flesh faces flanking a thin steel center). They cross-fade by clip(se/1.8), so the closed cut opens into two flesh faces rather than a wide steel band. The steel band gets a soft specular boost localized at glint_y; lower fruit rows get a deep red-black shadow; the knife-point rows are narrowed (×0.38 at the point) and collapsed to a dark point. Sub-px deterministic dither (±2.5) avoids banding. Paint alpha ramps with parting; point rows are fully opaque.
  4. Parting halves. Flesh-mask pixels left/right of the cut are resampled from f0 with fractional horizontal shifts of ±se (linear interpolation, mask-weighted alpha), so the halves slide apart without pulling background pixels with them.

5. Glass and juice

  • The soft orange fruit reflection on the glass is intentionally left untouched (an earlier attempt to displace it only shuffled faint board texture).
  • Blade on glass (y≥94): gated by pg = clip(4p) so the physical dark blade appears only once the tip reaches the board (around t=8). A 1–3 px bluish streak at x≈123 is extended down to refl_end with an end fade.
  • Four pixels — two droplets with highlight pixels at (119,96–97) and (130–131,100–101) — fade in from t=13 to t=17 as orange juice with pale-yellow highlights.

6. Outputs

artifact_io.save writes pixels.npz (key pixels, uint32 0xRRGGBB, transposed to (144,256,20)), frames/00.png–19.png, inspection.png (frame contact sheet), and index.html (standalone player). transition_check.png is a 2× nearest-neighbor enlarged strip of the work region for frames 00, 05, 09, 13, 19, with timestamps.

7. Verification performed

  • Frame 00 (and held frame 01) byte-identical to first_frame.png (0 differing pixels).
  • Every frames/XX.png unpacked from the uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor in pixels.npz with exact equality; tensor shape (144,256,20).
  • Changed-pixel counts vs frame 0 grow smoothly (≈2.0k at t=2 peaking ≈3.2k by t=13) then settle to 0 per-step change at t=18–19; per-step mean |ΔRGB| stays small (peak ≈6.7), no jumps.
  • All final-frame edits are confined to the work window (bbox x≈95–170, y≈39–112); zero changed pixels outside x 88–175 / y 34–116.
  • Juice specks confirmed numerically: board tone → saturated orange with a light highlight in late frames.
  • The cut-zone structure (dark slit/steel, parting orange faces, tapered point) was checked via numeric ASCII dumps, not an image viewer.

Known simplifications

Cut faces and steel are procedural 1-D color profiles (not simulated lighting); parting is a rigid per-row horizontal shift of the flesh mask (the halves do not rotate or deform); juice is four static pixels. These keep every edit local, reversible, and consistent with the photographic frame-0 palette.
