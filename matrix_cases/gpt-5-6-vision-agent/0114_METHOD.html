match-blows-balloon · 生成方法

Source and measurements

Only first_frame.png and prompt.txt were used as scene inputs. I measured the balloon silhouette, match endpoints, platform pivot, diagonal wall/table seam, and fixed cable directly in the 256×144 source. The measured match pivot is (179, 83), its free-tip radius is about 89 pixels, and the initial left-pointing image-space angle is about 180.65°. OBSERVATION.md was written before generate.py.

Pixel construction

Frame 00 is a direct copy of the source array. For later frames, the scene stays locked off.

The region hidden by the balloon is reconstructed locally. Smooth second-order RGB illumination surfaces are fitted separately to visible wall and table pixels. Wall residual texture is sampled from unobstructed pixels to the right and aligned by distance from the diagonal seam. Wood residual texture is sampled from the same x-coordinate lower on the unobstructed tabletop and illumination-corrected back to the target row. A feathered manual occlusion mask limits this reconstruction to the old balloon/body-shadow area. The original narrow match and clip are removed with local OpenCV Navier–Stokes inpainting; the platform, cable, chair edge, and all pixels outside the local masks remain sourced from the original frame.

The match is redrawn at 4× resolution and downsampled with Lanczos filtering. It consists of a shaded wooden shaft, reddish live head, tiny amber flame/contact glow, and a rotating pink mounting clip. Its angular motion uses a smoothstep curve from the measured initial angle through 14° clockwise, ending at frame 16 and holding through frame 19. During the pre-pop frames, the original photographic balloon is composited back over the moving shaft so the contact has correct depth ordering.

At frame 04, the photographic balloon skin is anisotropically warped down toward its knot and clipped to a ragged, lobed outline, with muted fold lines. This creates a flexible-latex contraction rather than brittle shards. From frame 05 onward, eight deterministic irregular rubber scraps follow short gravity-driven arcs, contact individually chosen table heights, and complete diminishing slides. A larger torn neck remnant remains near the original knot. The original broad balloon shadow disappears with the volume; tiny settled scraps receive faint local shadows.

Output and verification

artifact_io.save writes the 20 PNG frames, the compressed uint32 pixel tensor, the inspection sheet, and the standalone HTML player. transition_check.png contains selected before/after pairs and amplified absolute-delta views. I visually reviewed both inspection.png and transition_check.png, revised the initial glass-like rupture into a rubber collapse, then reviewed the revised sheets again.

generate.py finally asserts all of the following:

  • array shape is exactly (20, 144, 256, 3) and dtype is uint8;
  • frame 00 is pixel-identical to first_frame.png;
  • every saved PNG is pixel-identical to its generated RGB frame;
  • pixels.npz has dtype uint32, shape (144, 256, 20), and exactly equals the RGB values packed as 0xRRGGBB.

Run python3 generate.py from this directory to reproduce and re-verify every generated artifact.
