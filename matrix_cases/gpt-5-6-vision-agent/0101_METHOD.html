+

+    brightness = np.clip((luminance - 47.0) / 48.0, 0.0, 1.0)

OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Frame measurements

  • Canvas: 256 x 144 RGB pixels; fixed, locked-off camera.
  • Background wall/table boundary: approximately y=28 at the left, rising slightly to y=26 at the right. The warm wood tabletop fills the lower 116 pixels.
  • Black circular platform: top ellipse approximately x=119..190, y=45..81; dark cylindrical side continues to y=92. Its visible top center is approximately (154, 64).
  • Moving wrench: small silver-gray diagonal shape on the left half of the platform, approximately x=122..142, y=57..78. Its useful rotation center is approximately (133, 68). In frame 00 its long axis points down-right, around 43 degrees in image coordinates.
  • Magnet apparatus: stationary at left, approximately x=76..104, y=49..103. It includes the purple strip/support, bright metal housing, and its tabletop shadow.
  • Cable: stationary dark cable from the platform's right side toward the upper-right edge, approximately x=185..255, y=36..66.
  • Lighting: soft cool ambient light from above/front-left, with low-contrast shadows cast on the table. No camera or exposure change is expected.

Motion design

Only the wrench moves. The platform, magnet assembly, cable, wall, table grain, shadows, and framing remain locked. The wrench completes about 1.4 clockwise turns (504 degrees) during the 5-second shot. Motion uses a smooth-start/smooth-stop timing curve so the first and last transitions are not abrupt. A very small soft contact shadow rotates with the wrench beneath it.

METHOD.md

Method

The sequence is a local pixel animation derived only from first_frame.png and the task text.

  1. I measured the platform, magnet assembly, cable, and wrench directly in the supplied 256 x 144 frame.
  2. I isolated the wrench inside a hand-localized polygon using its brighter, neutral/cool metal pixels. A soft alpha edge preserves the antialiasing and metallic shading from the photograph.
  3. I made a clean plate by OpenCV TELEA-inpainting only the compact original wrench footprint. The rest of the source image is copied without camera, color, exposure, or geometry changes.
  4. For frames 01-19, I rotate the extracted wrench clockwise about (130, 68). A cubic smoothstep drives 504 degrees over the shot, providing a gentle start and stop. A faint blurred contact shadow rotates with it.
  5. Frame 00 is a byte-exact copy of first_frame.png. The helper writes the 20 PNGs, the standalone HTML player, and a uint32 tensor packed as 0xRRGGBB with shape (144, 256, 20).
  6. The generator reloads every saved PNG and pixels.npz, checks them pixel-for-pixel against the in-memory frames, and asserts that all changed pixels remain in the compact wrench area.

inspection.png is an eight-frame contact sheet. transition_check.png shows five before/after pairs and their amplified absolute differences for transition review.
