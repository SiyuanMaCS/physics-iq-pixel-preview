magnet-wrench · 生成方法

The sequence is a local pixel animation derived only from first_frame.png and the task text.

  1. I measured the platform, magnet assembly, cable, and wrench directly in the supplied 256 x 144 frame.
  2. I isolated the wrench inside a hand-localized polygon using its brighter, neutral/cool metal pixels. A soft alpha edge preserves the antialiasing and metallic shading from the photograph.
  3. I made a clean plate by OpenCV TELEA-inpainting only the compact original wrench footprint. The rest of the source image is copied without camera, color, exposure, or geometry changes.
  4. For frames 01-19, I rotate the extracted wrench clockwise about (130, 68). A cubic smoothstep drives 504 degrees over the shot, providing a gentle start and stop. A faint blurred contact shadow rotates with it.
  5. Frame 00 is a byte-exact copy of first_frame.png. The helper writes the 20 PNGs, the standalone HTML player, and a uint32 tensor packed as 0xRRGGBB with shape (144, 256, 20).
  6. The generator reloads every saved PNG and pixels.npz, checks them pixel-for-pixel against the in-memory frames, and asserts that all changed pixels remain in the compact wrench area.

inspection.png is an eight-frame contact sheet. transition_check.png shows five before/after pairs and their amplified absolute differences for transition review.
