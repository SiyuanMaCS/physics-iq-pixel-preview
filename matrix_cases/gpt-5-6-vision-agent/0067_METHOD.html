The red surface already visible in the receiving glass is locally resampled upward inside an analytic elliptical interior mask. Its level follows a smooth 5-pixel rise after the stream connects. Bright neutral pixels from the original glass are composited back in place so stationary glass reflections do not move with the liquid. The much wider reservoir reveals a thin band of nearby clear-glass texture in two one-pixel steps, representing a subtle 2-pixel fall.

The stream, startup droplets, impact ripple, and occasional tiny splash glints are procedural RGBA shapes rendered at 4x resolution and Lanczos-downsampled for antialiased edges. Their red, orange, and dark tones are sampled by eye from the photographed beverage. All effects are confined to the nozzle-to-glass path and the two liquid surfaces; the camera, basket, dispenser, glassware, table, lighting, and shadows remain locked.
