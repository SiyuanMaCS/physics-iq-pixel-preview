liquid-on-duck · 生成方法

The animation is a deterministic local Python/Pillow pixel composite. It does not use a generative model, network access, external engine, optical flow, or content from any other case.

first_frame.png is loaded as RGB and copied directly into frame 00. Frames 01-19 each begin again from that same locked source image. The only animated pixels are hand-positioned liquid layers:

  • a cubic-curve bright-red falling stream anchored to the visible nozzle and the duck's crown;
  • a clipped translucent crown coating and two curved rivulets inside a conservative hand-measured duck silhouette;
  • short-lived deterministic splash beads and runoff drops;
  • a perspective-flattened, irregular reflective puddle clipped out of an expanded duck silhouette so that it stays visually behind the duck;
  • small moving highlights, meniscus lines, and stream bulges to communicate glossy fluid motion at 4 fps.

The liquid is drawn on a transparent layer at 4x resolution and downsampled with Lanczos filtering for antialiased subpixel edges. That small action layer is then composited onto the untouched 1x source, leaving every pixel outside the liquid footprint exactly unchanged. Its palette uses opaque bright scarlet cores, darker crimson menisci/edges, translucent red coating, and pale red highlights consistent with the source's upper-left lighting. Coating and puddle size increase monotonically, while only narrow fluid details oscillate.

artifact_io.save writes the required PNG sequence, packed uint32 tensor, inspection sheet, and standalone HTML player. generate.py additionally creates transition_check.png, then reopens every PNG, repacks it as 0xRRGGBB, and asserts exact equality with the corresponding pixels.npz plane. It also asserts exact RGB equality between frame 00 and first_frame.png.
