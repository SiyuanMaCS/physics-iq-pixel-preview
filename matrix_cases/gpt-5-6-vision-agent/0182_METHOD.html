OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Measured composition

  • Source frame: 256 x 144 RGB, fixed camera, landscape view.
  • Wall/table boundary runs from about (0, 44) to (207, 35) to (255, 62); the pale blue wall fills the upper field and the wood table fills the lower field.
  • Red upright support: approximately x=125..143, y=91..134, with a bright front face and darker right face.
  • Yellow horizontal beam: approximately x=112..174, y=74..95; it projects left from the support and turns upward slightly in perspective at the right end.
  • Blue vertical wooden block: approximately x=158..175, y=22..82. Its lower edge is already in contact with the yellow beam's top surface. The bright cyan-blue front and darker blue right face make its rectangular depth readable.
  • Orange/black grabber: enters cropped from the top. The left finger occupies roughly x=150..162, y=0..27, and the right finger x=172..188, y=0..27; black pads touch the blue block near its upper end.
  • Illumination is soft from the upper left. Contact shadows are subtle; the camera and background remain completely stationary.

Physical interpretation

Frame 00 captures the end of placement: the blue block is vertical, its weight has transferred to the yellow beam, and the grabber still lightly grips it. The continuation will show the tool release and withdraw while all three blocks remain balanced. This avoids an implausible second placement motion after visible contact has already occurred.

20-frame event plan (4 fps, 5 seconds)

  • Frame 00 (0.00 s): exact supplied image; grabber closed and blue block seated.
  • Frames 01-02 (0.25-0.50 s): brief hold after contact; no camera or block motion.
  • Frames 03-07 (0.75-1.75 s): opposing grabber fingers open smoothly by about 7 px each. The pads separate from the blue block without disturbing it.
  • Frames 08-13 (2.00-3.25 s): the open grabber retracts vertically upward until it leaves the frame.
  • Frames 14-19 (3.50-4.75 s): static proof of stable balance; blue remains upright on yellow, yellow remains balanced on red.

Only the grabber moves. The motion uses eased interpolation so acceleration and stopping are not abrupt. Frame 00 will be copied byte-for-byte before any compositing.

METHOD.md

Method

Generation approach

This animation is a local source-pixel composite, not a rendered 3D scene or an externally generated image sequence.

  1. first_frame.png is loaded as the fixed source plate. Frames 00-02 retain it exactly, and frame 00 is explicitly reassigned from the untouched source array before saving.
  2. The two orange/black grabber fingers and the small dark connector/contact pad are traced with polygon masks around their source silhouettes. A separate protection mask prevents any blue-block pixel from entering a moving layer or a background replacement.
  3. The small portion of pale-blue wall hidden by the tool is reconstructed with a quadratic RGB color surface fitted only from visible wall samples on both sides of the setup. Replacement is limited to the dilated tool footprint. The table, red support, yellow beam, and all visible blue-block pixels remain unchanged.
  4. From frames 03-07, the source-pixel finger layers rotate in opposite directions around offscreen pivots. A smoothstep curve gives gradual acceleration and deceleration. The opening reaches about 13 degrees per finger.
  5. From frames 08-13, both opened fingers and the connector translate upward by 35 pixels with another smoothstep curve, leaving the frame. Frames 14-19 hold on the stable balanced blocks.
  6. Layers are transformed with bilinear sampling in premultiplied-alpha form, composited over the repaired plate, rounded, clipped, and stored as RGB uint8 frames.

Output construction

  • artifact_io.save writes the 20 PNG frames, the packed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, inspection contact sheet, and standalone HTML player.
  • transition_check.png is a nearest-neighbor enlargement of the grabber/block contact region at eight selected frames, showing release, withdrawal, and the clean final state.
  • The motion is 20 frames at 4 fps (5 seconds), with a locked 256 x 144 camera.

Visual review and revisions

Both inspection.png and transition_check.png were reviewed with the image viewing tool. Early passes revealed blue-edge contamination in broad jaw masks, an under-masked right-jaw edge, and a stationary dark connector remnant. The masks were revised to protect the blue block exactly, the border-touching wall repair was changed from inpainting to a fitted wall surface, and the connector was given its own retracting layer. The final review shows a stationary block stack, symmetric jaw release, upward tool withdrawal, and no residual tool pixels in the final hold.
