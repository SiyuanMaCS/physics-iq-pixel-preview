ball-and-block-fall · 生成方法

The animation is generated entirely with local Python pixel operations using NumPy and Pillow.

  • Frame 00 is copied byte-for-byte from first_frame.png.
  • For later frames, the held ball and block are removed from the locked background with small horizontal color interpolations and a local blur. The surrounding wall, pillows, table, camera, and lighting remain fixed.
  • The visible ball and block pixels are extracted from the supplied first frame with analytic alpha masks. These source sprites are translated, scaled, and rotated with bicubic resampling rather than replaced with unrelated imagery.
  • Motion follows hand-authored damped ballistic trajectories. Both objects accelerate downward, compress the pillow surfaces, rebound once, make a softer second contact, and settle. The ball squashes at impact; the block progressively rocks into a tilted resting pose.
  • Pillow response is drawn with broad feathered compression shadows and narrow lower-rim highlights. Separate blurred approach/contact shadows tighten as the objects near the surfaces. Small colored finger-tip accents clarify that both grabbers have opened.
  • transition_check.png shows frames 04–07 above amplified consecutive-frame difference maps, covering the approach and first impact.
  • The final verification reloads every PNG, packs it as 0xRRGGBB, and compares it exactly with the saved uint32 tensor. It also asserts the exact equality of frame 00 and the supplied first frame.

No network access, external model, external engine, other case, reference image, mask, or score was used.
