OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation

Fixed-frame measurements

  • Canvas: 256 x 144 RGB pixels; locked camera and constant 4 fps.
  • Background: plain warm-gray wall, softly shaded darker at the far left and right.
  • Table: warm medium-brown wood. Its rear edge runs from about (0, 99) through (68, 77), (158, 85), to (246, 102); it fills the bottom of frame. Fine grain follows the table perspective.
  • Tennis ball: centered about (111, 96), circular silhouette radius about 10 px, chartreuse/yellow-green with a darker lower-right side. A black smiley face consists of two small eyes near y=92 and a curved mouth near y=97. A soft contact shadow extends down-right.
  • Suspended clear acrylic box: top corners about (85, 44) and (132, 44), bottom corners about (87, 73) and (130, 78). It is transparent, with faint gray edge lines and subtle glass highlights/refraction. Its center aligns slightly left of the ball center; the ball will still fit beneath it.
  • Suspension: black connector centered around x=106 from y=38 to 45. An orange-brown cord/line rises vertically through a small black hanging mechanism and hook/chain to the top edge. The visible chain spans approximately y=0..39.
  • Lighting: diffuse and soft, from upper-left/front. No hard moving shadows.

Motion and event plan (20 frames)

  • Frame 00 (0.00 s): exact source frame, box suspended above the smiling tennis ball.
  • Frames 01-03 (0.25-0.75 s): near-still anticipation; cord becomes taut and the box starts moving down by subpixel-to-small increments.
  • Frames 04-12 (1.00-3.00 s): smooth, clearly readable descent. The box, connector, and lower cord segment translate together; transparent interior remains visually subtle so the ball is visible as the box approaches it.
  • Frames 13-16 (3.25-4.00 s): eased deceleration as the bottom of the box reaches the tabletop plane and the box encloses/covers the ball. The ball itself remains stationary.
  • Frames 17-19 (4.25-4.75 s): gentle 1 px settling/bounce to a stable final position, with the clear walls surrounding the smiling ball and the bottom edge resting visually on the table.

The camera, wall, table, ball, lighting, and all unrelated pixels remain locked. Only the suspended box/connector and the necessary exposed cord pixels change.

METHOD.md

Method

The animation is a local, source-derived pixel continuation made entirely by generate.py. No external images, models, engines, masks, or references were used.

  1. first_frame.png is loaded as the only scene image. Frame 00 is copied from it without modification.
  2. A small mask is constructed from measured acrylic-box edge coordinates and the hanging collar. OpenCV's local TELEA inpainting reconstructs the narrow wall/table regions hidden by those edges. The rest of the source frame is never replaced or repainted.
  3. The signed RGB difference between the source and that clean plate captures the source photograph's real antialiased box edges, highlights, and black hanging hardware.
  4. For frames 01-19, this signed residual is translated vertically with bilinear subpixel resampling and added to the clean plate. This lets the original clear-box detail combine with the wall, table, and ball at its new position without pasting an opaque source rectangle.
  5. A very faint translucent gray face and source-matched edge reinforcement make the acrylic readable over the table while leaving the smiling tennis ball visible. The exposed suspension extension is drawn as a narrow dark orange-brown cord behind the moving hardware.
  6. The 19 displacement samples use an ease-in, smooth descent, deceleration, and a restrained subpixel settle. The ball and all non-apparatus scene pixels remain stationary.
  7. artifact_io.save writes the RGB PNGs, packed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, inspection sheet, and standalone player. generate.py then reloads every PNG, packs it independently, and asserts exact equality with the corresponding tensor plane.

Visual review was performed on first_frame.png, inspection.png, transition_check.png, and the full-resolution final frame. The reviewed final frame shows the box resting around the stationary ball with the face unobscured and no camera motion.
