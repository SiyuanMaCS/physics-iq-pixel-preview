OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Frame measurements

  • Canvas: 256 x 144 RGB; fixed, eye-level camera with no intended motion.
  • Background: light gray wall from the top through roughly y=54, with a soft central glow and darker corners. A warm wood tabletop begins around y=54, with perspective grain and a soft contact-shadow field under the construction.
  • Gate: blue lintel spans approximately x=113..153, y=63..73. The red left leg spans approximately x=115..121, y=72..109; the yellow right leg spans approximately x=144..152, y=72..109.
  • Pink block: narrow vertical rectangular piece centered near x=138, approximately x=134..142, y=29..66. Its lower end is already aligned with/occluded by the blue lintel, so the opening frame depicts the instant of final placement.
  • Grabber: blue-black rounded tool enters from the top, approximately x=129..145, y=0..31. The darkest core is nearly black, with strong blue edge highlights. Its rounded lower tip overlaps the top of the pink block.
  • Lighting: soft overhead/front illumination. Existing shadows, table grain, and every part of the gate should remain locked. Only the placed block during its final settling and the grabber during retraction should change.

Generation approach

Frame 00 will be copied byte-for-byte from the source pixels. Later frames will be produced with local Python/Pillow and NumPy only: isolate the moving grabber/pink regions, reconstruct the newly exposed wall from nearby source-wall samples with a smooth illumination-preserving interpolation, composite antialiased translated object layers, and retain all untouched pixels exactly. The pink block receives only the subtle final settling before staying fixed; the grabber then follows a smooth upward trajectory.

METHOD.md

Method

The animation is a deterministic local pixel construction using Python, NumPy, and Pillow. No external images, models, engines, masks, or network resources are used.

  1. first_frame.png is loaded as the sole source. Frame 00 is copied directly and later asserted byte-for-byte equal to it.
  2. A small clean plate is reconstructed only beneath the grabber and pink block. For each affected scanline, colors are interpolated between nearby undisturbed pixels. Because this is done row by row, the reconstructed pixels naturally follow the wall illumination above, wood at the horizon, and blue lintel below.
  3. The visible pink block face is copied from the source. The three top rows hidden by the grabber are extrapolated from the nearest unobscured face row, with a darker one-pixel top edge. The block settles downward by one pixel and then remains completely fixed.
  4. The original blue-black grabber RGB pixels are retained inside a soft analytic capsule mask. That layer is translated upward on the integer-pixel path 0, 0, 0, 0, 1, 2, 4, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 22, 25, 28, 31, 33..., producing a held placement followed by smooth retraction out of frame.
  5. All pixels outside the narrow central action region remain copied from the original locked-off shot.
  6. artifact_io.save writes the 20 RGB PNG frames, packed uint32 tensor, inspection sheet, and standalone player. A separate transition sheet contains selected frames and amplified inter-checkpoint difference images. The generator verifies every PNG against the packed 0xRRGGBB tensor and checks that frame 00 exactly matches the source.
