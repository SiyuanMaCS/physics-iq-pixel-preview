Actual generation method

The animation is a deterministic local Python/Pillow pixel composite built from first_frame.png; it uses no external assets, masks, models, engines, or network access.

  1. The source RGB image is the locked background for every frame. Frame 00 is copied byte-for-byte at the pixel level.
  2. All new liquid geometry is drawn analytically at 4x resolution and downsampled with Lanczos antialiasing. The geometry includes a slightly wobbling vertical stream, a crown impact cap, branching face/body rivulets, deterministic splash beads, a shallow perspective puddle, specular streaks, and decaying ripples.
  3. A fixed duck silhouette assembled from measured ellipses clips the wet coating and keeps the red liquid on the rubber surface. Broken low-opacity sheet regions retain readable yellow rubber, eyes, and beak beneath/around the flow.
  4. The puddle is constructed as a blurred irregular ellipse with advancing lobes. Dark crimson edges, a translucent bright-red interior, and warm highlight arcs retain the baking tray's texture and make the pool appear shallow and glossy.
  5. For frames 14–19, where the initially connected stream tapers and stops, only the narrow frame-00 stream strip is reconstructed. Each row is interpolated between its immediate left/right neighbors, preserving the basket's horizontal weave and the duck crown beneath it. Segmented drops are then composited over that local reconstruction.
  6. The dispenser, basket, tray, duck pose, camera, framing, and illumination are otherwise unchanged. No global warp, camera movement, or whole-frame color change is applied.

Timing

  • Frames: 20 RGB frames, 00.png through 19.png
  • Dimensions: 256 x 144 pixels
  • Playback: 4 fps, 5 seconds
  • Flow: existing thin stream at frame 00; accumulation and peak runoff through frames 01–13; taper and segmented drops through frames 14–17; settling pool/coating through frames 18–19

Validation and visual review

generate.py reloads every PNG and compares its packed 0xRRGGBB pixels against the saved pixels.npz tensor. It also asserts that frame 00 equals first_frame.png exactly. The generated inspection contact sheet and transition sheet were viewed with the provided image-view tool; the visual review checked fixed framing, continuity of the existing stream, readable duck features, confinement of the puddle to the tray, localized transitions, and the final stopped-flow state.
