OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Measured scene

  • Source is 256 x 144 RGB. Camera is locked, with a level tabletop and a soft-focus gray wall.
  • The tabletop begins at about y=81 at the far edge and fills the lower 63 pixels. Its perspective grain runs mainly downward/outward from the center.
  • The black 30 lb kettlebell is centered near x=139. Its body is approximately x=111..164, y=21..80; the handle occupies approximately x=100..176, y=0..42 and continues beyond the top edge.
  • The white foam cup is centered under the bell. Its visible rim/upper portion is approximately x=125..164, y=81..90 and its tapered body reaches y=102. The body is already bearing the bell at the first frame.
  • Illumination is diffuse from upper left. The bell has narrow gray specular highlights on the handle and a softer highlight on the left body. The cup casts a soft shadow toward the lower right.
  • The first frame contains no other objects, people, or camera motion.

Physical interpretation

The 30 lb load greatly exceeds the buckling strength of a thin styrofoam cup. Because frame 00 already shows contact, the plausible continuation is a short settling interval followed by asymmetric wall buckling and crushing. The bell descends only as the cup loses height; it does not bounce or slide across the table. The final state is a low, irregular white crushed-foam remnant under the bell.

20-frame event plan (4 fps)

  • Frame 00 (0.00 s): exact supplied frame, untouched.
  • Frames 01-02 (0.25-0.50 s): nearly static contact; only a subpixel/one-pixel settling of the load begins.
  • Frames 03-05 (0.75-1.25 s): upper cup wall compresses; rim broadens slightly and the bell descends about 2-4 px.
  • Frames 06-09 (1.50-2.25 s): visible asymmetric buckle develops, with the cup leaning/folding slightly right; bell descends smoothly to about 8-11 px below its initial position.
  • Frames 10-13 (2.50-3.25 s): main collapse; the cup height rapidly decreases and its white side wall folds outward into an irregular flattened strip. Bell descends to about 15-18 px.
  • Frames 14-16 (3.50-4.00 s): load reaches the table through the crushed foam, with a tiny damped overshoot and correction of at most 1 px.
  • Frames 17-19 (4.25-4.75 s): stable final rest; flattened cup remnant remains visible at both lower sides of the bell.

Throughout all frames the composition remains a single fixed shot at constant exposure. No new objects appear, and wall/table texture is unchanged except where previously occluded areas are revealed by the descending bell and collapsing cup.

METHOD.md

Method

This animation is generated locally from first_frame.png with deterministic Python pixel operations; no external images, models, engines, masks, or references are used.

  1. The kettlebell is isolated as the connected dark component inside measured analytic body/handle support regions. The cup is isolated from its measured outline by brightness and low color-saturation tests.
  2. A clean plate is made only for pixels hidden by those two objects. The smooth gray wall is reconstructed with a quadratic RGB surface fitted to visible wall pixels. The narrow hidden wood strip is filled by row-wise interpolation of the visible tabletop on either side.
  3. The original kettlebell pixels, including its highlights and “30 LB” marking, are translated downward on an integer displacement curve. Because the handle begins beyond the upper image boundary, its hidden continuation is extrapolated from the visible top-edge handle pixels while tapering inward.
  4. The original cup pixels are perspective-warped between the descending bell and the fixed table contact. The warp progressively shortens and widens the cup, adds a small asymmetric buckle, and produces two low outward foam folds late in the collapse.
  5. A soft contact shadow is composited under the collapsing setup. The final one-pixel overshoot/correction is intentionally damped; the last frames hold still.
  6. Frame 00 is copied byte-for-byte from the supplied image. artifact_io.save writes the uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, PNG sequence, inspection sheet, and standalone player. The generator then reloads every PNG and the tensor and asserts exact equality with the in-memory RGB frames.

transition_check.png shows selected before/after pairs together with a 4x absolute pixel difference image. It is intended to make temporal discontinuities and unintended background changes easy to spot.
