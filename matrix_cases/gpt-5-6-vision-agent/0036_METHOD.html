OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Measured scene

  • Source is a 256×144 RGB image with a fixed, slightly elevated camera and soft studio lighting.
  • The table occupies the lower scene from an oblique boundary near (0,45) to (256,50); the upper region is a pale gray wall/background.
  • A matte black circular motor platform occupies approximately x=171..255, y=41..96, with its visible top centered near (216,59) and a dark front cylindrical wall below it.
  • The wooden striker is a thin tan rod running from the hub near (196,62) to its free end near (152,79). The free end is already at the right side of the purple block.
  • Seven upright wooden blocks form a gently receding left-to-right row. Approximate colors and image bounds, from left to right: yellow (50..67,47..94), orange-red (65..83,51..98), salmon (81..99,55..98), dark green (99..119,57..104), teal-blue (118..137,58..108), navy (132..149,60..109), purple (140..158,59..111).
  • The blocks are roughly 11–15 px wide and 40–51 px tall. Their bottom contact points advance down/right across the image. Soft shadows fall mostly down/right on the tabletop.
  • The initial frame is clean and contains no people, labels, or camera motion.

Motion/physics plan (20 frames at 4 fps)

  • Frame 00: exact source image, unchanged.
  • Frames 01–02: motor hub and rod begin a visible clockwise sweep; the rod presses the upper-right side of the purple block. Purple starts tipping left.
  • Frames 03–05: purple accelerates leftward and contacts navy; navy begins tipping while purple continues toward the tabletop.
  • Frames 06–08: the wave transfers through navy to teal-blue, with earlier blocks lying or nearly lying flat. The rod continues clockwise past the contact point.
  • Frames 09–11: teal-blue knocks dark green; compact contact spacing makes the collapse overlap naturally.
  • Frames 12–14: dark green knocks salmon, then orange-red begins to move.
  • Frames 15–17: orange-red strikes yellow; all right-side blocks are settled in a left-leaning/fallen pile with small damped residual motion.
  • Frames 18–19: yellow finishes falling left; the full row rests on the table. The rod/platform rotation continues smoothly through the final frame.

Rendering approach

  • Keep the camera and untouched environment locked by reusing the source pixels.
  • Recover a clean local tabletop plate beneath the blocks/rod with texture-aware local interpolation and subtle grain matching.
  • Extract each colored block as a softly antialiased sprite, then rotate it about its own bottom contact point according to staggered ease-in/ease-out fall curves. Add contact-consistent soft shadows beneath moving blocks.
  • Redraw the rod analytically from the fixed hub with a clockwise angle trajectory, including a dark underside and highlight so it remains integrated with the original lighting.
  • Preserve the platform itself from the source, layering the moving rod correctly around the hub.

METHOD.md

Method

The animation is a deterministic local pixel-compositing render driven only by first_frame.png and the written task prompt.

I traced the seven visible block silhouettes, including their top and side faces, and used the original pixels inside those silhouettes as antialiased sprites. The hidden tabletop is reconstructed by interpolating between clear wood immediately to the left and right of the row, then adding low-amplitude high-frequency grain sampled from a clear foreground strip in the same input. Only the thin outer portion of the original striker uses local inpainting.

Each block rotates in the image plane about its measured lower contact point. The rightmost purple block begins first, and the remaining blocks start at two-frame intervals from right to left. A cubic ease models the accelerating fall and a small damped sinusoid models the final wooden contact bounce. Soft shadows are generated from each transformed alpha silhouette and tighten as a block approaches the tabletop.

The platform remains source-photographic and fixed. Its wooden striker is redrawn analytically from the measured hub with a monotonically clockwise angle, with separate underside, wood, highlight, endpoint, and hub layers. The locked source image remains the environment plate, so there is no camera movement.

artifact_io.save writes the PNG sequence, uint32 packed-pixel tensor, inspection sheet, and standalone HTML player. The generator also creates transition_check.png from selected adjacent-frame pairs plus amplified absolute differences, then reads every PNG back and asserts exact equality with the saved 0xRRGGBB tensor. Frame 00 is separately asserted byte-for-byte equal in RGB values to first_frame.png.
