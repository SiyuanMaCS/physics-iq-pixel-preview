glass-stays-same · 生成方法

Actual generation method

I used a direct pixel-preserving static continuation. The supplied first_frame.png is opened with Pillow, converted to RGB without resizing or geometric transformation, converted to an unsigned 8-bit NumPy array, and repeated exactly 20 times along the time axis.

This is intentional: the prompt specifies a static locked-off shot with fixed framing and says that the beverage dispenser is stationary over the glass. It describes no pouring, person, camera move, or other action. Consequently, every frame keeps the exact source pixels, including the closed spigot, level red liquid surfaces, glass reflections, woven basket texture, wall, table, and shadows.

Output encoding

artifact_io.save writes:

  • frames/00.png through frames/19.png as RGB PNG files;
  • pixels.npz with shape (144, 256, 20) and dtype uint32, packed as 0xRRGGBB;
  • inspection.png, an eight-frame contact sheet;
  • index.html, a standalone 4 fps sequence player.

generate.py also writes transition_check.png, showing frame 00, frame 19, and their absolute RGB difference. The difference panel is black because the prescribed setup is completely static.

Verification

The generator verifies all of the following after writing the artifacts:

  1. The frame array is exactly (20, 144, 256, 3) with dtype uint8.
  2. Frame 00 is pixel-identical to first_frame.png.
  3. All 20 PNG files decode to the in-memory RGB frames.
  4. Every PNG, repacked to 0xRRGGBB, exactly equals its corresponding pixels.npz tensor slice.
