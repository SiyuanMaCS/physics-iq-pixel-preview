+def object_masks(src):
+    """Return antialiased masks for the bell, paper, and their original cords."""
+    f = src.astype(np.float32)
+    lum = 0.299 * f[..., 0] + 0.587 * f[..., 1] + 0.114 * f[..., 2]
+    yy, xx = np.mgrid[:H, :W]

+    # The kettlebell is the only near-black connected form in this upper ROI.
+    bell_roi = (

+        | (((xx - 97.0) / 20.5) ** 2 + ((yy - 44.0) / 21.0) ** 2 < 1.0)

+    bell = np.clip(bell, 0, 1)

+    dominance = f[..., 1] - np.maximum(f[..., 0], f[..., 2])
+    paper_roi = (xx >= 166) & (xx <= 204) & (yy >= 20) & (yy <= 65)
+    paper = np.clip((dominance - 18.0) / 42.0, 0.0, 1.0) * paper_roi

OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and animation plan

Measured scene geometry

  • Source: first_frame.png, 256 x 144 RGB, fixed camera.
  • Background: neutral gray wall over the upper ~58 px; wood support/table from about y=128 downward.
  • Left pillow: approximately x=0..137, top contour y=56..71, lower seam/edge y=110..132.
  • Right pillow: approximately x=124..255, top contour y=56..72, lower seam/edge y=111..132.
  • Kettlebell: dark body/handle approximately x=71..118, y=4..63. Its lowest point is near (97, 62), directly over the left pillow.
  • Kettlebell suspension: dark strap enters the top of the handle around x=92..99.
  • Green paper/card: approximately x=168..202, y=23..63, centered near x=185 over the right pillow.
  • Paper suspension: blue/black clip or cord approximately x=183..190, y=0..27.
  • The two object bottoms are initially almost tangent to the pillow tops. This is treated as the instant just before the demonstrator lowers both objects.
  • Lighting is soft and left/front biased. Shadows and existing image grain must remain coherent.

Physical interpretation

Both objects are lowered at the same time. The 30-lb kettlebell produces a deep, broad pillow indentation and visible local compression; the very light paper/card produces only a small shallow dent. Both cords remain attached and lengthen vertically as the objects descend. Motion eases into contact, overshoots slightly, and settles without a cut or camera motion.

Twenty-frame event plan (4 fps)

  • Frame 00: exact supplied image, before downward motion.
  • Frames 01-03: synchronized slow downward start; pillows essentially unchanged.
  • Frames 04-06: both objects approach/enter their pillow surfaces; first contact shadows appear.
  • Frames 07-10: loading phase. Kettlebell moves down strongly as the left pillow compresses; paper moves down modestly with a shallow right-pillow dent.
  • Frames 11-13: maximum compression with a small physically plausible overshoot.
  • Frames 14-16: slight rebound upward, stronger for the left pillow than the paper side.
  • Frames 17-19: damped settling into stable final positions. The kettlebell remains substantially embedded and the paper rests lightly on its pillow.

Throughout all frames the viewpoint, support, wall, illumination direction, object identity, and 256 x 144 framing remain fixed.

METHOD.md

Method

The animation is a deterministic local Python pixel transformation of first_frame.png; no external images, models, masks, engines, or network resources were used.

Construction

  1. The kettlebell is isolated inside a measured upper-left region with a luminance mask constrained by two analytic ellipses (handle/body). The green paper is isolated by green-channel dominance inside its measured bounds. Both masks receive a small Gaussian edge softening to retain the photographed antialiasing.
  2. Those two objects and their original short cords are removed from a clean plate with local OpenCV Telea inpainting. This fills only revealed pixels using surrounding pixels from the supplied frame.
  3. Each pillow is compressed with an inverse coordinate remap. Horizontal Gaussian fields center the deformation beneath each object; a vertical weight peaks at the pillow surface and falls to zero in the wall and at the wood support. The 30-lb kettlebell uses a broad 13.8-pixel maximum deformation, while the paper uses a shallow 2.4-pixel response.
  4. Contact shadows are analytic soft ellipses modulated by the same loading phase. The cords are redrawn as top-anchored antialiased lines, so they lengthen continuously rather than drifting with the objects.
  5. Source pixels for the kettlebell and paper are translated subpixel-wise and alpha-composited over the deformed plate. Both use the same 20-sample time phase: smooth loading, slight maximum-compression overshoot, rebound, and damped rest. Their displacement magnitudes differ because the pillow yields strongly under the kettlebell but very little under the paper.
  6. Frame 00 bypasses all processing and is copied byte-for-byte from first_frame.png.

Output and checks

  • artifact_io.save writes the 20 RGB PNGs, the uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor in pixels.npz, inspection.png, and the standalone index.html player.
  • generate.py additionally creates transition_check.png and reloads every PNG and tensor slice. It asserts exact equality among generated RGB frames, saved PNGs, and packed tensor values, and separately asserts that frame 00 exactly equals the source image.
  • I visually reviewed inspection.png, transition_check.png, and the final frame with the image viewing tool. The locked framing, simultaneous lowering, cord continuity, contact, contrasting deformation, overshoot, and final rest were all checked.
