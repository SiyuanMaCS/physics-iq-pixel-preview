OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

First-frame measurements

  • Canvas: 256 × 144 RGB, fixed landscape camera.
  • Background: pale gray-blue wall above an irregular dark wood tabletop; the wall/table boundary runs near y=14 at the left, dips to about y=20 near x=120, and meets the dark rear edge near y=15–20 on the right.
  • Glass cutting board: perspective quadrilateral with approximate corners (64,59), (194,55), (239,124), (34,126). It is translucent gray-green with a thin cyan-green rim, granular texture, and a broad warm sun patch toward the right.
  • Folded paper: compact crumpled/folded white object centered around (136,82), approximate occupied bounds x=122–151, y=70–96. It has sharp white facets, gray folds, a dark charred center, and a small brown-black burned area at its base.
  • Combustion zone: centered around (143,82). The first frame has a tiny bright white-yellow burning point and a faint blue-white vertical plume immediately above it. The paper/base shadow occupies roughly x=122–156, y=87–99.
  • Smoke: two thin pale strands already visible. The left strand rises from about (128,76), bends through (121,61), (119,43), and (111,12), then exits the top. The brighter central/right strand rises from about (145,72) through (145,52), (147,32), and (146,0). Smoke is semi-transparent and blurred, never an opaque painted line.
  • Lighting: strong natural sunlight from upper left, including a broad diagonal shaft across the left tabletop and a bright gold patch along the right/bottom. All static geometry, grain, highlights, and camera framing must remain unchanged.

20-frame event plan (4 fps, 5 seconds)

  • Frame 00: exact source image, byte-for-byte pixel content after RGB load.
  • Frames 01–03: tiny yellow-white flame wakes above the charred paper center; existing smoke rises and begins a gentle side-to-side curl. Ember at the fold glows slightly.
  • Frames 04–07: flame reaches its first modest peak, still only about 8–12 pixels tall, leaning right then left. A soft warm reflection flickers on nearby paper facets and glass. Smoke thickens close to the source and develops wispy, translucent lobes higher up.
  • Frames 08–11: flame briefly contracts and splits into a small main tongue plus a short secondary lick. The burned center darkens and expands subtly; white smoke continues upward with a slow leftward drift.
  • Frames 12–15: second flame pulse, brighter and slightly taller but localized to the folded paper. New denser smoke rolls off the paper, curls right near y=55, then blends into the pre-existing upper trails.
  • Frames 16–19: flame settles to a low flicker while ember remains visible. Smoke becomes the dominant motion, with its newest lower curls moving upward and fading; the last frame remains actively burning rather than extinguishing.

Continuity constraints

  • No camera motion, cuts, zoom, object translation, added props, hands, or text.
  • Preserve the source frame outside a compact combustion/smoke region. Only smoke overlays, flame/ember pixels, a subtle local reflection, and slight progressive charring may change.
  • Motion is continuous at 4 fps: plume features advect upward, fade with age, and vary smoothly rather than teleporting.

METHOD.md

Method

The animation is generated locally from first_frame.png with deterministic Python, NumPy, and Pillow pixel operations. No external images, models, masks, engines, or references are used.

Frame 00 is the unmodified RGB source. Frames 01–19 always begin from that same locked source frame, which prevents camera drift and preserves the tabletop, glass board, sunlight, and paper geometry exactly outside the animated region. Semi-transparent high-resolution layers are drawn at 4× resolution and reduced with Lanczos filtering for natural antialiasing.

The smoke is an analytic particle field emitted continuously from two nearby points on the folded paper. Each puff follows an upward accelerating path with deterministic sinusoidal wind and curl, expands with age, fades over its lifetime, and receives a blurred filament edge. The low plume is slightly warm while the upper plume is daylight white. The pre-existing smoke in the source is retained, and new smoke blends into it.

The fire is a compact layered construction: blurred orange reflection, slowly expanding translucent char, pulsing ember, an orange outer flame, yellow inner flame, and white-hot core. Smooth multi-frequency functions control flame height, lean, secondary licking, and brightness across five seconds. Changes remain confined to the burn site and rising plume.

artifact_io.save writes the uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, PNG sequence, inspection sheet, and standalone player. The generator additionally creates transition_check.png, then reloads every PNG and the NPZ tensor and asserts exact equality with the in-memory RGB frames and exact equality of frame 00 with the source.
