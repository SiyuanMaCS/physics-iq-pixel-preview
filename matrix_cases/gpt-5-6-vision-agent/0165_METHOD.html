OBSERVATION.md

Scene observations and animation plan

Measured scene

  • Source frame is 256 x 144 RGB, landscape, with a locked low tabletop camera.
  • Background is a softly lit pale blue-gray wall occupying roughly y=0..56. The warm wooden tabletop begins near y=55 and fills the remainder, with diagonal grain and a mild depth blur.
  • The shallow transparent glass bowl spans about x=73..199 and y=20..99. Its elliptical rim is near y=21..30; its curved base is near y=92..98.
  • Opaque/translucent red liquid fills the bowl from about y=68 to the curved bottom, darkest at the front and edges.
  • A narrow central match bundle occupies roughly x=127..149. The colored sticks/body are visible from y=33 down into the liquid. A small orange flame/smolder highlight is visible near x=137, y=13..27.
  • A tall inverted transparent jar is centered over the bundle, about x=111..176, and continues beyond the top edge. Its glass is subtle: pale vertical refraction bands, fine dark edge lines, and transmitted background/bowl colors. In frame 00 its open lower edge is visually integrated with the center of the bowl rather than presented as an opaque object.
  • Illumination is soft and diffuse from upper left. Shadows are low contrast. All generated additions must retain photographic softness, glass transparency/refraction, and the original table/bowl occlusions.
  • Frame 00 must be copied byte-for-byte in pixel content from first_frame.png.

20-frame event plan (4 fps, 5 seconds)

  • Frame 00: exact supplied still; jar partially lowered over the lit match bundle.
  • Frames 01-03: jar continues downward very slightly; glass edge/refraction cues shift downward while the match flame remains visible and lively.
  • Frames 04-07: steady descent becomes clearer. The jar mouth approaches and then meets the liquid; a small compressed/reflected ripple appears near the central contact region.
  • Frames 08-10: jar settles into the red liquid. The flame narrows and dims as oxygen becomes limited; orange light on the inner glass weakens.
  • Frames 11-13: flame collapses to a tiny ember and goes out. A faint gray smoke wisp rises inside the jar.
  • Frames 14-16: jar is stationary and sealed by the liquid; smoke softens and disperses. Red liquid begins rising slightly inside the jar around the central bundle.
  • Frames 17-19: final stable demonstration state. Flame is gone, jar remains down, smoke is faint, and the inner red liquid level is visibly but subtly above the outer level.

Motion is deliberately limited to the jar/refraction, flame/smoke, central liquid/ripples, and their local shadows. The camera, background, table, and bowl remain fixed.

METHOD.md

Method

The animation is a deterministic local pixel continuation made with Python, NumPy, and Pillow. No external images, masks, models, engines, or network resources are used.

first_frame.png is loaded as the photographic base for every frame, and frame 00 is retained exactly. Because the descending jar is transparent, its movement is rendered through physically relevant cues rather than an opaque cutout: a shallow moving mouth ellipse, faint traveling glass highlights, subtle refractive/dimming at the rim, and short-lived contact ripples as the rim enters the red liquid. The camera, wall, tabletop, bowl, and matches remain registered to the source.

The original flame is reduced with a spatially soft warm-color mask, which leaves the black match heads intact. A restrained blurred orange glow supplies early flicker. After enclosure, analytic translucent gray smoke wisps rise and diffuse. In the final seconds a softly feathered red tint and elliptical meniscus create a six-pixel liquid rise inside the jar. All overlays are low-alpha and blurred to match the source frame's soft photographic resolution.

artifact_io.save writes the packed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, PNG sequence, inspection sheet, and standalone HTML player. The generator additionally writes transition_check.png, then reloads all 20 PNGs and pixels.npz to assert exact equality with the generated RGB frames and exact equality of frame 00 with the supplied image.
