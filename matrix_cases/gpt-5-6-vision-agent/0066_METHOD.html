fill-glass-red-drink · 生成方法

The animation is generated locally from first_frame.png with deterministic NumPy and Pillow pixel operations. No external images, models, masks, engines, or network resources are used.

Frame 00 is copied byte-for-byte at the decoded RGB pixel level. For frames 01–19, the source frame remains the locked background and only a conservative mask inside the receiving glass plus the already-visible stream is changed. The liquid surface follows a smooth 15-pixel rise over five seconds. Newly filled pixels retain the source basket/glass texture but receive a red refraction transform whose channel coefficients were matched to the existing liquid in the bottom of the glass. As the input surface becomes submerged, its old reflective band is faded into real liquid texture sampled from lower in the same source glass. A curved meniscus, low-amplitude traveling ripple, narrow pulsing stream highlight, and occasional one-pixel impact glints supply real-time motion without moving any object or the camera.

artifact_io.save writes the frame PNGs, packed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, inspection contact sheet, and standalone HTML player. The generator separately builds transition_check.png. It then reloads every PNG and verifies exact equality to the corresponding pixels.npz plane, checks exact equality of frame 00 to the input, and confirms that changes stay localized.

Run with:

python generate.py
