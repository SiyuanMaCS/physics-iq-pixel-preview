OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Locked-frame measurements

  • Source is an RGB image at 256×144. The camera is fixed, with a broad wooden tabletop filling the lower image and a pale blue-gray wall above it.
  • The back edge of the table rises from about (0, 54) to (79, 28), then runs gently behind the objects. Fine diagonal wood grain is visible and must remain stationary.
  • A low black circular platform occupies the right edge, approximately x=191..255, y=42..86; its center is offscreen near (226, 62). The upper face is a dark ellipse and the front rim is nearly black.
  • The arm pivot/green clamp is near (207, 57). The initial tan wooden arm points left and slightly down to the contact zone near (157, 69).
  • Domino group nearest the platform has three alternating pieces: dark at about x=130..138, light at x=142..151, and dark/leaning at x=150..162; their table-contact region is around y=78..83.
  • The separated left group remains around x=62..107: light, dark, light, light in the visible perspective, standing approximately 21–24 pixels high. The open gap between groups is about 23 pixels edge-to-edge (x=107..129).
  • Lighting comes from upper left. Upright pieces have brighter left/top edges and soft shadows displaced down-right. The platform shadow/rim is also fixed.
  • Only the wooden arm and the three dominoes nearest the platform move. The camera, table grain, wall, platform, and separated left group remain locked.

20-frame event plan (4 fps, 5 seconds)

  • Frame 00: exact source frame, with arm just reaching the first dark domino.
  • Frames 01–03: arm rotates clockwise around (207,57) and pushes the rightmost dark domino leftward; that domino falls into the middle light domino.
  • Frames 04–06: the middle light domino tips left and transfers the impulse to the leftmost dark domino; the first dark domino lands on the table.
  • Frames 07–09: the leftmost dark domino falls toward the gap while the middle domino settles behind it. The rotating arm continues a short follow-through.
  • Frames 10–12: the last domino lands at the right edge of the gap. It cannot bridge the deliberately large separation, so the left group remains upright.
  • Frames 13–15: small damped settling of the three fallen dominoes and the arm; soft contact shadows stabilize.
  • Frames 16–19: final static scientific result: right-hand row down, gap visibly interrupts propagation, left-hand row still standing, arm at rest.

Continuity constraints

  • Frame 00 is copied byte-for-byte from first_frame.png.
  • No zoom, pan, exposure change, cuts, labels, people, or new objects.
  • Motions use eased angular curves with overlap between successive domino falls; the arm motion is monotonic clockwise and all settling is damped.

METHOD.md

Method

This continuation was produced entirely with local Python pixel operations. No external assets, models, engines, masks, or network resources were used.

Construction

  1. first_frame.png is loaded as RGB and copied directly into output frame 00 without resampling or modification.
  2. For later frames, a local OpenCV Telea inpaint removes only the initial right-hand dominoes, their shadows, the wooden arm, and the green arm clamp. The wall, table edge and grain outside that compact motion region, the black platform, and the separated left domino group remain sourced from the original image.
  3. The arm is a tapered, shaded wooden polygon rotating monotonically clockwise around the measured pivot (208,58). A small green clamp rotates with it. Its angle follows a smoothstep curve and then rests.
  4. Each moving domino is an analytically shaded rigid polygon rotating about its table-contact edge. The fall curves are staggered by three frames to create causal contact transfer from the already-leaning black piece to the white piece and then to the last black piece. A small one-frame damped landing correction is applied to each piece.
  5. Soft shadows are derived from the moving polygons, offset down-right to match the source lighting, and Gaussian-blurred. New geometry is drawn at 4× resolution and Lanczos-downsampled for antialiased edges before compositing over the 256×144 plate.
  6. The last fallen domino stops short of the deliberately wide gap. Consequently the left-hand row remains standing, which makes the gap's interruption of the domino effect visible.

Outputs and verification

  • artifact_io.save writes the 20 PNG frames, packed pixels.npz, inspection contact sheet, and standalone HTML player.
  • transition_check.png shows selected before/after transitions beside their pixel-difference heat maps.
  • The generator reloads every saved PNG and pixels.npz, reconstructs the uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, and asserts exact equality against the in-memory frames.
  • It also asserts that frame 00 is pixel-identical to first_frame.png, all 20 frames are RGB uint8, and the frame tensor shape is exactly 20×144×256×3.

Visual review

I visually reviewed the source crop, inspection.png, transition_check.png, and an enlarged crop of frames 00–04. The review confirmed a fixed camera/background, a readable right-to-left impulse sequence, clockwise arm follow-through, and a stable final state with the left group untouched.
