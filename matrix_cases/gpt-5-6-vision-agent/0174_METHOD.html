+def smoothstep(edge0, edge1, x):

+    """Return the static scene after the tool has let go.

+    Only the small top-center tool footprint is reconstructed. Wall values are

+    face is extended from its visible material immediately below the jaws.

+    # right neighbor retains the source's subtle raster/noise character.

+            clean[y, x] = np.clip(base + 0.18 * (residual - neighbor_mean), 0, 255)

+    # comes from pink/red chroma, preserving the antialiased edge naturally.
+    for y in range(17, 29):

OBSERVATION.md

Observation and event plan

Source-frame measurements

  • Canvas: 256 x 144 RGB; locked camera, tabletop horizon/meeting line runs diagonally from about (0, 48) to (77, 31) and then behind the objects toward (256, 52).
  • Background: pale gray wall with a soft central light and darker left/right falloff. Foreground is a warm wood tabletop with visible diagonal grain and soft contact shadows.
  • Gate structure: centered near x=111. A blue horizontal lintel spans approximately x=90..133 and y=56..69. Its left red/orange post extends to y=101; its right yellow post extends to y=110. The opening between the posts is about x=101..117.
  • Pink block: a slim vertical rectangular prism, approximately x=110..123 and y=17..63. Its bottom is visually occluded by the blue lintel, making it read as seated on top of the lintel.
  • Grabber: enters from above. Its black jaw/body occupies roughly x=103..117 at y=0..25, and a blue jaw/body occupies roughly x=116..129 at y=0..24. It overlaps the pink block's upper end.
  • Lighting and shadows are already baked into the photographed/rendered pixels. The only moving object will be the grabber; the placed pink block and colorful gate remain fixed after contact.

20-frame event plan (4 fps, 5 seconds)

The motion is deliberately completed early enough to provide a long, stable final-state read. Frame-to-frame displacement remains consistent with real-time 4 fps footage rather than a jump cut.

METHOD.md

Method

This sequence is a deterministic local pixel animation derived only from first_frame.png and prompt.txt.

  1. The exact source RGB array is used verbatim for frame 00.
  2. The locked background, tabletop, gate, pink block, lighting, wood grain, and shadows are retained from the source. Only a small region under the original grabber is reconstructed.
  3. The concealed wall is filled by row-wise interpolation between untouched wall pixels immediately to the left and right, with a small local residual to retain source texture.
  4. The formerly concealed top of the pink block is reconstructed from the visible block material just below the grabber. A small antialiased top plane makes the released block read as a rectangular prism rather than a cut-off stripe.
  5. Soft mattes for the black and blue grabber parts are derived analytically from their luminance, blue chroma, and difference from the reconstructed clean plate. The two parts move independently for a subtle jaw release, then together along a smooth upward, integer-pixel trajectory.
  6. Frames 10–19 hold on the completed placement. No camera motion, zoom, unrelated object, or synthetic environmental motion is introduced.
  7. artifact_io.save packs the RGB data as uint32 0xRRGGBB in (144, 256, 20) order, writes all PNG frames, the inspection sheet, and the standalone HTML player. generate.py then reloads every PNG and pixels.npz, verifies exact equality, and separately verifies that frame 00 equals first_frame.png.

transition_check.png shows selected actual next frames beside 4x-amplified absolute RGB differences. This makes the initial hold, jaw release, upward retraction, and final disappearance auditable without altering the deliverable animation.
