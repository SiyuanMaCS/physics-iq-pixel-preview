light-on-mug · 生成方法

The sequence is a deterministic local Python pixel animation made from first_frame.png; no external assets, models, masks, or engines were used.

Frame 00 is copied byte-for-byte at the RGB pixel level from the supplied image. For later frames, OpenCV inpainting removes only the original left-side yellow handle and the original left-side lobe of the wall shadow. The original wall, wood grain, platform, spotlight pool, mug body, and body-shadow pixels otherwise remain locked to the camera.

The mug handle is modeled as a vertical C-shaped loop attached at two radii to a cylindrical body. Its horizontal coordinates are projected with cosine over one 20-frame revolution, causing it to move left → rear/occluded → right → front/overlaid → left. A soft supersampled alpha raster provides antialiased edges; source-like warm yellow shading and very light deterministic grain are applied only to the newly painted loop. During the rear half of the revolution, a color-derived mask restores the original mug cylinder over the handle to produce occlusion. The large wall-shadow loop uses the same rotational phase with a larger projection, broad Gaussian softness, and wall-relative darkening.

A small low-contrast scuff rotates around the elliptical top of the black platform to disambiguate the clockwise platform motion. The strong illumination and platform silhouette remain fixed, as required for a stationary spotlight and camera.

artifact_io.save writes the RGB PNG sequence, packed tensor, contact sheet, and standalone player. generate.py then verifies that frame 00 equals the source, every saved PNG equals its in-memory RGB frame, and pixels.npz equals the frames packed as uint32 0xRRGGBB with shape 144×256×20.
