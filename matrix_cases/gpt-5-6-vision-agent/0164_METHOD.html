OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Source-frame measurements

  • Canvas: 256 x 144 RGB; static camera at tabletop height, with a centered, slightly downward view.
  • Background/table boundary: nearly horizontal at y=65. The upper background is blue-gray; the lower surface is warm, wood-grained brown.
  • Glass bowl: outer silhouette spans approximately x=73..211 and y=41..123. The top elliptical rim is centered near (143, 47), about 138 px wide. Its red-liquid region begins around y=91 and reaches the rounded bowl base near y=122.
  • Match bundle: centered around x=146. The broad pink/red bundle spans roughly x=134..158 and y=37..101; dark blue bindings are visible around y=40..47. A small orange-yellow flame occupies approximately x=155..165, y=0..17, with smoke and heat-darkened glass above it.
  • Descending cover jar: an inverted, transparent cylindrical jar is already partly in frame. Its side/refraction boundaries are approximately x=119 and x=176, with its closed end above the frame; its lower mouth/rim is the faint ellipse around y=94..100 near the red-liquid surface. (The strong horizontal edge at y=43..46 is the bowl rim.) The jar is translucent, showing the background, flame, and match bundle through gray/red refraction.
  • Lighting: soft diffuse room light from upper left. The bowl has dark olive-gray rim edges and bright thin specular streaks; the liquid is deep translucent red with a brighter upper surface and dark red lower band.
  • Camera and all supporting objects remain fixed. Only the inverted cover jar, flame/smoke, and enclosed liquid/refraction evolve.

20-frame plan (4 fps, 5 seconds)

  • Frame 00 (0.00 s): exact supplied first frame.
  • Frames 01-04 (0.25-1.00 s): the inverted jar descends smoothly about 2 px per frame. Its mouth ellipse and lower glass refraction travel downward; the flame still burns, flickering slightly and leaning left/right by 1-2 px.
  • Frames 05-08 (1.25-2.00 s): the jar mouth enters the red liquid around the match bundle and completes a total descent of about 11 px. Descent eases to a stop. The submerged mouth ellipse becomes darker and redder. Tiny concentric disturbances form on the red surface. Flame contracts and becomes more orange than yellow.
  • Frames 09-12 (2.25-3.00 s): jar is stationary and sealed by the liquid. The flame rapidly shrinks, briefly sputters, and goes out by frame 12. A thin gray smoke wisp replaces it.
  • Frames 13-16 (3.25-4.00 s): smoke curls upward inside the jar and fades. The enclosed liquid level begins rising around the match bundle, while the outer liquid surface stays nearly fixed; red refraction strengthens inside the cover.
  • Frames 17-19 (4.25-4.75 s): final settled state. The flame is absent, faint residual smoke remains, and the liquid inside the jar has risen several pixels. Surface ripples damp out. Camera, bowl, tabletop, and background stay locked.

Intended pixel method

Frame 00 will be copied byte-for-byte. Later frames will be generated locally from that image using masked affine/crop warps for the descending transparent jar, analytic antialiased ellipse/line masks for glass edges, liquid and ripples, and soft procedural alpha masks for flame/glow/smoke. Unchanged background and bowl pixels will remain sourced from the first frame.

METHOD.md

Method

I generated the continuation entirely with local Python pixel operations using Pillow and NumPy; no external images, masks, models, engines, or network resources were used.

  • first_frame.png is loaded as the fixed photographic base. Frame 00 is passed through unchanged and is checked for both pixel and PNG-byte identity.
  • The inverted jar's short final descent is represented by a smoothly eased 11-pixel movement of its submerged elliptical mouth, continuation of the faint vertical glass edges, and very low-alpha glass refraction. The old faint mouth trace is locally softened with a narrow blurred annular mask; the match bundle is explicitly excluded from that mask.
  • The existing flame is retained while it burns. Local supersampled translucent flame/core shapes add 1-2 pixel flicker. For extinction, a small local background estimate interpolated from adjacent glass pixels is blended over the original flame during frames 09-12.
  • Soft supersampled gray curves form the smoke wisp after extinction. Its opacity decays over the remaining frames.
  • Analytic ellipses/arcs create the submerged glass rim, concentric surface disturbances, and the rising enclosed red-liquid surface. The liquid rise begins only after the flame has gone out.
  • All thin synthetic elements are drawn at 4x resolution and downsampled with Lanczos filtering. The camera, bowl, match bundle, table, and surrounding background remain sourced from the locked first frame.
  • artifact_io.save writes the required uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, PNG frames, inspection sheet, and standalone player. The generator also writes a transition sheet with 8x absolute differences and reloads every output PNG to verify exact equality with pixels.npz.
