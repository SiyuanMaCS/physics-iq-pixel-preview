OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Measured scene

  • Source is a 256 x 144 RGB image; camera and illumination are fixed.
  • The light gray rear wall occupies most of the upper field. A warm wood tabletop begins at the diagonal edge from approximately (0, 141) through (119, 101) to (150, 91), then fills the lower part of the image.
  • A vertical mirror occupies approximately x=148..233. Its left edge is a dark gray vertical strip near x=145..150, and a near-black right edge/frame begins around x=234.
  • The real orange cord is centered around x=121, descending from the top edge to about y=43. Its dark connector, magnet/hook, and short black suspension occupy roughly x=117..128, y=41..86.
  • The real orange ball occupies roughly x=115..134, y=84..103, with center near (124.5, 93.5) and radius about 9 pixels. It is just above the table.
  • The mirror image of the cord is centered near x=222 at the top and leans leftward to x=219 near y=43. The reflected connector and suspension occupy about x=214..224, y=40..69.
  • The reflected orange ball occupies roughly x=210..225, y=68..84, centered near (217.5, 76.5). It appears smaller and higher because it is seen in the angled mirror.
  • The ball is a mottled orange sphere rather than a regulation yellow-green tennis ball; the animation must preserve its source texture, highlights, dark upper contact area, and soft shadowing.
  • No object other than the hanging assembly and its reflection should move. There is no camera motion, cut, zoom, or change in exposure.

Motion design

The apparatus is slowly lowered by paying out more orange cord from above. The rigid connector/magnet, short black hanger, and orange ball move together. The real assembly follows an almost vertical path, drifting only about 1 pixel right over the shot. Its mirror image follows the corresponding slightly down-left perspective path. Motion uses smoothstep easing so it starts and finishes gently.

The final real-ball center is planned near (125.5, 119.5), 26 pixels below its initial center, while the final reflected center is near (215.5, 98.5), 22 pixels below and 2 pixels left of its initial center. The dark connector/hanger follows the same displacement. Newly exposed cord is rendered continuously between the fixed top entry point and the descending connector. Old object locations are restored from nearby wall/mirror/table texture, with contact-region shading added as the real ball nears the tabletop.

METHOD.md

Method

The animation is a deterministic local pixel compositing process implemented in generate.py.

  1. The supplied RGB frame is loaded unchanged. Frame 00 (and the intentional one-frame opening hold) are direct copies; frame 00 is checked byte-for-byte against first_frame.png.
  2. A clean background plate is made with OpenCV Telea inpainting over hand-measured, narrow masks for the cords and reflected rig. To prevent the nearby dark mirror edge from bleeding into the real ball's vacated area, that narrow region is replaced by an object-free donor patch offset (-40,+13.33) pixels along the tabletop's measured -1/3 slope. OpenCV gradient-domain (Poisson) cloning blends the patch into the original illumination while preserving the diagonal wall/table boundary and genuine texture. The repaired plate is then restricted to the measured removal matte, leaving all untouched background pixels identical to the source.
  3. The photographed ball, magnet, clip, and ring pixels are retained as two RGBA-like layers. Their soft alpha mattes combine measured elliptical ball silhouettes with source-versus-clean-plate differences for the thin mechanical pieces. This preserves the original mottling, highlights, antialiasing, and reflected appearance.
  4. The real layer is translated from (0, 0) to (1, 26) pixels; the reflected layer follows a perspective-consistent path from (0, 0) to (-2, 22) pixels. The integer path is deliberately eased and includes short start/end holds.
  5. Thin orange cords are antialiased at 4x resolution, anchored at their original top-edge positions, and extended to meet the descending connectors. A subtle blurred tabletop shadow strengthens near the end.
  6. artifact_io.save writes the RGB PNGs, packed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, inspection sheet, and standalone HTML player. transition_check.png pairs selected output frames with amplified adjacent-frame deltas. The script reloads all 20 PNGs and pixels.npz and asserts exact equality with the generated RGB array.

No network resources, external models, external engines, masks, or assets were used.
