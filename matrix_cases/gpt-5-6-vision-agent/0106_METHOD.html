marble-run-y · 生成方法

I used a local, deterministic Python pixel pipeline. The supplied photograph is the clean visual source for every frame. Frame 00 is copied byte-for-byte at the RGB pixel level. For later frames, only the original yellow marble near (147,34) is removed with a tightly masked OpenCV Telea inpaint; the white vertical specular reflection near (133,64) is intentionally preserved as part of the static scene.

The new marble is drawn as a small supersampled RGBA composite. Layered amber and yellow ellipses create a translucent glass body, a darker lower rim suggests refraction, a near-white upper-left glint matches the scene lighting, and a blurred down-right ellipse supplies a small shadow. Fast steps receive a short, low-opacity exposure trail aligned with the measured velocity. The trajectory is manually keyed to the photographed ramp surfaces and alternates ramp-aligned motion with short falls, ending in a damped stop inside the purple catch.

No external images, models, engines, masks, or network resources were used. generate.py saves the frames through artifact_io.py, creates a transition review sheet, then independently reconstructs and checks the required uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor against every PNG and confirms that frame 00 exactly equals first_frame.png.
