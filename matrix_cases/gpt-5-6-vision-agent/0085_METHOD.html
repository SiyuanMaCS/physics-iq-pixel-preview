liquid-on-duck · 生成方法

I generated the animation entirely with local Python pixel operations using Pillow and NumPy. The source photograph remains the locked background in every frame, and frame 00 is copied exactly from first_frame.png.

The bright-red liquid is assembled from supersampled analytic layers at 4x resolution and downsampled with Lanczos filtering for natural antialiasing. The layers comprise:

  1. a subtly oscillating shaded stream from the existing tap to the duck's crown;
  2. frame-varying impact lobes and tiny ballistic droplets;
  3. progressive masks clipped to approximate head and body silhouettes, filled with translucent crimson and separate dark-edge/specular strokes;
  4. narrow runoff connecting the body to an irregular perspective puddle;
  5. a growing tray puddle with an organic outline, dark wet contact edge, red interior, and pale reflections. The puddle mask subtracts the duck silhouette so it reads as lying behind and around the duck rather than painted over it.

No generative model, external engine, network resource, optical-flow source, or external reference was used. artifact_io.py writes the pixel tensor, individual PNGs, contact sheet, and standalone player. The generator then verifies every saved PNG against its corresponding uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor plane and verifies frame 00 against the source pixels.
