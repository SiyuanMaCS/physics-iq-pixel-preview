+def smooth_inpaint(source):
+    """Replace the original potato with interpolated clear-glass interior."""
+    out = source.copy().astype(np.float32)
+    x0, x1 = 107, 133
+    y0, y1 = 5, 63
+    # Horizontal interpolation follows the illumination at every scanline.
+    left = source[y0:y1, x0 - 2].astype(np.float32)
+    right = source[y0:y1, x1 + 2].astype(np.float32)
+    for x in range(x0, x1 + 1):
+        u = (x - x0 + 1) / (x1 - x0 + 2)
+        # Smoothstep suppresses a visibly linear ramp.
+        s = u * u * (3.0 - 2.0 * u)
+        out[y0:y1, x] = left * (1.0 - s) + right * s
+    # A gentle 2-D blur only inside the repaired patch removes scanline seams.
+    patch = Image.fromarray(np.uint8(np.clip(out[y0:y1, x0:x1 + 1], 0, 255)))

+    out[y0:y1, x0:x1 + 1] = np.asarray(patch)
+    return np.uint8(np.clip(out, 0, 255))

OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Measured scene

  • Source is RGB, 256 x 144. The camera is static and level.
  • Pale gray-green wall occupies roughly y=0..111; warm wooden table begins on a diagonal from about (0,122) to (70,101), then runs behind the glass near y=108.
  • A tall, narrow clear glass is centered at x=119. Its visible outer silhouette is about x=94..144 and y=0..143. The upper glass is empty/transparent.
  • Opaque cyan-blue liquid fills the lower glass from about y=79 to y=130. A vivid green horizontal water-level band spans about x=93..143 at y=75..81.
  • The brown potato is an upright capsule-like shape centered near x=120, with visible extent about x=109..130 and y=7..59. It has a bright vertical highlight at x=114..119 and darker brown edges.
  • The holding tool is visually subdued against the transparent upper glass; the prompt establishes that it releases the potato. The first frame is treated as the exact held state.
  • Lighting is soft and comes primarily from camera-left. Cast effects are subtle; there is no camera or scene motion.

Animation principles

  • Frame 00 is copied byte-for-byte from first_frame.png.
  • The wall, table, glass outline, green marker, and all pixels outside the localized action region remain fixed.
  • The original potato is removed from later frames by a deterministic local reconstruction of the transparent glass interior from neighboring columns, retaining the glass's vertical highlights.
  • A cutout derived from the actual potato pixels is translated during the fall. Above the water it remains sharp; at/under the surface it is clipped, blue-tinted, mildly compressed/warped by refraction, and increasingly obscured by the liquid.
  • Entry creates a small crown/dimple and a few droplets, followed by damped surface ripples. Effects stay within the glass.

METHOD.md

Method

The sequence is generated entirely with deterministic local Python pixel operations using NumPy and Pillow; no external images, masks, models, engines, or network resources are used.

  1. first_frame.png is loaded as the sole visual source and inserted unchanged as frame 00.
  2. The original potato is isolated with a hand-measured, softly feathered silhouette. Its RGB texture is copied directly from the source frame.
  3. For frames after release, the potato's former location is reconstructed scanline-by-scanline from nearby clear-glass pixels, using smooth horizontal interpolation and a small local blur. The camera, wall, table, glass, liquid, and marker outside this repair/action area remain fixed.
  4. The actual potato cutout is translated along a manually timed gravity-and-drag path. The part above the liquid is composited normally. The part below the surface is tinted toward the measured liquid color, lowered in contrast/opacity, and given a slight entry tilt to suggest refraction and water drag.
  5. Small analytic arcs, polygons, and ellipses create the contact dimple, restrained splash crown, droplets, and damped ripples. These are clipped by placement to the glass interior. The original green exterior marker and glass side edges are restored in front to preserve correct occlusion.
  6. artifact_io.save writes the uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, 20 RGB PNG frames, the inspection contact sheet, and the standalone HTML player. A separate diagnostic sheet shows amplified consecutive-frame differences.
  7. The generator verifies that frame 00 exactly equals the input, every PNG exactly equals its generated RGB frame, and pixels.npz exactly equals those frames after 0xRRGGBB packing and (H,W,T) transposition.
