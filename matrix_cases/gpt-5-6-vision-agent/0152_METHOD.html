OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Locked-frame measurements

  • Source frame: 256 x 144 RGB, camera fixed, tabletop horizon/back edge near y=20.
  • Light wood table fills almost the entire frame. Its grain runs horizontally and the illumination is brighter near the center than at the corners.
  • Left black tube occupies approximately x=0..23, y=41..70. Its circular opening is centered near (10, 56), points right, and must remain stationary.
  • Brown tennis ball is already outside the tube in frame 00, centered near (109, 61). Its visible body is roughly 19 x 19 pixels, with a dark underside/contact shadow and some horizontal motion blur.
  • Olive fabric-covered box occupies the right edge. Its nearest slanted impact boundary runs approximately from (224, 0) through (210, 68) to (237, 101); the lower front corner is near (238, 101). The box is large relative to the ball.
  • Expected first contact occurs when the ball center reaches about x=200..201 at y=61, because the ball radius is about 10 pixels and the box face at that height is near x=211.
  • No camera movement, cuts, added labels, hands, or extra objects.

METHOD.md

Method

The source photograph is kept as the locked camera plate. Frame 00 is copied directly and exactly from first_frame.png.

For frames 01–19, a small soft mask covering only the initial ball and its contact shadow is reconstructed scan line by scan line. Each affected table row is fit from unoccluded pixels immediately to the left and right. This follows the real table's horizontal grain and brightness gradient while excluding the ball's broad motion/contact shadow.

The ball itself is not a generic painted circle. Its photographic color, highlight, dark underside, and surface variation are extracted as a premultiplied difference layer between the source and cleared plate. That layer is transformed along a hand-authored, physically coherent center trajectory. Rotation follows traveled arc length; a very short direction-aware exposure trail reflects the visible motion blur in the source. A separate soft, grounded contact shadow follows the ball. At impact the ball is briefly compressed horizontally and expanded vertically.

The heavy olive fabric box receives only a subtle 1–3 pixel rightward jolt over five frames. Its visible polygon is translated with antialiasing while the narrow exposed edge is extended from adjacent tabletop pixels. It settles back to the source pose as the ball rebounds and loses speed through rolling resistance.

Outputs are generated at 256×144 RGB, 20 frames, interpreted at 4 fps. generate.py calls the provided artifact_io.save, creates the transition diagnostic, then independently verifies every PNG against the packed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor and asserts exact equality between frame 00 and the source.
