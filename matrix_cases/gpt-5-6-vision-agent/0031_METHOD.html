balls-collide · 生成方法

The continuation is generated entirely with local Python pixel operations from first_frame.png.

  1. I measured the two ball centroids and used the line joining them as the shared collision axis.
  2. Frame 00 is copied directly from the source array without recompression or modification.
  3. I extracted 25 x 25 RGB cutouts of the photographed balls with softly antialiased circular alpha masks. This retains the source lighting, color variation, and optical softness.
  4. I created a fixed clean tabletop plate by masking each original ball plus its cast shadow. For each covered pixel, the script finds unobstructed samples on both sides along the measured diagonal wood-grain direction and linearly interpolates between them. This continues the actual photographed grain through the two small footprints; all other background and pipe pixels remain from the source.
  5. Both centers advance by the same Euclidean distance each frame in opposite directions. At contact they undergo a symmetric equal-mass elastic rebound, so their velocity magnitudes remain equal and unchanged. The contact frame has a subtle axis-aligned squash/cross-axis expansion.
  6. Each moving ball receives a soft analytic lower-left tabletop shadow matching the measured source-light direction. The original RGB ball cutout is then alpha-composited above it.
  7. artifact_io.save writes the uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, PNG sequence, inspection sheet, and standalone HTML player. The script additionally creates transition_check.png and re-opens every output PNG to verify exact agreement with pixels.npz.

No external assets, network resources, models, masks, other cases, or rendering engines were used.
