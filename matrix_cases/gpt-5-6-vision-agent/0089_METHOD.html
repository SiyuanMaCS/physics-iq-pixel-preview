OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Measured composition

  • Source frame is 256 x 144 and uses a fixed, eye-level close shot.
  • A dark charcoal baking tray occupies approximately x=22..247, y=57..143. Its inner floor is x=48..224, y=76..132; its raised rim and front lip remain static.
  • A clear cylindrical receiving glass is centered beneath the tap at approximately x=134..173, y=27..92. It already contains bright red liquid; the glass edge, base, and specular highlights must remain legible through the animation.
  • The red beverage dispenser is centered and cropped by the top edge. Its blue tap/valve occupies about x=146..161, y=0..18. The outlet is centered near x=154, y=17, directly above the glass opening near y=29.
  • A woven multicolor basket/backdrop lies behind the dispenser at x=89..207, y=7..61. A wooden table and pale wall fill the rest of the locked background.
  • Illumination is soft and frontal-left. The red liquid has a saturated coral-red body, darker red sides/base, and narrow pale highlights. Any added liquid should inherit this lighting rather than appearing as a flat graphic.
  • Only the dispenser action changes. The camera, tray, table, wall, basket, dispenser body, valve body, and glass geometry stay fixed.

20-frame event plan (4 fps, 5 seconds)

  • Frame 00: Exact untouched source frame; a very faint red onset thread is already visible directly below the outlet.
  • Frame 01: A tiny dark-red bead gathers under the blue outlet.
  • Frame 02: The bead elongates downward toward the glass.
  • Frame 03: A thin broken red thread begins, with a small gap and first contact glint at the glass opening.
  • Frame 04: The thread becomes a continuous narrow stream.
  • Frame 05: Continuous stream strengthens; a compact pale-red contact splash appears inside the rim.
  • Frame 06: Stream reaches its early maximum width; subtle ripples brighten the liquid surface.
  • Frame 07: Steady pour; surface contact shifts slightly right.
  • Frame 08: Steady pour with a gently wavering edge and a small highlight fleck.
  • Frame 09: Steady pour; the visible liquid level/highlight in the glass rises subtly.
  • Frame 10: Strongest stable stream and clearest contact crown.
  • Frame 11: Steady flow continues; contact crown relaxes into concentric surface glints.
  • Frame 12: Stream narrows slightly while remaining continuous.
  • Frame 13: Flow pulses narrower, preserving a realistic glossy core.
  • Frame 14: Stream begins shutting off; lower section pinches.
  • Frame 15: Stream breaks into a long descending segment and an upper bead.
  • Frame 16: Only two falling droplets remain between outlet and glass.
  • Frame 17: One final small droplet falls; surface ripple fades.
  • Frame 18: A tiny residual bead clings to the outlet; no connecting stream.
  • Frame 19: Pour has stopped; the glass retains a subtly higher liquid surface and settled highlight.

The motion is deliberately small because the outlet-to-glass gap is only about 11 pixels in this close shot. Temporal changes will be subpixel-antialiased and restricted to a narrow mask around the outlet, opening, and glass interior so the locked-off scene remains stable.

METHOD.md

Method

The animation is a deterministic local pixel composite made only from first_frame.png; no external images, models, engines, masks, or network resources are used.

Frame 00 is copied byte-for-byte from the RGB source. For frames 01–19, the original locked-off photograph remains the background. The liquid is synthesized analytically at 4× resolution with a tapered, slightly wavering silhouette, a darker red boundary, a saturated body, and a narrow warm specular core. Small antialiased arcs at the glass opening represent contact ripples, while a faint translucent caustic immediately below the rim suggests refraction through the disturbed liquid. The shutdown uses separated ellipsoidal droplets.

The source already contains a faint red thread directly below the outlet. During the final shutdown frames, only that 7×13-pixel region is gradually replaced with a local horizontal interpolation from its immediate neighbors. This is the sole inpaint-like operation. It lets the stream visibly stop while preserving the tap, glass, tray, and all background geometry. All other changing pixels are confined to the outlet/contact region.

artifact_io.save packs the RGB frames into the required uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, emits the PNG sequence, inspection sheet, and standalone HTML player. The generator then reloads the tensor and every PNG, reconstructs RGB, asserts exact equality to the in-memory frames, asserts exact frame-00 equality to the input, and checks that all motion remains inside the intended small central bounds. transition_check.png shows selected adjacent-frame crops and amplified differences for temporal inspection.
