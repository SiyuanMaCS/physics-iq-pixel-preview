OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Frame measurements

  • Canvas: 256 x 144 RGB, landscape, fixed camera.
  • Background wall: upper half, pale gray; wood tabletop begins around y=61 at the left and slopes visually behind the tray.
  • Woven basket: approximately x=63..165, y=8..83. The dispenser's ribbed red reservoir occupies x=108..172 and enters from above the frame.
  • Blue tap/nozzle assembly: approximately x=164..196, y=0..22. Its outlet points downward over the glass near x=191. No separate movable handle is visible in-frame.
  • Receiving glass: approximately x=162..210, y=18..103. Its red contents/body occupy most of that silhouette; glass base is an ellipse around x=166..207, y=94..103.
  • Dark baking tray: near x=77..255, y=50..143, with a raised rim. It remains completely static.
  • Intended flow path: outlet near (191, 17) to impact point near (191, 26), mostly against the already-red mouth/neck of the glass. A bright edge, darker red core, and tiny mouth ripple are needed to distinguish the stream.
  • Lighting: soft upper-left illumination. The glass has strong white highlights near x=183..188, y=30..36 and x=165..169, y=57..91; shadows fall subtly rightward/downward.

20-frame event plan (4 fps, 5 seconds)

  • 00: Exact source frame; tap closed and no added motion.
  • 01: First red bead forms under the stationary outlet.
  • 02: Bead elongates toward the glass.
  • 03: Thin stream connects outlet and liquid surface; small bright impact glint appears.
  • 04: Stream thickens to steady flow; first shallow ripple across the mouth.
  • 05: Steady pour; ripple expands and a tiny red splash rises left of impact.
  • 06: Steady pour; splash subsides, highlight moves across the ripple.
  • 07: Steady pour; a second, smaller ripple forms.
  • 08: Flow remains continuous with slight natural side-to-side narrowing.
  • 09: Steady pour; liquid boundary in the neck rises subtly.
  • 10: Stream briefly thickens; compact impact crown/glint.
  • 11: Stream returns to normal; crown collapses into ripples.
  • 12: Continuous pour; traveling highlight shifts right.
  • 13: Continuous pour; surface is calmer but visibly disturbed at impact.
  • 14: Flow starts shutting off; stream narrows.
  • 15: Stream becomes a thin thread; final small ripple.
  • 16: Thread breaks into a falling droplet.
  • 17: Last droplet reaches the mouth; only residual ripple remains.
  • 18: Tap is closed; ripple fades and raised liquid boundary settles.
  • 19: Fully settled end state, with no stream and the glass fractionally fuller than at frame 00.

Only the tap/flow and the liquid inside the glass change. Camera, tray, table, basket, reservoir, background, and glass footprint remain locked.

METHOD.md

Method

The animation is a deterministic local pixel composite made with Python, NumPy, and Pillow. No external models, engines, images, masks, or references are used.

first_frame.png is loaded as the immutable scene plate. Frame 00 is copied from it exactly. For frames 01–19, generate.py draws only a small transparent RGBA overlay around the dispenser outlet and glass mouth. The liquid motion is analytic: a time-keyed flow-strength curve controls a gravity-narrowed red polygon, a moving specular bead, shaded stream edges, impact indentation, elliptical ripple arcs, and two brief splash events. The layer is drawn at 4x resolution and Lanczos-downsampled for antialiased edges, then alpha-composited over the untouched source plate. The final three frames taper the stream to a droplet and decay the surface ripple.

The static background, table, basket, tray, reservoir, and glass footprint are never synthesized or warped. A very low-alpha red ellipse confined to the glass's top shoulder suggests the small accumulated increase in fullness.

artifact_io.save packs RGB into the required uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor with shape (144, 256, 20), writes the 20 PNG frames, creates inspection.png, and embeds lossless delta packets in the standalone index.html player. The generator additionally creates transition_check.png, a magnified 5x4 chronology of the outlet/mouth region. It then reloads every PNG and pixels.npz, recomputes all packed values, and asserts exact equality; it separately asserts that frame 00 equals the source image pixel-for-pixel.
