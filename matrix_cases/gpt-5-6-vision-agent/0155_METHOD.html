OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and animation plan

Locked scene measurements

  • Source frame: 256 x 144 RGB, fixed camera, eye-level/downward view of a wooden tabletop against a pale gray wall.
  • Table occupies the lower part of the image. Its rear edge runs approximately from (0,112) to (145,58) and onward to the right edge near (256,65). Wood grain is predominantly diagonal/near-horizontal.
  • The physical 30 lb black kettlebell occupies approximately x=103..142, y=21..87; its rounded body is centered near (123,65) and its handle near (123,37).
  • A darker, slightly smaller reflected kettlebell occupies approximately x=159..200, y=18..69, consistent with the mirror/view geometry. Both bell and reflection remain fully static.
  • The yellow-green tennis ball begins in the near foreground, clipped by the bottom border, centered at roughly (150,143) with an apparent radius of about 18 px. Its visible top begins around y=124. It has a bright upper-left highlight, darker lower/right shading, soft edge, and a curved pale seam.
  • Intended collision point is the lower-right/front side of the physical kettlebell. In image coordinates the final incoming ball center is about (134,93) with a perspective-reduced radius around 7 px, placing its upper-left edge against the bell near (129,86).
  • Illumination comes mainly from upper left. The moving ball therefore needs a soft contact shadow displaced slightly right/down. No camera motion, exposure change, or motion elsewhere is permitted.

Twenty-frame event plan (4 fps, 5 seconds)

  • Frame 00 (0.00 s): exact supplied first frame; ball is partly outside the bottom edge.
  • Frames 01-04 (0.25-1.00 s): ball rolls slowly onto the visible tabletop, following a shallow leftward/upward perspective path. Its apparent radius decreases from about 17 to 14 px. Seam orientation rotates to establish rolling.
  • Frames 05-09 (1.25-2.25 s): steady approach toward the kettlebell, center travels through roughly (145,123) to (140,108), apparent radius decreases to about 10 px. Contact shadow tightens as the ball recedes.
  • Frames 10-14 (2.50-3.50 s): ball continues toward the bell's lower-right side, slowing slightly, center reaches roughly (136,97), radius about 8 px.
  • Frames 15-16 (3.75-4.00 s): final short approach; ball reaches first contact near (133,92). A one-pixel squash along the approach direction conveys impact while the heavy kettlebell does not move.
  • Frames 17-19 (4.25-4.75 s): ball makes a very small physically plausible recoil away from the immovable 30 lb bell and settles near (136,95). Kettlebell, reflection, mirror, wall, and table remain locked.

Generation constraints

  • Preserve every source pixel outside the local ball/removal/shadow regions.
  • Reconstruct the small foreground patch uncovered by the source ball using nearby tabletop color and grain only.
  • Render the ball locally with analytic shading, fuzzy antialiased silhouette, curved rotating seam, and soft contact shadow.
  • Frame 00 is copied verbatim from first_frame.png.

METHOD.md

Method

The animation is a deterministic local Python pixel composite; no external model, engine, asset, mask, or reference was used.

  1. first_frame.png is loaded as RGB and copied byte-for-byte to frame 00.
  2. For frames 01-19, the starting ball's small clipped foreground region is removed with OpenCV TELEA inpainting from neighboring tabletop pixels. Two very low-amplitude analytic bands continue the local diagonal wood variation through this repaired patch.
  3. The moving ball follows an explicit measured 2D perspective path from (150,144) to contact near (133,93). Apparent radius shrinks from 19 px to about 8 px as it rolls away from the camera. The 30 lb kettlebell is treated as immovable: frame 16 is slightly squashed at contact, followed by a tiny two-frame recoil and rest.
  4. Each ball is synthesized locally from an analytic sphere normal field, upper-left diffuse/specular illumination, deterministic felt variation, an optically softened antialiased silhouette, and a phase-advanced subdued curved seam. A soft elliptical contact shadow is composited underneath.
  5. Every pixel outside the starting-ball repair and current moving-ball/shadow patches comes from the locked source image, so the camera, wall, mirror, kettlebell, and reflection remain stable.
  6. artifact_io.save writes the 20 PNGs, the packed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, inspection sheet, and standalone player. The generator additionally creates transition_check.png and verifies all saved PNG pixels against pixels.npz.
