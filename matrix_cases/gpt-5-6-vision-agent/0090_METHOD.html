OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and animation plan

Source measurements

  • Source is a 256×144 RGB image; camera is fixed at a slightly elevated three-quarter view.
  • The dark baking tray occupies approximately x=0..235, y=48..143; its interior floor is x=21..221, y=67..132. The wood tabletop fills the remaining lower/background area.
  • The receiving glass is centered near x=132, with an outer silhouette about x=110..152, y=24..97. Its base/foot is near y=94; its elliptical mouth is near y=27..34. It already contains opaque bright red liquid, with useful translucent rim/highlight and darker side shading.
  • The dispenser/tap assembly enters from the top. A blue-gloved hand is around x=119..144, y=0..17; a red horizontal control/reservoir region is around x=143..191, y=0..21; the outlet is aligned over the glass near x=128..132. A faint red neck already connects the outlet area to the nearly full glass, so the continuation can strengthen this into a visibly flowing stream without changing the apparatus.
  • A woven cylindrical dispenser basket/backing occupies roughly x=103..226, y=0..58. It and all background objects remain static.
  • The visible outlet-to-glass gap is short, about 7–10 pixels, so the pour should read through a narrow bright stream, changing specular highlights, a small impact dimple/splash, and a subtle rising liquid surface rather than through a long arc.
  • Illumination comes from upper-left: the liquid/glass has pale coral highlights on its left/center and deep red shading on its right/lower edge.

METHOD.md

Method

I used deterministic local Python/Pillow and NumPy pixel compositing only. The supplied first_frame.png is loaded as RGB and copied exactly into frame 00. Every later frame starts from the same untouched source, keeping the camera, tray, tabletop, dispenser, basket, and glove completely locked.

The animated region is deliberately small because the fixed outlet is only several pixels above an already nearly-full glass. Supersampled analytic masks create a short red stream with darker refractive edges, a warm inner body, a traveling specular core, and a rounded meniscus. Low-opacity ellipse and curve masks add an impact dimple, short surface ripples, tiny splash lips, and a subtle transmitted-light shimmer inside the liquid. The flow ramps up, varies slightly, pinches off, and ends with one falling droplet before the surface settles. Masks are rendered at 4× resolution and downsampled for antialiased edges, then alpha-composited over the source; no external model, engine, source image, mask, or network resource is used.

artifact_io.save writes the 20 PNG frames, packed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, inspection contact sheet, and standalone HTML player. generate.py additionally creates transition_check.png from amplified consecutive-frame differences and verifies every decoded PNG pixel against pixels.npz, including an exact array equality check between frame 00 and first_frame.png.
