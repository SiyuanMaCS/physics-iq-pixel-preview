balls-collide · 生成方法

The animation is generated entirely from first_frame.png with local Python pixel operations.

  1. The fixed tabletop plate is reconstructed by masking the two original balls plus their soft shadows and feathering in same-scanline texture patches from unobstructed regions between the balls. This preserves the tabletop's horizontal grain and plank seams. The pipes and every pixel outside the two feathered repair regions remain fixed.
  2. Each ball is extracted from the source using a color-range matte in HSV space, keeping the original photographed color, highlight, and texture. The matte is morphologically closed and softly feathered for antialiased edges.
  3. An analytic equal-and-opposite trajectory moves the balls horizontally at identical incoming speed. They contact on frame 06 near the center, receive a subtle one-frame contact squash, then rebound with identical damped speeds and gradual rolling-resistance deceleration.
  4. Soft blurred ellipses recreate moving contact shadows. A restrained roughly one-pixel exposure trail conveys motion at 4 fps while preserving opaque ball bodies.
  5. Frame 00 is copied directly from the supplied image and never reconstructed. artifact_io.save writes the PNG sequence, packed uint32 tensor, inspection sheet, and standalone player. The generator then reloads every PNG and pixels.npz and checks exact equality against the in-memory RGB frames.
