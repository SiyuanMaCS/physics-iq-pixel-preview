marble-run-x · 生成方法

I used the supplied photograph as an immutable background and added only a locally rendered moving marble and its shadow. No external images, models, engines, masks, or references were used.

Motion construction

  • I measured the ramp endpoints directly in first_frame.png and authored a 20-frame, piecewise path through them.
  • On-ramp sections accelerate in the downhill direction. The marble travels from right to left on the two blue ramps, is redirected at the purple piece, then travels left to right on the green ramp and lands on the yellow ramp.
  • The three inter-ramp transfers use short ballistic-looking arcs that preserve the incoming horizontal momentum while adding downward displacement.
  • A cumulative path-length value drives the phase of a small internal inclusion, making the sphere visibly rotate rather than translate as a static disk.

Pixel rendering

  • The marble is rendered analytically at 4x resolution as a 9-pixel-wide amber/yellow glass sphere.
  • Its shading combines an amber rim, yellow transmitted-light center, upper-left white specular reflection, lower-right caustic, and rotating internal inclusion.
  • A small blurred elliptical shadow is tight during ramp contact and lighter/broader during airborne frames.
  • Only the RGBA marble layer is downsampled. It is then alpha-composited onto the original 256 x 144 photograph, preserving all pixels outside the local marble/shadow footprint.
  • Frame 00 is copied from the source with no compositing at all.

Output and verification

artifact_io.save writes the 20 PNGs, packed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, inspection sheet, and standalone HTML player. The generator also creates transition_check.png, showing every adjacent transition with a box around changed pixels. It then reloads every PNG and verifies it exactly against the corresponding tensor plane, and verifies frame 00 exactly against first_frame.png.

I visually reviewed both inspection.png and transition_check.png after the final render. The review confirmed a fixed background, localized per-frame changes, a readable yellow glass marble, downhill ramp motion, continuous staggered transfers, and final contact on the yellow ramp.
