OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and animation plan

Source-frame measurements

  • Canvas: 256 x 144 RGB, locked-off camera, level tabletop view.
  • Background: pale gray wall above a warm wooden tabletop; the wall/table boundary runs diagonally from about (0, 86) to (70, 53) on the left and symmetrically toward (256, 84) on the right.
  • Dispenser: ribbed clear-glass reservoir spans approximately x=81..164, y=0..67. Its grapefruit juice occupies roughly x=87..159, y=29..61, with a slightly curved upper surface.
  • Stand/basket: woven circular support spans about x=66..190, y=40..116.
  • Tap: dark blue-black body and handle occupy about x=116..136, y=22..66; the outlet is centered near x=127, y=61.
  • Falling liquid: a narrow, warm brown-orange stream is already visible below the outlet, centered around x=126..128, down to the glass surface.
  • Receiving glass: outer silhouette is approximately x=108..150, y=61..141. The interior narrows gently from about x=113..145 near the top to x=113..145 near the base. Its current liquid surface is near y=94, and the visible liquid continues to about y=136.
  • The glass contains a diluted peach/orange mixture (the stated initial water plus incoming grapefruit juice). Strong vertical highlights lie near x=112..115 and x=143..146; the rim is an ellipse around y=63..70.
  • Lighting is diffuse from upper-left. All geometry and camera framing remain stationary; only liquid surfaces, the stream, mixing currents, small bubbles, and a tiny reservoir-level change should move.

20-frame event plan (4 fps, 5 seconds)

  • Frame 00: preserve the source image byte-for-byte in pixel content; the stream is already active and the glass is filled to about y=94.
  • Frames 01-03: continuous thin stream; the receiving surface rises by roughly 1 pixel, with a small central impact dimple and pale mixing cloud beneath it.
  • Frames 04-06: glass level rises to about y=89; diluted peach liquid becomes marginally more saturated near the impact column; one or two tiny bubbles drift upward.
  • Frames 07-09: level reaches about y=84; stream width/brightness pulses subtly while remaining unbroken; surface ripples alternate around the center.
  • Frames 10-12: level reaches about y=79; soft orange currents spread outward and downward while old water and juice continue mixing.
  • Frames 13-15: level reaches about y=75; highlight streaks remain locked to the glass, and a few bubbles appear at changing heights.
  • Frames 16-18: level reaches about y=72; surface ripples settle slightly between stream impacts; dispenser reservoir is lower by only about 1-2 pixels.
  • Frame 19: glass level is about y=72; pour continues, with a stable narrow stream and a final subtle impact ripple. No cuts, camera motion, object motion, or new objects occur.

The rise is eased but monotonic. Temporal details use deterministic sinusoidal phases so animation is repeatable and there is no random pixel flicker.

METHOD.md

Method

I generated the sequence entirely with deterministic local Python/Pillow pixel operations over the supplied first_frame.png; no external models, assets, masks, references, or engines were used.

  • Frame 00 is a direct RGB copy of the source image.
  • The fixed photographed image is retained as the base of every later frame, so the camera, tabletop, wall, dispenser, wicker stand, tap, and glass do not drift.
  • The rising drink is an antialiased, translucent interior mask fitted to the measured glass walls. Its upper surface follows a monotonic smoothstep trajectory from approximately y=94 to y=72.
  • Grapefruit color is composited into the new volume while preserving underlying refraction. Feathered analytic polygons and curves create a coherent mixing plume and curl below the impact point.
  • The narrow pour is redrawn from the stationary spout to the moving liquid surface with small deterministic width/shape modulation, a shadow edge, and a highlight.
  • Meniscus lines, impact ripples, sparse deterministic rising bubbles, and stationary vertical glass highlights provide restrained temporal detail without random flicker.
  • The dispenser surface receives only a subtle 0-2 pixel boundary change over the five seconds.
  • All synthesized masks are rasterized at 4x resolution and Lanczos-downsampled for antialiased edges.
  • artifact_io.save packs the final RGB frames as uint32 0xRRGGBB values in (144, 256, 20) order and writes the PNG frames, inspection sheet, and standalone player. transition_check.png visualizes selected adjacent-frame absolute differences at 5x intensity plus the intended surface trajectory.
