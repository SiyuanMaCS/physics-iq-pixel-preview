OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Source measurements

  • Source is 256 x 144 RGB, a locked landscape shot with a neutral gray wall, a wood tabletop at roughly y=132..143, and two light-gray pillows spanning the lower two-thirds of the frame.
  • Left pillow occupies approximately x=24..165, y=50..137. Its top crown is near y=50 beneath the kettlebell and its lower seam runs from about (25,132) to (162,124).
  • Right pillow occupies approximately x=130..255, y=51..132. Its top crown is near y=53..58 beneath the paper; the front seam is around y=116..129.
  • The black kettlebell occupies approximately x=77..123, y=0..60. The bell body is x=84..123, y=21..60; its lowest point is near y=59. The handle extends to and is cropped by the top edge. It is centered near x=101 over the left pillow.
  • The vivid green paper occupies approximately x=176..203, y=14..54, with a nearly rectangular silhouette and slight corner irregularities. A dark blue gripping strip enters from the top at x=184..193 and meets the paper around y=17. It is centered near x=189 over the right pillow.
  • Both object bottoms are initially at or just above the local pillow surfaces. Shadows are soft, consistent with diffuse light from the upper left.
  • The physically meaningful contrast is load, not drop speed: the 30-lb kettlebell should create a deep, broad indentation and surrounding bulge, while the paper produces only a tiny local settling of the fabric.

20-frame event plan (4 fps)

  • Frame 00 (0.00 s): exact source image, mandatory byte-for-pixel match.
  • Frames 01-03 (0.25-0.75 s): both objects descend together by about 1, 3, then 5 pixels. Pillow deformation begins at the two contact patches. Motion has gentle ease-in rather than free-fall acceleration because the objects are being placed.
  • Frames 04-07 (1.00-1.75 s): simultaneous placement continues. Both objects descend to their final supported positions. The kettlebell sinks roughly 10-11 pixels into a visibly deep left-pillow depression; the paper settles only about 5 pixels and leaves the right pillow almost unchanged. Contact shadows strengthen.
  • Frames 08-10 (2.00-2.50 s): small damped settling. The kettlebell/pillow pair overshoots by roughly one pixel and rebounds; the paper has at most a subpixel-to-one-pixel flutter. No bounce or sliding separates either object from its support.
  • Frames 11-19 (2.75-4.75 s): both objects remain simultaneously at rest on the pillows. The left pillow stays compressed and bulges around the heavy bell; the right pillow supports the light paper with only a shallow crease. Camera, wall, table, lighting, and all unrelated content remain fixed.

Generation intent

Use only local pixel operations on first_frame.png: isolate the two foreground objects with measured masks, reconstruct the small exposed background regions using neighboring pixels, warp localized pillow regions analytically for load-dependent deformation, then composite translated object pixels with soft contact shadows. Avoid adding hands, captions, new props, camera motion, or off-frame scene content.

METHOD.md

Method

The animation is generated entirely from first_frame.png with local Python pixel operations (numpy, Pillow, and OpenCV). No external images, masks, models, engines, or references are used.

  1. The kettlebell is isolated as the dark connected component in its measured bounding region. The green chroma of the paper and the blue/black gripper colors provide the second foreground mask. Both masks receive a subpixel feather for antialiased compositing.
  2. A clean plate is reconstructed only beneath those silhouettes by interpolating nearby pixels across each image row. This retains the existing wall-to-pillow boundary and does not alter unrelated areas.
  3. The pillow pixels are inverse-warped with analytic displacement fields. The 30-lb kettlebell drives a broad, roughly 9-pixel vertical depression plus a small outward side bulge; the paper drives only about a 1-pixel local depression. Soft contact shadows and restrained fabric creases reinforce contact.
  4. Both extracted foregrounds descend on the same eased progress curve. The kettlebell moves about 10.5 pixels and the paper about 5.3 pixels, reflecting their initially different clearances and the kettlebell's deeper sink. A small damped overshoot occurs around frames 08-10, after which both remain at rest.
  5. As the kettlebell descends, an analytic dark elliptical cap reconstructs the small part of its handle previously cropped above the image. The paper's two gripper jaws are extended to the top edge so the tool never appears disconnected.
  6. artifact_io.py writes the frame PNGs, packed uint32 tensor, inspection sheet, and standalone player. The generator additionally creates transition_check.png, reloads every PNG, reloads pixels.npz, and asserts exact equality with the in-memory RGB frames. Frame 00 is explicitly copied from and asserted equal to first_frame.png.
