ball-and-block-fall · 生成方法

The animation is generated deterministically by generate.py with local Python pixel operations only.

  1. first_frame.png is loaded as the sole visual source. Frame 00 is copied directly from it without recompression or compositing.
  2. A clean static plate is formed by tightly masking the ball and block and filling those small wall regions with OpenCV's local Telea inpainting. Source pixels belonging to the blue holder are explicitly restored, and very faint deterministic microtexture is added only inside the filled regions.
  3. The ball and block become small RGBA sprites. Their colors and texture come from the source image; analytic feathering repairs portions originally blended with the wall or hidden by the jaws. This avoids introducing external assets.
  4. Fixed, hand-tuned real-time trajectories simulate gravitational acceleration, first pillow contact, a damped rebound, second contact, and rest. The block gains a small rotation while falling; the ball rebounds more than the block.
  5. Pillow response uses a localized remap of the original pillow pixels, contact-dependent soft shadows, restrained compression shading, and a small fabric occlusion at maximum compression. The rest of the locked shot remains stable.
  6. artifact_io.save packs the RGB values as uint32 0xRRGGBB, writes all 20 PNGs, creates pixels.npz, inspection.png, and the standalone index.html. The generator additionally makes transition_check.png from selected release, impact, and settling frames.

No network, external model, external engine, additional scene image, mask, or reference asset is used.
