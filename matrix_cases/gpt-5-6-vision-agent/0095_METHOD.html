+def clean_plate(source: np.ndarray) -> np.ndarray:
+    """Remove the source domino from its small footprint using local inpainting."""
+    mask = np.zeros((HEIGHT, WIDTH), np.uint8)
+    # Includes the antialiased rim, visible vertical side, and one-pixel contact edge.
+    cv2.rectangle(mask, (109, 52), (133, 62), 255, thickness=-1)
+    # TELEA follows the strong table/platform boundary better than a flat color fill.
+    return cv2.inpaint(source, mask, 3.0, cv2.INPAINT_TELEA)

+def polygon(draw: ImageDraw.ImageDraw, points, fill):
+    draw.polygon([(round(x * SCALE), round(y * SCALE)) for x, y in points], fill=fill)

+def domino_layer(angle_degrees: float):
+    """Return supersampled RGB and alpha layers for a low cuboid and contact shadow."""
+    theta = np.deg2rad(angle_degrees)
+    # Positive image-plane theta is clockwise because screen y points downward.
+    u = np.array([np.cos(theta), np.sin(theta)], dtype=np.float64)
+    v = np.array([-np.sin(theta), np.cos(theta)], dtype=np.float64)
+    center = np.array([121.5, 56.5], dtype=np.float64)
+    half_length, half_width = 10.5, 2.25
+    top = [
+        center - half_length * u - half_width * v,
+        center + half_length * u - half_width * v,
+        center + half_length * u + half_width * v,
+        center - half_length * u + half_width * v,
+    ]
+    drop = np.array([0.0, 2.7])
+    bottom = [p + drop for p in top]
+
+    size = (WIDTH * SCALE, HEIGHT * SCALE)
+
+    # A restrained, platform-local contact shadow. It turns with the object but the
+    # screen-space light offset stays fixed, matching the locked upper-left light.
+    shadow = Image.new("L", size, 0)
+    sd = ImageDraw.Draw(shadow)
+    shadow_shape = [p + np.array([0.7, 1.8]) for p in bottom]
+    polygon(sd, shadow_shape, 72)
+    shadow = shadow.filter(ImageFilter.GaussianBlur(1.05 * SCALE))
+
+    rgba = Image.new("RGBA", size, (0, 0, 0, 0))
+    dr = ImageDraw.Draw(rgba)

+    # Bottom projection first, followed by two connecting faces and the pale top.
+    # The slight lavender cast is sampled from the source domino.
+    polygon(dr, bottom, (128, 121, 155, 255))
+    polygon(dr, [top[2], top[3], bottom[3], bottom[2]], (145, 135, 166, 255))

OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and animation plan

Source measurements

  • Canvas: 256 x 144 RGB, fixed camera, 4 fps.
  • Background: pale gray wall above a warm wooden tabletop; the table/wall break is near y=29, with soft vignetting toward the upper corners.
  • Black platform: an oblique circular/cylindrical puck centered near (153, 68). Its visible top spans approximately x=119..185 and y=44..79; the dark side wall extends to about y=90.
  • White plastic domino: the only moving object, initially a slim pale rectangular bar spanning approximately x=111..132 and y=54..61. It rests on the upper-left portion of the black platform, points almost horizontally, and slightly overhangs the platform toward the magnet.
  • Magnet assembly: fixed at left around x=79..105, y=51..102. It includes a faceted silver/black head, a small tan support/contact region, and a violet strip hanging downward.
  • Cable: fixed thin dark cable leaving the rear-right area of the setup, looping across the tabletop toward the upper-right edge.
  • Lighting: broad, soft illumination from upper-left; shadows are soft and low contrast. The domino is warm white with a brighter top and muted gray lower/contact edge.

Motion interpretation

The prompt specifies a clockwise rotation of the small white plastic domino on the platform near the magnet. The domino therefore rotates in the image plane about a stable contact/pivot near its right end on the platform, while all other pixels remain locked. The motion is smooth, modest, and physically readable: it accelerates gently, rotates through about 78 degrees clockwise, and eases to rest without translating away from the platform.

Twenty-frame event plan

  • Frame 00 (0.00 s): exact source image, initial nearly horizontal domino.
  • Frames 01-03 (0.25-0.75 s): subtle start, clockwise rotation grows from about 1 to 7 degrees.
  • Frames 04-07 (1.00-1.75 s): clearly visible clockwise turn, about 12 to 30 degrees.
  • Frames 08-11 (2.00-2.75 s): steady middle phase, about 39 to 57 degrees.
  • Frames 12-15 (3.00-3.75 s): motion slows, about 64 to 73 degrees.
  • Frames 16-18 (4.00-4.50 s): final easing, about 76 to 78 degrees.
  • Frame 19 (4.75 s): held final orientation at about 78 degrees, emphasizing completion.

At every generated time step, the original domino is removed with a local reconstruction of the black platform top; the domino and its tiny contact shadow are then resampled and composited at the planned angle. The platform silhouette, magnet, cable, table grain, lighting, framing, and exposure remain constant.

METHOD.md

Method

The sequence is generated locally from first_frame.png; no external images, masks, models, or engines are used.

  1. The source RGB image is loaded unchanged. Frame 00 is a direct array copy and is verified byte-for-byte against the source.
  2. For frames 01-19 only, the original domino's 25 x 11 pixel footprint is removed with OpenCV's local Telea inpainting. This reconstructs the tabletop-to-platform boundary from immediately neighboring pixels.
  3. The moving domino is represented as a small projected cuboid centered at approximately (121.5, 56.5). Its top dimensions and lavender-white colors are measured from the source. It follows the explicit clockwise angle schedule documented in OBSERVATION.md, ending at 78 degrees.
  4. The cuboid, fixed-light edge highlight, and soft contact shadow are drawn at 4x resolution and reduced with Lanczos filtering for stable subpixel edges. Only this compact local area changes; the magnet, platform, cable, table, wall, camera, and exposure stay locked.
  5. artifact_io.save writes the uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, PNG frames, inspection sheet, and standalone HTML player. The script additionally creates transition_check.png, then reloads every PNG and verifies it against the corresponding tensor plane. It also asserts exact equality between frame 00 and first_frame.png.

Run with:

python3 generate.py
