ball-rolls-off · 生成方法

This continuation was made entirely with local Python pixel operations on first_frame.png. No external image, mask, model, engine, or reference was used.

Construction

  1. The supplied 256 x 144 RGB frame is loaded without resizing. It is copied directly to output frame 00 and is never redrawn.
  2. For frames 01-19, the original ball and its small shadow footprint are removed from a 28 x 24 px tabletop region. Each affected row is reconstructed by robust interpolation between visible wood pixels on its left and right. Subtle high-frequency variation is borrowed from unobstructed wood pixels at equal offsets, and only the repair boundary is softly feathered.
  3. The ball's color and lighting are sampled from the supplied image. A low-pass component remains fixed in screen space so the room lighting does not rotate. A small high-frequency residual is rotated according to no-slip rolling, with angular displacement equal to horizontal distance divided by the measured 8.15 px radius. A faint 2.4-level curved nap/seam variation rotates with that surface residual.
  4. The sphere is placed with a subpixel antialiased circular matte. A low-opacity anisotropic Gaussian contact shadow travels with it and remains directly below the sphere.
  5. Horizontal position follows a nearly constant-speed trajectory with mild rolling resistance: the center travels from x=148.5 in frame 00 to x=254.0 in frame 19. Vertical center remains y=116.35; there is no bounce or camera motion. The final sphere is partially cropped as it continues beyond the right edge.
  6. artifact_io.save packs every RGB frame as uint32 0xRRGGBB, transposes the tensor to 144 x 256 x 20, saves the PNG sequence and pixels.npz, and embeds the same packed animation in the standalone index.html player.

Verification and review

  • Confirmed 20 frames, each RGB and exactly 256 x 144.
  • Confirmed frames/00.png and the in-memory frame 00 exactly equal first_frame.png pixel-for-pixel.
  • Reloaded all 20 PNGs and confirmed exact equality with the generated uint8 frame array.
  • Reloaded pixels.npz, confirmed its dtype is uint32, and confirmed every packed pixel equals the corresponding PNG RGB value.
  • Visually reviewed inspection.png at original resolution for the full trajectory and transition_check.png at original resolution for consecutive early and late transitions. The background remains locked, the repaired footprint is unobtrusive, and the ball stays in contact with the tabletop while rolling right.
