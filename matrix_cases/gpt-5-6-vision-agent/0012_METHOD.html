ball-hits-nothing · 生成方法

Actual generation method

This sequence was made entirely with deterministic local Python pixel operations. No external image, model, engine, mask, or reference was used.

  1. first_frame.png is loaded as an RGB uint8 array. Frame 00 is assigned directly from that array and is never redrawn or recompressed before the output PNG is saved.
  2. A clean locked-camera plate is created for frames 01-19. Two overlapping local ellipse masks cover the photographed orange ball and its faint down/right shadow. OpenCV's local Telea inpainting propagates the surrounding pale wood surface into only that masked area. The sofa, pipe, table boundaries, brown ball, and red ball are untouched.
  3. The orange ball follows a nearly constant-speed path from (37,45) to (217,52). The small downward slope is the image-space projection of a straight course at roughly constant tabletop depth. A subpixel vertical term of at most 0.16 px represents minor surface unevenness.
  4. The replacement ball is an analytic shaded sphere rendered with 4 x 4 supersampling per output pixel. Its color and lighting are matched to the photographed orange ball: warm orange diffuse material, upper-left illumination, dark lower-right limb, subtle front highlight, and fine deterministic surface variation.
  5. A faint curved tennis-ball seam is evaluated in the ball's material coordinate system. The material coordinates rotate about the screen-vertical axis by distance / radius, producing no-slip rolling rather than a sliding texture.
  6. A soft neutral contact shadow is composited before the sphere. It combines a compact contact core with a wider Gaussian cast shadow and remains anchored below/right of the ball throughout.
  7. artifact_io.save writes the 20 PNGs, the packed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, inspection sheet, and standalone player. generate.py separately creates transition_check.png from every consecutive pair.

Visual review and revision result

I visually reviewed inspection.png, .tmp/clean_plate.png, and transition_check.png with the image-view tool. The clean plate does not retain an orange silhouette; the only temporal changes after frame 01 are the moving sphere and its attached shadow; the course is straight and stays on the tabletop; and the stationary balls and all scene boundaries remain stable. The initial 00-to-01 difference correctly contains both the removal at the photographed start position and the ball's new position.

Verification

The generator performs these checks after saving:

  • frame 00 RGB pixels are exactly equal to first_frame.png;
  • all 20 PNGs are RGB, 256 x 144, and exactly equal to the in-memory frames;
  • pixels.npz contains a uint32 tensor with shape (144, 256, 20);
  • every packed tensor pixel exactly matches the corresponding PNG's 0xRRGGBB value.

The generation is deterministic and can be reproduced with:

python3 generate.py
