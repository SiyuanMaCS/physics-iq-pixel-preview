ball-hits-duck · 生成方法

Actual generation method

This continuation is made entirely from the supplied first_frame.png with deterministic local Python pixel operations. No network resources, other cases, generative models, masks, or external rendering engines were used.

  1. The input is loaded as an RGB uint8 array at 256 x 144.
  2. A clean tabletop plate is reconstructed under the original ball, duck, and shadows. Because the wood grain runs approximately horizontally, clear pixels from the same image rows are shifted in x, broad illumination is median-matched at each replacement rim, and the patches are softly feathered. This approach was selected after visual review showed that an initial local-inpainting version created a visible star-shaped artifact behind the moving duck.
  3. The yellow/orange duck is segmented by local RGB color criteria inside a tight region of interest. Morphological closing and hole filling preserve the black eye; a small dilation and Gaussian feather retain the source photograph's soft edge.
  4. The brown ball is extracted with a soft elliptical alpha boundary. Its original internal shading and pale curved seam remain source pixels.
  5. The ball center follows a straight shallow down-right line in image space, which represents straight left-to-right motion on the foreshortened tabletop. The ball sprite rotates according to traveled distance and effective radius, making the seam visibly turn as it rolls.
  6. At contact, the ball loses forward speed and rebounds a short distance. The duck shifts right, tips clockwise about a bottom contact pivot, and performs a progressively smaller counter-wobble before settling.
  7. Both sprites use bicubic affine resampling on premultiplied RGB/alpha layers. Soft elliptical cast/contact shadows are rebuilt beneath the moving objects and composited before the sprites.
  8. Frame 00 explicitly bypasses every reconstruction step and is copied directly from the input array.

Timing

The output contains 20 frames at 4 fps. Ball travel occupies frames 00-08, collision transfer and the largest duck motion occupy frames 08-12, damping occupies frames 13-17, and frames 18-19 hold the settled result.

Output and verification

artifact_io.save writes the RGB PNG sequence, the standalone HTML player, the inspection sheet, and pixels.npz. The generator also writes transition_check.png, focusing on the initial transition, contact, maximum tip, settling, and end hold.

The generator verifies all of the following after writing:

  • array shape is exactly (20, 144, 256, 3) and dtype is uint8;
  • frames/00.png and the in-memory first frame exactly equal the supplied first frame;
  • each saved PNG exactly equals its generated frame;
  • pixels.npz has shape (144, 256, 20), dtype uint32, and every value exactly matches packed 0xRRGGBB pixels from the PNG sequence.

The final inspection.png, transition_check.png, and reconstructed tabletop plate were visually reviewed with the image-view tool after the cleanup revision.
