ball-train · 生成方法

The animation is a deterministic local pixel composite generated by generate.py. No external images, models, masks, engines, network resources, or other cases are used.

  1. Load first_frame.png as the sole visual source. Frame 00 is copied directly and is checked for exact array equality.
  2. Measure the pipe and ball centers/radii from the source image. Build a static plate with two oversized elliptical masks covering the gray-ball region and the touching brown/orange region, including their original shadows. Each affected table scanline is filled by interpolation between clear source pixels immediately to its left and right, lightly blurred to match the source camera softness, and feathered at the mask boundary. This specifically follows the table's horizontal wood grain without retaining a colored ball rim.
  3. Extract 25 x 25 RGB patches centered on the photographed gray, brown, and orange balls. Apply analytic, feathered circular alpha masks so the original highlights, shading, colors, and tennis-ball seams remain intact.
  4. Move the gray ball along a straight, very slightly downward table-perspective trajectory. It contacts the brown ball at frames 06-07. Momentum then propagates from brown into the already touching orange ball; the gray ball almost stops with a tiny rebound, the brown rolls a short distance, and the orange rolls farthest. All trajectories ease toward rest to model tabletop friction.
  5. Rotate each source texture slightly in proportion to horizontal displacement, add restrained horizontal softness only at the highest speeds, and render soft low/right ellipses behind the balls for cast/contact shadows.
  6. Save through artifact_io.save, producing the uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, 20 PNGs, inspection sheet, and standalone player. transition_check.png enlarges the approach/contact/separation sequence. The script finally reloads every PNG and the tensor and verifies exact pixel agreement.

The delivered motion is 20 RGB frames at 256 x 144, played at 4 fps for 5 seconds with a fixed camera and unchanging background.
