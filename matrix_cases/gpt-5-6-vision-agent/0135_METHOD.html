METHOD.md

Method

The animation is generated locally from first_frame.png with deterministic Python pixel operations.

  1. The supplied RGB image is loaded unchanged and used directly as frame 00.
  2. A hand-measured mask covers the suspended grabber, paper, and their soft wall shadow. The exposed wall is reconstructed by row-wise interpolation between clean pixels on either side, with a feathered boundary that retains the wall's original low-frequency shading.
  3. The photographed grabber is extracted with a hand-bounded black/orange color matte. Its original pixels are premultiplied by that matte, slightly widened to show the jaws releasing, then translated upward with cubic resampling.
  4. The photographed paper is extracted with an antialiased irregular polygon following its visible silhouette; a narrow saturation key removes source pixels belonging to the orange jaws where they overlap its edge. Affine transforms preserve its real fold texture while changing position, angle, width, and height. Its trajectory accelerates downward, flutters, compresses on water contact, broadens while floating, and receives a gradual cool/dark wet tint.
  5. The bowl and static scene remain untouched outside localized masks. A soft elliptical shadow approaches the water. At contact, analytically drawn clipped elliptical ripples, small splash strokes, and droplets are composited onto the existing water; their radius grows and their contrast decays. A secondary damped ripple and tiny paper bob complete the settling motion.
  6. artifact_io.save writes the uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, all frame PNGs, the inspection contact sheet, and standalone HTML player. A separate transition sheet shows the pre-impact/contact/post-impact frames and their consecutive-frame delta maps.

The generator finally reloads pixels.npz and every saved PNG, checks them against the in-memory RGB frames, and asserts that frame 00 exactly equals the supplied source image.
