OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Locked composition and measured geometry

  • Source is an RGB 256 x 144 image. The camera is fixed at table height with a slightly downward view; nothing in the frame suggests camera motion.
  • The rear wall occupies roughly y=0..109. A soft circular spotlight pool is centered near (111, 62), with an approximately 92 x 113 visible oval and a grey-white interior fading to black.
  • A matte black rotating platform/table crosses the foreground. Its back rim runs from about (0, 114) through (127, 99) to (256, 102); its front face fills the lower edge. A warm beam streak lies around x=104..205, y=119..143.
  • The porcelain statue occupies about x=138..174, y=60..120. Its rotation axis is approximately x=156; its luminous plinth is around x=141..174, y=104..119. It is strongly side-lit from the right and has pale ivory highlights, charcoal occlusion contours, and warm yellow bounce.
  • The large shadow occupies about x=70..145, y=0..105. It is anchored near (111, 102), reaches beyond the top edge, and is much softer than the statue. Its darkest torso/head region lies around x=96..137, y=0..82; the broad lower shadow merges into the platform edge.
  • A black cylindrical flashlight is fixed at lower right, approximately x=197..255, y=102..143, aimed left/up toward the statue. Its small specular edge and every static highlight must remain locked.
  • Illumination is low-key and photographic: nearly black corners, cool grey wall light, warm grazing light over the platform, clipped porcelain highlights, and mild sensor/noise texture.

Motion interpretation

The black platform turns clockwise at a steady real-time rate, carrying the statue around its vertical axis without translating its base. From this single view, clockwise rotation is conveyed by a consistent right-to-left drift of near-side porcelain details, gradual profile narrowing/broadening, shifting contour occlusion, and a correspondingly changing projected shadow. The wall, spotlight pool, flashlight, camera, and platform outline stay fixed. The platform itself has little readable texture, so its rotation is supported by a faint warm surface glint moving around the statue's base rather than by camera-relative motion.

METHOD.md

Method

This sequence is a local pixel-based continuation of first_frame.png; it does not use external images, external engines, learned models, masks, or network resources.

Construction

  1. frames/00.png is a direct RGB array copy of first_frame.png.
  2. Two native-resolution polygon regions identify the porcelain figurine and the large wall shadow. The figurine's final alpha is refined from its per-pixel contrast against a small OpenCV inpaint of the same region. This preserves genuine dark porcelain edges while removing the visible wall gaps between the head, arm, and dress.
  3. A clean plate is reconstructed only where the statue or shadow can uncover pixels. The wall fill comes from a robust radial-median model measured from unobscured pixels of the existing elliptical spotlight. The lower statue footprint is reconstructed with narrow-radius Telea inpainting so the diagonal platform rim and warm beam continue through it. Pixels outside these local regions are never replaced.
  4. The statue rotates at a constant analytic rate of 7 degrees per frame. A base-anchored horizontal affine warp changes projected width and upper-body drift while keeping the plinth centered on the turntable. At the narrowest pose, an occlusion switch reveals a mirrored reverse-side approximation. A restrained angle-dependent intensity band makes fixed flashlight illumination roll across the turning porcelain.
  5. The original shadow density is calculated from the difference between the source wall and the reconstructed spotlight. It receives a separate, softer base-anchored affine transform. Its foot remains at the wall/platform junction while the head and shoulders change width and drift coherently with the statue. The result is blurred by less than one pixel and composited as a charcoal, slightly warm optical shadow.
  6. The black platform is intentionally almost textureless. A very faint warm sub-pixel glint follows an elliptical path around the statue's base to support clockwise turntable motion without inventing a conspicuous marker.
  7. Frame 01 uses a short optical handoff (70% synthesized / 30% source) because the physical motion is still sub-pixel. Subsequent frames contain no stationary foreground ghost. The flashlight, wall, spotlight outside reconstructed occlusions, platform outline, exposure, and camera framing remain locked.

Timing

  • Resolution: 256 x 144 RGB
  • Frames: 20
  • Playback: 4 fps
  • Duration: 5 seconds
  • Motion: uniform 7 degrees per frame, 133 degrees across the excerpt

Output and verification

artifact_io.save writes the twenty PNGs, the standalone player, the inspection sheet, and pixels.npz. generate.py then creates transition_check.png from four representative adjacent-frame differences and performs the following assertions:

  • frame array shape is (20, 144, 256, 3) and dtype is uint8;
  • frame 00 is exactly equal to the supplied source array;
  • every saved PNG decodes to its corresponding generated RGB array;
  • pixels.npz is a (144, 256, 20) uint32 tensor packed as 0xRRGGBB and exactly matches all PNG pixels.
