OBSERVATION.md

Observation and event plan

Scene measurements

  • Canvas: 256 x 144 RGB, fixed camera, soft indoor light from upper left.
  • Background: gray wall occupies the upper field; the wall/table boundary slopes from about (0, 65) to (190, 40) and then continues behind the plate. Warm brown wood fills the lower field with fine vertical/perspective grain.
  • Dish: shallow white/gray oval centered near (150, 94), outer bounds about x=91..209, y=69..120. The near rim is pale gray and the rear rim is white. Light cyan liquid occupies an ellipse around x=101..199, y=78..109.
  • Paper towel: a softly shaded off-white vertical sheet, approximately a skewed quadrilateral with corners (126, 29), (170, 20), (179, 98), (135, 106). Its lower edge is already at/in the liquid. The sheet has a bright center-right face, a gray left fold, feathered edges, and a subtle cyan cast near the immersed bottom.
  • Grabber: only its orange/black jaw tip is visible at the top, diagonally from about (132, 0) to (143, 12). The connection to the towel is out of frame.
  • Shadows/reflections: soft dark contact shadow beneath/behind the towel on the water and dish; no hard cast shadow or camera motion.

Motion interpretation

The towel is released while its bottom already touches the liquid. It therefore drops only a few pixels before gravity and drag make it tip to camera-right, buckle at the wet lower edge, and spread into a low folded shape on the liquid. The paper remains visibly paper-like rather than vanishing below the surface. The grabber tip retracts upward and leaves frame. Motion eases after contact, with a small damped settling wobble and subtle water ripples.

METHOD.md

Method

The animation is generated entirely from first_frame.png with local Python pixel operations (NumPy, Pillow, and OpenCV). No external images, masks, models, or rendering engines are used.

  1. I manually measured the paper, grabber, dish, and liquid bounds from the supplied 256×144 frame.
  2. Soft hand-authored polygon masks isolate the towel and the visible grabber tip. A clean static plate is made by local OpenCV inpainting from neighboring pixels; the small newly exposed wall region is regularized with a row-wise robust color estimate sampled from unobstructed parts of the same frame.
  3. The original paper pixels and their natural illumination are premultiplied by the mask and warped with a per-frame projective transform. The lower wet edge stays nearly pinned while the upper edge accelerates down and right. Hand-tuned states add a small post-contact rebound and slow final spreading.
  4. The grabber uses the original masked pixels translated upward with slight acceleration until it exits the frame.
  5. Procedural soft shadows, clipped elliptical water ripple arcs, a restrained fold valley, and a subtle cyan wet-edge tint reinforce contact and absorption. All overlays are antialiased and low contrast to match the soft source image.
  6. Frame 00 is copied directly and exactly from the input. artifact_io.save writes the packed tensor, PNG sequence, contact sheet, and standalone player. The generator additionally writes a transition diagnostic and asserts exact equality among the in-memory RGB frames, all PNGs, and the stored uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor.
