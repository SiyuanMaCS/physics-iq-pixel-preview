OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Source-frame measurements

  • Canvas: 256 x 144 RGB. Camera is locked, low and slightly oblique over a pale wood tabletop.
  • The table fills the frame. Its grain runs approximately from lower left toward upper right; illumination is soft and warm from the upper-left side.
  • A matte black cylindrical pipe enters at the lower-left edge. Its visible mouth/nozzle occupies roughly x=0..25, y=99..128 and points diagonally toward the ball/ramp (about 22 degrees upward in image space).
  • The yellow-green tennis ball is centered near (102, 74), with a visible radius of about 10 px. Its soft contact/cast shadow extends down-left, roughly x=72..108, y=70..91. This is the moving subject and its exact source appearance is retained in frame 00.
  • The cardboard ramp is a tan quadrilateral with near/lower edge from about (144, 34) to (180, 68), right edge toward (225, 30), and an upper portion leaving the frame around x=191. It rises toward the upper-right. A blue support block is visible behind its right side near x=222..247, y=4..40.
  • A dark olive-green out-of-focus object clips the top-left boundary and a tiny dark/red patterned object clips the lower-right corner; both remain static.
  • The pipe center near (14,113) through the ball center near (102,74) defines a line with image slope about -0.44. Extrapolated, it meets the middle of the ramp's low edge near (157,49). The ball should follow that same straight axis onto the cardboard, then climb toward the upper-right.

Motion and compositing plan (20 frames at 4 fps)

  • Frame 00: exact source image, unchanged.
  • Frames 01-04: ball rolls across the tabletop on the pipe axis, centers approximately (113,69), (124,64), (135,59), (146,54). Its ground shadow follows down-left.
  • Frames 05-06: ball reaches the foot of the ramp near (157,49), then transitions onto the cardboard near (166,42). The shadow tightens and begins to lie on the cardboard surface; overlap with the low edge sells the transfer onto the incline.
  • Frames 07-12: ball climbs the cardboard plane along its long axis and slows under gravity: centers approximately (174,35), (181,29), (187,24), (192,20), (196,17), (199,14). Apparent radius gently decreases with perspective.
  • Frames 13-15: it eases to a momentary stop short of the raised end, near (201,12), (202,11), (201,12).
  • Frames 16-19: gravity starts a modest rollback down the same line, with centers near (199,14), (196,17), (192,21), (187,25). The ball stays visibly on the ramp at the end.
  • The camera, pipe, table, ramp, blue support, exposure, and crop never move. Only the ball and its local shadow change.

Planned pixel method

Create a clean plate by locally reconstructing the initial ball/shadow area from nearby tabletop texture using directional sampling and feathered blending. Extract/approximate the ball as a shaded yellow-green sphere with a thin curved seam, soft edge, and motion-dependent rotation; render an analytic soft shadow on the tabletop or ramp. Composite both into the source frame along the measured path with local occlusion masks at the ramp boundary. Preserve frame 00 directly from first_frame.png without any resave transformation in the tensor source array.

METHOD.md

Method

Actual generation method

The sequence is generated deterministically by generate.py using only first_frame.png, NumPy, Pillow, and local OpenCV pixel operations.

  1. The 256 x 144 RGB source is loaded directly. Frame 00 is assigned from that source array without alteration.
  2. A clean tabletop plate is made only for frames 01-19. The ball-and-shadow region is replaced with a real wood-grain sample from an undisturbed part of the same visible board (down-right of the ball). The sample is median color-matched at the destination boundary and blended with a soft elliptical feather. This retained visible grain better than a flat painted fill.
  3. The actual ball appearance is taken from a 31 x 31 crop centered at approximately (103,73). Its alpha matte is estimated from yellow-green channel excess plus a soft radial support, preserving the source highlight, shading, blur, and color rather than inventing a new ball texture.
  4. The ball sprite is scaled and rotated with cubic interpolation. Its measured centers follow the pipe axis from (102,74) to the ramp's lower edge around (157,49), then continue along the cardboard plane. Step distance decreases on the incline, reaches a brief stop near (202,11), and reverses for the start of a gravity-driven rollback.
  5. Shadows are analytic blurred ellipses. On the tabletop, the cast shadow is broad and offset down-left like the source. At ramp contact it transitions to a smaller, tighter ellipse clipped to the ramp polygon. The camera and every apparatus/background pixel outside the small reconstructed area remain fixed.
  6. artifact_io.save packs each RGB frame as uint32 0xRRGGBB, transposes the tensor to shape (144,256,20), writes the 20 PNGs, builds the inspection sheet, and embeds lossless frame deltas in the standalone HTML player. generate.py additionally creates transition_check.png from selected current frames and amplified absolute RGB differences.

Validation

  • Exactly 20 frames, each RGB uint8 at 256 x 144.
  • frames/00.png and tensor frame 0 equal the decoded first_frame.png pixel-for-pixel.
  • pixels.npz contains uint32 pixels of shape (144,256,20) and equals the recomputed 0xRRGGBB tensor.
  • Every emitted frames/00.png through frames/19.png was decoded and compared pixel-for-pixel with its in-memory source frame.
  • I visually reviewed inspection.png and transition_check.png with the image-view tool. The initial inpaint revision was rejected for smearing wood grain, and a Poisson-clone revision was rejected for leaving a faint green ghost. The final color-matched, feathered same-board texture sample removes that ghost while retaining plausible grain. The reviewed final sequence keeps the frame locked, maintains the straight pipe-to-ramp trajectory, shows a continuous contact transition, and has a smooth stop/reversal.
