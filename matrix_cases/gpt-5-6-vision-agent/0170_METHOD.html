OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Measured scene

  • Source is an RGB image at 256 x 144. The camera is locked, with no motion or zoom.
  • A pale cool-gray wall occupies the upper field. The wooden table begins at about y=91 near the center and at about y=108 at the left and right edges; its rear edge is a shallow perspective chevron behind the centered apparatus.
  • The cylindrical woven basket spans approximately x=96..168 and y=64..133. Its top is an ellipse around y=64..82; its flat lower contact line is near y=130. The apparent center is x=132.
  • A black/silver magnet sits at approximately x=124..145, y=58..69. Dark metal hook/linkage continues up around x=129..141, y=8..60. A narrow orange cord enters from the top near x=135 and runs to y=9.
  • The apparatus casts a soft shadow down/right on the tabletop. Illumination is diffuse from the upper left, so right and lower surfaces are darker.
  • The orange tennis ball is specified as already on the table but is fully occluded by the basket in frame 00. It will be revealed centered at x=132, with radius about 13 px and table contact near y=129. It must remain stationary while the cover rises.
  • Motion is a straight vertical lift. The basket, magnet, hook, and visible cord move together. The stationary background and ball do not move. The moving basket occludes the ball until its lower edge clears the ball.

METHOD.md

Method

The animation is a local 2D pixel reconstruction made entirely from first_frame.png plus analytic drawing. No external images, models, masks, or engines were used.

  1. I measured the visible basket, magnet, hook/linkage, orange cord, table edge, and contact shadow in the 256 x 144 source.
  2. I made a hand-shaped, softly anti-aliased opacity mask around the complete hanging assembly. The visible apparatus pixels are copied directly from the source rather than redrawn.
  3. I reconstructed the wall and tabletop that the initial apparatus hides with OpenCV local inpainting. On the wall, row-wise quadratic interpolation preserves the broad illumination gradient. On the table, subtle high-frequency residuals sampled from nearby wood restore restrained grain. The original apparatus shadow is included in the repaired area.
  4. I rendered the specified hidden orange tennis ball as a fixed anti-aliased sphere at x=132, with diffuse upper-left lighting, a small highlight, mild fibrous noise, pale curved seams, and a soft tabletop contact shadow.
  5. Across frames 02–19 the source-derived hanging assembly is translated vertically upward using the measured eased displacement schedule in OBSERVATION.md. It is composited last, so it correctly occludes the stationary ball until the lower basket rim passes it. A soft basket shadow fades and broadens with increasing height.
  6. Frames 00 and 01 are the untouched source; in particular frame 00 is exactly equal to first_frame.png. The camera and background remain locked throughout.

artifact_io.save packs every RGB frame as uint32 0xRRGGBB into pixels.npz, writes the 20 PNGs, creates inspection.png, and builds the standalone player. generate.py also creates transition_check.png and performs an exact assertion that every saved PNG equals its tensor slice and that frame 00 equals the source.
