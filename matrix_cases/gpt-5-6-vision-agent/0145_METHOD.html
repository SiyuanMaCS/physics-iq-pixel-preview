OBSERVATION.md

Scene observations and animation plan

Measured scene

  • Canvas: 256 × 144 RGB pixels. Camera is locked off; no crop, zoom, or exposure change.
  • Tabletop occupies essentially the whole frame below the dark rear strip at about y=18–31. It is pale warm wood with long perspective grain running mainly left/right and slightly diagonally.
  • Black tube enters from the left edge. Its visible mouth/body occupies approximately x=0–13, y=51–82 and points toward the right. The opening is near (7, 66).
  • Grey tennis ball in frame 00 has center approximately (52, 65), an apparent radius of 10–11 px, a soft highlight toward upper-left, a darker lower/right edge, and a faint contact/cast shadow extending mostly rightward. It has already emerged from the tube.
  • White open-topped lampshade stands near the middle-right. Its silhouette is approximately x=130–185, y=31–111. The front lower face spans roughly x=130–169, y=61–111. It stays completely static.
  • The ball's visible contact point is near y=75, placing its track farther from the camera than the lampshade's base near y=110. Therefore, when their image projections overlap, the ball and its shadow pass behind the lampshade rather than through its front face.
  • Light is broad and comes from above-left. The ball should retain its upper-left highlight and lower-right shading while its surface texture rotates.

Motion/event plan (20 frames at 4 fps)

  • Frame 00: preserve first_frame.png byte-for-pixel in RGB appearance.
  • Frames 01–06: the ball rolls right from x≈62 to x≈113 on a straight, nearly horizontal ground track centered at y≈65. Use about 9–10 px of travel per frame. The shadow translates with it; subtle rotating mottling/seam cues show rolling.
  • Frames 07–08: the ball approaches the lampshade and becomes progressively occluded by the shade's left/rear silhouette.
  • Frames 09–13: the ball continues at constant speed behind the opaque lampshade. It is mostly or completely hidden while its physical center travels from about x=137 to x=176; no motion discontinuity is introduced.
  • Frames 14–15: the ball reappears from behind the lampshade's right side, first as a narrow partial crescent and then as a full sphere.
  • Frames 16–19: it rolls across the remaining visible tabletop to the right. In frame 19 its center is near x=245, so it is still visibly crossing the table and is partly clipped by the right frame edge.
  • Static setup, grain, lighting, tube, and lampshade remain identical after removal of the original ball at its starting position. Only the translated ball, rotating internal texture, and its local shadow change.

Planned implementation

  1. Build a clean static plate by locally inpainting only the frame-00 ball and shadow region from neighboring wood texture, preserving every other pixel.
  2. Derive a soft ball alpha/matte and appearance from frame 00, supplementing it with analytic spherical shading and very subtle rotating tennis-seam/mottle detail.
  3. Translate it along the measured straight path, composite its soft shadow onto the table, then restore the lampshade foreground mask so overlap is physically occluded.
  4. Keep frame 00 exactly equal to the source; save and verify all requested tensor, PNG, contact-sheet, transition, player, and method artifacts.

METHOD.md

Method

The animation is generated entirely with deterministic local Python pixel operations. No external model, engine, reference image, mask, or network resource is used.

generate.py reads first_frame.png as RGB. Frame 00 is assigned directly from that source array. For later frames, the original ball and its faint shadow are removed with a feathered local matte. The replacement pixels are interpolated scanline by scanline from clean samples on the left and right, preserving the real row-to-row bands of the almost-horizontal wood grain. The tube, lampshade, and rest of the photographed scene are retained.

The moving ball is an analytic antialiased sphere matched to the source ball's measured 10.6-pixel radius and blue-grey color. Fixed upper-left Lambertian light, soft rim shading, and a small specular response give it volume. A subtle local-space mottle and curved tennis seam rotate at distance / radius, the no-slip angular rate for rolling. Two elliptical Gaussian components form the soft moving tabletop shadow.

The center travels at constant real-time speed from (52, 65) to (245, 65) over the 20-frame shot. The ball and shadow are composited first; then a hand-measured lampshade silhouette restores the original photographed lampshade pixels over them. This makes the farther-away ball remain in continuous motion while correctly disappearing behind the shade and reappearing on its right.

artifact_io.save writes the 20 PNG frames, packed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, inspection contact sheet, and standalone player. The generator additionally writes transition_check.png, containing four adjacent-frame comparisons as previous frame / next frame / amplified absolute difference. Finally, it reloads every PNG and the NPZ tensor and asserts exact agreement with the in-memory RGB frames, including exact equality of frame 00 to first_frame.png.
