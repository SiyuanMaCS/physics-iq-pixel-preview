The animation is a deterministic local Python continuation of the supplied photograph. generate.py uses NumPy, SciPy, OpenCV pixel operations, and Pillow. It uses no external images, models, network requests, or rendering engines. OBSERVATION.md was written before implementation, using the attached initial frame and numeric source-image measurements.

The original RGB pixels form the stationary background of every frame. Frame 00 is copied exactly. A yellow-color test within the measured duck region, followed by selection of the largest connected component, provides the surface matte. A filled version occludes the puddle at the duck silhouette. A small analytic guard preserves the dark eye. These masks are computed directly from the input; they are not supplied masks.

A narrow analytic path extends the existing red fall from the dispenser outlet near (191, 18) to the head crown near (194, 54). Its width and a narrow reflective stripe vary smoothly with time. Surface runoff follows interpolated curved paths over the crown, cheek, neck, and body. A moving arrival front causes the wet rivulets to extend downward before reaching the tray. Red alpha compositing retains source lighting and surface shading; a thin brighter stripe supplies wet highlights. Most of the duck stays visibly yellow and the solid objects do not move.

Sparse crown droplets follow explicit ballistic trajectories with constant downward acceleration. A few drops also descend beside the head. Once runoff reaches the base, an irregular shallow elliptical pool grows around the duck, with a small adjoining lobe. Its spreading radii scale with the square root of elapsed accumulation time. Red transparency preserves some tray texture and lighting. Soft rubber and stream reflections, a narrow meniscus, and compressed decaying ripple rings give the pool surface variation. This is an approximate two-dimensional liquid animation, with analytic motion and compositing rather than a volumetric fluid simulation.

The falling stream, droplets, and puddle boundary are calculated at four times the output resolution and reduced by area averaging. The curved wet-film masks are Gaussian profiles at output resolution. Final pixels are rounded and clipped to uint8 RGB. The provided artifact_io.py writes the packed tensor, individual PNGs, inspection montage, and embedded-data HTML player. The custom transition sheet presents selected adjacent frames and amplified absolute RGB differences.
