METHOD.md

Method

This sequence was generated entirely with local Python pixel operations on first_frame.png. The prompt supplied the action. There were no external references, network calls, AI generation models, physics/rendering engines, or subagents. The initial attachment was the visual reference; generated images were never opened with an image Read/View tool. Subsequent inspection used array measurements and numeric validation only.

Scene construction

generate.py uses NumPy, Pillow, OpenCV, and SciPy. The source image is 256 × 144 RGB. Frames 00–02 directly retain its pixels. Frame 00 is therefore an exact decoded-pixel match to first_frame.png.

The woven basket is cut out with a measured polygon rasterized at 8× resolution and reduced to an antialiased matte. Chroma refines the upper weave edges against the neutral wall. Dark-pixel differences against a reconstructed wall identify the orange cord, metal linkage, and magnet while retaining the open centers of the metal loops. The foreground matte equation removes wall-colored edge contamination. The opaque interior of the basket, magnet, and linkage consists of unchanged photographed pixels.

The hidden background is reconstructed locally. Small wall gaps use OpenCV Telea inpainting; the larger wall area uses row-wise Hermite interpolation between visible gray-wall samples. For the table, a cubic polynomial fits the illumination from unobstructed source wood. Photographic grain from the left side is copied at matching depth relative to the table's sloping rear edge, with its low-frequency brightness replaced by the fitted illumination. A feathered mask confines the reconstruction to the source object's footprint and contact-shadow area. This is an inferred background rather than recovered ground truth.

The ball is inferred from the prompt because it is concealed in the first frame. It is a stationary analytic sphere centered at (133.0, 116.8), radius 10.9 pixels. Its orange color uses spherical surface normals, an upper-left light, warm fill, restrained highlights, deterministic fine felt noise, and two curved pale seams. A 6× supersampled premultiplied sprite gives antialiased edges and seams. Its own small tabletop contact shadow remains in place. The photographed basket shadow fades and broadens as the basket rises.

The foreground assembly translates vertically through the recorded lift distances: 0, 0, 0, 1, 3, 7, 13, 21, 31, 43, 56, 69, 81, 91, 99, 105, 108, 110, 110, 110 pixels. This prescribed trajectory accelerates, then decelerates to a hold. It is a pixel animation, not a simulated magnetic interaction. The basket occludes the ball until it clears it; the ball does not rise with the magnet. The camera, photographic scale, and surrounding background remain fixed. The final basket silhouette occupies approximately y = 0–17, with its upper part naturally clipped by the frame.
