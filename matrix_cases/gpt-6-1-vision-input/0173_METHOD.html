METHOD.md

Method

This continuation was generated entirely with local Python pixel operations using first_frame.png as the only visual source and prompt.txt as the action description. No external scene assets, network access, image generation model, physics engine, or additional agent was used.

generate.py uses NumPy, Pillow, and SciPy for layered source-pixel compositing. It reconstructs the narrow gray-wall area hidden by the grabber by interpolating neighboring wall pixels from the same first frame. The pink block retains its source RGB texture and shading, with a small supersampled polygon reconstruction of its occluded upper face. Per-pixel opacity and premultiplied RGB separate the black/blue tool from the reconstructed wall and block. Bilinear sampling translates these layers vertically. Extending the tool's clipped upper cross-section beyond the frame keeps it attached to the top border during the small initial descent. This is a 2D pixel continuation, not a simulated or newly rendered 3D scene.

The tool and block descend together by 0.40 pixel over frames 00–03 to complete seating on the crossbar. Frame 04 holds that position. From frame 05, the grabber retracts smoothly upward while the block stays seated. It clears the frame at frame 13; frames 13–19 hold the completed demonstration. The crossbar front, both legs, tabletop, support shadows, camera framing, and all pixels outside the small object-motion region remain fixed. The front of the crossbar occludes the block's lower antialiased edge to maintain support contact.

Frame 00 is an exact RGB copy of the supplied image. The 20 RGB frames are 256 × 144 and are played at 4 fps for 5 seconds. pixels.npz contains pixels, a uint32 tensor with shape (144, 256, 20) and values packed as 0xRRGGBB. The PNG files are numbered frames/00.png through frames/19.png. index.html is a standalone player containing the first frame and lossless successive pixel deltas. inspection.png is an eight-frame montage; transition_check.png contains four frames and four amplified interframe differences.

Verification

Inspection of generated outputs used local code and numeric checks only. No generated frame, montage, diagnostic, or player was opened with an image Read/View tool. The attached initial image was the visual reference.

The generator independently reopens all 20 PNGs and compares every RGB pixel with the decoded uint32 tensor. It also checks exact initial-frame identity, unchanged pixels outside x=129–145/y=0–66, unchanged support/front-beam pixels from y=67 downward, a stationary seated block body after release, monotonically decreasing tool coverage during retraction, and an identical final hold across frames 13–19. The zero-motion layer decomposition reconstructs every initial RGB pixel exactly before the required explicit frame-00 copy. Numeric trajectories and per-transition changed-pixel bounds are recorded in verification.json.

A numeric inspection revision corrected top-border sampling and the tool's black-core mask. The final version passes the checks above. Additional local verification decodes the player packets and compares all embedded frames against the PNG/tensor outputs. The recovered wall and reconstructed pink cap are inferred local pixels; the hidden scene cannot be recovered exactly from a single input image.
