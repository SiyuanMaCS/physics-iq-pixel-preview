The moving ball uses the actual input's RGB surface texture, including the dark panel seams and photographic lighting. The original ball's footprint is removed from a static copy of the input. Rowwise gradients between visible wall samples reconstruct the gray backdrop, with low-amplitude deterministic source-scale noise.
