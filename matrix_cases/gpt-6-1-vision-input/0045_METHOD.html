METHOD.md

Method

This animation was made entirely with local Python pixel operations on first_frame.png. The only scene description used was prompt.txt. No external images, networks, models, simulation engines, or subagents were used.

The source image supplies the paper texture, both blue-black grabber textures, the silver shaft, wall colors, wood grain, and initial shadows. Green chroma isolates the paper; restricted dark/blue connected components isolate the grabbers. Their open interiors remain transparent. Soft edge mattes are estimated from the source colors, nearby foreground colors, and the reconstructed background. Source foreground colors are stored premultiplied to avoid moving a brown background fringe along the paper's outline.

The clean background uses robust low-order color surfaces fit to the visible wall and tabletop. The measured tabletop boundary is kept in place. Texture is transported from the unobstructed lower portion of the same photographed tabletop; local OpenCV inpainting contributes to the narrow exposed shaft region. Reconstruction is confined to the original objects and their soft shadow support. The camera view, table geometry, and lighting do not move.

The paper is parameterized as a textured strip between measured curved top and bottom boundaries. Two adjoining triangle meshes map its photographed source pixels into each subsequent frame. An eased, prescribed pull translates the left held point by (-14, -3.5) pixels and the right held point by (+18, +3.3) pixels. The tools undergo corresponding rigid transforms about their contacts, with rotations below one degree. The sheet gradually approaches a flatter projected quadrilateral, while the darker folded face gains some illumination.

A deterministic irregular crack in material coordinates begins at the lower central paper edge in frame 07 and advances toward the opposite edge. Separate copies of the two sides of that boundary open only behind the crack tip; the remaining ligament stays connected. Frame 13 completes the tear. Subsequent edge separation, local curl, gravitational sag, and a damped flutter are prescribed analytic deformations. Thin pale-green edge accents represent exposed paper fibers. These are geometric and shading approximations, not a measured force simulation or finite-element solution.

The initial photographed shadow residuals are separated from the foreground. Tool shadows follow tool transforms. The initial paper shadow fades as the fold opens, and a blurred shadow follows the changing paper silhouette. Moving layers are rasterized at four times the requested resolution, area-integrated, and composited over the native-resolution background. Pixels outside the finite motion/shadow support are restored exactly from the source. Frame 00 is assigned the original RGB array directly and is exact.
