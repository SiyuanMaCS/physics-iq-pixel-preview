Method

Pixel construction

Frame 00 is an unmodified RGB copy of the source. Each later frame begins with the same source photograph. NumPy and SciPy bilinear resampling move the original juice surface upward within a soft mask for the glass interior. The deformation decreases toward the bottom so the base and glass remain stationary. The strongest original glass reflections are kept in place. The table, woven stand, wall, upper dispenser, and other solid geometry retain their source pixels outside the liquid/stream region.

The inflow begins over a short startup transient and then continues throughout the clip.
