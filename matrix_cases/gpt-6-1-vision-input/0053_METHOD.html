METHOD.md

Method

This is a deterministic local pixel continuation of the supplied photograph. It uses only first_frame.png and the action specified by prompt.txt. No external images, models, rendering engines, network resources, or subagents were used. The initial scene was observed in the user attachment and measured with local RGB/luminance code. Generated images were never opened with an image Read/View tool; subsequent inspection consisted of array measurements and file checks.

generate.py uses NumPy, Pillow, OpenCV, and SciPy for local pixel operations. It creates a static clean plate by masking the grabber, held spheres, old reflections, and thin suspension strands. Small occlusions are reconstructed with OpenCV Telea inpainting. The larger wood occlusion is filled using a polynomial illumination fit to visible wood plus grain residuals sampled from another part of this same photograph. The obscured left chrome uprights use transverse pixel profiles sampled from unobscured portions of those same supports. These are approximate reconstructions of content hidden in the source.

Sphere colors come from intact chrome spheres in the original image. Elliptical sprite masks preserve their captured highlights, dark bands, and warm table reflections. The two originally held spheres receive sampled textures because their original surfaces are covered by the tool. Original base reflection patches are also translated beneath the moving spheres. Suspension strands and tiny attachment eyes are locally drawn at fixed measured anchor points. The blue and black tool is extracted from the original with a polygon and RGB-based opacity mask, with small metal jaw patches, then translated left and slightly downward. A blurred shadow follows it across the wood. Four-times spatial sampling provides subpixel positions and antialiased edges, followed by an area average to 256 × 144.

The motion is an analytic, damped small-angle Newton's cradle approximation. A signed pendulum angle starts at the left amplitude and oscillates with a 1.12-second period and exponential damping of 0.038 per second. Negative angles move the left pair; positive angles move the right pair. Thus the instantaneous velocity transfer at a center crossing activates two outgoing balls and leaves the center ball at rest. The nominal projected suspension radius is 79.8 pixels, initial lateral amplitude 12 pixels, and touching-center spacing 13 pixels. Vertical rise is computed with the pendulum cosine. This is a measured image-plane approximation, rather than a full 3D collision or optical simulation. The drawn front/rear strings retain the photographed support perspective; the exact length check applies to the nominal pendulum model. Four fps samples do not necessarily capture the instantaneous contact moment.

Frame 00 is copied exactly from the original. The rest of the sequence samples t = 0.25 through 4.75 seconds. Each of the 20 frames is displayed for 0.25 seconds, giving a 5-second sequence. By frame 03 the withdrawing grabber has cleared the view. The camera stays locked. Upper rails, the wall outside the thin strand area, the right table, and the front black foot retain their original pixels.
