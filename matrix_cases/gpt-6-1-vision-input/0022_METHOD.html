Actual pixel method

This is local 2D image compositing with analytic sphere texture coordinates and a mathematical rolling trajectory. It is not a generated photograph from an AI model, a full 3D scene reconstruction, or an external renderer. Scene information comes only from first_frame.png and prompt.txt. NumPy, Pillow, SciPy interpolation, and OpenCV's local pixel inpainting are used; the provided output helper only serializes pixels and builds the player. No network, external images, references, models, engines, or subagents were used.

OBSERVATION.md was written before the generation program and includes initial measurements and the final 20-frame event plan. Numeric checks located the tabletop's rear edge near (200, 62) and (255, 85–86); the trajectory was revised slightly toward the foreground to keep the contact on that surface.

  1. Reconstruct the original sphere and shadow footprint using local Telea inpainting on 477 pixels, with a feathered boundary. All other background pixels remain the supplied source pixels.
  2. Estimate the source sphere's soft silhouette around (161.30, 79.85), radius approximately 7.15 px. Recover its foreground RGB by removing the estimated background contribution through that alpha. Preserve the photographed, fixed illumination rather than rotating the highlight.
  3. Move the sphere with x(t)=161.30+25t−0.45t² and y(t)=79.85+0.018[x(t)−161.30]. Speed decreases smoothly from 25 to 20.725 image pixels/s. Apply a small perspective scale reduction, with scale=1−0.00013[x(t)−161.30].
  4. Add subtle deterministic fuzz and seam shading through sphere normals rotated with inverse Rodrigues rotation about a transverse axis tilted in camera coordinates. Rotation angle is traveled image distance / 7.15, approximating rolling without slipping. This rotates a low-contrast synthetic surface signal; the source color and broad illumination remain fixed. This is a source-matched sphere sprite with texture animation, not recovery of unseen real surface detail.
  5. Fit two per-channel elliptical shadow profiles from the source: a diffuse cast shadow offset to the left and a smaller, darker contact shadow. Move them with the sphere, scale them mildly with depth, and clip them to the measured tabletop. Thus the shadow does not darken the background furniture.
  6. Average three shutter samples over approximately 1/80 second. The resulting displacement is about 0.3 pixels, consistent with the already soft photographed edges. Blend the source foreground and shadows into the reconstructed background. Preserve the first frame directly, without processing it.

Files and reproduction

  • transition_check.png: frames 00→01 and 01→02 plus amplified absolute RGB difference maps.
  • index.html: a standalone embedded-pixel player with playback, seeking, replay, and looping, requiring no network.

generate.py, OBSERVATION.md, and this METHOD.md complete the required source and documentation artifacts.
