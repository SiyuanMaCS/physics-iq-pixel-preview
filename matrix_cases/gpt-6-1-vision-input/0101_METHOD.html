METHOD.md

Method

This animation uses local source-pixel compositing with prescribed rigid rotation. The scene inputs are first_frame.png and prompt.txt. generate.py uses Python, NumPy, Pillow, SciPy, and OpenCV pixel operations. The source photograph supplies the wrench's actual shape, fork, handle, color, texture, and antialiasing. The provided artifact_io.py and HTML template package the resulting frames.

The camera remains fixed. The wrench rotates clockwise in the horizontal plane about the black platform's center, traveling along its rim while the nearby magnet stays in place. The source wrench initially overhangs the platform slightly; the rotation retains that overhang. Motion advances by 7 degrees per frame, at 28 degrees per second, reaching 133 degrees in frame 19. The twenty samples are at 0.00, 0.25, …, 4.75 seconds, each displayed for 0.25 seconds, giving a five-second sequence.

The platform's unobstructed upper outline and front rim provide a fitted tilted ellipse: center (155.361, 60.577), major radius 32.716, minor radius 15.931 pixels, and tilt 6.067 degrees. The affine transform projects a circular rigid rotation through that ellipse. It preserves projected area and gives the appropriate changing apparent wrench orientation in this oblique view.

A neutral silver threshold identifies 110 source core pixels. A localized 252-pixel repair mask includes the wrench edges and its contact shadow. OpenCV Navier–Stokes image inpainting reconstructs the covered table and platform edge, and a polynomial fitted to intact nearby dark pixels reconstructs the covered interior of the upper face. A foreground matte estimated from the source and clean plate retains the fork opening and soft edge pixels. Premultiplied metal colors are warped with cubic interpolation. A small Gaussian contact shadow follows the tool, offset under fixed room lighting. Metal reflectance changes gently by up to 1.8 percent with angle. Each later frame averages three poses across a 90 ms exposure for subtle motion blur. Frame 00 is copied directly from the source, including the exact original PNG bytes.

The table, wall, magnet, purple strip, cable, and platform outside the small reconstruction footprint retain their source pixels. Across the whole sequence, 993 spatial pixels change; their bounding box is x=113–177, y=40–80. The slight extension above and left of the platform comes from the original wrench overhang as it rotates.
