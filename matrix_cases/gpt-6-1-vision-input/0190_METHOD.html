METHOD.md

Generation method

This continuation uses only the supplied first_frame.png and the scene described in prompt.txt. The scene interpretation and frame-by-frame plan were written in OBSERVATION.md before the generator. The cup is interpreted as lying sideways, with its thin foam sidewall buckling under the already resting 30 lb kettlebell. Collapse timing and hidden geometry are inferred.

generate.py performs deterministic local pixel operations in Python using NumPy, Pillow, OpenCV, and SciPy. It does not use a generative model, learned segmentation, a physics engine, external images, or network access. The provided artifact_io.py and player_template.html are used only to serialize the result and construct the standalone player.

  1. The bell is isolated from the source using a local dark-region component and a small geometric mask at the cropped upper handle. Edge alpha and premultiplied foreground colors are estimated against a reconstructed stationary background. The visible metal highlights and blue marking remain source pixels.
  2. The newly exposed wall is reconstructed with harmonic interpolation using neighboring source pixels as boundary conditions. The table uses a locally fitted polynomial color surface and wood grain copied from the same input with an offset that follows the table edge. The upper background strip is restored from an unobstructed source column. The recovered plate is fixed throughout the sequence.
  3. The bell translates vertically as one rigid object. Its cropped upper arch is extrapolated with an ellipse and local procedural metallic shading for the portion that becomes visible after the drop. This inferred arch is approximately centered at (135.2,17), with radii 25.3 and 28 source pixels, extending about 11 pixels above the initial crop. The visible source portion is retained.
  4. The cup retains its source texture, with a geometric alpha boundary and a small inferred foam patch where the bell occludes it. An inverse nonrigid warp compresses its height around a fixed table contact level of y=98.5 while widening it by at most 21%. Small sinusoidal folds and subdued grooves suggest buckled foam. A compact local contact shadow accompanies the collapse.
  5. The programmed descent is 0, 1, 5, 12, 17.2, 19, 18.65, 18.82, and 18.8 pixels, followed by rest at 18.8 pixels. At maximum compression the projected cup height is about 3 pixels. The small final motion represents damped settling. Several moving frames average three subpixel translations over a shutter span of at most 0.85 pixels.
  6. Frame 00 is copied directly from the decoded RGB input, bypassing all compositing. Every later frame retains exact source pixels outside the local object/shadow support. No additional objects, debris, camera motion, or cuts are introduced.

This is a source-pixel animation with a hand-specified deformation and motion schedule. It is not a measured reconstruction of actual material failure or a numerical structural simulation.
