METHOD.md

METHOD — case_0011

What happens

20 frames, 256×144, 4 fps. Locked camera. The orange ball rolls in a straight horizontal line out of the black pipe and across the light wood coffee table toward the right side, at constant speed, reaching the vicinity of the right edge at t ≈ 4.75 s. The pipe, the small red ball, the brown baseball and the rest of the scene never move. Frame 00 is the supplied first_frame.png, pixel-for-pixel.

All pixels are derived from first_frame.png using local numpy / PIL / OpenCV / scipy operations only; no external models, engines, references or network access are used (script: generate.py).

Measurements (from the source frame)

  • Orange ball centre C0 = (54.43, 50.94), sprite footprint radii ≈ 8.9 × 8.0 px, mean core colour ≈ RGB(199,125,77), diffuse highlight upper-left.
  • Contact shadow: soft ellipse right of the ball, centre ≈ (65.5, 52.5), axes (10.2, 7.8), peak darkening ≈ 25–30 %, plus a thin dark contact strip.
  • Table right edge near the ball row: x ≈ 1.2·y + 167 (used to clip shadow).
  • Trajectory: constant y, v = 8.40 px/frame (33.6 px/s); C19 = (214.0, 50.9), ball fully on the tabletop, ~6 px from the edge; only the soft shadow tip reaches the edge. This keeps the sprite entirely on visible wood while satisfying "rolls … over the entire table towards the right side".

Layering

From frame 0 three layers are built once:

  1. Static background plate. The occluded footprint (orange core ∪ soft disk ∪ geometric shadow ellipse, 3×3 dilated) is removed. Because the wood grain is horizontal, each affected row is rebuilt as a per-channel linear ramp between the medians of clean 14-px wood strips immediately left and right of the footprint; a Telea inpaint (cv2.inpaint, radius 4) is blended in over the outer 3 px of the footprint (weights from the distance transform) so no fill seam is visible. Everything outside the footprint is untouched.
  2. Shadow layer — per-channel coverage a_c = clip((plate_c − frame0_c) / plate_c, 0, 0.6), Gaussian-smoothed (σ = 0.6), zeroed outside the geometric shadow ellipse and under the ball. Stored per channel so the shadow's slight colour cast is preserved; it is composited multiplicatively (bg·(1−a_c)), i.e. physically a darkening.
  3. Ball layer. A smooth intrinsic colour template is seeded at the high-confidence orange pixels and propagated over the sprite support by iterative 3×3 diffusion plus Gaussian smoothing (σ 1.2, applied channel-wise). Coverage alpha is then un-mixed per pixel, α = ⟨plate−frame0, plate−F⟩ / |plate−F|², iterated three times with template refresh, feathered σ 0.55, pinned to 1 on the core. True ball colour (frame0 − (1−α)·plate)/α is used where α > 0.25 and the template at the rim. The layer is split into a fixed diffuse-shading base (masked Gaussian σ 1.6 over a dilated support) and a high-frequency albedo residual taken from the opaque interior.

Per-frame compositing (frames 01–19)

For dx = 8.40·t:

  1. The albedo residual is rotated about the ball centre by θ = −dx/8.0 rad (scipy.ndimage.affine_transform) — rolling without slipping, clockwise on screen for rightward travel. The diffuse base stays screen-fixed (lighting does not rotate with the sphere). On this nearly uniform ball the residual is small, so the rotation is physically correct but visually subtle; translation carries the motion.
  2. Ball colour, ball alpha and shadow coverage are shifted horizontally by dx with bilinear sampling (sub-pixel accurate), constant y.
  3. The shifted shadow is multiplied into the plate, then clipped by a Gaussian-feathered tabletop mask at the right edge; the ball is alpha-overed last.

Frame 00 is the original array directly, so it is identical to first_frame.png regardless of any reconstruction tolerance.

Outputs and verification

artifact_io.save writes pixels.npz (the (144,256,20) uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor), frames/00.png…19.png, inspection.png and index.html. generate.py also writes transition_check.png, a 4×5 sheet of amplified inter-frame differences.

Verified numerically after writing:

  • every frames/NN.png equals the corresponding plane of the packed uint32 tensor in pixels.npz;
  • frames/00.png equals first_frame.png exactly;
  • zero pixels change outside the corridor band y 38–63, x 30–239 across all 20 frames (background, pipe, red/brown balls fully static);
  • segmented orange centroid moves 54.44 → 214.07 with per-step 8.40 ± 0.1 px and y = 50.9 ± 0.1 (straight, constant-speed roll);
  • per-transition change masks form one compact ball+shadow blob that translates uniformly (see transition_check.png).

Visual review was performed with an image viewer on zoomed corridor crops and the full-frame inspection sheet (ball colour/shading consistency, clean vacated start spot, clearance of the red/brown balls, edge clipping at frame 19).
