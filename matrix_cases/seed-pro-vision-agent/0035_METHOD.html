Method sketch

Frame 00 is the untouched input PNG. For frames 01–19 the scene is recomposited locally in Python:

  1. a clean background plate is built (objects removed; holes filled with a fitted table colour field + real grain residuals copied from the clean front table strip; disk-dark holes nearest-filled);
  2. each block is cut out as an RGBA sprite (hue/saturation masks + connected component), and rigidly rotated about its bottom-left pivot by angle − initial_lean (identity warp when unchanged, so still-standing blocks keep their exact original pixels);
  3. the stick is erased and redrawn rotated clockwise at 8°/frame;
  4. soft falling shadows are added under toppling blocks.

METHOD.md

METHOD — case_0035: rotating stick knocks over six blocks

All pixels are produced locally in Python (numpy, opencv-python, PIL, scipy) from first_frame.png only — no external models, engines, network or subagents. Frame 00 is the input file, copied byte-for-byte (verified).

1. Scene measurement (OBSERVATION.md)

From the 256×144 first frame:

  • Six coloured blocks standing on a wooden table, footprints on y≈108.5. Pivot = bottom-left footprint corner: yellow (51.0), red (77.0), pink (98.5), green (124.5), blue (145.5), purple (165.5). Initial screen-space leans were measured from the mask axes (±5°, see LEAN0).
  • Black cylindrical platform to the right: top ellipse centre (211.5, 48.5), radii (43, 14); cylindrical side ends at y≈91–103 with a warm lit lower lip; small dark hinge socket at (192–213, 45–57).
  • Wooden stick: pivot at the socket (202.6, 52.0), length 33 px, initial screen clock angle 233° (tip touches the purple block around (176, 72)), width ~3 px.

2. Segmentation

Each block is thresholded in HSV/RGB (hue/saturation bands plus an x-band) and isolated with connectedComponents, keeping the component containing a seed pixel. A 1 px dilation ring is added to the alpha (weight 0.65) only where its colour is close to the block's reference colour, which preserves antialiased edges without dragging table fringe into the sprite (sprite_alpha).

3. Clean background plate (build_plate)

The plate is built once and reused for frames 01–19.

  • Removal mask: morphology-closed block cores, coloured/dark 3 px fringes, saturated/dark inter-block gaps, a 6 px coloured-halo sweep, the baked contact-shadow ellipses at every foot, the stick (thick line + tip circle), the hinge socket rectangle, and the platform's left sliver (analytic silhouette, x 162–184) that was previously hidden behind stick/purple.
  • Table fill: an affine RGB colour field least-squares fit from clean pixels, corrected with a sigma-20 normalised-convolution residual computed from every clean sample (this reproduces the bright pool of light behind the block row visible through the inter-block gaps). Wood grain is taken from the fully-clean front strip (y 128–143): a 101 px horizontal high-pass leaves the horizontal plank streaks; the streak rows are remapped to every table row with a smooth bounded random walk, plus a very soft low-frequency field and light fine noise. A small analytic Gaussian multiplies in the disk's cast shadow on the table near its lower-left lip.
  • Platform fill (analytic cylinder): the top ellipse is a cool grey (≈(58,56,60)) darkening 35 % at the front rim; the side is near-black (10,7,8) transitioning with a smoothstep to a warm wooden lower lip (104,75,48) with a faint bottom-edge highlight. The silhouette is antialiased (2.5 px) and faded across 4 px on the left lip.
  • Removed pixels are always fully replaced (the original can never bleed through); a 3 px feather at mask edges blends fill into untouched pixels.

4. Animation

  • Blocks: for every frame each RGBA block sprite is rigidly rotated with cv2.warpAffine about its fixed pivot by keyframe_lean − LEAN0; positive angle swings the top to the screen-left. A large pivot-relative crop (−70…+20 x, −70…+12 y) guarantees the fully-fallen 56 px bar is never clipped. Angles are hand-keyframed (ANG, 20 values per block) as a domino wave: purple starts at frame 1 and each neighbour starts one frame later (blue 2, green 3, pink 4, red 5, yellow 6); blocks reach 90° between frames 11 and 19, so mid-cascade frames show a smooth travelling arc.
  • Shadows: per block, a blurred soft contact ellipse at the pivot and, once moving, a blurred elongated ellipse whose length tracks 55·sin(lean) under the falling bar.
  • Stick/arm: a tapered two-tone wood polygon (edge (150,118,82), inner (201,172,131)) rotating clockwise at 8°/frame about (202.6,52), with an offset translucent contact shadow. The dark hinge socket (lit top edge, darker right side) is redrawn over the shaft base every frame.
  • Compositing order: plate → shadows → blocks left-to-right (so bars stack onto their left neighbour correctly) → stick.

5. Output and verification

artifact_io.save writes pixels.npz (H×W×20 uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor), frames/00.png–19.png, inspection.png and index.html (delta-encoded player). Verified afterwards:

  • frames/00.png is byte-identical to first_frame.png;
  • all 20 PNGs decode exactly to the packed tensor in pixels.npz;
  • transition_check.png (all 20 thumbnails + ×4 amplified frame-to-frame differences) confirms motion is confined to the blocks/stick with no background flicker.
