+_arc = np.concatenate([[0], np.cumsum(_seg)])

Motion model

  • Frames 0–6 (table): straight line from (104, 72) to E (150, 42), constant speed 9.2 px/frame, heading equal to the pipe axis.
  • Frames 6–19 (ramp): quadratic Bézier E(150, 42) → C(185, 19) → End(216, 12), so the entry tangent matches the incoming heading and the path bends up along the ramp toward the high edge by the block. Arc length is tabulated numerically (4001 samples) and parametrised by s(τ) = S·(2τ − τ²), τ = (t−6)/14 — constant deceleration from ≈ 8.6 px/frame at entry to zero at the rest point (frame 19 is at τ = 13/14, ≈ (215.6, 12.1)), modelling a ball rolling up an incline against gravity and stopping.

Per frame, the ball sprite is affine-warped: scale k = r(y)/r(72) (1.00 → 0.58), a small anisotropic stretch proportional to instantaneous speed (motion-blur suggestion, ≤ 11 %) aligned to the velocity direction via rotation about the sprite centre; the shadow is scaled only (no rotation) and composited first, then the ball alpha-over.
