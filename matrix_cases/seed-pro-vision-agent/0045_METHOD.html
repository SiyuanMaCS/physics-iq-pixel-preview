METHOD.md

METHOD — case_0045

Task: continue first_frame.png with 20 RGB frames (256×144, 4 fps, 5 s) of two blue-black grabber tools pulling a buckled green paper sheet from opposite corners until it tears, then dragging the halves apart.

Everything is local pixel math in generate.py (NumPy / SciPy.ndimage / OpenCV remap; no network, no external models or reference images). Runs deterministically (numpy.random.default_rng(11)) in a few seconds and writes all artifacts through the provided artifact_io.save.

1. Masks (frame 0 only)

  • Paper: green threshold (g>r+18 & g>b+18 & g>70), largest connected component, holes filled.
  • Tools: blue grip (b>90, b−r>30, b−g>20, g<120) + dark plastic (r,g<72, b<84, r−b<20); the chrome shaft is caught by distance <4 px to the fitted segment (−2,12)→(40,31) intersected with neutral-bright pixels. A 1-px neutral rim-light dilation (|r−g|,|g−b|<32, r>50, r−b<28) recovers hardware edges/rivets; components smaller than 40 px are dropped. Separate masks are restricted to x<88 (left) and x≥172 (right).

2. Static background plate

The tools and paper sweep across the table, so the pixels they uncover are reconstructed once as a single static plate, feathered into the real image:

  • Table/wall boundary: cubic fit to the topmost "brown" onset per column, excluding dilated object masks.
  • Table field: 4 robust iterations of, per row, median color + linear x-slope fit over clean table pixels (trusted strips at the bottom, left, and a narrow band behind the paper), with a luminance-residual gate that rejects green / brown-shadow bleed; rows are smoothed vertically.
  • Table grain: residual color covariance (PCA) of trusted pixels; the horizontal-streak power spectrum measured on a clean sub-window is sampled with random phases per channel and projected back through the PCA vectors, so the synthesized grain keeps the real chroma/statistics without per-channel rainbow noise.
  • Wall: quadratic regression (x, y, x², y², xy) over clean wall pixels.
  • Wall/table are blended across a 2.8-px soft edge, with faint darkening bands for the wall-side shadow and the table rim.
  • The plate replaces the real image only inside a feathered union of every frame-0 footprint, swept paper/sprite position, and shadow footprint (Gaussian σ≈1.3). Pixels never touched stay byte-identical to frame 0; the repaired strip is identical in all frames, so uncovered regions never flicker.

3. Grabber sprites

Crops SL=(0,8,88,61) and SR=(172,50,256,91) are cut from frame 0 with alpha = Gaussian blur (σ=.7) of the tool mask, cut below .18; jaw openings remain transparent. The soft 1-px border is decontaminated toward the nearest core pixel's color, darkened ×.65 (distance-transform indices, 55/45 mix) — black body edges stay dark while blue-grip and chrome-shaft edges keep their local hue; this avoids the gray "glowing outline" a component-mean decontamination produces. Each frame the sprites are alpha-pasted at integer offsets following the jaw trajectory (no deformation).

4. Paper — triangulated ruled mesh

  • Two contour polylines (far and near edges) are resampled on a shared arc-length grid via landmark-matched keypoints, so e.g. the gripped corners keep u=0 / u=1 in both the buckled source (FAR0/NEAR0) and a hand-fitted flattened destination (FARF/NEARF). A 41×7 grid interpolates between them (u across, v far→near), split into ~480 triangles.
  • A barycentric rasterizer fills, per destination pixel: source (x,y), (u,v), and interpolated vertex attributes.
  • Source texture (texsrc): frame 0 with every non-green pixel inside the silhouette painted green (notch gaps, jaw overlap), the deepest crease shadows lifted (g channel pulled halfway to 112 so transported texels can't smear near-black bands during the collapse), and a 6-px dark-green fringe outside the silhouette. Unmapped soft-alpha texels are forced to dark green, so silhouettes can never pick up wall/table colors (no brown rims).
  • Intact sheet (f0–f9): destination grid = buckled→flattened chains mixed by KFLAT (0,.15,.32,.5,.66,.8,.92,.98,1,1) plus a per-u corner translation interpolating the two jaw motions. Color blends warped source shading into a flat lit-albedo field (26,158,33)·(1.025−.05v+.012(1−u)) with weak deterministic shading waves. A Gaussian-softened alpha (σ=.55) antialiases edges.
  • Tear (f10+): the jagged cut is u*(v)=.48+.022·sin(11v+1.3)+.014·sin(23v+.5) (≈42–55 % across). Each half is rendered from the flat grid through an exact per-pixel star test, rigidly transformed about its grip: left rotates to −8° and right to +6° by f19 (p^1.2 ease), with a small sinusoidal free-edge flutter. Grip corners stay in the jaws: the pieces use the jaw q exactly at f10 (continuous with the intact taut sheet) and within .004 of it afterward. Any vertex the drag or rotation would lift past the fitted table edge is clamped to the edge, so both halves always lie on the tabletop. Torn edges get a thin, subtle dark rim (|u−u*|<.022, ×.85) suggesting paper thickness.

5. Motion and shadows

  • Jaw progress q(f): ease-in .16(f/9)² through f9 (q=.16), q10=.20 at the tear, then .20+.80·((f−10)/9)^1.4 to 1.0. Total pulls: left (−48,−13) px, right (+70,−2) px (measured from shaft/handle axes; left travel is bounded so the piece remains on the table).
  • Paper contact shadows: dilation + Gaussian of the paper mask, offset (+1,+1), table-side only, phase-dependent — broad/soft while the buckle lifts (f1–3: dil5/σ2.4/.38), tighter when flat (f4–9: 3/1.6/.42), tightest for the torn pieces (1/σ.9/.26) — plus three analytic ellipses following the tool heads/handle. Shadows multiply the plate (×(1−.24·s)) before the paper and sprites are composited.

Per frame: static plate → multiply soft shadows → alpha paper → paste both sprites. Frame 0 is the exact decoded first_frame.png (asserted np.array_equal), and generate.py never draws on it.
