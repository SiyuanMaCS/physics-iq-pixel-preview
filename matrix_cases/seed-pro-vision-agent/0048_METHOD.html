METHOD.md

Method — case_0048_attempt_01

A blue/black grabber drops a white ceramic domino into a dark-liquid-filled blue mug. All 20 frames are produced by generate.py using only local Python pixel operations on first_frame.png (NumPy / SciPy ndimage / Pillow). No network, no external models or engines, no other cases.

Frame 00 is the untouched first_frame.png (asserted pixel-identical; the saved frames/00.png is byte-identical to the input).

1. Measured geometry (from frame 0)

element parameters (px)
liquid ellipse center (121, 72), rx 25, ry 14.5
cup opening center (120, 72), rx 33, ry 26
rim outer edge center (119.5, 72.5), rx 37, ry 31
impact point P = (108, 56), on the far edge of the liquid
wave projection aspect ry/rx = 0.58
domino white/lavender pill, lean ≈ 10° in frame 0

2. Clean background plate

Every frame 1–19 starts from a single inpainted plate with the tool and domino removed:

  1. Silhouette masks. A soft domino alpha comes from a brightness/blueness threshold ((minChannel−108)/52, B>115 or bright), closed, hole-filled, kept as the largest connected component, then Gaussian-blurred (σ 0.7). The grabber mask is saturated blue (B−max(R,G)>35) plus a black-body region, closed, pruned to the prong geometry, largest component, blurred σ 0.8; bright pixels are rejected so no white leaks in.
  2. Hole. 5×5 dilation of the domino mask, 7×7 of the tool mask, plus the rim/liquid contact sliver (x 102–121, y 55–62).
  3. Wood inpainting (normalised diffusion). On a corridor bbox (x 68–162, y 0–66), each hole row is seeded by linear interpolation between clear warm-wood reference pixels, then 70 Jacobi iterations of gaussian-normalised diffusion fill it, so no zeros bleed in from outside the bbox. A harvested anisotropic grain field (gaussian σ(1.0, 3.8), amplitude ≈ 2.4 RGB, wood-warm tint) is added only in the wood zone, and the 1 px hole edge is softly blended.
  4. Mug interior. Hole pixels inside the rim ellipse are filled by mirroring about the cup axes (119.5 / 121), choosing sources on the same surface (inside/outside the liquid ellipse), with a same-row outward search fallback. The back-left rim is genuinely shadowed in the source photo, so its dark edge is preserved; the lit ring colour comes from the mirrored right side.

3. Sprites (extracted from frame 0)

Pixels inside the masks are unmixed against the plate (colour = (obs − (1−α)·plate)/α), giving soft RGBA sprites.

  • Domino (34×52 crop): holes in the silhouette are sealed opaque with the per-row clean face colour. Any opaque pixel that is not ceramic-like (luminance < 128 or warm R−B > 26) — rim/liquid contamination — is repainted with that row's face colour; contaminated fringe pixels are pulled 95 % toward it. The rounded top end, occluded by the jaw in frame 0, is synthesised by mirroring the visible rounded bottom end about the pill axis, then sealed the same way.
  • Jaws: one 38×24 crop from the strict tool mask, split at x 104 into left/right sprites (a 1 px shared column). Bright contamination is rejected before unmixing.

4. Per-frame animation

Poses are anchor/lean/blur keyframes (the anchor is the pivot on the pill axis at the tip):

f1 (109.5, 56.8, 0.2°, 0 blur) … f6 (111.2, 90.0, 2.8°, 4) …
f7 (111.6, 99.0, 3.4°, 2); from f7 on the domino is fully hidden.

The sprite is bilinear-rotated about its anchor, pasted at the pose, and alpha erased wherever it overlaps the cup-opening ellipse (the dark liquid/interior hides the submerged body); fast frames (f4–6, f7) get a weak downward motion-blur ghost. Only the part above the far rim remains visible, shrinking to the rounded top nub in f6. The two jaw sprites rise (0.4 → 19.5 px) and spread (~3 px) and are gone by f7.

Liquid effects (all in the dark-maroon family, clipped to the opening / feathered to the liquid ellipse):

  • Two ripple trains, Gaussian rings in elliptical coordinates d = sqrt(dx² + (dy/0.58)²): ring 1 radii 1.5 → 26 (f1–16), rebound ring 2 radii 1.2 → 19.8 (f7–17). Each band is shaded toward the near camera side (0.35 + 1.05·gny), applied as channel gains (1+0.62v, 1+0.40v, 1+0.34v) plus a narrow additive crest (20, 7, 5)·v so the rings stay visible on near-black liquid.
  • Entry splash: a short surface collar of maroon points around P (f2–6, radius 2.6–4), two 2–3 px flanking spikes at peak splash (f4), and four airborne droplets on parabolic arcs (f3–6, jittered apex heights, drawn before the domino so they never blend onto its white edge).
  • Bubbles: five light points drifting inside the liquid ellipse f8–15.

Composite order per frame: plate → ripples → collar/spikes/bubbles layer (opening-clipped) → droplets → domino → jaws. Frame 0 bypasses all of this.
