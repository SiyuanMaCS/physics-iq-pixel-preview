Scene interpretation

Numeric RGB inspection places the partly cropped red dispenser above a textured wooden support, its blue tap near (190,17), and the receiving glass at approximately x=162–211, y=18–105, on a dark baking tray. A red connection already exists below the nozzle, and the receiving glass appears nearly full. The continuation therefore maintains the existing pour, tops up the small inferred headroom, and introduces a narrow overflow on the right exterior that feeds a shallow puddle on the tray. The initial meniscus is an interpretation of the numeric evidence at this resolution. Measurements and the original frame-by-frame plan are in OBSERVATION.md.

Pixel synthesis

Each frame starts from the exact source RGB array. Local liquid layers are composited at four times the output resolution and reduced by area averaging. No apparatus or background geometry is regenerated.

  • The short jet is attached to the existing tap outlet and glass surface. Its radius contracts according to constant flux and a gravity-dependent speed, using r² v = constant. Moving intensity bands and a narrow highlight suggest downward advection.
  • The glass surface rises by 1.25 pixels over the first 1.65 seconds. An elliptical mask resamples the original surface texture with small wave displacements. Damped elliptical ripples, an impact depression, and subdued meniscus reflections provide liquid motion while retaining the original glass lighting.
  • At 1.65 seconds, the surface reaches the inferred lip. A thin rivulet follows the measured tapered right-hand glass contour. Its advancing wetting front uses distance = 5 age + 75 age² in pixel units. This is an analytic timing model for a film on the glass. A small leading bead stays attached to the film until it reaches the tray around 2.64 seconds.
  • Once runoff arrives, a shallow puddle grows with area proportional to elapsed arriving flow. An ellipse compressed in the tray's perspective, mildly irregular contact line, source-dependent transmission, reflected highlights, and contact ripples depict the puddle. The existing glass base occludes the part behind it.

These are deterministic analytic liquid masks, source texture warps, and RGB shading/compositing. The acceleration, residual headroom, film thickness, and flow rate are chosen locally for a plausible five-second continuation; they are not measured material properties. No network access, additional scene imagery, external model, external rendering or simulation engine, or subagent is used.
