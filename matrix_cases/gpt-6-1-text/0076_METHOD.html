OBSERVATION.md

Scene observations and event plan

Only prompt.txt and numeric RGB measurements of first_frame.png were used. No image read/view tools were used. Coordinates below are zero-based image pixels, with x increasing right and y increasing down.

Measurements

  • Input: RGB, 256 × 144. Frame 00 will be copied exactly.
  • Locked scene: a dim tabletop below an illuminated gray wall. The wall/table junction slopes from approximately (40, 114) through (100, 110) to (220, 101). The broad spotlight pool is brightest near x = 120–145, with darker borders.
  • Yellow mug: top/rim approximately x = 158–187, y = 84–92; cylindrical body approximately x = 160–187, y = 90–120. Bright yellow connected pixels occupy x = 154–187, y = 87–124 (939 pixels using a chromatic threshold). The body rotation axis is approximately x = 173, and the contact level is y = 123–124.
  • Mug handle: on the left, roughly x = 153–164, y = 94–117. Its outside edge has yellow highlights; the small aperture is dark and partly occluded by the mug body.
  • Enlarged wall shadow: dark connected pixels occupy x = 111–178, y = 47–109. The main cylindrical shadow runs approximately x = 138–172, y = 48–102. The handle loop protrudes left to approximately x = 114–141, y = 53–89; its illuminated opening lies near x = 122–132, y = 60–81. The handle shadow is several times larger than the real handle.
  • The black support/turntable and foreground lamp overlap the lower mug area. Their boundaries are approximate because they merge into dark table pixels. A white foreground light/lens region occupies x = 165–184, y = 124–136 and will be retained as fixed equipment. The black disk is rotationally symmetric, so its fixed silhouette is compatible with rotation.
  • Observed yellow body samples include (230, 195, 14) near (172, 92), (245, 212, 8) near (170, 102), with saturated white/yellow illumination on the lower front. Preserve the source texture and these lighting features.

Motion and reconstruction plan

Use local Python/NumPy/Pillow/SciPy pixel operations. Keep the original background, wall lighting, mug axis, spotlight, support silhouette, and camera fixed. Reconstruct only the areas revealed by the moving handle and its wall shadow from nearby RGB samples. Rotate a local analytic vertical handle at a constant angular velocity of 40 degrees/second clockwise viewed from above. The mug body is circular and unmarked, so preserve its image texture under rotation. Project the same handle angle into the wall-shadow loop, allowing the opening to narrow, become hidden, and appear on the opposite side. Preserve image-derived shading and use soft antialiased edges. Do not add any objects, text, camera motion, or flicker.

METHOD.md

PixelWorld-Text generation method

Final result

20 RGB frames at 256 × 144, played at 4 fps for 5 seconds. Frame 00 is an exact decoded RGB copy of first_frame.png. Frame samples are t = n/4 seconds, n = 0…19. Clockwise rotation viewed from above advances by 10° each sample (40°/s), reaching 190° in the final sample. The camera and spotlight remain fixed.

Actual pixel method

generate.py uses local NumPy, Pillow and SciPy operations, with artifact_io.py solely for output serialization. The source was inspected through numeric RGB samples, threshold/component statistics, row profiles and numeric differences. All measurements and the event plan were recorded in OBSERVATION.md before generation code was written.

The continuation uses image-derived layers and approximate analytic depth projection:

  1. Fit a fourth-degree smooth RGB wall surface to 4,324 unobstructed source-wall pixels around the shadow. Per-channel fitting errors are approximately 1.63, 1.75 and 1.86 RGB levels. Source color deficits against that surface give the handle shadow's attenuation and penumbra. Separate the protruding loop from the stationary cylindrical shadow using its measured slanted body boundary. Reconstruct only the wall pixels revealed by that moving loop.
  2. Recover the small real-handle strip from the source. Its revealed background comes from nearby source RGB samples at the same vertical level. Keep the photographed yellow ceramic texture and bright highlights as a premultiplied moving layer.
  3. Apply constant angular yaw to the vertical handle plane. The horizontal component follows cosine; the depth component follows sine and moves the far-side handle slightly upward in the camera projection. The opaque cylindrical body hides the far-side handle. It reappears on the right and starts moving toward the near side after the half turn.
  4. Project the source-derived shadow with the same angle. A depth-dependent magnification factor, 1/(1 + 0.18 sin θ), accounts approximately for its distance from the fixed lamp and wall. The large shadow loop narrows, merges into the body shadow, and opens on the right. Exclude the foreground mug from the wall receiver so its photographed lighting remains fixed.
  5. Sample the moving layers at 4 × 4 subpixel positions per output pixel with bilinear interpolation and composite over the fixed source scene. The circular body, black rotationally symmetric support silhouette, tabletop, foreground lamp and source lighting are retained. The mug handle provides the visible rotational cue. Frame 00 bypasses reconstruction and is copied exactly.

This is a source-conditioned pixel reconstruction with approximate radial-plane geometry and calibrated projection. The unmarked cylindrical body and support use rotational symmetry; the generator preserves their photographed shading rather than inventing a surface marker. Hidden background is reconstructed from this frame's neighboring pixels.

Verification

The final generator verifies all 20 PNGs pixel-for-pixel against the uint32 tensor and checks frame-00 equality. It also decodes every binary delta packet embedded in the HTML player and checks it against that same tensor.

Numerical region checks report exactly zero change in the stationary scene region, spotlight lens, and central mug lighting. Adjacent-frame mean absolute RGB differences range from 0.000479 to 0.984303 across the full frame. Lower differences occur while the handle is hidden by the rotationally symmetric cylinder. Numeric chromatic counts in the left handle region change from 121 at frame 00 to zero at frame 09; the right region grows from one source edge pixel to 116 yellow pixels at frame 18. The right wall-shadow region grows from zero dark pixels initially to 393 at frame 19.

The inspection sheets were generated as review artifacts. No image Read/View tool was used and no visual review is claimed.

Reproduce
