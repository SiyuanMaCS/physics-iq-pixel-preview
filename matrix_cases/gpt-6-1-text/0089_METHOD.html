METHOD.md

Actual generation method

This continuation uses only the supplied first_frame.png and prompt.txt. Pillow and NumPy were used to load and inspect local numeric RGB values. The photograph was never opened with an image Read/View tool. There was no external model, network request, additional reference, simulation engine, or subagent.

OBSERVATION.md was written before implementing the generator. Its measurements and initial event plan are based on RGB coordinate tables and red color-threshold spans. Container geometry is inferred from this limited evidence. In particular, the exact receiving meniscus is uncertain because the glass overlaps the dispenser and both contain red liquid. The implementation places it around y=27 initially and raises its center to y=25.1 by the last sample. The inferred glass boundary and photographic rim stay fixed.

generate.py freezes the original photographic background and composites local, analytic liquid effects:

  1. A roughly two-pixel-wide red jet descends from (153.1, 16.3) to the receiving liquid surface. Traveling highlights, slight necking, and subpixel lateral variation depict flowing liquid. The blue faucet remains stationary.
  2. The red liquid surface is an oblique ellipse, with a 17.3-pixel horizontal radius and a 2.25-pixel projected vertical radius. A local impact dimple and damped expanding wave packets respond to the pour. The imposed level rise is 0.4 pixels per second, preserving the initially high red fill visible in the supplied frame.
  3. A faint tapered refracted plume beneath the incoming jet represents local circulation. Small deterministic bubbles first follow the incoming liquid and then rise and fade near the surface. The bulk red-liquid texture is advected by less than 0.3 pixels, and masks protect the original bright glass highlights and rigid edges.
  4. The original red tray reflection receives a very small intensity change. Its position, the glass base, and all background objects remain fixed.
  5. The pouring effect develops continuously during the first 0.7 seconds, avoiding a sudden replacement of the input liquid appearance. Frame 00 is copied directly from the original RGB array; no compositing or resampling is applied to it.

The frames are rendered at four times the output resolution in each axis, using fractional masks and three equally weighted exposure samples over 1/60 second. Box reduction returns each RGB frame to 256 × 144. This is analytic pixel compositing and modest local image warping, not a full fluid simulation or a reconstructed 3D scene. All scene colors and geometric estimates originate from the single provided image. The weak flow and the small level rise are chosen so the already red glass remains below its inferred rim without overflow.

The 20 frame-start sample times are 0.00, 0.25, ..., 4.75 seconds. The standalone player holds each frame for 0.25 seconds, yielding 5 seconds at a constant 4 fps.
