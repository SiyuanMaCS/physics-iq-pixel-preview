METHOD.md

Method and verification

Created a deterministic 20-frame RGB continuation using local Python, NumPy, Pillow, and SciPy pixel operations. generate.py uses first_frame.png and prompt.txt as its only scene inputs. artifact_io.py and the provided HTML template package the generated pixels. No network, external reference, AI model, rendering engine, subagent, or image Read/View tool was used.

The scene was inspected through numeric RGB samples, color-threshold connected components, and coordinate scans. OBSERVATION.md was written before generator code, and its event plan was revised before coding after further glass-edge measurements. The narrow glass region above y≈30 joins a wider transparent body close to the liquid. That full source-derived silhouette descends 7.7 pixels, easing into a settled position by frame 10. The stationary match bundle is separated from the overlapping glass, so its heads and shafts retain their locations during the descent.

For the glass, hidden background colors are reconstructed from neighboring source strips. A source-derived transmission quotient preserves glass tint, edge contrast, irregular reflections, and photographic texture. The quotient and its feathered silhouette are sampled at fractional vertical offsets and composited over the stationary reconstructed scene. The source supplies the bowl and tabletop texture. Fine local source grain is reused in reconstructed areas. These operations are an approximate optical composite, rather than a three-dimensional simulation.

The warm flame contribution is extracted from the source by interpolating the glass colors across its columns. It flickers through small horizontal warps while burning. After the jar mouth is immersed, this contribution contracts toward the fixed match heads and fades over frames 11–15. The navy heads gradually darken toward charcoal. A faint procedural smoke filament disperses above them. A shallow elliptical red meniscus rises about 5 pixels inside the glass; sampled source liquid texture, a weak surface highlight, and a decaying ripple supply its appearance. This depicts qualitative enclosure, extinction, and liquid rise without solving combustion or fluid dynamics.

Overlapping transparent surfaces make the jar/bowl boundary uncertain from RGB values alone. The continuation follows the measured source silhouette and assumes the glass starts close to immersion. It therefore uses a short final descent. No new hand or unrelated scene object is introduced.
