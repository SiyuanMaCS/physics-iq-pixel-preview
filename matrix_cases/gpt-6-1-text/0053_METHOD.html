Scene observations from local numeric RGB inspection

No image Read/View tool is used. Measurements below come from Pillow/NumPy RGB values, scanline runs, and numeric luminance maps of first_frame.png.

  • Cradle has two top rails: rear approximately (83,6)–(169,8), front approximately (71,16)–(180,18). Four posts include foreground left at x=70–74 and foreground right near x=172–180; rear posts are near x=84–91 and x=164–170. The broad dark base spans approximately x=75–181, y=101–132, with a cast shadow below it.
  • Three resting steel balls have centers about (126,96), (142,96), (158,96) and radii about 7.4–7.8 pixels. They have dark upper reflection bands and narrow bright horizontal reflections around y=95–99.
  • The two grabbed left balls appear near (85,95.5) and (100,95.5). Their inferred resting centers are (94,96) and (110,96): this is a modest leftward amplitude of roughly 10 pixels, not a large raised swing. Preserve that measured scale.
  • Fine suspension threads are low contrast, often only 5–10 RGB levels different from the wall. Front/rear thread attachments are vertically separated by roughly 9 pixels and converge at each ball top. Their horizontal perspective offset is about 12 pixels.
  • The blue-black grabber extends from outside the left border. Upper outline runs from approximately (0,93) through (43,80) to (63,77), ending near (85,84). Its open central area exposes wood between the jaw bands. Lower outline reaches about (40,115). It overlaps the foreground left post, so the post must be restored as a fixed foreground layer during retraction.

METHOD.md

Actual generation method

This sequence was made entirely with local Python pixel operations on first_frame.png, using the scene description in prompt.txt. The input was inspected through numeric RGB scanlines, color classifications, and luminance values. No image Read/View tool was used, and no visual review is claimed. No network, reference image, external AI model, external rendering engine, or subagent was used.

Scene reconstruction

generate.py uses Pillow and NumPy for image arrays and manually measured masks. OpenCV's classical Telea inpainting fills the small regions hidden by moving balls, threads, and the grabber. The broad tabletop patch uncovered by withdrawal is refined with an illumination surface fitted to visible wood RGB values and high-frequency texture copied from another clear region of the same input. The dark base exposed behind the two left balls is filled with colors sampled from its visible center section. SciPy interpolates source pixels and smooths mask boundaries.

The fixed rails, posts, table, and defocused background are retained from the original outside the local reconstruction regions. Post masks preserve their occlusion order. A revision corrected a mask that initially retained some tool-colored pixels beside the posts; the final old upper-jaw region contains zero strong blue residue after withdrawal.

Motion and appearance

The inferred resting centers are x = 94, 110, 126, 142, 158 at y = 96.5, with steel-ball radius about 7.6 pixels. The held pair is displaced about 10 pixels to the left. This small measured amplitude is preserved.

Motion uses an analytic ideal equal-mass Newton's cradle model. The left pair first moves toward contact. At contact the two right balls take the motion, return, and transfer it back to the left pair. The middle ball remains fixed. Half-sine excursions alternate between the two end pairs, with a 1.36-second nominal pendulum period and exponential amplitude damping of 0.038 per second. The first transfer occurs at 0.34 seconds; later transfers occur every 0.68 seconds. This is an idealized collision schedule, not a contact-force simulation.

The vertical rise follows a projected pendulum arc with effective length 79.5 pixels: rise = L − sqrt(L² − horizontal_displacement²). Front and rear suspension threads are drawn as faint subpixel lines from measured attachment locations. Their two-dimensional projection is approximate.

Metallic ball radiance is resampled from a complete original resting ball, retaining the dark bands and narrow bright reflections present in the source. Small sampling offsets differentiate the spheres. The center ball's original pixel core is preserved exactly. The grabber's original textured silhouette is copied, with its occluded post regions locally inpainted; its jaws separate by a few pixels as it retracts smoothly through the left border. Five temporal samples over a 1/60-second exposure provide restrained motion blur.

Frame 00 bypasses all reconstruction and rendering and copies the original RGB pixels exactly. The wall, table, and camera have no added motion.
