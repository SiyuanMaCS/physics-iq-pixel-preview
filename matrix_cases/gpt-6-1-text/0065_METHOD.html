Actual generation technique

This animation uses deterministic local Python pixel operations. Pillow loads and saves RGB pixels; NumPy calculates masks, coordinates, and color changes; SciPy performs bilinear source-pixel interpolation. The supplied artifact_io.py writes the packed tensor, frame images, inspection sheet, and standalone HTML player. No external models, network resources, references, image-view tools, or rendering/simulation engines were used.

OBSERVATION.md was written before the generation implementation. Source inspection used numeric RGB samples, per-row red-dominant intervals, and quantized luminance values to estimate the dispenser, tap, stream, glass, and current liquid surface. All geometry is an approximate inference from the input pixels.

Frame 00 preserves the complete original RGB array and its PNG is also copied byte-for-byte from first_frame.png. Subsequent frames begin as copies of those same background pixels. A constrained liquid mask introduces the continuation of the already active pour:

  1. The dispenser's curved red-liquid boundary recedes by up to 1.45 pixels. Bilinear source sampling is restricted to its original boundary; the central tap hardware and selected glass highlights are excluded.
  2. The receiving surface is modeled as an ellipse of horizontal radius 19.5 and vertical radius 8.2 pixels. Its center rises from y=105.5 to y=92.0 at a constant rate. The old photographed surface texture moves upward through source resampling, and transport fades toward the stationary glass base. This preserves the original red color, photographic texture, and most reflections.
  3. The existing jet stays anchored near x=134 below the outlet. Small subpixel center/width variations and downward-moving brightness bands animate its core. The visible jet shortens as the receiving surface rises.
  4. Low-amplitude elliptical waves represent projected horizontal ripples around the impact point. A small dark impact dimple and a nearby warm reflection follow the liquid surface. These changes remain inside the glass; pouring continues in the final frame.

The method is image-space texture transport and analytic compositing. It does not solve three-dimensional fluid dynamics or recover a full three-dimensional scene. The receiving-level rise and dispenser recession are chosen to be visually modest and qualitatively consistent with their different sizes; they are not derived from calibrated container volumes. Source pixels provide all photographic appearance. Pixel boundaries use soft coverage to maintain smooth transitions at this small resolution.

Time and output format

There are twenty 256 x 144 RGB frames. The sampling period is 0.25 seconds (4 fps), with samples at t=0.00 through t=4.75 seconds and a five-second playback duration. The camera remains locked throughout.

  • generate.py: reproducible local generator and verification routines; run python generate.py from this workspace.
  • pixels.npz: pixels array, shape (144, 256, 20), dtype uint32. Values use 0xRRGGBB, with the frame index as the final axis.
  • frames/00.png through frames/19.png: the twenty RGB frames.
  • inspection.png: eight selected frames in a contact sheet.
  • transition_check.png: four representative destination frames and their absolute RGB differences multiplied by six.
  • index.html: standalone browser player containing the first packed frame plus lossless delta packets; plays at 4 fps with seeking and replay controls.
  • verification.json: numeric verification results and adjacent-frame change metrics.

Verification and review limits

Every saved PNG is decoded and compared against the corresponding packed tensor slice, channel by channel. Frame 00 is checked against the input pixels and original file bytes. All pixels outside the two bounded liquid action regions are checked to remain identical to the original. Twenty frames and all required output files are checked to exist. The HTML player's embedded delta packets are independently decoded in Python and compared against all twenty tensor slices.

A red-dominant pixel count in the receiving region, excluding the incoming jet, increases on every successive frame. Adjacent-frame mean absolute RGB changes remain small and confined to the intended action regions. The image contact sheets are provided as artifacts for review. This agent performed numeric RGB/array inspection only and does not claim visual review or a browser playback review.
