Numeric scene observation

Coordinates use x rightward and y downward, with (0, 0) at the upper left. The shot is fixed. The upper background is gray, typically RGB (130–145, 128–143, 130–144) on the left. The lower foreground has a dark brown surface. The pale, textured pillow occupies roughly x25–255, y54–134, with its brighter left edge around x40–60. Its right side is darker. The precise outline is uncertain from numeric inspection; the source pixels will be retained except near the load.

The black kettlebell has a connected dark silhouette at x77–121, y0–59 (1,792 pixels below mean RGB 65). Its handle aperture is approximately x86–112, y8–23. The handle is cropped at the top of the frame; its missing top will be completed locally when it enters the frame. The rounded body is roughly x85–121, y30–59, with its bottom around (102, 59). Source metal texture and highlights will be copied.

The green paper has 1,054 strongly green pixels spanning x175–203, y14–53. Most interior pixels are near RGB (3–5, 95–105, 11–16). Its initial projected corners are approximately (176,14), (201,17), (203,53), (175,53), so it is initially nearly upright. A narrow blue holding detail is visible at x183–190, y0–18 (99 strongly blue pixels). This is treated as the already present fingertip/holder; its full form is not inferred or added to the scene.

The kettlebell will meet the left pillow surface with its bottom near y80. The paper's lower edge will meet the right surface near y80 at the same frame. The 30-lb load will sink about 11 pixels farther at rest (about 12 pixels at peak) as the pillow compresses, while the sheet rotates onto the surface without a substantial depression. No camera motion, cuts, text overlays, added equipment, or unrelated actions are planned.

METHOD.md

Actual generation method

This is a deterministic local 2.5D source-pixel animation. generate.py uses Pillow, NumPy, SciPy array operations, and OpenCV affine/perspective sampling. No network, other scene inputs, external models, image viewing tools, physics engines, or subagents were used. The source was inspected through numeric RGB values, connected-component bounds, row spans, and block luminance values. The supplied prompt determines the action. OBSERVATION.md was written before the animation code.

The source scene and photographed textures remain the basis of every frame. A measured silhouette separates the black kettlebell from its gray background, retaining its handle aperture and highlights. Contour pixels are unmixed from the original background before translation. The small part of the handle cropped above the original image is completed with an elliptical arch and sampled dark source texture as it enters the shot. The green sheet is extracted into an antialiased quadrilateral. Green source texture is interpolated beneath its holder's small occlusion.

Vacated object regions are repaired using horizontal interpolation between nearby source pixels on the same row, with narrow feathered boundaries. A small green cast beneath the original paper is locally neutralized. This reconstruction is an estimate of the hidden background, not a recovered photograph.

Both objects descend on synchronized smoothstep placement curves. Their different original gaps require a 21-pixel downward kettle translation and a 27-pixel paper translation to reach their respective pillow landing areas simultaneously in frame 07, at 1.75 seconds. The kettlebell then depresses the photographed pillow with an inverse displacement field localized below it. The deformation reaches 12.3 pixels at peak and settles at 11.35 pixels. Local hollow shading, contact shadows, and two short crease shading fields make the load's effect visible while retaining the original fabric detail. The deformation is prescribed analytically; it is not a calibrated material simulation.

The paper tips around its lower edge with a perspective warp from its upright projection into a roughly 31 × 14-pixel footprint on the right pillow surface. A small final flattening motion ends in frame 13. It has a weak edge shadow and no meaningful pillow deformation. The already visible narrow blue/dark holder descends with the paper, releases, and withdraws through the upper image edge. The initially cropped stem is continued by copying its top-row pixels. Its appearance beyond the visible source is an approximation.

The camera and all pixels outside local object/interaction regions stay fixed. Frames 16–19 are identical to provide a stable final comparison. Frame 00 bypasses all processing and is an exact copy of the source RGB array.

Revision and numeric inspection

The first generated sequence revealed green fragments during holder withdrawal: the initial holder mask included overlapping paper pixels. The mask was revised to retain only blue or neutral dark tip pixels, and its feathered colors were filled from the nearest valid holder pixels. The regenerated sequence passes a check that green pixels remain within the paper's projected vertical bounds throughout withdrawal. No image tool was used, so neither these revisions nor the final contact sheets are claimed to have received visual review.
