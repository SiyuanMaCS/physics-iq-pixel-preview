OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Measured source scene

  • Source: first_frame.png, RGB, 256 x 144.
  • Camera: fixed, eye-level/slightly elevated view; no crop, zoom, pan, or exposure change is called for.
  • Background: neutral gray wall on the left; a dark vertical mirror/frame strip at roughly x=145..166; the mirror occupies the right side. The wooden tabletop begins near (36,132), rises toward (137,83), and fills the lower-right.
  • Main teapot: approximately x=133..181, y=41..95. Its blue globular body is centered close to (155,70), with a small lid/top above it. The warm red appendages extend toward both sides, most prominently the loop handle on the right. Yellow/red floral marks provide useful rotation cues.
  • Turntable: fixed black elliptical base, approximately x=137..180, y=89..111, centered near (159,99). Only the teapot above the turntable rotates.
  • Mirror image: approximately x=196..249, y=36..64, with the reflected stand beneath it near x=210..240, y=61..79. It is smaller, darker, horizontally reversed, and displaced up/right relative to the real object because the mirror plane is angled in perspective.
  • Illumination: soft light from upper-left. Source shadows and table/wall texture must remain fixed.
  • A thin dark power cord exits the real turntable toward the right; it remains stationary.

Motion model

The teapot rotates steadily clockwise about its vertical axis for one complete revolution over the 20-frame, five-second shot. The body/lid silhouettes remain stable while their painted texture travels around analytic spherical/cylindrical row profiles. The handle and spout orbit the axis with depth-dependent horizontal foreshortening, shading, and occlusion. The mirror copy is derived from the same animated foreground state, horizontally reversed and perspective-scaled, so its motion is synchronized and optically opposite on screen. The platform, cord, tabletop, wall, mirror frame, lighting, and camera stay fixed.

METHOD.md

Method

This sequence is generated entirely with deterministic local Python pixel operations. No external images, models, masks, engines, or network resources are used.

generate.py loads the supplied RGB source and preserves frame 00 byte-for-byte. For later frames it locally inpaints only the two measured teapot silhouettes to recover a clean stationary plate, leaving the black platforms, cord, wall, mirror frame, tabletop, shadows, and every pixel outside the two motion neighborhoods fixed.

The blue body and lid are modeled as convex row profiles. The broad source-view handle overlap on the right half of the body is replaced by the symmetric painted-body sample because that handle is rendered separately; neutral backdrop pixels caught at the analytic silhouette edge are then replaced row-wise by the nearest connected painted-ceramic pixel. These two local repairs prevent either the handle or room from being stretched across the pot at a quarter turn. For every output row, the script maps screen x to an analytic surface longitude, advances that longitude by 18 degrees per frame, and resamples the original painted pixels. A fixed screen-space lighting envelope compensates for the lighting already baked into sampled pixels, preserving the upper-left illumination while floral and colored details travel around the curved body. The red spout/handle pixels and their connected dark outlines are extracted from the source with deterministic color masks. They orbit the vertical axis with cosine horizontal motion, edge-on foreshortening, mild depth shading, horizontal reversal on the opposite side, and explicit back/body/front compositing order.

The reflected teapot is handled from its own darker, smaller measured source regions rather than copying unrelated scenery. Its curved texture uses the same physical phase, and its measured left/right appendages move in reversed screen geometry, preserving the mirror relationship. The reflected stand remains fixed.

artifact_io.save packs the final uint8 RGB sequence into the required uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, writes all 20 PNG frames, builds inspection.png, and embeds the exact delta stream in index.html. transition_check.png is a programmatically generated diagnostic sheet of selected frames labeled with consecutive changed-pixel counts. Verification checks shape, dtype, exact source equality for frame 00, nonzero smooth motion in every transition, locked pixels outside the measured moving regions, and PNG/tensor equality.
