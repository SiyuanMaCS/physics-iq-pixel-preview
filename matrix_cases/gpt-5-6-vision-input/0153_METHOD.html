OBSERVATION.md

Observation and event plan

Source-frame measurements

  • The source is an RGB image at exactly 256 x 144 pixels. The camera is locked off, with a light, horizontally grained wooden tabletop filling the foreground and a darker olive/brown background above the table's rear edge near y=10.
  • A black cylindrical tube enters from the left. Its dark opening/body occupies approximately x=0..29, y=28..58 and points horizontally toward the right.
  • The moving brown tennis ball is already outside the tube. Its visible body is approximately x=93..111, y=41..59, centered near (102, 50), with a nominal radius of 9-10 pixels. It has a soft contact/shadow region and slight source motion softness.
  • The olive green fabric box occupies the right side. Its leading/lower-left outline slopes from about (222, 0) through (204, 57) to (216, 97). The collision surface relevant to the ball is near x=204 at y=50; therefore the ball center should not advance past about x=195 at impact.
  • The box bottom rests around y=97. The ball's path and table-contact height remain essentially constant at center y=50, matching a straight roll from the tube.
  • Numerically, the strongest vertical image gradients occur at x=201..206 (box edge) and x=26..29 (tube rim); the strongest horizontal scene gradients include y=9..11 (rear edge), y=40..58 (ball/tube), and y=95..97 (box bottom). These fixed structures must stay unchanged except for a very small local fabric response at impact.

METHOD.md

Method

I generated the continuation deterministically with local Python pixel operations (numpy, OpenCV, and Pillow). No external images, masks, models, engines, network access, or other cases were used.

  1. frames/00.png is the RGB source array copied directly from first_frame.png.
  2. I reconstructed a clean tabletop plate beneath the source ball with a tightly bounded elliptical OpenCV Telea inpaint. A seeded, very low-amplitude residual sampled from untouched same-row tabletop pixels prevents the filled area from becoming unnaturally flat.
  3. I isolated the genuine source ball colors with a feathered ellipse and premultiplied alpha. Thus the moving ball retains the input's actual brown shading, softness, and edge colors rather than being redrawn. Its brightness adapts mildly to the locally measured tabletop illumination so it remains brown, but darkens naturally as it nears the box's shadow.
  4. I moved the sprite on the measured horizontal line using subpixel affine transforms. A compact analytic contact shadow remains aligned with the table illumination. At frame 10 the sprite is horizontally compressed and slightly vertically expanded while its front edge stops at the measured box edge.
  5. A local sub-two-pixel remap around the box's leading fabric edge supplies a restrained contact response at frames 10-11. The ball then rebounds with decreasing displacement and settles for the last two frames.
  6. artifact_io.save packs every frame into the required uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, writes the 20 PNGs, creates inspection.png, and embeds identical packed pixels in index.html. transition_check.png contains selected adjacent pairs and amplified absolute-difference maps for numerical transition inspection.

Review was performed through array statistics, object/difference bounding boxes, contact geometry, temporal pixel-change counts, and exact file/tensor comparisons. I did not use an image viewing tool on generated outputs.
