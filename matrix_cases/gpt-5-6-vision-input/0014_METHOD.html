ball-in-basket · 生成方法

The continuation is generated deterministically by generate.py using only first_frame.png, NumPy, Pillow, and local OpenCV pixel operations.

  1. The supplied first frame is loaded as RGB and copied unchanged to output frame 00.
  2. A clean static plate is reconstructed behind the starting ball. The wall is smooth, so hidden wall pixels are filled by a per-row quadratic fit to unaffected wall samples on the left and right. A tiny top-edge release remnant is removed the same way. Only the already-visible orange fragments in the crate's upper central slots are locally inpainted to recover the dark, empty interior; the wooden table is excluded from this mask.
  3. The real photographed basketball texture is reused. For each later frame, inverse coordinate mapping translates and slightly scales the source sphere, retaining its original orange shading, highlight, and dark seams. A soft analytic ellipse supplies subpixel antialiasing.
  4. The crate remains fixed. Above its rear rim, the warped ball is composited normally. Below the rim, it is darkened and composited only through analytic slot and diamond-opening masks aligned to the photographed lattice. The untouched photographed crate pixels therefore remain in front and provide consistent occlusion.
  5. The center trajectory is an explicitly damped gravity/bounce path: acceleration into the crate, a first squash at frame 05, a smaller rebound and impact at frame 10, then short residual bounces and settlement. Horizontal scale increases and vertical scale decreases briefly at contacts to model an inflatable ball.
  6. artifact_io.save writes the uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, all 20 PNGs, the inspection contact sheet, and the standalone player. transition_check.png is a separately generated labeled sheet of 3.5x-amplified absolute differences between each consecutive frame.

Verification

  • Dimensions/type: 20 frames, 256 x 144, RGB uint8.
  • Frame 00 equals first_frame.png exactly.
  • Every PNG was unpacked and compared exactly against the saved (144, 256, 20) uint32 tensor.
  • Per-frame color-mask measurements show the ball's visible weighted y-centroid following the planned fall, rebound, and settlement while remaining centered near x=133.
  • Consecutive-frame changed-pixel bounds remain localized to the ball/crate action area; the camera, wall outside the revealed patch, tabletop, and scene edges remain static.
  • Later outputs were inspected only with local code, array statistics, color masks, bounding boxes, and difference measurements. No image Read/View tool was used on generated frames, inspection.png, or transition_check.png.
