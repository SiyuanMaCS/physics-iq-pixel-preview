Generation

The continuation is produced entirely with deterministic local Python/NumPy/Pillow pixel operations in generate.py; no external models, engines, images, masks, or network resources are used.

Frame 00 is the original first_frame.png loaded as RGB and copied without alteration. For frames 01-19, the source photograph is inverse-warped only around the burning left flame. The warp pivots about the measured wick position at approximately (131.2, 29.4), so the base of the flame remains attached while the tip receives a small time-varying lean. Independent height and width factors produce subtle narrowing and elongation. All sampling is bilinear, and the edit is feathered across the surrounding wall halo to avoid hard patch boundaries.

A broad Gaussian illumination term centered on the left flame adds or subtracts at most roughly one output code value across the nearby wall, synchronized to flame strength. No procedural object is drawn over the photograph: the visible flame colors and texture come from the supplied photographic flame itself. Pixels below y=56, left of x=88, and right of x=173 are invariant across all frames, which keeps the unlit right candle, both holders, table, framing, and camera fully locked.

The explicit low-amplitude motion samples were selected to form several irregular flicker cycles rather than a mechanically repeated loop. The sequence is 20 frames at 4 fps, representing 5 seconds.

Output and verification

artifact_io.save writes the RGB PNG sequence, the (144, 256, 20) uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, contact sheet, and standalone player. generate.py also creates transition_check.png, whose panels are magnified frame-difference heat maps annotated with changed-pixel counts.

Automated checks performed after writing assert:

  • exactly 20 frames of shape 144 x 256 x 3 and dtype uint8;
  • frame 00 is pixel-identical to first_frame.png and its written PNG SHA-256 is 6a1a4ae8c14ea8f00ac877f520607dbc01b2362fc023a489fcdae1b8de99c742;
  • every written PNG is pixel-identical to its in-memory frame;
  • every packed value in pixels.npz equals the corresponding PNG RGB value;
  • all lower-scene and right-candle pixels remain unchanged;
  • the union of changed pixels is confined to x=98..162, y=0..48;
  • the measured bright-flame tip varies from y=13 to y=16 and its horizontal centroid stays within x=130.25..131.29.

Later generated frames were assessed only by these local numeric, tensor, bounding-box, and temporal-difference checks. They were not opened with an image viewing tool.
