+def bilinear_sample(image, sx, sy):
+    """Bilinearly sample an RGB uint8 image at floating point coordinates."""
+    x0 = np.floor(sx).astype(np.int32)
+    y0 = np.floor(sy).astype(np.int32)
+    x1 = np.clip(x0 + 1, 0, W - 1)
+    y1 = np.clip(y0 + 1, 0, H - 1)
+    x0 = np.clip(x0, 0, W - 1)
+    y0 = np.clip(y0, 0, H - 1)
+    fx = (sx - x0)[..., None]
+    fy = (sy - y0)[..., None]
+    src = image.astype(np.float32)
+    top = src[y0, x0] * (1.0 - fx) + src[y0, x1] * fx
+    bot = src[y1, x0] * (1.0 - fx) + src[y1, x1] * fx
+    return top * (1.0 - fy) + bot * fy
+
+
+def smoothstep(edge0, edge1, value):
+    q = np.clip((value - edge0) / (edge1 - edge0), 0.0, 1.0)
+    return q * q * (3.0 - 2.0 * q)
+

OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Measured scene

  • The source is an RGB image measuring 256 x 144 pixels. The camera is locked off in a landscape, medium-wide composition.
  • A pale gray wall fills the background. A warm brown wooden tabletop occupies the lower portion, with a slightly diagonal wall/table boundary caused by perspective.
  • The left brass candlestick is centered near x=130. Its red candle spans approximately x=125..137 and y=30..106. Its flame occupies roughly x=121..137 and y=13..30, with the brightest core concentrated near x=130, y=21.
  • The right brass candlestick is centered near x=190. Its red candle spans approximately x=186..197 and y=31..99. It is unlit; its dark wick/top must remain unchanged.
  • Both holders, the table, wall, framing, exposure, and shadows remain fixed. The only physically motivated motion is the burning left flame and a very faint corresponding warm-light fluctuation immediately around it.
  • Motion should stay sub-pixel to a few pixels in amplitude: a candle flame may narrow, lean, shorten, and recover, but it must remain attached to the same wick. No smoke, melting, camera motion, new object, or human action is introduced during this short interval.

20-frame event plan at 4 fps

  • Frame 00 (0.00 s): exact supplied source frame.
  • Frames 01-03 (0.25-0.75 s): flame narrows slightly, leans about one pixel right, then straightens; local glow breathes down and back up.
  • Frames 04-06 (1.00-1.50 s): tip leans left while the flame briefly shortens, with its base fixed on the wick.
  • Frames 07-09 (1.75-2.25 s): flame grows a little taller and brighter, passing through an upright pose into a small rightward lean.
  • Frames 10-12 (2.50-3.00 s): a gentle dip in height and brightness; tip wavers left while the lower flame stays stable.
  • Frames 13-15 (3.25-3.75 s): flame recovers, elongates, and becomes nearly upright; the immediate warm halo rises subtly.
  • Frames 16-18 (4.00-4.50 s): one final low-amplitude right-left flicker with no displacement of the wick or candle.
  • Frame 19 (4.75 s): flame settles close to, but not identically on, the opening pose so the continuation remains natural rather than mechanically looped.

All changes after frame 00 are confined to a small region around the left flame and its weak light halo; every other scene pixel remains locked.

METHOD.md

Method

Generation

The continuation is produced entirely with deterministic local Python/NumPy/Pillow pixel operations in generate.py; no external models, engines, images, masks, or network resources are used.

Frame 00 is the original first_frame.png loaded as RGB and copied without alteration. For frames 01-19, the source photograph is inverse-warped only around the burning left flame. The warp pivots about the measured wick position at approximately (131.2, 29.4), so the base of the flame remains attached while the tip receives a small time-varying lean. Independent height and width factors produce subtle narrowing and elongation. All sampling is bilinear, and the edit is feathered across the surrounding wall halo to avoid hard patch boundaries.

A broad Gaussian illumination term centered on the left flame adds or subtracts at most roughly one output code value across the nearby wall, synchronized to flame strength. No procedural object is drawn over the photograph: the visible flame colors and texture come from the supplied photographic flame itself. Pixels below y=56, left of x=88, and right of x=173 are invariant across all frames, which keeps the unlit right candle, both holders, table, framing, and camera fully locked.

The explicit low-amplitude motion samples were selected to form several irregular flicker cycles rather than a mechanically repeated loop. The sequence is 20 frames at 4 fps, representing 5 seconds.

Output and verification

artifact_io.save writes the RGB PNG sequence, the (144, 256, 20) uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, contact sheet, and standalone player. generate.py also creates transition_check.png, whose panels are magnified frame-difference heat maps annotated with changed-pixel counts.

Automated checks performed after writing assert:

  • exactly 20 frames of shape 144 x 256 x 3 and dtype uint8;
  • frame 00 is pixel-identical to first_frame.png and its written PNG SHA-256 is 6a1a4ae8c14ea8f00ac877f520607dbc01b2362fc023a489fcdae1b8de99c742;
  • every written PNG is pixel-identical to its in-memory frame;
  • every packed value in pixels.npz equals the corresponding PNG RGB value;
  • all lower-scene and right-candle pixels remain unchanged;
  • the union of changed pixels is confined to x=98..162, y=0..48;
  • the measured bright-flame tip varies from y=13 to y=16 and its horizontal centroid stays within x=130.25..131.29.

Later generated frames were assessed only by these local numeric, tensor, bounding-box, and temporal-difference checks. They were not opened with an image viewing tool.
