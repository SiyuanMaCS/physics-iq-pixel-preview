OBSERVATION.md

Observation and event plan

Measured scene

  • Source: first_frame.png, 256 x 144 RGB. The camera is locked in a wide, eye-level view with a neutral gray backdrop and a dark wooden tabletop along the bottom.
  • Left pillow: approximately x=24..170, y=50..137. It is pale gray, plump, and slightly forward of the right pillow. Its dark piped lower edge and folds must remain stable except near the load.
  • Right pillow: approximately x=126..255, y=48..124. It overlaps behind the left pillow and extends out of frame at right.
  • 30-lb black kettlebell: approximately x=77..123, y=0..60, centered near x=101. Its lower body is already at first contact with the left pillow near y=55. The handle is partly clipped by the top frame.
  • Green paper: saturated green region approximately x=175..202, y=14..53, centered near x=189. A narrow blue/dark holder rises to the top edge. The paper's bottom is at first contact with the right pillow.
  • Illumination is soft and diffuse from upper left. No camera movement, hands, new objects, text, or cuts are called for.

Physical interpretation

Frame 00 shows the instant both items first meet their respective pillows. During load transfer, the kettlebell settles several pixels and creates a smooth, broad basin in the soft filling. The very light paper settles only about one pixel and leaves the right pillow essentially unchanged. A small damped rebound follows; the two motions share the same start time.

20-frame event plan (4 fps)

  • 00 (0.00 s): exact source frame; simultaneous first contact.
  • 01-02 (0.25-0.50 s): both begin loading. Kettlebell and left surface move down visibly; paper starts a minute descent.
  • 03-05 (0.75-1.25 s): kettlebell reaches maximum compression, with a wide curved depression and subtle side bulging. Paper reaches its shallow final support height without appreciable pillow compression.
  • 06-09 (1.50-2.25 s): first damped rebound; kettlebell rises slightly and returns. Paper has a subpixel-scale visual settle only.
  • 10-13 (2.50-3.25 s): smaller second oscillation in the heavy pillow/load system.
  • 14-16 (3.50-4.00 s): motion decays to the supported positions.
  • 17-19 (4.25-4.75 s): stable final scientific comparison: deep left indentation versus nearly unchanged right pillow.

Continuity targets

  • Frame 00 must be byte-for-byte identical to the input.
  • Changes remain localized around x=35..165, y=0..137 for the kettlebell/left pillow and x=166..213, y=0..126 for the paper/holder and its tiny contact response.
  • Background, tabletop, pillow seams away from the contact zones, exposure, and color remain fixed.

METHOD.md

Method

Generation

The animation is a deterministic local pixel continuation implemented in generate.py with NumPy, Pillow, and OpenCV. No external assets, models, engines, masks, or network resources were used.

  1. The source RGB frame is loaded without rescaling. Frame 00 is copied directly from it.
  2. The kettlebell is isolated with a luminance-based soft matte constrained by measured handle/body geometry. The green paper and narrow holder use constrained green/blue/dark chroma masks. Soft alpha edges retain the source anti-aliasing.
  3. The two source silhouettes are removed from a working background with a small, local Telea inpaint. This reveals only the backdrop/pillow material needed when each object settles downward.
  4. Pillow response is made by analytic inverse remapping of the original texture. The kettlebell drives a broad super-Gaussian depression whose displacement decays into the pillow depth, plus a very small lateral bulge and bounded contact shadow. The paper has a separate narrow response only 0.28 times its roughly one-pixel descent. All displacement maps are exactly zero outside measured pillow regions.
  5. The kettlebell and paper are translated as source-pixel layers. A supersampled graphite-colored elliptical cap reconstructs the small part of the kettlebell handle that was above the original top edge and becomes exposed as the bell moves down.
  6. A hand-authored displacement schedule gives simultaneous load onset, one main overshoot, two progressively smaller corrections, and a stable end. The kettlebell peaks at 10.6 px of descent and rests at 9.2 px; the paper peaks at 1.14 px and rests at 1.0 px.
  7. artifact_io.save writes the 20 RGB PNGs, uint32 packed tensor, inspection contact sheet, and standalone delta-encoded player. transition_check.png is a four-panel numerical temporal-difference heatmap for transitions 00->01, 03->04, 07->08, and 18->19.

Verification

Verification was performed with local code and numerical checks only; later rendered images were not opened with image Read/View tools.

  • Output array: (20, 144, 256, 3), uint8, RGB.
  • frames/00.png is byte-for-byte identical to first_frame.png, and its decoded pixels are exactly equal.
  • pixels.npz contains pixels with shape (144, 256, 20) and dtype uint32 in 0xRRGGBB layout.
  • Repacking every PNG reproduces the NPZ tensor exactly.
  • The final frame differs from the source at 13,870 pixels, with zero changed pixels outside the bounded x=35..213, y=0..137 action region.
  • Constrained photometric centroids move downward from y=30.789 to y=36.055 for the kettlebell and from y=34.513 to y=35.541 for the green paper.
  • Motion begins in frame 01 for both objects, decays through frame 17, and frames 17, 18, and 19 are identical for a stable ending.
