Observation and event plan

Scene measurements from the supplied first frame

  • Canvas: 256 x 144 RGB, fixed camera.
  • Background/table boundary: the pale wall meets the wood tabletop at about y=75 on the left and y=61 on the right. These pixels must remain static.
  • Dispenser: ribbed clear-glass reservoir occupies approximately x=72..171, y=0..72. Its woven round stand/basket occupies x=43..171, y=48..130.
  • Tap assembly: dark handle/body is concentrated around x=132..159, y=34..61. The small outlet is above the receiving glass near x=159, y=58..64.
  • Receiving glass: outer silhouette is approximately x=139..181, y=62..138. The narrow mouth is x=149..171, y=62..70; shoulders widen through y=70..80; the body interior is about x=141..178 below y=81.
  • Existing grapefruit juice is orange/coral and visually begins around y=84..89, continuing to the bottom near y=134. Strong core samples around x=154..172, y=102..126 are roughly RGB (132..148, 56..72, 11..34). The outline and specular glass highlights must remain visible over the animated liquid.
  • The only intended motion region is the tap-to-glass gap and the inside of the receiving glass, approximately x=140..179, y=61..135. Everything else remains copied exactly from frame 00.

20-frame event plan (4 fps, 5 seconds)

  • Frame 00 (0.25 s): exact source frame, before the visible water stream begins.
  • Frames 01-03 (0.50-1.00 s): a narrow clear stream appears beneath the already-open outlet and reaches the juice. A small impact glint/ripple appears. The level starts to rise.
  • Frames 04-08 (1.25-2.25 s): continuous slightly wavering water stream; the liquid surface climbs steadily. Subtle moving ripple arcs and a few small bubbles show mixing.
  • Frames 09-14 (2.50-3.75 s): the glass becomes visibly fuller. Added water slightly lightens/desaturates the orange juice from the surface downward, with a soft turbulent plume below the impact point.
  • Frames 15-18 (4.00-4.75 s): the level approaches the lower neck/shoulder of the glass while the stream remains continuous; ripples diminish slightly as the available headroom shrinks.
  • Frame 19 (5.00 s): final full state, surface near y=73, still safely below the lip; clear stream, small surface ripple, and diluted grapefruit color remain visible.

The motion is intentionally continuous and modest: no camera movement, cuts, object deformation, hand, or new apparatus.

METHOD.md

Method

Actual generation method

The animation is made entirely with deterministic local Python pixel operations using NumPy and Pillow. No network access, external assets, external models, masks, references, render engines, or other cases were used.

  1. first_frame.png is loaded as an RGB NumPy array. Frame 00 is a direct unmodified array copy.
  2. A hand-measured antialiased polygon, inset from the receiving glass outline, defines only the glass interior. The polygon is rasterized at 4x resolution and reduced with Lanczos filtering so its edge does not look jagged.
  3. For frames 01-19, a smoothstep curve raises the modeled liquid surface from y=88 to y=73. The new liquid band is produced by translucent orange/coral pixel compositing over the source texture. Tint strength increases gently with depth.
  4. Existing source highlights are measured from local luminance/chroma and protected from most of the tint, retaining the original glass reflections. A low-opacity, horizontally localized plume lightens the original juice beneath the impact point to suggest water mixing into grapefruit juice.
  5. The falling water is a narrow, slightly wavering, translucent blue-gray quadrilateral with a subpixel white refractive edge. It runs from the fixed tap outlet to the current liquid surface.
  6. The impact is drawn with an animated meniscus, small ripple arcs, a soft impact glint, and four deterministic transient bubble outlines. These are all drawn at 4x resolution and downsampled for natural antialiasing.
  7. Every frame begins from the unchanged source. Therefore no cumulative warping or accidental background drift occurs. Modified pixels are limited to the tap-to-glass gap and receiving-glass interior.

Output and verification

  • 20 RGB frames, each 256 x 144, were generated at the requested 4 fps / 5 second duration.
  • artifact_io.save writes the PNG sequence, packed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, inspection sheet, and standalone player.
  • transition_check.png contains selected frames and 5x-amplified consecutive-difference panels, generated for offline inspection.
  • The generator reloads all 20 PNGs and asserts pixel-for-pixel agreement with the in-memory frames.
  • It independently reconstructs the packed uint32 tensor and asserts exact agreement with pixels.npz.
  • Frame 00 is asserted exactly equal to first_frame.png.
  • Numeric review found the union of changed pixels at x=140..178, y=57..135; pixels outside the intended local action region have zero difference from the source.

The supplied first frame was the only visually observed image. Generated/later output frames were not opened with an image viewing tool; they were evaluated through local code, array statistics, bounds, hashes, and exact pixel comparisons only.
