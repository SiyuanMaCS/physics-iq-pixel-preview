This continuation is made entirely with deterministic local Python pixel operations using NumPy, Pillow, and OpenCV. No network access, external assets, external models, masks, engines, or other cases were used.

Construction

  1. first_frame.png is loaded as RGB. Frame 00 is copied directly and is never resampled.
  2. The tangerine is isolated inside its measured 62 x 62 local rectangle with seeded GrabCut. Only the largest connected component is retained (2,123 pixels). The measured knife axis, x = 132 - 0.12*y, divides that component into the photographed left and right fruit portions while excluding the narrow blade seam.
  3. A clean plate is needed only where a few board pixels become exposed. It is reconstructed from same-row samples of the visible glass immediately to the left and right of the fruit. The original blade corridor is filled from its immediate horizontal neighbors. Pixels outside the compact action region stay equal to the source.
  4. Both real fruit textures are moved with eased affine transforms. During the downstroke they compress vertically by at most 2.4%. After the blade reaches the board they release, slide about 3–4 pixels outward, rotate about 1.5–1.75 degrees in opposite directions, and settle by roughly half a pixel. A small analytic soft shadow follows each portion.
  5. The edge-on knife is not redrawn as a new object. Its narrow photographic strip is locally remapped along the measured axis. It travels 2.8 pixels along the downstroke, rocks by less than one pixel, and receives a tiny cool contact glint where it meets the board.
  6. Motions use continuous smoothstep curves plus a damped fractional rebound. The final four transitions retain a 0.16-pixel blade relaxation to avoid an abrupt early freeze.

Output and verification

artifact_io.save packs RGB as uint32 0xRRGGBB, writes pixels.npz, the 20 frame PNGs, the inspection contact sheet, and the self-contained HTML player. generate.py additionally creates transition_check.png, an adjacent-frame difference contact sheet.

The generator checks:

  • tensor shape (144, 256, 20) and dtype uint32;
  • exact equality of frame 00 and first_frame.png;
  • exact equality of every decoded PNG to its tensor plane;
  • nonzero but localized motion in every adjacent transition;
  • a compact all-change bounding box, measured as x=89..164/y=0..115 in the generated sequence.

The generated frames were inspected only through local code and numeric statistics, as requested. No image Read/View tool was used on later outputs.
