marble-run-x · 生成方法

Generation

generate.py is a deterministic local pixel compositor. It loads first_frame.png as an immutable locked-camera background and generates a 20-point, hand-measured screen-space trajectory through the colored run elements. Frame 00 is the unmodified supplied RGB array.

For frames 01–19, the script draws only:

  1. a soft shadow offset down/right on the board;
  2. a short capped exposure trail when displacement is large;
  3. a 4.35-pixel-radius yellow-green glass marble.

The marble is rendered at 4× resolution and Lanczos-downsampled. Its body uses an analytic sphere-thickness field to mix a yellow-green tint with locally sampled source pixels, producing restrained transparency/refraction. A dark olive rim, fixed upper-left specular highlight, lower warm caustic, and rotating amber inclusion supply depth and rolling cues. Landing frames briefly squash the sphere. Airborne frames use a slightly wider, softer shadow.

The path follows the upper purple rail leftward, turns in the purple catcher, moves rightward to the blue rail, rolls left along blue, falls to the left end of green, rolls right along green, and finally drops to and moves off the yellow rail. All motion is composited over the original frame; there is no camera movement, global redraw, external image, model, engine, or network input.

Outputs and verification

artifact_io.save writes the PNG sequence, the packed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, inspection.png, and the standalone HTML player. generate.py additionally creates transition_check.png, containing four before/after pairs and 4× absolute RGB differences.

Verification performed by the script:

  • shape is exactly (20, 144, 256, 3) and dtype is uint8;
  • frame 00 equals first_frame.png pixel-for-pixel;
  • every saved PNG decodes to its generated RGB frame;
  • pixels.npz has shape (144, 256, 20), dtype uint32, and exactly matches all PNG pixels after 0xRRGGBB packing.

Generated frames were assessed only with local numeric checks, per the task constraint. Those checks measured per-frame changed-pixel counts, change bounds, difference-weighted centroids, peak deltas, yellow-pixel counts, frame-to-frame mean absolute differences, and the union of all changed locations. No image viewer or image-reading tool was used on later outputs.
