Actual pixel method

The animation is a deterministic local Python/OpenCV/Pillow render based only on first_frame.png and prompt.txt.

  1. The input is loaded as a 256×144 RGB array. Frame 00 is kept as an unchanged array copy.
  2. The source ball was measured at centroid (72.7, 51.4) with an approximately 19×16 px visible outline. A 31×31 local source patch and a soft elliptical alpha matte were extracted there.
  3. The original ball and its contact shadow were removed for frames 01–19 with a small elliptical OpenCV Telea inpaint. The result was blended with per-row color interpolation to maintain the table's horizontal shading. Because numeric gradient checks found the first result slightly too smooth, high-frequency-only wood grain from an unobstructed patch 28 px to the right at the same rows was feathered into the center of the repair. Broad color and shading from that donor patch were not copied.
  4. Within the ball patch, normalized Gaussian convolution separated smooth illumination from fine surface residual. Only the fine residual rotates. The broad highlight/shading remains fixed relative to the scene lighting.
  5. The center translates horizontally by 9.8 px per frame at constant y=51.4. Fine texture rotates clockwise by distance / 8.7 px, implementing rolling arc length. A small Gaussian-softened multiply shadow is composited immediately below the ball before its antialiased matte is placed.
  6. The ball reaches the right boundary in frame 18 and is mostly outside the image in frame 19. The pipe, red ball, camera, and all pixels outside the narrow swept band remain fixed.

Revision and verification

  • Revision 1 added donor high-frequency grain to the repaired source footprint after numeric checks measured gradient energy below neighboring wood. After revision, the repair measured gx=1.09, gy=1.92, versus unobstructed comparison patches at gx=1.18–1.25, gy=2.01–2.58.
  • Orange-object connected-component checks tracked the planned x coordinate within 0.21 px through frame 17. Its detected unclipped footprint stayed 17–19 px wide, 16 px high, and 225–245 thresholded pixels before boundary clipping.
  • All 20 frame hashes are unique. Inter-frame changed-pixel counts range from 599–730 during full visibility, then fall to 556 and 339 as the ball exits.
  • Frame 00 is pixel-identical to first_frame.png.
  • Every saved PNG was decoded and compared to the in-memory RGB frames.
  • pixels.npz was decoded and compared exactly against the uint32 packed 0xRRGGBB tensor; its shape is (144, 256, 20).
  • Changes are confined to rows 37–67 and columns 57–255. Dedicated checks confirm the red-ball region and black-pipe region are exact source copies in every frame.
  • inspection.png and transition_check.png were generated programmatically. In accordance with the task constraint, no generated or later output image was opened with an image Read/View tool; assessment used array statistics, connected components, hashes, bounds, and exact comparisons only.
