+def partial_polyline(points, fraction):
+    """Return a length-based prefix of a piecewise linear path."""
+    if fraction <= 0:
+        return []
+    if fraction >= 1:
+        return points[:]
+    lengths = [math.dist(a, b) for a, b in zip(points[:-1], points[1:])]
+    target = sum(lengths) * fraction
+    out = [points[0]]
+    for a, b, seg in zip(points[:-1], points[1:], lengths):
+        if target >= seg:
+            out.append(b)
+            target -= seg
+        else:
+            q = 0 if seg == 0 else target / seg
+            out.append((a[0] + (b[0] - a[0]) * q,
+                        a[1] + (b[1] - a[1]) * q))
+            break
+    return out

OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Measured scene layout

  • Canvas: 256 x 144 pixels, fixed camera, landscape frame.
  • Baking tray: dark graphite interior spanning roughly x=7..247, y=55..139; raised rim is strongest along the left, top, and right edges.
  • Rubber duck: centered slightly right of the image midpoint. Its visible silhouette spans about x=105..157, y=51..103. The head occupies x=111..151, y=51..87; the body occupies x=105..158, y=76..103.
  • Duck details to preserve: orange beak near x=106..125, y=70..79; black eye near x=123..128, y=66..71; bright yellow highlights and darker yellow-orange underside shading.
  • Dispenser/basket: background structure centered at x=102..229. The bright-red beverage reservoir occupies the upper crop, approximately x=136..195, y=0..19. A blue control/spigot/handle region is visible near x=120..141, y=0..18.
  • Existing pour stream: narrow warm red-orange line beginning near x=125..129, y=17 and ending around x=135, y=60, already contacting the top/front of the duck. This establishes that pouring is active at frame 00.
  • Lighting: cool gray-blue wall, warm wooden tabletop, soft upper-left/front light. All generated overlays should use anti-aliased edges, translucent highlights, and dark tray reflections consistent with this lighting.

Five-second event plan (20 frames at 4 fps)

  • Frame 00 (0.25 s): exact source image, active narrow stream already reaching the duck.
  • Frames 01-03 (0.50-1.00 s): stream subtly wavers and thickens; a small red wet spot appears at the crown and begins following the duck's right-facing contour.
  • Frames 04-06 (1.25-1.75 s): impact splash becomes readable; two or three tiny droplets arc away while a glossy red rivulet advances over the head and neck.
  • Frames 07-10 (2.00-2.75 s): beverage coats more of the duck's crown/back, with yellow still clearly visible; runoff reaches the tray at the duck's lower-right edge and forms the first small pool.
  • Frames 11-14 (3.00-3.75 s): continuous pour feeds the coating. The red pool expands outward on the dark tray as a shallow irregular ellipse, with specular highlights and a darker translucent rim.
  • Frames 15-17 (4.00-4.50 s): the stream remains continuous but gently oscillates. The pool broadens primarily to the right/front; occasional impact droplets land nearby.
  • Frames 18-19 (4.75-5.00 s): final stable pouring state. The duck is visibly streaked/coated in bright red beverage and surrounded by a modest glossy red puddle, while its face and yellow identity remain legible.

Continuity constraints

  • Frame 00 is copied byte-for-byte in pixel content from first_frame.png.
  • Background, camera, tray, basket, dispenser, and duck silhouette remain fixed.
  • Motion is limited to liquid stream deformation, wet coating growth, splash droplets, and pool growth.
  • Growth is gradual and temporally smooth; no object teleportation or cut.

METHOD.md

Generation method

Actual pixel-generation approach

The sequence is a deterministic local Python/Pillow/OpenCV composite derived only from first_frame.png and the written prompt. No external references, models, engines, masks, or network resources were used.

  1. first_frame.png is loaded as the fixed photographic base. Frame 00 is an unchanged array copy of it.
  2. Scene geometry was measured from the supplied first frame and recorded in OBSERVATION.md before generation code was written.
  3. All motion is rendered as supersampled (4x) RGBA pixel layers, then Lanczos-downsampled to 256 x 144 and alpha-composited over the unchanged base:
    • The existing pour is reinforced with a narrow dark-red edge, saturated core, and warm highlight. Two anchored sine terms create sub-pixel mid-stream wavering while its source and contact point remain fixed.
    • The duck coating is built from length-revealed connected polylines, irregular impact ellipses, and small wet islands. It is clipped by a hand-measured soft duck silhouette. The geometry stays on the crown/right shoulder, leaving the beak, eye, and front face readable.
    • Small deterministic ballistic droplets are emitted for several short three-frame arcs around the crown impact.
    • The tray puddle begins at frame 07 and grows with a square-root area curve. Nested irregular ellipses provide a wine-dark edge, bright-red body, translucent center, and low specular reflection. An expanded duck silhouette excludes the puddle beneath the visible duck.
  4. The background, dispenser, basket, tray structure, duck silhouette, and camera remain fixed. Outside x=92..210, y=12..121, every pixel remains exactly equal to frame 00.
  5. artifact_io.save packs the RGB frames as uint32 0xRRGGBB, writes the 20 PNGs, creates pixels.npz, builds inspection.png, and embeds delta packets in the standalone player.
  6. transition_check.png is produced from max-channel absolute differences for six adjacent-frame pairs. It is a diagnostic heat-map sheet, not an additional animation source.

Numeric verification performed

  • Shape and type: (20, 144, 256, 3), uint8.
  • Frame 00 equals the decoded RGB pixels of first_frame.png exactly.
  • All 20 saved frame PNGs repack exactly to the uint32 tensor in pixels.npz, whose shape is (144, 256, 20).
  • Changed pixels relative to frame 00 increase gradually from 372 at frame 01 to 2,779 at frame 19.
  • Mean absolute RGB delta from frame 00 grows from 0.321 at frame 01 to 2.833 at frame 19.
  • In sampled frames, every row of the stream corridor from y=17 through y=58 contains changed red liquid pixels, confirming source-to-impact continuity.
  • In the wet side of the duck, strongly red pixels increase from 124 in the source to 815 in the last frame, while all 456 pixels in the designated face-preservation region remain unchanged.
  • Later generated images were assessed only through local array/code statistics and consistency checks, not through image Read/View tools.
