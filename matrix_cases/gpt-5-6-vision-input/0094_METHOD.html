Output and verification

The generator reloads every PNG and pixels.npz, checks exact equality with the in-memory RGB frames, verifies frame 00 against the source, and checks that the union of changed pixels stays inside the local domino neighborhood. Numeric results are written to .tmp/verification.txt. transition_check.png is generated from numeric frame differences: unchanged pixels are dimmed, changed pixels retain color, and their one-pixel boundary is marked red.
