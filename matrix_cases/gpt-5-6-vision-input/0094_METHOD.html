OBSERVATION.md

Observation and event plan

Measured scene

  • Source: first_frame.png, RGB, 256 x 144.
  • Camera: locked, slightly elevated oblique view of a wood tabletop. Nothing in the prompt calls for camera motion, exposure change, or a cut.
  • Black platform: projected top occupies approximately x=120..210, y=26..69; its cylindrical lower rim continues to about y=80. The top-plane projection is strongly foreshortened vertically.
  • White plastic domino: the bright quadrilateral occupies approximately x=128..155, y=40..52. Its center is about (141.6, 46.2), on the platform top. Its projected long axis points about 12 degrees above screen-right. The useful source footprint is about 29 x 14 pixels including antialiasing and contact shadow.
  • Powerful magnet: fixed assembly directly left of the platform, around x=96..127, y=42..79. It overlaps the platform boundary and must remain completely static.
  • Purple tape/indicator: fixed L-like strip around x=82..116, y=49..111. It remains static.
  • Other fixed detail: a thin dark cable enters from the top and bends behind the platform. The wood grain, platform, magnet, tape, and cable are all treated as immutable background.
  • Intended action: the white domino spins clockwise in place on the platform. Its center remains essentially fixed; only a tiny subpixel eccentricity/contact-shadow variation is allowed. A horizontal-plane rotation is projected with vertical foreshortening so the motion belongs to the platform rather than looking like a flat screen-space turn.

20-frame event plan (4 fps, 5 seconds)

The listed angle is cumulative clockwise rotation from the exact source orientation. Rotation is steady at 24 degrees per frame (96 degrees/second), giving a little over one full turn and making direction unambiguous at this low frame rate.

Generation constraints

  • Frame 00 is copied without alteration.
  • For frames 01..19, only a compact region around the domino and its contact shadow changes.
  • The original domino is removed with a platform-aware reconstruction; a perspective-projected, antialiased domino and a restrained contact shadow are composited at each planned angle.
  • No new object, camera motion, crop, text, or lighting effect is introduced.

METHOD.md

Method

This sequence was produced entirely with deterministic local Python pixel operations from first_frame.png. No external model, engine, reference image, mask, or network resource was used.

Background reconstruction

Frame 00 is the unchanged source. For later frames, a small polygon enclosing only the original domino and its contact shadow is replaced from the horizontally symmetric, unoccluded portion of the black platform top. The platform's approximate symmetry axis is x=166.5. A soft 1.15-pixel blend and a slight illumination correction prevent a hard repair seam. The magnet, purple strip, cable, table, and all pixels outside the compact repair/render area are copied from the source.

Domino model and motion

The domino is represented as a low rectangular prism with platform-plane dimensions 26.5 by 9.5 source pixels and a projected height of 3.15 pixels. Platform coordinates use the fixed affine camera projection

screen_x = center_x + X - 0.12 Y
screen_y = center_y + 0.52 Y - height

which captures the strong vertical foreshortening visible on the circular platform. The center is fixed at (141.7, 48.25). The measured initial platform-plane angle is -21.5 degrees. Each subsequent frame subtracts 24 degrees, producing clockwise image motion at 96 degrees/second and a cumulative 456-degree turn by frame 19.

Visible side faces are selected from their world-space outward normals, shaded blue-gray to match the source plastic, and drawn beneath the pale blue-white top. The top uses a restrained fixed-direction light gradient and narrow molded rim. A blurred, low-opacity footprint supplies the moving contact shadow. All polygon edges are rendered at 4x resolution and area-downsampled for antialiasing.

Output and verification

artifact_io.save writes the uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, 20 PNGs, inspection sheet, and standalone player. The original source file is copied directly to frames/00.png. The generator reloads every PNG and pixels.npz, checks exact equality with the in-memory RGB frames, verifies frame 00 against the source, and checks that the union of changed pixels stays inside the local domino neighborhood. Numeric results are written to .tmp/verification.txt. transition_check.png is generated from numeric frame differences: unchanged pixels are dimmed, changed pixels retain color, and their one-pixel boundary is marked red.
