OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Measured scene

  • Source frame: 256 x 144 RGB, fixed camera, landscape framing.
  • Background: pale gray wall above a warm wood tabletop. The tabletop boundary slopes from about (0, 82) to (82, 54) and then remains behind the objects.
  • Beverage dispenser: centered in the upper-right half, approximately x=115..201, y=0..75. Its bright/dark red reservoir occupies approximately x=136..195, y=37..63; the apparent reservoir volume is much larger than the receiving glass volume.
  • Woven basket/stand: approximately x=98..232, y=39..121. It must remain static.
  • Spigot: dark metal/plastic assembly around x=117..145, y=24..65. The outlet aligns with a narrow existing red stream around x=121..127, extending from roughly y=59 down to the receiving glass.
  • Receiving glass: approximately x=98..141, y=55..134, with slightly tapered sides. The clear upper interior contains the basket seen through the glass. The initial liquid surface is around y=94..96, and the red body extends to the base around y=130.
  • Numeric red-color scans confirm the continuous narrow stream around x=120..127 above the surface and the broad contiguous liquid run around x=101..139 below y=95.
  • Lighting is soft and comes mainly from upper-left. All stationary texture, shadows, glass edges, dispenser, basket, wall, and table should be copied from the source rather than redrawn.

20-frame event plan (4 fps, 5 seconds)

  • Frame 00 (0.00 s): exact source frame, including the already-running stream and initial liquid level.
  • Frames 01-03 (0.25-0.75 s): uninterrupted narrow pour; the liquid surface begins rising slowly. Add only tiny alternating stream highlights to imply flowing fluid.
  • Frames 04-07 (1.00-1.75 s): surface rises through the lower clear portion of the glass. The fill has translucent red color and retains attenuated basket/glass texture.
  • Frames 08-11 (2.00-2.75 s): continued steady rise with a gently curved meniscus and subtle surface highlight. No camera or object motion.
  • Frames 12-15 (3.00-3.75 s): glass reaches roughly two-thirds full. The stream remains connected continuously to the liquid surface.
  • Frames 16-19 (4.00-4.75 s): level approaches about three-quarters full and is still rising at the end; the dispenser's much larger reservoir shows no perceptible level change at this resolution.

The rise is eased slightly at the beginning but is otherwise close to constant-volume flow. The meniscus moves upward by about 20 pixels total, from near y=95 to near y=75, while remaining inside a tapered analytic glass-interior mask.

METHOD.md

Method

The animation is generated locally from first_frame.png with NumPy and Pillow pixel operations. No external model, renderer, reference image, or image-generation engine is used.

Frame 00 is copied exactly from the source. For frames 01-19, the source photograph remains the background so the locked camera, wall, table, dispenser, basket texture, glass outline, lighting, and shadows do not drift. Motion is restricted to the receiving-glass interior and the already-present pouring stream.

The receiving glass is represented by a measured, slightly tapered analytic interior mask. A volume-like progress curve moves its liquid level from about row 95 to row 75. The surface is a shallow concave meniscus with a localized impact dimple and sub-pixel ripple. Newly submerged pixels are alpha-composited toward a dark bright-red target; depth-dependent opacity retains attenuated photographed basket texture through the liquid. The source frame's initial surface highlight is progressively suppressed after it becomes submerged, avoiding a false second liquid level. A thin warm highlight follows the new surface.

The photographed stream is preserved. Small phase-shifting glints and a restrained dark edge are composited inside it above the current surface, making flow apparent while keeping it connected from spout to liquid. The much larger dispenser reservoir is held fixed because the transferred quantity corresponds to less than a resolvable level change there.

artifact_io.save writes the PNG sequence, packed uint32 pixel tensor, inspection sheet, and standalone HTML player. The generator separately writes an amplified false-color adjacent-frame delta sheet as transition_check.png, then reloads every PNG and pixels.npz to assert exact equality with the in-memory RGB frames and exact source equality for frame 00.
