METHOD.md

Method

Generation

I used first_frame.png as the only visual scene source. The source was loaded as a 256 x 144 RGB NumPy array and copied without any global filtering, repainting, camera transform, or color change. Frame 00 is the untouched source array.

Frames 01–19 were produced locally by generate.py with NumPy and Pillow:

  1. A measured list of subpixel center coordinates carries one marble from the upper release area down the staggered magnetic guides and into the purple catcher.
  2. A compact elliptical contact shadow is drawn beneath the marble. Its opacity and tightness vary according to whether the planned state is rail contact or free fall.
  3. The yellow glass marble is rendered analytically with 5 x 5 samples per output pixel. It has an amber translucent center, denser dark rim, fixed upper-left specular highlight, small transmitted-light caustic, and a rotating internal color crescent to suggest rolling.
  4. After compositing, selected original source pixels are restored along the near-facing colored edge of a guide or catcher. This supplies local foreground occlusion and makes the marble sit behind the physical lip rather than on top of the whole photograph.
  5. All pixels outside the marble/shadow neighborhood remain exact copies of the source photograph.

artifact_io.save writes the packed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, the 20 PNG frames, inspection contact sheet, and standalone player. generate.py additionally writes transition_check.png, whose rows contain selected previous/current frames and a 4x absolute-difference panel.

Verification

Verification was programmatic/numeric only for generated outputs; I did not use an image Read/View tool on later frames, inspection.png, or transition_check.png.

  • PNG stack: (20, 144, 256, 3), uint8, RGB.
  • Packed tensor: (144, 256, 20), uint32.
  • Frame 00 equals first_frame.png byte-for-byte after RGB decoding.
  • Every decoded PNG pixel equals its corresponding packed 0xRRGGBB tensor value.
  • Changed-pixel counts for frames 01–19 range from 119 to 176 pixels, confined to compact 14–15 pixel-wide neighborhoods around the planned marble positions.
  • A numeric amber/yellow color test finds 53–82 marble-colored changed pixels in every generated frame.
  • Exactly 20 numbered PNGs are present; the inspection sheet is 1024 x 330 and the transition sheet is 768 x 810.

No network resources, external models, external engines, masks, reference cases, or other images were used.
