OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and animation plan

Source-frame measurements

  • Canvas: 256 x 144 RGB pixels; fixed camera, medium-wide tabletop shot.
  • Background: neutral gray wall from the top through roughly y=82 at the left and y=81 at the right. Illumination is soft, brighter near the upper center/right and darker toward the lower wall.
  • Table: warm brown wood begins along a shallow perspective edge, approximately from (0, 95) through (190, 82) to (255, 98), and fills the lower frame. Fine wood grain runs mainly vertically/diagonally in image space.
  • Suspended setup is centered near x=138:
    • thin orange cord clips into the frame near x=140, y=0..8;
    • dark linked hook/chain occupies about x=134..145, y=7..58;
    • dark magnet/coupler occupies about x=126..153, y=55..68;
    • woven cylindrical basket spans about x=98..168, y=61..131. Its top is elliptical, its side walls taper slightly, and its bottom edge casts a small soft shadow on the table.
  • The prompt establishes that an orange tennis ball is already on the table under the basket. It is fully occluded in frame 00. I will place it at approximately x=133, with radius 13 px and table contact near y=128, so it becomes naturally revealed from the bottom upward as the basket rises.
  • The initial basket shadow is concentrated beneath the lower rim, around x=101..165 and y=126..135. It must fade/shrink as the basket leaves the tabletop while a compact ball contact shadow remains.

20-frame event plan (4 fps, 5 seconds)

  • Frame 00 (0.25 s): exact source pixels, basket covering the ball.
  • Frames 01-03 (0.50-1.00 s): brief realistic tension/settling phase; basket begins a very small upward movement and the bottom shadow lightens.
  • Frames 04-07 (1.25-2.00 s): steady lift accelerates; lower edge rises enough to reveal the bottom, then the orange body, of the stationary tennis ball.
  • Frames 08-13 (2.25-3.50 s): basket, magnet, and chain continue upward together with smooth ease-in/ease-out motion. The ball is fully visible and remains fixed on the table.
  • Frames 14-17 (3.75-4.50 s): basket reaches the top part of the frame; its upper portions clip naturally at the image boundary. The cord/chain assembly shortens out of view with it.
  • Frames 18-19 (4.75-5.00 s): lift settles at the top edge, leaving the tennis ball unobstructed on the table and the setup otherwise unchanged.

The continuation will be built with local pixel operations only: a hand-measured soft object matte, texture-preserving background reconstruction from the source frame, subpixel vertical translation of the original basket/rig pixels, analytic shaded tennis-ball rendering, and source-derived grain/noise. No later frame will be assessed with an image-viewing tool.

METHOD.md

Method

The animation is a deterministic local pixel continuation generated by generate.py.

  1. The original 256 x 144 RGB frame is loaded and preserved byte-for-byte as frame 00.
  2. A soft, hand-measured matte isolates the woven basket, magnet/coupler, dark chain, and orange cord. The basket silhouette uses ellipses plus a tapered polygon; the narrow rig is refined using source-frame luminance and orange-color tests.
  3. A clean plate is reconstructed only beneath the isolated setup. Clear source strips 82 pixels to either side are remapped while retaining each pixel's signed distance from the measured rear table edge. This keeps wall samples on the wall and wood samples on the tabletop. A small local Telea inpaint contribution stabilizes the boundary, and the source texture remains the only texture source.
  4. An orange tennis ball is rendered analytically at the covered position on the table. It has spherical top-left lighting, deterministic fine fuzz variation, a curved pale seam, an antialiased edge, and a compact contact shadow.
  5. The original rig pixels are premultiplied by their matte and translated upward over a monotone 96-pixel ease/settle path. A subpixel horizontal sway of less than one pixel prevents mechanical-looking motion while retaining the locked camera. The basket's table shadow shrinks and fades with lift height.
  6. artifact_io.save writes the uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, 20 PNG frames, inspection sheet, and standalone player. transition_check.png contains selected before/after frame pairs and 4x absolute-difference maps.
  7. The generator reopens every PNG, reloads pixels.npz, and asserts exact equality with the in-memory RGB frames. It also asserts exact equality between frame 00 and first_frame.png.

No external images, models, engines, masks, or network resources are used. Later generated frames are evaluated only with scripted array statistics and numeric checks, not with image viewing tools.
