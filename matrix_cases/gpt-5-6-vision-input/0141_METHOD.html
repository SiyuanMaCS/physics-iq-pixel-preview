OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and animation plan

Source-frame measurements

  • Canvas: 256 x 144 RGB pixels; locked camera and fixed exposure.
  • A dark brown wooden tabletop fills the background. Its grain and the broad existing light/shadow pattern must remain stationary.
  • The transparent glass cutting board is a perspective quadrilateral with approximate corners (13,73), (88,24), (226,72), (191,127). Its cyan-green rim, texture, and specular highlights are fixed.
  • A small crumpled/folded piece of white paper occupies roughly x=103..134, y=61..88, centered near (118,74). The lower folds rest on the board; the hottest region is near (120,72).
  • A narrow blue-white smoke column already rises from the paper. The principal plume runs from the ignition point toward the top edge, broadly within x=88..133; it bends left and right in several translucent strands.
  • The first frame is already an active burn, so the requested event is a continuation rather than an ignition from an untouched sheet.
  • Motion will be limited to a conservative effect region around the paper and smoke. Board edges, table, and camera framing remain pixel-locked.

METHOD.md

Method

The animation is a deterministic local pixel animation built from the supplied first_frame.png; no external image, model, mask, engine, or network resource is used.

  1. The original 256 x 144 RGB source is retained as the base photograph, and frame 00 is copied byte-for-byte.
  2. A hand-measured soft mask follows the four visible smoke strands above the paper. For later frames, the pixels beneath that narrow mask are reconstructed by scanline interpolation between nearby pixels from the same source photograph. The handoff is eased across the first five frames, and some photographed plume texture is retained beneath the animated layers to keep the transition and fine detail natural.
  3. Smoke is rendered as four layered, antialiased curved filaments. Their lower endpoints remain attached to the paper while height-dependent sinusoidal advection bends the upper portions. Broad translucent blue-gray bodies and fine pale cores reproduce the backlit plume. Three blurred puffs are emitted on a schedule; each rises, expands, drifts, hollows, and fades with age.
  4. The flame is a small high-resolution layered mask: soft warm halo, orange exterior, golden middle, pale core, blue base, and a thin ember line. Its height and lean use smooth oscillations plus short flare envelopes, so consecutive 4 fps frames remain coherent.
  5. The folded paper texture is never redrawn. A brightness-gated irregular radial mask gradually shifts only its central fold toward a dark warm char color.
  6. All masks are drawn at 4x resolution, blurred where appropriate, reduced with Lanczos filtering, composited in floating point, and quantized once to RGB uint8.
  7. artifact_io.save writes the 20 PNGs, the uint32 packed 0xRRGGBB tensor, inspection sheet, and standalone HTML player. The generator also creates an amplified signed adjacent-frame difference atlas in transition_check.png and verifies every PNG against pixels.npz.

The camera, cutting board, table texture, framing, and exposure stay fixed for the entire five-second real-time shot.
