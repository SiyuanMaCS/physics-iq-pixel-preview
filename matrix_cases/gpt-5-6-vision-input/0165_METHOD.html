OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Measured first-frame layout

  • Canvas: 256 x 144 RGB, fixed camera, tabletop horizon near y=57.
  • Background: cool gray wall above the horizon; warm wood tabletop below it, with perspective grain running generally down/outward.
  • Bowl: transparent shallow glass bowl, approximately x=73..199 and y=21..97. Its rim is an ellipse centered near (136, 27); its curved lower contour bottoms near y=96.
  • Liquid: translucent dark red liquid, approximately x=79..190 and y=67..94, with a nearly horizontal free surface around y=69 and curved bowl-conforming lower edge.
  • Match bundle: centered around x=126..151, rising from the red liquid to roughly y=18. It has a dark crimson/purple body, a brighter orange-red central strip, and small dark/brown match heads near the top.
  • Jar: a tall transparent inverted cylinder centered near x=143. Its vertical walls are approximately x=113 and x=173 and continue out of the top edge. In frame 00 it is still high, with its open lower mouth visually around y=71 and overlapping the liquid/bundle.
  • Lighting: soft diffuse light from upper left; low-contrast glass edges and refraction; no camera motion.

The animation will preserve the photographed frame as its background and build the continuation from local transformed/copied pixels plus subtle analytic glass, flame, smoke, liquid, and shadow layers. The jar is treated as an inverted vessel being lowered over the already-lit central match bundle. Its downstroke is slow at first, clearest through the middle, then eases as its mouth seats in the liquid. The flames shrink after enclosure because oxygen is depleted; a small pale smoke plume remains trapped in the jar. The red liquid rises slightly inside the jar near the end, consistent with cooling/pressure reduction.

METHOD.md

Method

The supplied first_frame.png is used directly as the photographic plate and is copied byte-for-byte into frame 00. No external images, models, engines, masks, or network resources are used.

Frames 01-19 are made locally in generate.py with deterministic NumPy and Pillow pixel operations. The original plate remains fixed. The continuation adds only localized, antialiased transparent layers:

  • a smoothstep-timed 20-pixel descent for the inverted jar's open lower rim;
  • faint paired glass highlights, shadows, and vertically moving imperfections to communicate transparent-glass motion;
  • restrained elliptical liquid-contact ripples and a settling contact shadow;
  • nested translucent orange/yellow flame polygons with deterministic flicker, followed by a shrinking ember;
  • blurred gray smoke puffs advecting upward inside the jar after extinction;
  • a final 4.5-pixel rise of red liquid inside the sealed jar, representing the pressure change as the trapped gas cools.

All generated marks are supersampled at 4x resolution and Lanczos-downsampled for soft, photographic edges. The camera, bowl, tabletop, wall, and match bundle stay locked to the source image. artifact_io.save packs the frames into the required uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor and writes the PNG sequence, contact sheet, and standalone player. transition_check.png contains four rows of before, after, and 5x-amplified absolute-difference images for local auditing; it is not an animation frame.

Validation is performed numerically in generate.py and by a separate local verification pass: shape/dtype, exact frame-00 equality, frame-to-frame changed-pixel counts, PNG-to-tensor identity, RGB mode and dimensions, motion localization, and artifact presence. Generated outputs were not inspected with image Read/View tools.
