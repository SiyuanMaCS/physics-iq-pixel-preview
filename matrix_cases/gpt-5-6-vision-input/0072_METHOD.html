OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Measured scene

  • Source frame: 256 x 144 RGB. The camera is locked and the illumination is steady.
  • The beverage dispenser occupies roughly x=83..191, y=0..80. Its grapefruit-colored reservoir is above the action.
  • The dark spigot body is around x=113..134, y=39..61. The downward outlet terminates near x=118, y=66.
  • The receiving glass occupies approximately x=99..141, y=62..136. Its usable inner cavity narrows slightly toward the bottom: about x=104..137 at the top and x=107..136 near the base.
  • The pre-existing water surface reads near y=99. It is optically transparent and already picks up warm table/reservoir reflections; the important cue for added grapefruit juice is progressively stronger salmon/orange absorption plus a rising meniscus.
  • The dispenser, wicker stand, glass outline, wall, and tabletop remain rigid. No camera motion, hand, labels, extra objects, or cuts are introduced.
  • Expected stream centerline is x=118..120 from the outlet near y=65 to the liquid surface. It is only 2–4 pixels wide at this resolution, with a bright specular edge and darker grapefruit core.

Rendering constraints

  • Preserve frame 00 byte-for-byte.
  • All later edits are derived from first_frame.png with local pixel operations only.
  • Keep changes within a conservative action mask around x=103..139, y=57..132 so the locked background remains identical.
  • Use subpixel/anti-aliased analytic masks, source-derived shading, and temporally smooth parameters; avoid opaque flat shapes and frame-to-frame jitter.

METHOD.md

Method

The sequence is a deterministic local pixel animation generated by generate.py. No network service, external model, external engine, reference image, mask, or asset is used.

  1. first_frame.png is loaded as the immutable source. Frame 00 is copied exactly.
  2. The glass interior is modeled as a softly inset tapered polygon, with the original glass rim and side highlights left untouched. Its analytic meniscus rises from y=99 to y=81 over the pour.
  3. Grapefruit color is produced with a source-luminance-preserving absorption transform: red is lifted slightly while green and blue are attenuated. Concentration grows over time and includes a narrow, descending, gently sinuous mixing plume beneath the impact point.
  4. The obsolete initial waterline is replaced only after it is submerged, using vertical interpolation from neighboring pixels in the supplied source. This is local, source-derived inpainting and avoids a false second surface.
  5. The stream, bead, tail, last droplet, impact glint, ripple, and one brief bubble are analytic anti-aliased masks. The stream has a darker grapefruit core and a thin warm specular edge. Its width varies fractionally over time while its path stays aligned with the fixed outlet.
  6. Everything outside the compact spout/glass action envelope remains byte-identical to frame 00, preserving the locked camera and stable apparatus.
  7. artifact_io.save writes the uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, individual PNG frames, inspection sheet, and standalone HTML player. generate.py additionally writes an amplified signed-difference transition sheet and verifies exact frame-00 identity, PNG/tensor equality, and the changed-pixel bounds.

inspection.png and transition_check.png are generated diagnostics. They were not visually reviewed with an image-viewing tool; validation is performed through local code and numeric measurements only, as requested.
