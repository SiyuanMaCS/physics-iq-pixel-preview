  • Wrench: the small pale bluish-gray metal object at the platform's left edge, roughly x=104..115 and y=49..72. It is distinct from the purple strip on the tabletop. Its initial radial position relative to the platform center is about (-35, -9), and its long axis is approximately vertical in the image.
  • Cable: a thin dark cable exits the platform to the right. It remains fully stationary.
  • Lighting: soft ambient illumination from upper left, with a quiet highlight on the metal and deep charcoal platform tones.

Motion model

The wrench rotates clockwise about its own center near (111, 61), remaining at the platform's left edge beside the magnet. This directly depicts a magnetically driven rotation rather than an orbit that would carry the wrench away from the magnet. Motion eases in during the first second, reaches a steady angular speed, and eases slightly at the end. The total turn is about 630 degrees (one and three-quarter turns), enough to read unmistakably as clockwise and finish in a sideways pose different from the start. A soft rotating contact shadow keeps the motion grounded without introducing translational travel.

METHOD.md

Method

The sequence is a deterministic local pixel animation built from first_frame.png; no external assets, models, references, masks, engines, or network access were used.

Frame 00 is copied byte-for-pixel from the decoded source RGB. For frames 01-19, the original small wrench footprint is removed with a hand-measured local polygon and a bilateral sample from the unobstructed right side of the nearly symmetric black platform. Only this small cleanup region is blended; the rest of the photographed scene stays locked.

The moving wrench is an antialiased procedural drawing at 6x resolution. It has a tapered steel shaft, asymmetric open jaw, ring end, blue-gray metal gradient, dark rim, and a faint contact shadow. The sprite is rotated clockwise about the fixed point near (111, 61) through a measured 630-degree angle schedule, with gentle acceleration/deceleration. The center remains fixed beside the magnet at the platform's left edge.

Frames are saved as RGB PNGs. Using the supplied artifact_io output helper, pixels.npz stores the exact same pixels as a (144, 256, 20) uint32 array under the key pixels, with time on the last axis and colors packed as 0xRRGGBB. Generation rereads every PNG and decodes the tensor to assert exact equality, asserts frame 00 equals the source, and asserts that all adjacent changes remain inside the local wrench region. inspection.png is the helper's labeled 4-by-2 sheet of eight sampled frames. index.html is its standalone delta-encoded 4 fps player. transition_check.png is a labeled 4x-enhanced adjacent-frame absolute-difference sheet produced numerically, not visually reviewed.
