The animation is a deterministic local Python/Pillow and NumPy construction based only on first_frame.png and prompt.txt.

Frame 00 is copied directly from the supplied first frame. For later frames, the held paper's small original area is reconstructed by feathered scanline interpolation between nearby clean pixels inside the transparent glass; the strong glass highlights on either side are left untouched. A compact irregular paper sprite is built from shaded facets, crease strokes, a soft separation shadow, and antialiased edges. It follows a hand-authored gravity trajectory, tumbles, enters the liquid at the marked level, rebounds, bobs, drifts slightly, and gradually receives a blue-green wet tint below the surface. Small antialiased splash cusps and decaying elliptical meniscus ripples are localized around the impact. No background, camera, glass, table, or global lighting motion is introduced.
