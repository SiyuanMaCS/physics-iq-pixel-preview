liquid-on-duck · 生成方法

The animation is a deterministic local Python/Pillow/NumPy pixel composite over first_frame.png. No external model, engine, reference image, mask, or network resource was used.

Construction

  1. The source photograph is loaded as a 256 x 144 RGB array and is reused unchanged as the background of every frame.
  2. A duck-surface mask is derived locally from yellow/orange source pixels inside the measured duck region. A small morphological close fills color holes, and a subpixel blur softens its edge.
  3. All new geometry is rasterized at 4x resolution and Lanczos-downsampled. This supplies antialiasing for the thin stream, droplets, runnels, and puddle edges without altering the source outside the action.
  4. Frames 01-04 establish the pour with a growing stream, rounded leading bead, and small precursor drop. From frame 05 onward a continuous slightly wavering red column connects the dispenser outlet to the duck crown.
  5. Crown impact ellipses, oscillating lateral jets, and short-lived ballistic droplets model the splash. Droplet positions use fixed launch velocities and a quadratic downward acceleration.
  6. A crown wet patch and two progressively revealed runnel paths are intersected with the duck mask. Later frames add a patchy body wet region. Dark runnel cores and pale red glints supply wet-surface depth. Local masks reduce the coating over the two eyes and beak so the subject remains clearly readable.
  7. From frame 11, a horizontally widening, vertically shallow ellipse models liquid accumulating on the perspective tray floor. It is excluded from the duck silhouette, then given a dark rear rim and a narrow reflected highlight.
  8. Liquid uses layered dark-red edges, a saturated bright-red body, and pale red-orange specular marks consistent with the photograph's upper-left light.

Output and verification

  • artifact_io.save writes pixels.npz, the 20 RGB PNGs, inspection.png, and the standalone delta-coded index.html player.
  • generate.py separately writes transition_check.png, labeled with changed-pixel counts and mean absolute source deltas at eight event frames.
  • Frame 00 is asserted pixel-for-pixel equal to first_frame.png.
  • All 20 decoded PNG arrays are 256 x 144 RGB and exactly equal the decoded uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor of shape (144, 256, 20).
  • Every consecutive pair differs. For frames 05-19, every row from y=15 through y=50 contains introduced red stream pixels in the central x=149..159 band, numerically confirming nozzle-to-crown continuity.
  • Changed-pixel bounds remain inside the intended central action area; the final source-relative changed region is x=122..185, y=12..110.

Generated outputs were assessed only with local code and numeric checks, in accordance with the task instruction; the generated PNGs and contact sheets were not opened with image viewing tools.
