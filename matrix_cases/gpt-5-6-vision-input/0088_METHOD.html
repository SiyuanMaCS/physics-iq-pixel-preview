OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Locked scene measurements

  • Canvas: 256 x 144 pixels, landscape, fixed camera.
  • Background wall occupies roughly y=0..64. The wooden tabletop fills the lower field.
  • A dark metal baking tray begins around (77, 61), extends beyond the right and bottom edges, and remains stationary.
  • The receiving glass is centered near x=185. Its visible body spans about x=162..209 and y=20..103. It is already substantially filled with bright red liquid, with glass-edge highlights and a dark contact shadow on the tray.
  • The woven beverage-dispenser body is behind and left of the glass, approximately x=63..179 and y=8..82. A red upper vessel is cropped by the top edge.
  • The blue outlet/lever assembly is above the glass, around x=165..193 and y=0..23. Its downward outlet aligns with the glass mouth near x=184..188.
  • Illumination comes from upper left. Shadows and all background objects must remain pixel-locked.
  • Motion should therefore be localized to the outlet-to-glass gap and the red liquid surface/interior; no camera motion, object translation, or scene cuts.

Twenty-frame event plan (4 fps, 5 seconds)

  1. Frame 00: exact source frame; no generated change.
  2. Frames 01-02: a thin bright-red thread appears beneath the outlet and reaches the glass mouth.
  3. Frames 03-05: the stream becomes continuous, with a narrow highlight and slight side-to-side contour variation.
  4. Frames 06-11: steady pour. The glass's red surface rises gradually by a few pixels; a small impact dimple, bright rim glint, and restrained internal ripple move around the entry point.
  5. Frames 12-15: flow remains continuous but begins narrowing; the surface approaches its final level without reaching the rim or overflowing.
  6. Frames 16-18: the stream tapers into a short thinning tail/drop, while the surface ripple damps.
  7. Frame 19: flow has stopped; the glass remains slightly fuller and a final weak surface highlight settles.

Pixel-generation constraints

  • Frame 00 will be byte-for-byte equal in RGB pixels to first_frame.png.
  • Later imagery will be produced only with local deterministic pixel compositing and analytic masks derived from the first frame.
  • Edits will use feathered alpha masks, local source-color statistics, and small temporal variation; all untouched pixels will remain exact copies of the source.
  • Later outputs will be inspected only by numeric scripts (pixel deltas, bounds, colors, continuity, temporal differences), not by image Read/View tools.

METHOD.md

Method

The animation is a deterministic local pixel composite built from first_frame.png; no external images, models, masks, or engines are used.

generate.py first copies the source frame for every time sample. Frame 00 is returned without modification. For later frames, it draws antialiased analytic masks at 4x resolution and downsamples them: a gravity-aligned red stream with a changing width and centerline, a narrow stream highlight, two shallow meniscus arcs, a compact impact dimple/glint, and a faint refractive streak inside the liquid. The stream strength follows a hand-authored onset/steady/taper envelope, while accumulated fill progress raises the meniscus by approximately two pixels. During the final frames the stream shortens to a bead and disappears while the meniscus settles at its final level.

The original blue outlet is identified with a local blue-dominance test, softly protected during compositing, and then restored exactly so the stream reads as passing behind the fixture. Liquid-surface effects are clipped by a local red-dominance mask inside the receiving glass. Outside the outlet/glass-top region all pixels remain copied from the source, preserving the locked camera, tray, basket, tabletop, shadows, and illumination.

artifact_io.save writes the RGB PNG sequence, the packed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, the inspection contact sheet, and the standalone HTML player. The generator also creates transition_check.png, containing selected output frames and 5x-amplified absolute RGB transition deltas. Its verification pass checks the frame count, dimensions, dtype, exact source equality of frame 00, and equality of every PNG against the packed tensor. Later-frame quality checks are numeric only; no later output was reviewed with an image Read/View tool.
