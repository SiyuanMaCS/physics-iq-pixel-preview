OBSERVATION.md

Observation and event plan

Source measurements

  • The input is a 256 x 144 RGB photograph and the camera is locked off.
  • A warm wooden tabletop fills the lower/right field; a gray wall occupies the upper-left background. The tabletop/wall boundary slopes from about (0, 54) to (170, 24) and a dark background region occupies the upper-right.
  • The clear cylindrical drinking glass is centered at approximately x=139, with an outer extent near x=112..165, y=35..129. Its upper ellipse is near y=35..55; the visible water surface is near y=61..66; the base is near y=113..129.
  • The match enters from beyond the upper-left edge. Its shaft follows a diagonal from about (27, 0) toward the glass opening around (142, 57). The shaft is roughly 2 pixels wide, warm tan/orange, with a dark match head and a compact yellow-orange flame near its lower-right end.
  • Illumination is soft and stable. The glass casts a muted shadow toward the lower-left. No camera movement, hand, text, or unrelated object is present.
  • Numeric source checks: size 256 x 144, RGB extrema [1,0,1]..[254,254,255], mean RGB approximately [129.59,104.20,81.50].

Twenty-frame event plan (4 fps, five seconds)

  • 00: Preserve the supplied photograph byte-for-pixel after RGB decoding. The lit match is already inside the mouth of the glass and above the water.
  • 01-03: The match pivots/lower-translates gently into the glass. The flame flickers subtly while remaining compact; the camera and all background geometry stay fixed.
  • 04-06: The burning head approaches the water surface. Small warm flame variations and a slightly stronger local reflection convey a live flame without changing the overall exposure.
  • 07: The match head first touches the water. The flame contracts sharply and a tiny bright contact glint appears.
  • 08-10: The flame extinguishes. A brief pale steam/smoke wisp rises inside the glass; two small concentric water ripples expand from the contact point.
  • 11-14: The dark, wet head settles a little deeper. The ripples broaden and fade; the smoke becomes thinner and drifts slightly upward/right.
  • 15-17: Downward motion eases to a stop. Only a faint residual wisp and weak surface disturbance remain.
  • 18-19: Final hold: the extinguished match remains partly submerged, the water is nearly calm, and the static photographic scene is unchanged elsewhere.

The motion is intentionally small and localized: a real demonstration would not move the glass, tabletop, shadows, or camera when only the match is lowered.

METHOD.md

Method

Generation

The continuation is deterministic and uses only first_frame.png, local Python code, and mathematical pixel operations.

  1. The first image is decoded as RGB and stored directly as frame 00. No redraw or recompression is used for that frame.
  2. A narrow source mask follows the measured match shaft from the upper-left boundary to its head near (146,58). Separate local masks cover the wooden shaft/head and the flame.
  3. OpenCV's local Telea inpaint reconstructs a clean plate only under that mask. The foreground is represented as the signed RGB difference between the original photograph and this clean plate; this retains the photographed shaft, head, flame, local antialiasing, and illumination rather than synthesizing a replacement object from scratch.
  4. For frames 01-16, the source-derived foreground pivots clockwise about an off-canvas point near (27,-3), reaching seven degrees of rotation. This moves the head from about (146,58) to (138,72). Frames 17-19 hold that final pose.
  5. The part below the measured waterline is shifted 1.25 pixels and reduced to 77% foreground contrast, with a four-row blend across the interface. This is a small analytic approximation to refraction and attenuation in water.
  6. The flame component has deterministic subpixel flicker through frame 06, contracts on contact at frame 07, fades sharply in frames 08-09, and is absent thereafter.
  7. Small alpha-composited analytic effects provide the extinction cues: one contact glint, low elliptical water ripples, a short-lived bubble, and two antialiased smoke/steam curves that rise and fade. Source glass highlights outside the match-removal mask are composited back as a stable front optical layer.
  8. Everything outside the localized upper/middle region remains numerically identical to the input, preserving the locked camera, tabletop, glass, shadow, and lighting.

Packaging and verification

artifact_io.save writes the 20 RGB PNGs, the standalone HTML player, the contact sheet, and a compressed uint32 tensor in 0xRRGGBB layout with shape (144,256,20). transition_check.png is built from four representative adjacent-frame pairs and 5x-amplified absolute differences.

The generator then independently decodes all 20 PNGs and checks them pixel-for-pixel against the generated uint8 array. It also repacks every RGB pixel and verifies exact equality to pixels.npz. Frame 00 is asserted equal to the decoded first_frame.png. Generated outputs were inspected through local code and numeric diagnostics only; no image viewing tool was used on later frames, the contact sheets, or the player.
