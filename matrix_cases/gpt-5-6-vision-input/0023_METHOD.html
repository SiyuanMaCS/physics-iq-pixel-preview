ball-rolls-off · 生成方法

The sequence was generated locally with deterministic NumPy and Pillow pixel operations. Frame 00 is copied byte-for-byte from first_frame.png.

For frames 01–19, the original ball and contact shadow are removed once from a small elliptical area by fitting the neighboring tabletop's horizontal color trend on each scanline and feathering that estimate into the source. Everything outside the measured tabletop motion strip remains exactly equal to the supplied frame.

The observed ball is retained as a 21 x 21 pixel asset. Its broad lighting/shading is separated with a Gaussian blur and kept stationary relative to the scene illumination, while the residual surface detail rotates clockwise according to rolling distance divided by the measured 8.55 px radius. A soft analytic contact shadow stays under the ball. The ball center advances 6 px per frame (24 px/s) from x=150 until it leaves the right edge.

pixels.npz stores the required uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor in (144, 256, 20) order. The PNG frames and standalone player are written by the supplied output helper. inspection.png is the required sampled contact sheet, and transition_check.png contains amplified consecutive-frame numeric differences with changed-pixel counts.

No external assets, models, engines, masks, or network resources were used. Later generated images were not visually opened or reviewed; validation was performed through array shapes, exact pixel comparisons, motion-region checks, hashes, and numeric transition statistics.
