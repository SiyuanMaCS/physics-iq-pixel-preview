fill-glass-red-drink · 生成方法

Actual generation method

I used deterministic local Python/NumPy pixel compositing, with Pillow only for PNG I/O and diagnostic sheets. No external model, image source, mask, engine, or network resource was used.

  1. first_frame.png is loaded as the sole RGB scene source. Frame 00 is an unchanged array copy.
  2. For frames 01-19, an eased six-pixel rise is calculated for the liquid surface in the receiving glass.
  3. The narrow newly filled band is limited by an analytic, feathered mask following the measured taper of the glass. Its transmitted texture is sampled from already-filled rows lower in the same source glass using fractional vertical interpolation. Red absorption is increased slightly while source luminance and glass detail are retained.
  4. A shallow analytic meniscus, a small damped impact dimple, and a restrained reflected highlight are composited at the rising surface.
  5. The pre-existing falling stream is retained. A deterministic sub-pixel traveling shimmer is added along the measured stream center from the spout to the moving liquid surface; this is intensity motion rather than a large geometric displacement.
  6. Everything outside the stream/glass-liquid region is copied exactly from the source in every frame. The camera, dispenser, tap, basket, table, and background do not move.

Timing

  • 20 frames at 4 fps, representing 5 seconds.
  • Frame 00: exact source pixels.
  • Frames 01-16: continuous pour and gradually rising surface.
  • Frames 17-19: rise eases as the liquid nears the inner rim; no overflow occurs.

Verification

generate.py performs these checks after writing:

  • shape is (20, 144, 256, 3) and dtype is uint8;
  • frame 00 equals the decoded RGB pixels of first_frame.png;
  • all 20 PNGs equal their generated frame arrays;
  • pixels.npz has shape (144, 256, 20), dtype uint32, and every value equals packed 0xRRGGBB from the PNG frame tensor;
  • every consecutive pair changes;
  • the union of changed pixels is confined to x=118..150 and y=64..105 (the last row is only the antialiased impact tail).

inspection.png is the standard eight-frame overview. transition_check.png contains source-derived stream/glass crops at five times plus 8x-amplified consecutive-frame delta tiles for temporal diagnostics. Later generated images were assessed through local numeric array checks only, not through image viewing tools.
