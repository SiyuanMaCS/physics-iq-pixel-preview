OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and animation plan

Source measurements

  • Input is RGB, 256 x 144. Camera and all solid objects remain fixed.
  • Background is a cool light-gray wall above a warm wood tabletop. The tabletop starts near y=76 at the left/right edges and is partly occluded by the basket and glass.
  • The woven cylindrical basket occupies approximately x=80..193, y=48..115.
  • The glass dispenser body occupies approximately x=101..176, y=0..69. Its bright-red reservoir liquid is mainly x=103..174, y=36..64, behind the textured glass.
  • The dark tap/handle is around x=126..143, y=35..66. Its outlet is centered near x=134, y=63.
  • The falling liquid is a narrow, translucent red-brown stream centered at x=133..134 from about y=64 to the receiving glass at y=100. The original frame already contains slight horizontal/intensity irregularity in the stream.
  • The receiving glass occupies approximately x=114..153, y=94..138. Its bright-red contents currently occupy approximately x=117..150, with the visible top surface centered near y=102..104 and the curved glass bottom near y=137. This means it begins roughly three-quarters full.
  • There are no people, labels, camera moves, or unrelated objects. Highlights and shadows in the source are retained.

20-frame event plan (4 fps, 5 seconds)

  • Frame 00 (0.00 s): exact source image, including its current stream and liquid height.
  • Frames 01-04 (0.25-1.00 s): uninterrupted narrow stream; the liquid surface in the glass rises from about y=103 toward y=102. Tiny deterministic stream-width and highlight changes convey flowing liquid.
  • Frames 05-08 (1.25-2.00 s): fill surface rises through y=101..100. A shallow elliptical meniscus and small impact dimple/ripple move subtly under the stream.
  • Frames 09-12 (2.25-3.00 s): level reaches y=99. The red body becomes slightly darker with depth while source glass reflections remain visible.
  • Frames 13-16 (3.25-4.00 s): level nears the inner rim at y=97..98; the rise slows as the glass approaches full. The stream remains aligned to the same outlet and impact point.
  • Frames 17-19 (4.25-5.00 s): the glass settles essentially full at y=97, with a very small meniscus/ripple but no overflow. The reservoir level lowers only fractionally (sub-pixel visual change) because its cross-sectional area is much larger than the receiving glass.

Pixel method constraints

  • Preserve frame 00 byte-for-byte.
  • Construct later frames only from local pixel operations on first_frame.png: sampled source colors, masks, feathering, local copy/warps, and analytic ellipses/lines.
  • Restrict motion to the falling stream and liquid inside the receiving glass. No global jitter or scene relighting.
  • Use deterministic math (no nondeterministic noise) so regenerated artifacts are identical.

METHOD.md

Method

Actual generation method

I used deterministic local Python/NumPy pixel compositing, with Pillow only for PNG I/O and diagnostic sheets. No external model, image source, mask, engine, or network resource was used.

  1. first_frame.png is loaded as the sole RGB scene source. Frame 00 is an unchanged array copy.
  2. For frames 01-19, an eased six-pixel rise is calculated for the liquid surface in the receiving glass.
  3. The narrow newly filled band is limited by an analytic, feathered mask following the measured taper of the glass. Its transmitted texture is sampled from already-filled rows lower in the same source glass using fractional vertical interpolation. Red absorption is increased slightly while source luminance and glass detail are retained.
  4. A shallow analytic meniscus, a small damped impact dimple, and a restrained reflected highlight are composited at the rising surface.
  5. The pre-existing falling stream is retained. A deterministic sub-pixel traveling shimmer is added along the measured stream center from the spout to the moving liquid surface; this is intensity motion rather than a large geometric displacement.
  6. Everything outside the stream/glass-liquid region is copied exactly from the source in every frame. The camera, dispenser, tap, basket, table, and background do not move.

Timing

  • 20 frames at 4 fps, representing 5 seconds.
  • Frame 00: exact source pixels.
  • Frames 01-16: continuous pour and gradually rising surface.
  • Frames 17-19: rise eases as the liquid nears the inner rim; no overflow occurs.

Verification

generate.py performs these checks after writing:

  • shape is (20, 144, 256, 3) and dtype is uint8;
  • frame 00 equals the decoded RGB pixels of first_frame.png;
  • all 20 PNGs equal their generated frame arrays;
  • pixels.npz has shape (144, 256, 20), dtype uint32, and every value equals packed 0xRRGGBB from the PNG frame tensor;
  • every consecutive pair changes;
  • the union of changed pixels is confined to x=118..150 and y=64..105 (the last row is only the antialiased impact tail).

inspection.png is the standard eight-frame overview. transition_check.png contains source-derived stream/glass crops at five times plus 8x-amplified consecutive-frame delta tiles for temporal diagnostics. Later generated images were assessed through local numeric array checks only, not through image viewing tools.
