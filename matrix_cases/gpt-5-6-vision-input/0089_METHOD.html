Generation

The sequence is generated deterministically by generate.py from only first_frame.png.

  1. Frame 00 is copied directly from the RGB source array, with no resampling or recompression before it is passed to the output helper.
  2. A conservative liquid-interior mask is measured from red-channel dominance in the source and intersected with an analytic tapered-glass shape. This prevents motion from leaking onto the tray, tabletop, woven stand, wall, or glass silhouette.
  3. Existing photographed liquid texture is sampled with a smooth sub-pixel sinusoidal displacement field and blended at low strength. This supplies refractive liquid motion while retaining the source lighting and material appearance.
  4. The active pour is an analytic, continuous red thread beginning at the bottom edge of the measured blue fixture. Its center meanders by less than one pixel, its width varies by less than one pixel, and its shading contains a traveling translucent highlight. Source pixels classified as part of the blue fixture are protected.
  5. The impact zone uses damped elliptical ripple shading. Three faint analytic caustics and three tiny deterministic rising bubbles animate the filled glass interior. Flow and ripple strength ease gently in the final four frames but the stream remains present at frame 19.
  6. artifact_io.save packs the RGB frames to uint32 0xRRGGBB, writes pixels.npz, writes all PNG frames, builds inspection.png, and embeds lossless frame deltas in index.html.
  7. transition_check.png is generated in code from amplified maximum-channel differences for eight selected adjacent transitions. It is a diagnostic artifact, not an input to generation.

No external models, engines, masks, media, network resources, or image-view inspection of generated frames were used.

Numeric verification

  • Frame array: (20, 144, 256, 3), uint8, RGB.
  • Packed tensor: (144, 256, 20), uint32, with exact 0xRRGGBB decoding agreement against all 20 PNGs.
  • frames/00.png has exact decoded RGB equality with first_frame.png.
  • All generated changes are confined to x=137..172 and y=15..86; every pixel outside the conservative x=128..179, y=13..90 safety region remains identical to frame 00 across the sequence.
  • Adjacent transitions change 878..1,169 pixels after the initial transition, with maximum adjacent channel differences of 9..17. The first transition changes 977 pixels with maximum channel difference 28 because it establishes the initially absent active stream and impact response.
  • Required preview dimensions are inspection.png 1024 x 330 and transition_check.png 1024 x 330.
