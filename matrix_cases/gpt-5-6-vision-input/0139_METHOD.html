paper-smoke · 生成方法

Actual pixel method

The sequence is generated locally and deterministically by generate.py; its only visual input is first_frame.png.

  1. The source is decoded as a 256 x 144 RGB array. Frame 00 is returned directly from that array, with no processing.
  2. A local clean plate is made from the source using two manually traced smoke centerlines and one small upper-flame polygon. OpenCV Telea inpainting fills only those masks, and a Gaussian feather blends the result into untouched source pixels. The folded-paper region itself is retained.
  3. For frames 01-04, a decreasing trace of the photographed effect is blended with the clean plate so the source flame and smoke hand off gradually to the procedural effect.
  4. Smoke is an analytic, continuously emitted set of soft anisotropic Gaussian puffs. Each puff has a deterministic release time, buoyant upward acceleration, lateral sinusoidal meander, age-dependent spreading, and fade-in/fade-out. A narrow fresh-emission field joins the plume to the flame. Pale smoke and a weaker offset grey detail layer are blurred and alpha-composited.
  5. Flame geometry is constructed from a tapered centerline mask with a 20-sample height/lean envelope. It has blurred warm halo, orange outer flame, yellow middle, and white-yellow core layers. A smaller right tongue appears intermittently from another deterministic mask. A low-opacity elliptical glow affects only the nearby glass/paper.
  6. Charring is a small irregular elliptical mask over the folded paper. Its radii and opacity increase monotonically, with a faint ember-colored center. The lower paper corner is protected to keep the folded silhouette legible.
  7. The fixed wall, baseboard, table, cutting board, and camera are copied from the source. Numeric locality verification confirms that all pixels outside x=133..223, y=0..116 remain exactly unchanged throughout.

Output and verification

artifact_io.save writes the 20 PNG frames, the packed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, contact sheet, and standalone HTML player. generate.py additionally creates transition_check.png, then verifies:

  • shape (20, 144, 256, 3) and uint8 RGB type;
  • exact equality of frame 00 and first_frame.png;
  • exact equality of every saved PNG and its in-memory frame;
  • exact equality of pixels.npz and the PNG-derived packed RGB values;
  • zero changed pixels outside the conservative physical-effect region;
  • non-frozen adjacent transitions and a bounded maximum adjacent mean absolute delta.

The final adjacent-frame mean absolute RGB deltas are 1.413, 0.692, 0.723, 0.533, 0.576, 0.375, 0.528, 0.499, 0.436, 0.394, 0.454, 0.326, 0.489, 0.491, 0.414, 0.434, 0.538, 0.396, and 0.524. The first value includes the transition from the unmodified photographed effect; the remaining procedural evolution is deliberately subtle.

No generated or later output was opened with an image Read/View tool. Later outputs were inspected only through local code and numeric checks, including transition deltas, effect-region bounds, flame-mask extents, smoke-field mass, and monotonic char-mask integrals.
