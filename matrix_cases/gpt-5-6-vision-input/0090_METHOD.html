Scene observation and event plan

  • Existing red liquid/body region in the glass: roughly x=115..149, y=43..91. The saturated coral-red central color is around RGB (240, 78, 49), with pale glass highlights near the sides.
  • Beverage dispenser: cropped by the top edge and positioned behind the glass. Its main reservoir occupies approximately x=104..230, y=0..66. The outlet assembly is above the glass, around x=120..145, y=0..36.
  • Pour target: glass center around x=132. The usable falling-stream corridor is x=128..136, from the outlet/lip near y=21..27 to the glass opening around y=35..42.
  • Lighting: soft, upper-left illumination. The red liquid has a bright warm center and darker red edge; nearby glass highlights are light gray/pink. Shadows and all non-action pixels should remain locked.

20-frame event plan (4 fps, 5 seconds)

  • Frame 00 (0.00 s): exact supplied first frame, byte-for-byte in RGB pixels.
  • Frames 01-03 (0.25-0.75 s): the outlet begins pouring; a thin, slightly irregular bright-red strand grows downward until it connects with the glass opening. A small impact glint appears at the surface.
  • Frames 04-08 (1.00-2.00 s): continuous coherent stream. The liquid surface in the glass rises gradually by about 4 pixels, with a small bright impact ripple and subtle internal red brightening.
  • Frames 09-14 (2.25-3.50 s): steady pour continues with mild subpixel/one-pixel lateral wavering and changing highlight beads. The liquid level rises another 5 pixels.
  • Frames 15-17 (3.75-4.25 s): flow tapers; the strand narrows and breaks into a short falling bead while the glass level reaches its final height.
  • Frames 18-19 (4.50-4.75 s): stream has stopped. The last droplet lands and the surface highlight settles; the glass remains visibly fuller. Background, dispenser, tray, and glass silhouette stay fixed throughout.

Continuity constraints

  • Only the stream corridor, liquid interior, and a tiny impact/surface region may change.
  • No new objects, labels, camera movement, hand motion, or scene-wide color shifts.
  • Motion is incremental at 4 fps: no cuts and no teleporting stream or liquid level.

METHOD.md

Method

The animation is a deterministic 2D pixel continuation built directly from the supplied first_frame.png. Frame 00 is copied exactly. Each later frame starts from that same locked source, so the dispenser, glass silhouette, tray, table, background, lighting, and camera never drift.

The only synthesized pixels are soft antialiased masks in the action area: a tapered coral-red stream below the existing spout, a moving specular bead, detached shutdown droplets, a clipped meniscus/ripple, and very subtle red turbulence inside the photographed liquid region. The stream grows over the first three continuation frames, pours steadily, then pinches off and settles. The meniscus rises by seven pixels using smoothstep timing. All liquid-interior effects are clipped away from the glass outline.

Masks are analytically drawn at 4x resolution, Gaussian softened, reduced with Lanczos filtering, and alpha-composited over the original RGB pixels. No external imagery, model, engine, or visual inspection of generated frames was used. Validation was performed by array shape/type checks, exact frame-00 comparison, pixel-tensor round-trip checks, changed-pixel bounds/counts, temporal delta measurements, and fixed-region invariance checks. inspection.png is emitted automatically by the required artifact helper; transition_check.png is an automatically generated before/after/5x-difference diagnostic sheet.
