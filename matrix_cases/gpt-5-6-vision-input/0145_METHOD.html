+def feathered_polygon(points, blur=0.8):
+    """Return an antialiased float alpha mask for an image-space polygon."""
+    scale = 4
+    m = np.zeros((H * scale, W * scale), np.uint8)
+    p = np.rint(np.asarray(points, np.float32) * scale).astype(np.int32)
+    cv2.fillPoly(m, [p], 255, lineType=cv2.LINE_AA)
+    m = cv2.resize(m, (W, H), interpolation=cv2.INTER_AREA).astype(np.float32) / 255.0
+    if blur:
+        m = cv2.GaussianBlur(m, (0, 0), blur)
+    return np.clip(m, 0.0, 1.0)

+def clean_plate(src):

+    mask = np.zeros((H, W), np.uint8)
+    # The ball plus its compact contact shadow.

+    # Shade and its broad, faint down-right cast shadow.

OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

First-frame measurements

  • Canvas: 256 x 144 RGB pixels; static, locked-off landscape camera.
  • Background/table: pale wood tabletop fills essentially the frame, with horizontal grain and a rear edge around y=8--29. Illumination comes from the upper left and stays constant.
  • Black tube: cropped by the left frame boundary, approximately x=0--13 and y=50--80, aimed toward the right along y about 65. It remains stationary.
  • Grey tennis ball: circular/spherical image extent approximately x=39--65 and y=52--77, center about (52, 65), projected radius about 13 px. Its lower contact region is around y=75. This initial appearance is preserved exactly in frame 00 and reused as the source texture for the moving ball.
  • White lampshade: standing upright, approximately x=130--184 and y=31--113. Its opening is the dark quadrilateral around x=140--181 and y=32--62; its base is around x=131--166 and y=105--113. It lies directly across the ball's left-to-right centerline and is treated as a light obstacle.
  • Cast shadows are soft and displaced mainly down/right. No camera or exposure change is planned.

20-frame plan (4 fps, 5 s)

Frame 00 is the supplied image byte-for-byte. The ball then rolls right at a clear, nearly constant table-plane speed; its texture rotates to convey rolling. Because the shade blocks the centerline, impact occurs rather than allowing an impossible pass-through.

Generation approach

Use deterministic local Python/Pillow and NumPy pixel operations only. Recover clean tabletop beneath the two movable foreground objects with texture-aware row interpolation and retained low-frequency grain. Extract the ball and shade from frame 00 with feathered analytic/polygon masks. Rotate/translate the ball source crop along the horizontal path; apply a soft contact shadow. Before impact, composite the original upright shade. During impact, rotate and slightly shear the shade around its lower contact area, translate it right, add a transformed shadow, and settle it on its side. Preserve all unaffected first-frame pixels from the clean plate. No external assets, models, engines, or image-generation systems are used.

METHOD.md

Method

Actual generation method

The sequence is a deterministic 2-D photographic continuation made entirely from first_frame.png with local Python, NumPy, Pillow, and OpenCV pixel operations. No external image, model, renderer, physics engine, mask, or network source was used.

  1. The 256 x 144 RGB input is loaded without resizing. Frame 00 is appended directly from that array and therefore retains the supplied pixels exactly.
  2. A clean tabletop plate is produced by masking the ball, shade, and their original shadows, applying local Telea inpainting, and restoring subdued high-frequency horizontal wood texture from an unobstructed strip of the same photograph. Pixels away from those masks, including the black tube, remain the original input pixels.
  3. The grey ball is extracted with a feathered ellipse following its observed silhouette. For each frame the photographic ball pixels are rotated according to distance divided by radius and translated on a straight horizontal center path. A small blurred ellipse beneath it supplies a consistent contact shadow.
  4. The white shade is extracted with a six-point feathered polygon that includes its opening and all photographic face shading. It stays upright until contact. It then rotates clockwise about its lower support region, translates slightly right, and settles just above the lower image boundary. A transformed, blurred copy of its alpha produces a cast shadow that tightens as the shade approaches the table.
  5. During the collision the ball is composited behind the falling shade through frame 12. It reappears after the shade lies below its image-space path and continues to the right edge. This avoids interpenetrating silhouettes while retaining the prompt's straight traversal.
  6. artifact_io.save packs every RGB frame as uint32 0xRRGGBB, writes pixels.npz, all 20 PNGs, inspection.png, and the standalone index.html. transition_check.png contains four key before/current pairs and their 4x absolute RGB differences.

Timing and physics choices

  • Output: 20 frames, 4 fps, 5 seconds, fixed camera and constant illumination.
  • Ball center x positions: 52, 61, 70, 79, 88, 97, 106, 115, 124, 136, 148, 160, 171, 182, 193, 204, 215, 226, 241, 260 pixels.
  • The shade begins responding at frame 08 and reaches its side-resting orientation by frame 14. Small final angular changes represent settling.
  • The ball remains on the tube centerline and is fully beyond the right boundary by frame 19.

Revisions and verification

The first numeric inspection found that the shade's transformed lower edge would clip against the bottom border and that the ball came in front of the shade too early. The final pass raises the settled shade and retains correct foreground occlusion through frame 12.

Verification was performed through local code and numeric checks, not by opening later outputs with an image viewing tool:

  • frames/00.png is byte-identical to first_frame.png (SHA-256 c0aa8e61aa41ec082bd4832b22d0649250f9f2985da9db41ee1ee4f521e75148).
  • All 20 PNGs are RGB and 256 x 144.
  • The saved tensor has shape (144, 256, 20), dtype uint32, and exactly equals the PNG pixels packed as 0xRRGGBB.
  • Every adjacent frame differs; adjacent changed-pixel counts range from 1,208 to 10,653 and remain localized to the ball/shade action areas (the large first transition is the clean-plate/object recomposite).
  • Mean sequence luminance stays within 95.49 to 98.39 on an 8-bit 0--255 scale.
  • The fixed tube region is unchanged by the generator.
