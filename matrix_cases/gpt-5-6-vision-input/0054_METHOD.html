double-cradle · 生成方法

The sequence is a local pixel animation derived only from first_frame.png and the written prompt.

Frame 00 is copied byte-for-byte from the supplied RGB image. For frames 01–19, a clean plate is made by replacing the area initially hidden by the blue-black grabber with color-corrected tabletop texture sampled from an unobstructed part of the same source. The small original ball faces and suspension wires are removed with OpenCV's local Telea inpainting; the chrome support segment hidden behind the grabber is reconstructed with anti-aliased lines.

The five balls are then animated with a 73-pixel analytic pendulum constraint. Two displaced balls move together, meet the three-ball row at the bottom, and transfer motion to the opposite two balls. Successive excursions use smaller horizontal amplitudes to show damping. The positions are sampled at 4 fps according to OBSERVATION.md; nothing in the camera, table, wall, stand, or base moves.

Each metal sphere is rendered locally from an analytic normal field with dark rim, reflected horizontal lighting bands, and a compact upper-left specular highlight chosen to match the supplied chrome balls. Two thin, differently shaded suspension wires are drawn from fixed pivots to each ball. Fast near-bottom samples receive a short low-opacity directional trail, and every ball casts a small soft shadow on the base. These are deterministic NumPy/OpenCV/Pillow pixel operations; no external models, images, masks, engines, or network resources are used.

artifact_io.py writes the 20 PNG frames, the packed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, inspection sheet, and standalone player. transition_check.png is generated from consecutive-frame maximum-channel differences with numeric changed-pixel labels. Final verification compares every PNG against the packed tensor and checks frame count, RGB mode, dimensions, source identity, and temporal motion statistics.
