ball-train · 生成方法

This sequence is generated entirely from first_frame.png with deterministic local Python pixel operations (NumPy, Pillow, and OpenCV). No external images, masks, models, engines, or network resources are used.

  1. The measured gray, brown, and orange ball regions are extracted as small photographic sprites with soft circular edge alpha.
  2. Two static clean plates are made with local Navier-Stokes inpainting: one removes only the gray ball, and one removes all three balls plus their small cast-shadow regions. The former keeps the untouched brown/orange pair exactly as photographed until contact.
  3. Screen-space center tracks implement a short momentum-transfer demonstration. The gray ball accelerates toward brown, reaches contact at frames 7–8, then nearly stops. Brown shifts about one pixel, while the adjacent orange ball receives most of the impulse, rolls right, and decelerates under tabletop friction.
  4. Translation is subpixel and bilinearly sampled. Fine local color residuals from each ball rotate by distance/radius while broad illumination remains fixed, suggesting surface roll without rotating the light direction. Small blurred multiplicative contact shadows are rendered under moved balls.
  5. Frame 00 is copied directly and exactly from the source. artifact_io.save packs RGB into the required uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, writes all PNG frames, builds the inspection sheet, and embeds the same packed animation in the standalone HTML player. A separate transition sheet shows frames around contact and amplified frame-to-frame difference maps.

No generated frame was inspected with an image Read/View tool. Validation was performed with local code using array equality, decoded PNG/tensor equality, motion centroids, changed-pixel bounding boxes, temporal difference statistics, and file/format checks.
