ball-in-sand · 生成方法

The continuation is generated deterministically by generate.py using only first_frame.png, NumPy, Pillow, and OpenCV pixel operations. No external images, masks, models, engines, or network resources are used.

Construction

  1. Frame 00 is copied byte-for-byte from the supplied RGB image.
  2. The original ball footprint is identified with a measured ellipse centered at approximately (136,40). A second-order spatial color fit, sampled only from nearby neutral wall pixels, reconstructs the small patch of wall revealed after release. Pixels belonging to the grabber outside that footprint remain from the source.
  3. The ball is extracted as a feathered source-derived sprite. Its alpha combines an analytic ellipse with color distance from the fitted wall, retaining the original ball texture, rim, and shading while suppressing wall pixels around it.
  4. The ball follows a hand-authored screen-space gravity trajectory. It accelerates downward, contacts the mound, undergoes a small squash, rebounds weakly, and settles with rapidly damped motion. Three sub-exposure stamps provide restrained vertical motion blur during the fastest fall frames.
  5. A blurred, height-dependent contact shadow is applied to the mound. During impact, several tiny grains follow deterministic parabolic paths; their colors are sampled directly from the original sand.
  6. At and after contact, the source sand texture is composited back across a curved foreground lip. This occludes the lower part of the ball and makes it appear partially embedded rather than pasted over the pile. The lip height follows the damped penetration state.
  7. artifact_io.save writes the 20 PNGs, packed uint32 tensor, inspection sheet, and standalone player. transition_check.png contains frame 00, frame 19, a four-times absolute-difference field, and per-transition changed-pixel bars.

Numeric validation performed

  • Output array: (20, 144, 256, 3), uint8, RGB.
  • frames/00.png is exactly equal to first_frame.png.
  • Decoding every 0xRRGGBB value in pixels.npz reproduces every PNG channel exactly.
  • Cyan connected-component centroids track the ball from y=39.5 in the source through y=75.5 at first impact and y=80.5 at maximum penetration, then stabilize near y=79.9 after foreground sand occlusion.
  • Fixed grabber components retain identical measured positions throughout the continuation.
  • Per-transition mean absolute RGB changes remain below 1.0 on the full frame, reflecting localized motion in a locked shot.
  • Frames 14–19 are identical, confirming a stable rest state after damping.

Later generated frames were not opened with an image-viewing tool. They were evaluated only by local array decoding, color/component measurements, bounding boxes, difference counts, and temporal statistics.
