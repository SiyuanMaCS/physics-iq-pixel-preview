Generation

This continuation was generated deterministically with local Python, NumPy, Pillow, and OpenCV pixel operations. No network access, external model, external engine, reference image, or subagent was used.

  1. first_frame.png was loaded as a 256×144 RGB array. Frame 00 is a direct array copy of this source.
  2. The green paper was isolated by green-channel dominance, retaining the largest connected component. A small Gaussian feather reproduces the source boundary antialiasing.
  3. A local background plate was made by OpenCV Telea inpainting of only the paper-covered pixels, with low-amplitude analytic wood-scale variation. This plate appears only where paper motion exposes previously covered tabletop.
  4. Frames 01–10 reuse the photographed paper RGB pixels and apply a center-anchored affine horizontal stretch of at most 2%. A subtle center stress band darkens as load rises.
  5. Frames 05–10 progressively reveal an irregular one- to two-pixel slit from the lower edge of the strip's narrow center neck. The revealed pixels come from the fixed tabletop plate. Narrow, partially transparent green-cellulose rims distinguish a tear from a drawn dark line. Frame 10 retains a connected hairline upper bridge.
  6. For frames 11–19, the paper alpha is divided by a deterministic ragged seam. The two photographed halves are warped separately about measured clamp contacts near (62, 41) and (201, 42). A 4.7% maximum longitudinal contraction, approximately 6.3° clockwise recoil, and 2.2-pixel maximum outward displacement open the tear while keeping both outer ends at the grabbers. A damped release curve supplies the small final flutter.
  7. Soft shadows are derived from the transformed alpha masks by offset and Gaussian blur. A few short, attached fiber tips are included only during the first three release frames; there is no free-floating debris.
  8. artifact_io.save packs RGB as uint32 0xRRGGBB, transposes it to the required (144, 256, 20) tensor, writes all PNG frames, builds the inspection sheet, and embeds the same packed frames into the standalone player. transition_check.png is generated in code from selected frames and amplified absolute-difference panels.
