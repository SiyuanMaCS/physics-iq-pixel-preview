OBSERVATION.md

Observation and event plan

Reference-frame measurements

  • Canvas: 256 x 144 RGB pixels; fixed camera and fixed exposure.
  • The teapot is centered on the table. Its blue body occupies approximately x=105..179, y=51..116. The yellow-green lid occupies x=121..159, y=37..55 and its base/foot occupies x=122..159, y=116..136.
  • The red handle extends left to about x=79 and y=47..92. The red spout extends right to about x=207 and y=51..83. Thus a cover about 150 pixels wide, centered near x=143, is sufficient to hide the whole pot.
  • The wooden tabletop starts at about y=102 at the left and right edges, and fills the lower 42 pixels. It is warm brown, with mild perspective grain and a soft pot shadow.
  • The background is a light champagne/ivory satin or silk fabric with broad diagonal folds. It fills the area behind the teapot and provides source texture for the lowering silk. Highlights are bright but not clipped across most folds; shadows are neutral warm gray.
  • Camera and all background/table pixels should remain locked. The only motion is the silk sheet descending under gravity, contacting the lid and then draping over the pot, handle and spout.

20-frame event plan (4 fps)

The cloth will be produced from locally sampled reference-fabric texture, warped into a descending sheet, with analytic fold shading and a soft alpha edge. No external imagery or rendered assets are used.

METHOD.md

Method

The animation is a deterministic local pixel construction from first_frame.png.

  1. Frame 00 is copied byte-for-byte from the supplied RGB reference.
  2. A clean satin source strip is taken from the top 36 rows of that same frame, above the teapot. It is remapped with smooth coordinate warps into a long champagne-colored silk sheet; no outside images, models, or assets are used.
  3. A supersampled polygon mask lowers the sheet from the top of the locked frame. After the measured lid contact, the center of the hem briefly lags behind the sides. Shaded radial folds, a lid dimple, a narrow turned hem, and a soft cast shadow communicate contact and depth.
  4. From frame 14 onward, a shallow second polygon expands along the tabletop to represent excess silk pooling around the now fully hidden pot. The final frames use a small damped phase offset so the cloth settles rather than stopping abruptly.
  5. The original background and tabletop remain unchanged outside the cloth and its local cast shadow. The camera never moves.
  6. artifact_io.save writes the 20 PNG frames, packed uint32 tensor, inspection sheet, and standalone player. generate.py additionally writes transition_check.png and verifies every PNG against the 0xRRGGBB tensor.

The output was assessed with scripted measurements only (dimensions, equality, coverage, frame deltas, color statistics, temporal centroids, and tensor/PNG consistency). Generated frames were not opened with an image viewing tool.
