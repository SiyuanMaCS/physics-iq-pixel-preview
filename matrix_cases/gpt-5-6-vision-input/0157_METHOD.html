OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Source-frame observations

  • The source is an RGB image at 256 x 144. The camera is locked off, looking slightly downward at a wooden tabletop.
  • The tabletop occupies the lower portion of the frame. Its rear edge is approximately y=86 at the far left, y=94 behind the teapot, and y=98 at the far right. The wood is warm medium brown with subtle horizontal grain and a soft shadow below the teapot.
  • A cream/champagne satin backdrop fills the upper field. It has strong real folds: a bright vertical fold near x=52, a softer bright fold near x=94, a dark crease behind the teapot near x=157, and a bright/dark gathering at upper right.
  • The teapot and all of its visible parts occupy approximately x=122..226 and y=26..107. The main blue body is around x=144..207, y=48..96; the red handle reaches left to x=122; the red spout reaches right to x=225. The lid/finial is centered near x=170, y=28..49. The pale green foot reaches y=106.
  • The teapot is the only freestanding object. Its saturated blue, red and green colors contrast sharply with both the backdrop and table, which makes silhouette occlusion measurable.
  • The requested new piece of silk is interpreted as a separate, light ivory satin square lowered vertically from above by an off-screen operator. No hand or lifting hardware is introduced. It first appears as a soft, slightly rippled lower hem, then descends, contacts the lid, conforms into a tent-like drape, hides the handle/body/spout, and finally pools lightly on the tabletop.
  • The new cloth is slightly warmer and brighter than the rear backdrop. It has narrow specular ribbons, broad translucent shading, fine weave/noise, self-shadow at folds, and a soft cast shadow on the table. These cues keep it distinguishable from the background while remaining recognizably silk.

METHOD.md

Method

Generation

This sequence was generated locally and deterministically with Python, NumPy, and Pillow. No external model, engine, reference image, mask, score, or network resource was used.

first_frame.png is loaded as the immutable base of every frame. Frames 00 and 01 are exact copies of it; frame 00 is therefore byte-for-byte identical after PNG decoding. Beginning in frame 02, generate.py constructs a separate silk layer at 4x spatial resolution and composites only that layer and its shadow over the source.

The silk silhouette is analytic. A smoothstep-driven vertical position lowers its rippled hem from the upper edge to the table, while the width gradually expands around the teapot. After the virtual cloth reaches the lid, the hem changes from almost horizontal to a weighted curve with a lower center, raised outer corners, and two small pooling lobes. Slight damped lateral sway and low-amplitude edge waves provide settling motion without moving the camera or the scene.

The ivory satin appearance is also analytic: six meandering vertical folds combine broad light/dark lobes with narrow specular ribbons; weaker horizontal ripples, a fine deterministic weave pattern, warm channel-dependent coloration, and a brief cool reflected tint from the teapot add material cues. A localized blurred contact shadow grows only as the cloth approaches the tabletop. The 4x masks and generated material fields are reduced with high-quality resampling, then composited at native resolution so pixels outside the cloth/shadow remain sourced directly from the original frame.

Output encoding

The 20 generated RGB arrays have shape (20, 144, 256, 3) and dtype uint8. artifact_io.save encodes each pixel as uint32 0xRRGGBB, transposes the tensor to (144, 256, 20), and writes pixels.npz. The same arrays are written as frames/00.png through frames/19.png. The standalone index.html stores frame 00 plus lossless pixel-run deltas for the remaining frames and plays them at 250 ms per frame (4 fps).

inspection.png is the required programmatically assembled contact sheet. transition_check.png is a programmatically generated 5x4 atlas of amplified RGB absolute differences for all 19 consecutive transitions, annotated with mean absolute difference and changed-area ratio. Neither diagnostic image was opened or visually reviewed; later-output inspection was performed only with local code and numeric measurements, per the task instruction.

Verification

The generator asserts all of the following on every run:

  • 20 frames, each RGB 256 x 144, stored as uint8.
  • Exact array equality of frame 00 and the decoded first_frame.png.
  • Exact decoding equality between every saved PNG and the uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor.
  • Strong final replacement of the original teapot region and negligible change in a far static control region.

The final numeric pass measured 97.220% of the broad teapot ROI as changed by more than 15 levels in at least one RGB channel, 0.000% of the far static control region as changed by more than 2 levels, and zero strongly saturated pixels remaining in the original tight teapot box. The transition metrics and full verification report are saved under .tmp/ for reproducibility.
