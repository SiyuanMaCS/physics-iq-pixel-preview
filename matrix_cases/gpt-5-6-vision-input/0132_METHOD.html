Generation

This sequence was made entirely with deterministic local pixel operations in generate.py; no external model, renderer, scene, mask, or reference was used.

  1. first_frame.png is loaded as the photographed source, and frame 00 is kept as an exact copy.
  2. Numeric color/component measurements locate the red dollop near (137,53), the pale shaft from about (143,53) to (228,44), and the green clamp over the attachment point. The animation uses (228,44) as a fixed pivot.
  3. For frames 01–19, OpenCV Telea inpainting reconstructs only the narrow original shaft footprint from neighboring photographed table/acrylic texture. The red dollop, sheet, platform, holder, table, wall, and every area outside the local action region remain based on the original pixels.
  4. A soft foreground matte is calculated from distance to the measured shaft axis and local source/background contrast. The real source shaft pixels are affine-rotated about the pivot rather than replaced by a flat synthetic rod.
  5. Rotation follows smoothstep easing over 19 degrees in screen-space clockwise direction. The analytically calculated brush-tip position progresses from (137.0,53.0) to approximately (139.0,22.9).
  6. A small tapered brown bristle polygon and red pigment pickup are composited at the moving tip. A narrow, progressively thinner curved paint trace is deposited along the exact circular tip path, while the original main dollop remains stationary.
  7. A low-opacity local contact shadow seats the moving shaft in the source lighting. The original green clamp pixels are reapplied through a color-derived occlusion mask so the moving shaft stays visibly attached beneath the holder.
  8. artifact_io.save writes the packed tensor, PNG frames, inspection sheet, and standalone player. generate.py additionally builds transition_check.png from amplified adjacent-frame absolute differences.

Numeric verification

  • Output tensor: (144, 256, 20), uint32, packed as 0xRRGGBB.
  • PNG sequence: 20 RGB images at 256×14.
  • Frame 00 equals the source pixel-for-pixel and its saved PNG is byte-identical to first_frame.png in this run.
  • Every PNG was decoded and repacked, then compared exactly with its corresponding pixels.npz plane.
  • Changes above 3 RGB levels remain inside the measured local action bounds x=135..224, y=21..57; the locked background outside the conservative action ROI is unchanged.
  • Adjacent frames are all distinct. Changed-pixel counts range from 667 to 937, and mean absolute deltas rise and fall smoothly with the eased angular speed (peak near the middle of the sequence).
  • The sampled pale-shaft contrast stays positive throughout: roughly 60–72 mean RGB levels brighter than adjacent pixels along the predicted rotating axis.
  • Saturated red remains at the original dollop while extending along the path: its thresholded bounds progress from x=131..143, y=50..57 to x=131..145, y=23..57.

Inspection was performed through local code and numeric checks only; no image Read/View tool was used on later outputs.
