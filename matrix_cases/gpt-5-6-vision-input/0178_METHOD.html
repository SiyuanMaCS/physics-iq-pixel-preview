OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Measured scene

  • Source image: 256 x 144 RGB. The camera is locked off and looks slightly downward across a pale, low-contrast wooden tabletop.
  • The tabletop occupies essentially the whole image below the dark rear strip at about y=0..11. Subtle horizontal/plank grain is already present and must remain fixed.
  • Left black pipe: partly cropped by the left edge, with its visible outlet/body around x=0..13, y=58..92. Its opening is directed toward the right, centered near (4, 75).
  • Right black pipe: partly cropped by the right edge, around x=238..255, y=21..49. Its opening is directed toward the left, centered near (249, 35).
  • Grey tennis ball: soft/motion-blurred footprint around x=79..108, y=72..97, center approximately (94, 84), radius about 10 px. It is downstream of the left pipe. Its straight trajectory is approximately y = 75 + 0.095*x, moving right.
  • Brown tennis ball: footprint around x=134..153, y=23..39, center approximately (143, 31), radius about 7 px. It is downstream of the right pipe. Its straight trajectory is approximately y = 35 + 0.038*(x-249), moving left.
  • The two paths are vertically offset by roughly 45--60 px, so no collision occurs.
  • Illumination is dim and diffuse. Object shadows fall softly down/right; the grey ball has stronger motion softness than the brown ball.

Planned construction

Build a clean locked-off plate by locally reconstructing only the two initial ball regions from neighboring table texture. Extract color/texture statistics from each source ball, then render shaded, softly textured rotating spheres plus contact shadows and velocity-aligned motion softness along the measured paths. Keep both black pipes and every pixel outside the ball repair/composite areas unchanged. Frame 00 will be copied byte-for-byte at the pixel level from first_frame.png.

METHOD.md

Method

The animation is a deterministic local pixel construction; no external model, engine, reference image, mask, or network resource was used.

  1. first_frame.png is loaded as RGB and used unchanged for frame 00.
  2. A locked clean plate is made by masking only the original grey- and brown-ball footprints. OpenCV Telea inpainting reconstructs nearby tabletop color and grain, and a lightly blended bilateral pass suppresses fill-edge noise. The black pipes and all pixels outside these two soft masks come from the source frame.
  3. Each later ball is rendered mathematically as an antialiased sphere. Analytic surface normals provide diffuse top-left illumination, rim falloff, and a restrained highlight. Two deterministic sinusoidal components give subtle fuzzy surface variation. A curved low-contrast seam rotates according to traveled distance divided by radius.
  4. Each ball receives a soft elliptical contact shadow and three short exposure samples along its velocity vector. This produces the compact directional softness expected from high-speed rolling while retaining a readable current position.
  5. Motion follows two measured straight, vertically offset pipe axes. Brown travels right-to-left on the upper path; grey travels left-to-right on the lower path. Explicit quarter-second positions use mildly decreasing step sizes to represent rolling friction. Both leave the image after frame 08, after which the camera holds on the empty fixed setup.
  6. artifact_io.py writes the PNG sequence, the uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, the inspection sheet, and the standalone HTML player. generate.py writes transition_check.png as selected A/B frames with amplified absolute differences.
  7. The generator reloads all 20 PNGs and pixels.npz, asserts exact equality to the in-memory RGB frames, and separately asserts that frame 00 exactly equals the supplied source pixels.

No generated frame was inspected with an image Read/View tool. Validation is performed by array assertions and numeric diagnostics only.
