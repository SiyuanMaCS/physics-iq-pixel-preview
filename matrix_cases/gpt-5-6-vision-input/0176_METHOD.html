METHOD.md

Method

This continuation uses deterministic local Python pixel operations only. No external model, engine, image reference, mask, score, network resource, or other case was used.

The original 256×144 RGB photograph is the sole visual source. A constrained GrabCut matte isolates the teapot inside hand-measured silhouette priors for the rounded body, lid/finial, red loop handle, red spout, green base, and feet. The matte resolves to one connected component of roughly 5,200 pixels. A small Telea inpaint reconstructs a clean plate only under that matte so narrow wall/table/platform strips can be exposed as the asymmetric parts turn; all pixels outside an 11×11-dilated motion envelope are copied exactly from the source in every frame.

The motion is a 2.5D image-space approximation to rotation about the measured vertical axis at x≈133. The blue rounded body is inverse-remapped as a cylindrical/ellipsoidal surface, which carries the photographed floral decoration around the body rather than sliding the entire pot. The nearly axisymmetric lid and base receive smaller versions of the same surface remap. The handle and spout are separate photographed-pixel layers and undergo cosine foreshortening about the vertical axis, with tiny opposing vertical depth offsets and front/back compositing order. The black circular platform, tabletop, cast shadow, wall, camera, framing, and exposure remain locked. The angle follows a cosine ease from 0° to 31.6° clockwise over 20 frames.

Frame 00 is the unmodified source array. The resulting RGB arrays are packed to uint32 0xRRGGBB by artifact_io.py. generate.py then independently reloads pixels.npz and every PNG and asserts exact equality with the generated array. It also checks the exact first frame, nonzero adjacent motion, dimensions/dtype, and pixel-perfect stability outside the compact motion envelope. inspection.png is the required eight-frame contact sheet. transition_check.png places frames 00/06/12/19 above 5×-amplified adjacent-frame RGB deltas for local diagnostic use; it was generated but not visually inspected.
