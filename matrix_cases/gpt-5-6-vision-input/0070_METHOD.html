The animation is a deterministic local pixel composite built only from first_frame.png and the supplied prompt. No external model, renderer, engine, reference image, mask, or network service is used.

generate.py keeps the supplied image as a fixed background plate and changes only two small regions:

  1. A mathematically drawn, antialiased 1–2 pixel grapefruit-colored stream runs from the measured tap outlet near (165, 62) into the glass. Its formation, steady flow, small lateral wobble, taper, breakup, and final drop are explicitly scheduled by frame.
  2. The liquid inside a conservative analytic glass-interior mask rises from about y=108 to y=95. A translucent warm filter preserves the source texture and reflections. The upper boundary is a shallow curved meniscus with a small sinusoidal ripple during impact and a decay after pouring stops.

Every frame is recomposited directly from frame 00, preventing cumulative resampling or camera drift. The basket, dispenser body, tap silhouette, glass outline, tabletop, and background are never transformed. Frame 00 is a byte-exact copy of first_frame.png after RGB decoding.

Validation is numerical and local: the script asserts shape and dtype, exact frame-00 equality, a strict action-region bound, expected stream activity/removal, expected upward fill activity, and exact agreement among every saved PNG, the in-memory RGB array, and the packed uint32 tensor in pixels.npz. transition_check.png is generated programmatically from six action crops and 5x amplified absolute differences; it is not visually inspected.
