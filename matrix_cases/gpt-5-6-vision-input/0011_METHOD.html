+def smoothstep(edge0, edge1, value):
+    x = np.clip((value - edge0) / (edge1 - edge0), 0.0, 1.0)
+    return x * x * (3.0 - 2.0 * x)

+def bilinear_rgb(image, x, y):
+    """Sample an RGB image at floating point coordinate arrays."""
+    x = np.clip(x, 0.0, image.shape[1] - 1.001)
+    y = np.clip(y, 0.0, image.shape[0] - 1.001)
+    x0 = np.floor(x).astype(np.int32)
+    y0 = np.floor(y).astype(np.int32)
+    x1 = x0 + 1
+    y1 = y0 + 1
+    fx = (x - x0)[..., None]
+    fy = (y - y0)[..., None]
+    a = image[y0, x0].astype(np.float32)
+    b = image[y0, x1].astype(np.float32)
+    c = image[y1, x0].astype(np.float32)
+    d = image[y1, x1].astype(np.float32)
+    return (a * (1.0 - fx) + b * fx) * (1.0 - fy) + (c * (1.0 - fx) + d * fx) * fy

+def make_clean_plate(source):

+    # covers the low, right-biased cast shadow visible on the table.

OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

First-frame observations

  • Canvas: 256 x 144 pixels, landscape, fixed camera, natural indoor lighting.
  • A pale, lightly striped wooden coffee-table top fills approximately y=31 through y=129. Its front edge is a dark horizontal band at about y=130-136, with a dim space and furniture details below it.
  • A mustard/olive upholstered sofa spans the background. Its lower front rail is around y=0-31, with a dark gap immediately above the tabletop.
  • At the extreme left, a short black pipe/nozzle enters the frame. Its visible outlet is close to (7, 47), partially cropped by the left boundary.
  • The orange tennis ball is centered at approximately (55, 52), with an apparent radius of about 8 px. Its lit face is warm orange, its lower/right edge is darker, and it casts a soft, low shadow mostly to the right/below. This is the moving object.
  • A small red ball rests around (111, 38), radius about 5 px. It overlaps/stands in front of a darker brown ball or rounded object around (117, 34), radius about 6 px. Both remain stationary.
  • The tabletop is viewed obliquely: horizontal travel to the right is the clearest interpretation of "straight out of the black pipe over the entire table towards the right side." The moving ball therefore keeps essentially the same vertical center, with only a subpixel-scale natural wobble.
  • No camera movement, cuts, added objects, text, or changes to the sofa/table/background are planned.

Motion and compositing plan

  • Frame 00 is copied byte-for-byte from first_frame.png.
  • For frames 01-19, remove the original orange ball and its shadow by locally reconstructing the pale tabletop from neighboring texture.
  • Re-render the orange ball from a texture model derived from its first-frame colors, translating it from x=55 toward and finally partly beyond the right edge.
  • Use near-constant rolling velocity with slight physical deceleration. Rotate the modeled surface texture in proportion to traveled distance so the ball visibly rolls rather than slides.
  • Redraw a soft contact/ambient shadow beneath and slightly right of the moving ball. Keep its size and softness stable because the ball stays on the same table plane.
  • Restrict changes to the original orange-ball cleanup region and the compact moving ball/shadow region; all other pixels remain identical to the source.

METHOD.md

Method

The animation is generated entirely with local Python pixel operations from first_frame.png; no external model, engine, mask, reference, or network source is used.

Frame 00 is the decoded RGB source image copied without any pixel changes. For later frames, OpenCV's local TELEA inpainting reconstructs the small patch of pale tabletop hidden by the source orange ball and its soft shadow. This operation is limited to two overlapping ellipses around the original ball location.

The moving ball uses the original ball itself as its color and fixed lighting field. An antialiased elliptical sprite samples that field bilinearly, and a deterministic, very low-amplitude spherical texture modulation rotates according to distance traveled divided by ball radius. Thus its illumination remains locked to the room while its subtle surface texture rolls. A compact two-Gaussian warm-gray contact/ambient shadow is composited under the translated ball.

The center follows an analytic near-constant-speed trajectory with slight rolling-resistance deceleration, from x=54.5 in the source to x=258.05 at frame 19. The y center remains at 51.5 so the ball travels straight across the level tabletop. The red ball, brown ball, pipe, sofa, table edge, and all other pixels remain fixed.

artifact_io.save serializes the 20 RGB frames as PNGs, a transposed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, the inspection contact sheet, and the standalone HTML player. generate.py additionally creates transition_check.png with selected adjacent-frame pairs, amplified absolute differences, and binary changed-pixel masks. The script reopens all 20 PNGs and the NPZ tensor and asserts exact equality with its in-memory frames.
