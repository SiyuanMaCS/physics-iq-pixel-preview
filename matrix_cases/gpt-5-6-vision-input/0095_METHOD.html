OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Measurements from the supplied first frame

  • Source is an RGB image at 256 x 144 pixels. The shot is locked off and the table, pale wall, magnet, platform, and cable must remain stationary.
  • The wooden tabletop occupies the lower and middle field. Its grain and illumination are part of the fixed photographic background.
  • The black circular platform is centered at approximately (153, 67). Its visible top is an ellipse approximately 68 x 31 pixels, with a dark cylindrical front descending to about y=89.
  • The small white/lilac plastic domino begins nearly horizontal at the platform's left rim. Its visible footprint is approximately x=110..131, y=53..61, centered near (121, 57.5). It has a pale top face, a darker lavender lower/side face, and a subtle contact shadow.
  • The powerful magnet assembly is fixed at the left, around x=76..101 and y=51..102. The purple hanging/stand element is also stationary.
  • The domino partly obscures the platform's left elliptical rim, so later frames require a small reconstructed clean plate only under the original domino. No other region should be regenerated.
  • Motion is clockwise in the tabletop plane. Perspective foreshortens screen-space motion in the vertical direction, so the rotating rectangle is rendered as a projected low cuboid rather than as an unconstrained 2-D sprite.

METHOD.md

Generation method

Actual pixel method used

The continuation is generated entirely by generate.py with local NumPy, Pillow, and OpenCV pixel operations. No external model, engine, reference image, mask, score, or network resource was used.

  1. first_frame.png is loaded as RGB and copied without modification into frame 00.
  2. A clean plate is reconstructed only beneath the starting domino, inside a small soft-edged area around x=107..135 and y=50..65. The hidden left rim of the platform is modeled from its measured ellipse. Photographic table and platform texture is sampled from adjacent unobscured pixels in the same rows. The reconstructed patch is checked numerically to ensure that no lavender object pixels remain.
  3. For frames 01 through 19, the domino is treated as a low rectangular cuboid rotating clockwise in the tabletop plane. A vertical projection factor of 0.60 accounts for camera perspective. Its center stays near (121, 56.7), with less than a quarter-pixel deterministic wobble.
  4. The pale top face uses a projective remap of the real top-face texture from frame 00. Four thin side faces are rendered with orientation-dependent lavender shading. Supersampled polygon masks provide antialiased edges.
  5. A close, blurred contact shadow follows the domino. Its strength adapts to the local background luminance, so it is visible on wood without becoming unnaturally heavy on the black platform.
  6. The angular schedule is 0, 8, 24, 48, 78, 112, 150, 190, 232, 276, 322, 369, 416, 462, 506, 546, 582, 612, 634, and 646 degrees. This gives acceleration, sustained clockwise rotation, and a friction-like ease toward the end.

Artifact creation

artifact_io.save packs the RGB frames as uint32 0xRRGGBB values with tensor shape (144, 256, 20), writes the 20 PNG files, creates inspection.png, and embeds the same frames in the standalone index.html player. generate.py additionally creates transition_check.png, a programmatic heatmap sheet of every consecutive-frame RGB difference.

Verification performed

  • Frame array shape: (20, 144, 256, 3); dtype: uint8.
  • frames/00.png and the in-memory frame 00 are exactly equal to the supplied first_frame.png.
  • Every frames/00.png through frames/19.png is decoded and compared exactly with its in-memory RGB frame.
  • pixels.npz is decoded and compared exactly with all frames after uint32 0xRRGGBB packing.
  • All pixels outside the conservative motion envelope x=103..140, y=43..74 remain exactly equal to frame 00 in every later frame.
  • Consecutive changed-pixel counts range from 278 to 518, and their bounds stay local to the domino, its shadow, and the tiny clean-plate patch.
  • A weighted principal-axis calculation on the pale/lavender moving component confirms continuous clockwise pose progression modulo the rectangle's 180-degree symmetry.
  • The clean plate contains zero pixels matching the numeric lavender-residue test used for the original domino.

The supplied first frame was observed visually as allowed. Later generated frames were not opened with an image Read/View tool; they were inspected only through local decoding, array statistics, masks, bounding boxes, color tests, gradients, and orientation calculations.
