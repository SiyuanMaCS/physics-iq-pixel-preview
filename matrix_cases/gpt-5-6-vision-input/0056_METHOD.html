duck-and-dominos · 生成方法

Construction

The continuation is generated entirely by generate.py from first_frame.png with deterministic local NumPy, OpenCV, and Pillow pixel operations.

  1. The eight dominoes are isolated with measured, softly antialiased quadrilateral mattes. The duck matte is derived from its high yellow/orange chroma (R + G - 2B) and its filled outer contour, which preserves the original dark eyes, nostrils, and bill pixels inside the silhouette.
  2. A clean plate is reconstructed only underneath the source positions of the moving bodies and exposed portion of the original wooden arm using local OpenCV Telea inpainting. During animation, the exact source frame remains the base; a body's clean footprint is revealed only when that body begins to move. This keeps the locked camera, wall, table grain, platform, and not-yet-moving objects stable.
  3. Every domino uses its original source pixels and rotates leftward about its measured lower contact point. Smoothstep angular motion and a small terminal overshoot produce the right-to-left cascade. The right run reaches the duck first; the duck then translates/rocks left and initiates the left run. The outermost block settles from 88 degrees to 86 degrees between frames 18 and 19.
  4. The wooden arm is drawn as a shaded brown segment on a clockwise polar arc about the platform hub. Its exposed endpoint moves into the rightmost domino before the cascade begins.
  5. Soft analytic contact shadows are derived from transformed object mattes, blurred, and composited in brown-black at low opacity. Premultiplied-alpha affine resampling prevents black fringes around transformed cutouts.

Frame 00 is an exact byte-for-byte RGB copy of first_frame.png. Frame 01 is a still anticipation sample. No external model, engine, image, mask, network resource, or subagent was used.

Output and checks

artifact_io.save writes the canonical (144, 256, 20) uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, the 20 RGB PNGs, inspection sheet, and standalone HTML player. generate.py additionally creates transition_check.png, whose upper rows contain selected frames and lower rows contain amplified adjacent-frame absolute-difference maps for diagnostic use.

The generator performs these checks on every run:

  • frame array is exactly (20, 144, 256, 3) and uint8;
  • frame 00 exactly equals the source;
  • all intended post-anticipation transitions contain motion and remain spatially bounded;
  • every saved PNG round-trips exactly to its generated RGB array;
  • pixels.npz exactly matches every RGB frame after uint32 packing.

The final numeric audit also confirmed that no pixel outside the broad action region (x=20..229, y=35..112) changes after motion begins. Generated outputs were inspected only through local code, array statistics, geometry/state checks, hashes, bounding boxes, and round-trip comparisons; no image Read/View tool was used on later outputs.
