OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Measured scene

  • Source frame: 256 x 144 RGB PNG; the camera is locked off and the table fills most of the image.
  • The brown ball is centered at approximately (121, 62). Its visible body is about 19 x 19 px, with a soft contact/cast shadow extending the moving footprint to roughly 30 x 25 px.
  • The black tube enters from the left edge around y=51..82; its open mouth is cropped by the frame edge. The ball is already well clear of the tube in frame 00, so the continuation begins with it rolling rightward.
  • The olive-green fabric box occupies the upper-right. Its nearest slanted face starts around (192, 48) and meets the table near (221, 78). The strongest measured face edge at the ball's center row is x≈205, so first contact is expected when the ball center reaches x≈194.
  • The tabletop is pale wood with mostly horizontal, low-contrast grain. The original ball region can therefore be restored from neighboring row texture without altering the stationary tube or box.
  • Lighting is soft and directional: the ball has a darker lower-right portion and a diffuse shadow that trails mainly to its right/below. This shading and the source's slight softness should remain consistent.

METHOD.md

Method

The sequence is generated entirely with local Python pixel operations in generate.py. No external image/video generator, model, engine, mask, or reference material is used.

Construction

  1. Load first_frame.png as an RGB uint8 array and copy it unchanged into frame 00.
  2. Make a stationary clean plate for frames 01–19. Only the initial ball-and-shadow footprint is replaced. Because the visible wood grain is predominantly horizontal, each affected row is reconstructed by interpolating between robust five-pixel samples immediately to its left and right. An elliptical feather keeps the replacement joined smoothly to untouched source pixels.
  3. Extract a 31 x 31 source patch centered on the ball. Separate it numerically into broad illumination (a Gaussian-smoothed RGB field) and fine surface detail (the source divided by that field). During motion, only the fine detail rotates; the broad illumination remains fixed relative to the camera. This communicates rolling without rotating the scene lighting.
  4. Composite the ball over the clean plate with a feathered elliptical alpha. Its center follows the measured x positions 121, 129, 137, 145, 153, 161, 169, 176, 182, 188, 195, 185, 177, 171, 167, 164, 163, 163, 163, 163.
  5. At frame 10, apply horizontal scale 0.82 and vertical scale 1.10 to represent brief impact compression at the measured box boundary. The following frames reverse the translation and rotation, then damp them to a rest.
  6. Synthesize a soft, right-biased tabletop shadow from a finite-support elliptical Gaussian. It is clipped at the measured slanted box boundary (x ≈ y + 143) so it does not incorrectly darken the upright box face.
  7. Use artifact_io.save to pack the RGB frames into uint32 0xRRGGBB pixels, write all 20 PNGs, build inspection.png, and create the standalone index.html. transition_check.png contains four labeled before/after pairs plus 4x absolute-difference panels for the start, approach, impact, and rebound transitions.

Numeric inspection

Later generated outputs were inspected only through local code and numeric checks, not with image Read/View tools.

  • Tensor shape and dtype: (144, 256, 20), uint32.
  • Every packed tensor value equals its corresponding frame PNG value.
  • Frame 00 equals first_frame.png; both PNG files have SHA-256 4ee47954deccf152d34176c14c790509bb9be24437423c4802fe9f5a2152cbf7.
  • All frame PNGs are RGB and 256 x 144.
  • Difference-weighted x centers progress rightward through impact (129.16 ... 194.68), reverse leftward (184.09 ... 163.02), and remain fixed for the final hold.
  • Frames 16–19 are byte-identical, giving a stable settled ending.
  • The regenerated wood patch has mean luminance 121.26, standard deviation 4.65, and mean inner horizontal gradient 0.78; boundary jumps are below one intensity level on average.
