paper-fall-water · 生成方法

Generation

generate.py uses only first_frame.png, NumPy, Pillow, OpenCV pixel operations, and the supplied output helper.

  1. The first source frame is loaded as an RGB uint8 array. Frame 00 is inserted directly and is never recomposited.
  2. A hand-measured irregular polygon isolates the crumpled paper between the orange-black jaws. Its source RGB values are retained, and its polygon alpha is lightly feathered. A numeric color-composition check was used to revise the polygon so it excludes the orange jaw pixels (orange-like pixels fell from about 15.8% to about 2.1% of the cutout).
  3. The paper's original footprint is dilated by one pixel and repaired with local Telea inpainting. Because this footprint is on the smooth gray background between the jaws, the repair reveals an open gap while preserving the outer black/orange tool. All pixels outside the measured tool-to-bowl corridor remain source-identical.
  4. The paper is transformed as a premultiplied-alpha sprite. A sampled gravity/flutter path moves it down with increasing displacement, slight horizontal drift, clockwise rotation, and mild vertical compression. At water contact it rebounds once by about two pixels, then settles to a stable floating position.
  5. A soft projected ellipse on the water tightens as the paper approaches. Starting at frame 09, antialiased expanding ripple ellipses are clipped to the measured water interior. Their brightness and opacity decay analytically. The lower paper fibers receive a localized warm-gray wet tint after contact.
  6. Source-derived neutral front-glass highlights are selectively reapplied over the lower edge of the floating paper to retain bowl depth and transparency.
  7. artifact_io.save writes the PNG sequence, packed uint32 tensor, inspection contact sheet, and standalone HTML player. generate.py additionally writes transition_check.png, containing selected frames and amplified adjacent-frame deltas.

Timing

  • Frames 00-02: held state and release onset.
  • Frames 03-08: accelerating fall with mild flutter.
  • Frame 09: first water contact.
  • Frames 10-14: ripple expansion, small rebound, and settling.
  • Frames 15-19: fading residual ripple and final floating hold.

The cadence is fixed at 4 fps for 20 frames (5 seconds total).

Verification

  • Exactly 20 PNGs are written as RGB images at 256 x 144.
  • frames/00.png is pixel-identical and PNG-byte-identical to first_frame.png.
  • Repacking every PNG as 0xRRGGBB and transposing to (144, 256, 20) exactly equals the uint32 pixels tensor in pixels.npz.
  • Motion/difference pixels remain inside the measured corridor x=88..170, y=18..121; no source-relative changes occur elsewhere.
  • Connected-component centroids were checked numerically to confirm monotonic downward travel before contact, a small one-step rebound, and a stable final position.
  • Per-frame dimensions, data types, channel ranges, adjacent-frame change counts, file counts, and required artifact sizes were checked locally.
  • Generated/later images were not opened with an image Read/View tool and were not visually reviewed. Inspection was exclusively through local code and numeric statistics, as requested.
