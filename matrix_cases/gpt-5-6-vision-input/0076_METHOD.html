OBSERVATION.md

Source-frame observation

The input is a locked 256×144 photograph. A wood-toned tabletop begins at an uneven horizon around y=113. A low, glossy black turntable occupies approximately x=141–201, y=113–141. A yellow ceramic mug is centered near (170,106): the body spans about x=154–187 and y=85–124, its dark oval rim is near y=86–91, and its handle is initially presented on the right. The spotlight is frontal and low, producing a white hot spot on the mug and turntable near (170,119–129).

The wall is illuminated by a broad circular pool centered roughly near (123,63), fading to black at the left, right, and upper edges. The mug's magnified shadow lies up-left of the real mug. Its body is approximately x=130–171, y=48–113 and its handle projects to the left around x=115–134, y=55–91. Edges are soft, consistent with an extended light source. The camera, table edge, spotlight pool, and all unrelated image grain must remain fixed.

Pixel measurements from the input:

  • shape/dtype: 144×256 RGB uint8
  • full-image channel range: 0–255
  • strong yellow candidate extent below y=70: x=154–200, y=88–143 (the lowest candidates include reflected yellow on the base/table)
  • mug/turntable working region: x=140–202, y=80–143
  • wall-shadow working region: x=108–177, y=43–114

Clockwise direction is conveyed by the handle's projected azimuth, moving highlights/reflections on the black platform, and a synchronized large soft wall shadow. METHOD.md

Method

This sequence is a local, source-photograph pixel composite generated by generate.py. No external models, engines, assets, masks, or network resources were used.

The input photograph is the static plate. Frame 00 is a direct RGB-array copy of first_frame.png, bypassing every animation operation. Frames 01–19 sample an 18° clockwise angular increment, for 342° across the stored samples (0.95 revolution in 4.75 seconds).

The real mug remains centered on the turntable. Its handle is represented by supersampled antialiased ellipse strokes whose lateral extent follows abs(cos(angle)); the stroke moves between the right and left sides based on the cosine sign. A separate subdued U-shaped component handles the front-facing pose, and rear-facing overlap is occluded by an analytic mask of the photographed mug. A faint moving glaze streak supplies an additional object-fixed cue without replacing the photographed ceramic body.

The large photographed cylindrical wall shadow is intentionally preserved because the cylinder is rotationally symmetric. Only the photographed handle-shadow loop is locally removed with OpenCV Telea inpainting and reconstructed. Its new soft loop follows the same rotation, with the wall projection laterally inverted relative to the real handle. The loop is rasterized at 4× resolution, downsampled, Gaussian-softened, and applied as transmissive warm-black darkening. Its opacity fades continuously to zero at the front/back axes, avoiding a side-switch discontinuity. A foreground mug mask prevents the wall restoration and shadow layer from crossing the real mug.

Three very subdued antialiased markings follow elliptical paths on the existing black turntable. They provide a clockwise surface-motion cue while leaving the photographed turntable silhouette, tabletop, spotlight, camera, and surrounding scene unchanged.

artifact_io.save packs RGB as uint32 0xRRGGBB in a (144,256,20) tensor, writes the 20 PNGs, makes inspection.png, and embeds the lossless delta stream in index.html. transition_check.png is an automatically generated 4×-enhanced adjacent-difference contact sheet with per-transition mean absolute difference and changed-pixel counts. It was generated as a diagnostic artifact but was not opened or visually reviewed.
