OBSERVATION.md

Source observation and event plan

Scene measurements

  • Source frame: 256×144 RGB, locked camera, landscape orientation.
  • Background: a light warm-brown wooden tabletop fills the shot. The grain runs mostly diagonally/vertically through the image, and the illumination is soft and constant with mild object shadows.
  • Paper: a single vivid green sheet occupies the center. Its visible silhouette is approximately x=69–208, y=0–95, with the main body concentrated around x=70–160, y=20–95. It is already lifted and bent into a broad V/fold: the left held region leads into a high point around (119, 20), a tension valley/ridge lies around (136, 34), and the right side rises toward the upper-right grip. The low free point is around (112, 95).
  • Tools: two black grabber/scissor-like tools enter from the left and right upper edges. Both have blue inset/finger-loop areas. The left gripping end is near (69, 45); the right gripping end is near (207, 8). Their long axes point outward toward their respective frame edges.
  • Source-pixel measurements: the source contains 12,792 distinct RGB colors. A conservative green color classification covers roughly 4,700 pixels. The original PNG SHA-256 is 3c60077e6843de339025192ff74adf4df3dadfa78b987372778c0fd8a32fde37.
  • Motion constraints: static locked-off shot, constant 4 fps, real-time action. Background and illumination remain fixed. Only the two tools, their shadows, and the held paper move. The paper must stretch/fold first, then visibly split into two pieces whose newly torn edges are irregular rather than a clean straight cut.

Twenty-frame event plan

  1. Frame 00 (0.25 s): exact source image, no changes.
  2. Frame 01: both grabbers begin a subtle outward pull; paper still intact.
  3. Frame 02: outward displacement grows; the central fold sharpens.
  4. Frame 03: stronger tension straightens the upper paper span; no split yet.
  5. Frame 04: the paper narrows around the central tension ridge and starts to crease.
  6. Frame 05: a tiny dark/table-colored nick appears at the stressed upper-central fold.
  7. Frame 06: the nick becomes a short jagged tear; the halves remain connected below it.
  8. Frame 07: the tear advances downward in an uneven zigzag as both tools continue pulling.
  9. Frame 08: the split reaches about one third of the stressed paper width; edges curl slightly.
  10. Frame 09: the tear crosses the central bend; a narrow gap exposes the tabletop.
  11. Frame 10: the gap widens and the remaining paper bridge becomes small.
  12. Frame 11: the last fibers stretch; the two sides are nearly independent.
  13. Frame 12: complete separation occurs, with a small release snap outward.
  14. Frame 13: the left fragment moves left/down and rotates slightly counterclockwise; right fragment moves right/up and rotates slightly clockwise.
  15. Frame 14: both fragments continue outward with decreasing speed; torn edges are fully visible.
  16. Frame 15: brief elastic rebound/wobble in the fragments and grabbers.
  17. Frame 16: wobble damps; separation remains clear.
  18. Frame 17: fragments settle farther apart, each still held by its grabber.
  19. Frame 18: only a small residual motion remains.
  20. Frame 19 (5.00 s): stable final pose: two held green fragments separated by a tabletop-visible jagged gap.

The planned motion uses transformed source pixels for the paper and tools, analytic masks/warps for tension and separation, local tabletop reconstruction under moving objects, and subtle generated edge shading/fiber highlights along the progressive tear.

METHOD.md

Method

Generation approach

This continuation was generated deterministically from first_frame.png with local Python pixel operations only. No network source, external image, external engine, AI image model, reference case, or subagent was used.

  1. The source RGB image is classified in HSV/RGB space. The largest saturated-green component forms the paper mask. Separate cool/dark connected components inside fixed left and right regions form the two grabber masks.
  2. The tabletop hidden under the source objects is reconstructed with OpenCV Telea inpainting. A faint high-frequency residual sampled from visible lower-table pixels is added inside the hidden region so the exposed tear gap retains plausible wood texture rather than a flat fill.
  3. Frames 1–11 use a mild horizontal affine stretch about the paper's tension ridge. Both grabber masks translate outward on smoothstep timing. A hand-authored irregular polyline from the upper central notch toward the lower paper tip is progressively removed from the warped paper alpha mask to create the advancing tear.
  4. From frame 12, the paper mask is divided along that same jagged curve. Each source-pixel fragment receives its own affine transform: opposing translation, a small rotation about its held corner, and a damped release wobble. The tools move consistently with their held sides.
  5. Soft blurred masks add moving contact shadows. One-pixel darkening plus sparse light-green flecks along the new internal boundaries suggests exposed paper thickness/fibers. The source RGB pixels remain the basis of both paper fragments and both tools.
  6. artifact_io.save packs all frames as uint32 0xRRGGBB, writes the 20 RGB PNGs and pixels.npz, creates the inspection sheet, and embeds lossless delta packets in the standalone HTML player. transition_check.png is an automatically drawn numeric chart of adjacent-frame changed-pixel counts and mean RGB deltas.

Timing

  • Frames 00–04: initial outward pull and increasing tension.
  • Frames 05–11: progressive top-to-bottom jagged tear, retaining a lower bridge through frame 11.
  • Frame 12: complete release and outward snap.
  • Frames 13–19: opposing fragment separation followed by damped settling.

Verification

  • Frame tensor shape/dtype: (20, 144, 256, 3) / uint8 before packing.
  • Packed tensor shape/dtype: (144, 256, 20) / uint32.
  • frames/00.png was compared as decoded RGB and is exactly equal to decoded first_frame.png (maximum channel error 0).
  • All 20 PNGs are RGB, 256×14, and exactly equal to the corresponding frame reconstructed from the packed uint32 tensor.
  • Conservative green segmentation finds one principal paper component through frame 11, then two principal components in frames 12–19.
  • Adjacent-frame numeric deltas were checked for all 19 transitions. The intended separation transition (11→12) is the largest; late motion damps to a small residual by frame 19.
  • Generated outputs were inspected only through local code and numeric checks, as requested. inspection.png and transition_check.png were generated but not opened with an image viewing tool.
