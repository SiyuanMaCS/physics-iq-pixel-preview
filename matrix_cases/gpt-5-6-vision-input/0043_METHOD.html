Generation approach

This continuation was generated deterministically from first_frame.png with local Python pixel operations only. No network source, external image, external engine, AI image model, reference case, or subagent was used.

  1. The source RGB image is classified in HSV/RGB space. The largest saturated-green component forms the paper mask. Separate cool/dark connected components inside fixed left and right regions form the two grabber masks.
  2. The tabletop hidden under the source objects is reconstructed with OpenCV Telea inpainting. A faint high-frequency residual sampled from visible lower-table pixels is added inside the hidden region so the exposed tear gap retains plausible wood texture rather than a flat fill.
  3. Frames 1–11 use a mild horizontal affine stretch about the paper's tension ridge. Both grabber masks translate outward on smoothstep timing. A hand-authored irregular polyline from the upper central notch toward the lower paper tip is progressively removed from the warped paper alpha mask to create the advancing tear.
  4. From frame 12, the paper mask is divided along that same jagged curve. Each source-pixel fragment receives its own affine transform: opposing translation, a small rotation about its held corner, and a damped release wobble. The tools move consistently with their held sides.
  5. Soft blurred masks add moving contact shadows. One-pixel darkening plus sparse light-green flecks along the new internal boundaries suggests exposed paper thickness/fibers. The source RGB pixels remain the basis of both paper fragments and both tools.
  6. artifact_io.save packs all frames as uint32 0xRRGGBB, writes the 20 RGB PNGs and pixels.npz, creates the inspection sheet, and embeds lossless delta packets in the standalone HTML player. transition_check.png is an automatically drawn numeric chart of adjacent-frame changed-pixel counts and mean RGB deltas.

Timing

  • Frames 00–04: initial outward pull and increasing tension.
  • Frames 05–11: progressive top-to-bottom jagged tear, retaining a lower bridge through frame 11.
  • Frame 12: complete release and outward snap.
  • Frames 13–19: opposing fragment separation followed by damped settling.

Verification

  • Frame tensor shape/dtype: (20, 144, 256, 3) / uint8 before packing.
  • Packed tensor shape/dtype: (144, 256, 20) / uint32.
  • frames/00.png was compared as decoded RGB and is exactly equal to decoded first_frame.png (maximum channel error 0).
  • All 20 PNGs are RGB, 256×14, and exactly equal to the corresponding frame reconstructed from the packed uint32 tensor.
  • Conservative green segmentation finds one principal paper component through frame 11, then two principal components in frames 12–19.
  • Adjacent-frame numeric deltas were checked for all 19 transitions. The intended separation transition (11→12) is the largest; late motion damps to a small residual by frame 19.
  • Generated outputs were inspected only through local code and numeric checks, as requested. inspection.png and transition_check.png were generated but not opened with an image viewing tool.
