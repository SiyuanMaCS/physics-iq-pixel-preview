OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

The input is a 256 × 144 image. Coordinates below use the top-left origin, with x increasing rightward and y increasing downward. Bounds are approximate visual measurements, not segmentation masks.

  • A ribbed transparent beverage dispenser occupies approximately x=97–177, y=0–66. Its upper lid extends beyond the top of the frame. Bright red liquid occupies approximately x=99–168, y=32–61, with a nearly horizontal surface.
  • A dark faucet assembly is on the front-right of the dispenser, around x=143–172, y=29–60; its handle extends toward x=176, y=25. No liquid stream or falling droplet is visible below it.
  • The dispenser rests on a cylindrical woven basket, approximately x=63–184, y=48–122. Woven strands, the dark interior, and their shadows remain fixed.
  • A clear drinking glass stands in front of the basket, approximately x=142–182, y=66–139. Its upper rim is around y=68–75; the red liquid surface is around y=96–100 and its base around y=135. The glass is roughly half-filled, as specified in the prompt.
  • A brown wooden tabletop fills the lower part of the scene. A light gray wall occupies the background. The camera perspective, illumination, reflections, and shadows are fixed.

The prompt specifies a static setup and describes no actuation, pouring, displacement, or other action. The empty gap between the faucet and the glass also contains no existing stream to continue. The planned continuation therefore maintains physical equilibrium: no faucet motion, no flow, and no change in either liquid level. Each frame is an exact RGB copy of the initial image. This preserves the specified stationary scene without introducing additional events or objects.

METHOD.md

Generation method

This sequence uses exact local Python pixel replication. generate.py reads the original RGB image with Pillow, copies its uint8 pixels into twenty frames using NumPy, and exports them through the supplied output-only artifact_io.py helper. No image synthesis, external model, renderer, reference image, inpainting, warping, or simulated flow is used. The scene inputs are exclusively first_frame.png and prompt.txt; GENERATION_RULES.md supplies the output requirements.

The prompt describes a stationary dispenser over a glass that is half-filled with red liquid. It specifies no pouring action. The initial image contains no visible stream. A static equilibrium continuation therefore preserves both liquid surfaces and every object, reflection, shadow, and background pixel for the full five seconds. The twenty-frame plan was written in OBSERVATION.md before implementation.

Artifacts and timing

  • pixels.npz contains the pixels array: uint32, shape (144, 256, 20), axes (y, x, time), packed as 0xRRGGBB.
  • frames/00.png through frames/19.png contain 256 × 144 RGB images. Frame 00 retains the exact source PNG file bytes; all twenty frames retain its exact RGB pixels.
  • index.html embeds the pixels and supplies a standalone player with a 250 ms frame interval, seeking, replay, and looping. Twenty displayed intervals occupy 5.00 seconds. Sample timestamps run from 0.00 to 4.75 seconds.
  • inspection.png shows eight sampled frames using the supplied contact-sheet exporter. Its labels show each frame's interval-end time.
  • transition_check.png shows frames 00, 01, and 19 above three absolute RGB difference images. Black difference images mean zero pixel change; this is expected for the specified resting state.
  • verification.json records the output checks and all nineteen transition statistics.

Verification

The generator checks every PNG's size and RGB mode, repacks its pixels into 0xRRGGBB, and compares every value with its corresponding tensor slice. It also checks tensor shape, dtype, 24-bit range, exact source pixel equality for every frame, and source byte equality for frame 00. Every consecutive transition must have zero changed pixels and zero maximum channel difference. Run python generate.py inside this workspace to reproduce the output and checks.

The generator completed successfully. The initial image, generated inspection.png, and generated transition_check.png were actually opened with the image view tool. Visual review confirmed that the dispenser, glass, red liquid levels, basket, camera framing, and lighting are preserved across the contact sheet. The three difference panels are black, consistent with the recorded zero changes across all nineteen transitions. No revision was necessary.
