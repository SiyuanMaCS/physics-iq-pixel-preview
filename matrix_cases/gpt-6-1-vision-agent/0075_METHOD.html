This sequence is generated entirely with local Python pixel operations. The only scene inputs are first_frame.png and prompt.txt. No external images, models, rendering engines, or network resources are used. OBSERVATION.md was written before the generator.

The background is the supplied photograph. A local cubic polynomial fit reconstructs the wall hidden by the original upright shadow, with a small residual copied from adjacent wall pixels for texture. Row interpolation reconstructs the few pixels behind the original blue block and its reflected blue spill. The spotlight, tabletop, wall, platform silhouette, and broad horizontal platform shadow are retained from the photograph.

The moving object is a mathematical upright cuboid. Its cross-section is 0.25 × 0.19 in units of its height. It rotates clockwise about the vertical axis at 40 degrees per second, sampled every 0.25 seconds. Its initial cross-section angle is calibrated to the visible faces in the source image. Photographed strips from the two source faces supply blue surface textures; perspective warps map these strips onto projected cuboid faces. A fixed light direction adjusts their brightness as the surface normals turn. A dark top cap and a subtle highlight on the intersection of visible faces complete the block. Opposite faces reuse the photographed face textures because those surfaces are not visible in the input.

The wall shadow is calculated by extending rays from a fixed point light through all eight cuboid vertices to a fixed wall plane. The resulting convex hull changes width and top-edge slope continuously with the block. A soft, dark brown silhouette is composited over the reconstructed wall. It blends into the platform's existing horizontal shadow. The cuboid remains centered and upright throughout. The circular platform keeps its elliptical image silhouette; only a faint high-frequency component of its photographed top texture rotates. A blurred, attenuated reflection of the cuboid is clipped to the top of the platform.

Compositing uses 6× spatial supersampling, subpixel Gaussian edge softening, and area downsampling to 256 × 144 RGB pixels. Source pixels outside the reconstructed areas and the platform top are copied exactly. Frame 00 is the source image itself, without any resampling or modification. This is an image-based continuation with calibrated geometric projection, rather than a reconstruction of hidden room geometry.

Artifacts and validation

  • generate.py: reproducible local generator; run with python generate.py.
  • pixels.npz: pixels, a uint32 array of shape (144, 256, 20), packed as 0xRRGGBB.
  • frames/00.png through frames/19.png: twenty RGB frames.
  • inspection.png: eight representative frames.
  • transition_check.png: adjacent-frame pairs, amplified absolute differences, and overlays, including frames 00 and 01.
  • index.html: standalone embedded-data player at 4 fps, with playback, seeking, and replay controls.
  • verification.json: numerical checks and transition measurements.

The generator checks the tensor shape and dtype, exact equality of frame 00 to the source RGB image, and equality of every PNG to its decoded packed tensor slice. Twenty samples, each displayed for a quarter second, form five seconds of playback. Visual inspection and any resulting revisions are recorded below after using the image viewing tool.

Visual review and revision

The image viewing tool was used on the initial frame, enlarged object details, the reconstructed background, the modeled initial orientation, and both versions of the inspection and transition sheets. The first review identified an overly pointed shadow cap and a faint bright seam where the upright shadow met the horizontal platform shadow. The revision increased the calibrated light-to-object and object-to-wall distances while preserving shadow magnification, added the measured vertical projection shear, and extended the shadow hull into the platform shadow with a darkening composite. A subtle brown interior gradient reproduces the photographed shadow more closely. The final sheets were then viewed again; the shadow joins the horizontal shape continuously, and the block and shadow narrow and widen together while the background stays fixed.

The final tensor and all twenty PNGs passed the generator's equality checks. Mean absolute RGB change across the source-to-frame-01 transition is 0.392 on the 0–255 channel scale; subsequent transitions range from 0.059 to 0.342, with differences concentrated around the turning block, its shadow and reflection, and faint platform texture. These are numerical continuity measurements, not an external quality score. Details unseen in the original photograph are supplied by the explicit geometric and texture-reuse assumptions above.
