Method and validation

This is a local, deterministic Python pixel continuation of the supplied photograph. It uses NumPy, Pillow, and SciPy image operations; it does not use external images, AI models, a 3D renderer, a physics engine, network access, or other cases.

generate.py preserves the source photograph's fixed camera, background, tabletop, bowl, and match bundle. It separates the visible flame and glow from an interpolated local glass background. The cropped flame is extended a short distance above the image, then resampled about the stationary match heads, with small deterministic variations in position and scale. Its scale falls to zero after the glass opening enters the liquid. Faint procedural Gaussian wisps rise from the heads and disperse.

The source jar is already mostly in view, with its top cropped and its opening near the red liquid. The continuation moves its faint side reflections and rounded lower rim downward by 11 pixels over two seconds, using local background repair and bilinear sampling. The existing transparent interior remains registered to the stationary scene visible through it. The bowl's foreground lip and match bundle are restored as occluders. The glass and liquid effects are image-space approximations, not a simulation of combustion, pressure, or full optical refraction.
