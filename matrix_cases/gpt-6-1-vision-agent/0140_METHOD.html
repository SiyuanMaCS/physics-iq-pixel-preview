Intended local pixel method

METHOD.md

Method

This sequence was made entirely with local Python pixel operations using NumPy, Pillow, and SciPy. The only scene inputs were first_frame.png and prompt.txt. No network, external reference, learned image model, external rendering engine, or subagent was used. artifact_io.py and the supplied HTML template handle output packaging only.

The original photograph supplies the stationary table, wall, glass board, folded paper, shadows, and sunlight. Measured narrow masks locate the initial smoke filaments. Horizontal interpolation of neighboring unmasked photograph pixels estimates the background underneath those filaments. The positive RGB difference becomes a light-scattering smoke field, and subtracting that field from the input forms a stationary background. This decomposition is a local pixel estimate, not a reconstruction of a three-dimensional scene.

The smoke field is sampled at three times the output resolution and advanced in 1/32-second steps with semi-Lagrangian advection. An upward velocity that increases with height, small time-varying lateral currents, and mild diffusion transport the initial smoke out of frame. Two narrow Gaussian emitters at the paper continually replenish the smoke; the left emitter follows a leftward current. Emission fluctuates smoothly. The RGB smoke field is resampled and added to the recovered photograph, with paper occlusion. The first half second smoothly transitions from the original smoke decomposition into its advected state. Frame 00 bypasses generation and uses the exact input pixels.

Burning is represented by gradual irregular brown/char shading restricted to the existing jagged paper edge, a subpixel resampling of that edge to suggest slight curling, and a small supersampled translucent warm flame. A low glow and weak board reflection vary with the flame. The folded paper stays in place. This is a photographic pixel animation with an approximate smoke-flow model; it is not a calibrated combustion or fluid simulation.

The random seed is fixed at 140. Running python generate.py regenerates the 20 frames, packed tensor, contact sheet, transition diagnostic, standalone HTML player, and .tmp/verification.json.
