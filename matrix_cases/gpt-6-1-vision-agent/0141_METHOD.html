METHOD.md

Method

This sequence was generated locally from first_frame.png and prompt.txt using Python, NumPy, Pillow, SciPy and OpenCV pixel operations. No network, external scene assets, AI models, external rendering engines, or subagents were used. OBSERVATION.md was written before the generation code and records the measured scene and intended events.

The static photograph supplies the wood, glass board, folded-paper detail, perspective, and illumination. Small hand-defined coordinate masks isolate the existing flame and wisps. OpenCV Telea inpainting estimates the underlying background only for these masks. During this estimation the bright paper and reflection are also masked to avoid pulling their white pixels into the flame background; the source paper and reflection are retained in the final composite. Subtracting the estimated background from the source creates source-textured flame and smoke residuals.

The blue flame residual undergoes bilinear coordinate warps anchored above the paper. Smooth time-dependent functions vary its height, bend and brightness. Two small, supersampled pale amber flame tongues form at exposed paper folds. The original wisps deform and fade while replacement smoke appears smoothly.

The white smoke uses a deterministic stream of overlapping elliptical density parcels, initialized with emission before time zero. Parcel age controls upward motion, gently changing lateral drift, expansion and dissipation. Continuous, advected multiscale noise modulates their density. Off-white smoke is alpha-composited at low opacity, so the background remains visible. Smoke and supplementary flame layers are computed at four times the output resolution and downsampled before compositing; the photograph itself is not globally resampled.

The paper retains its photographic folds. A small spatial warp lowers its upper folds by at most 0.85 pixel, anchored at its foot. Exposure masks gradually darken localized edges and creases toward brown and charcoal, with a slight nearby yellow stain. The reflected light on the glass fluctuates subtly with the fire. A spatial support mask limits all synthesis to the combustion region; the rest of the scene remains pixel-exact.

These are image-based procedural approximations of combustion and buoyant smoke, not a full fluid or chemical simulation. The motion is deliberately modest over the five-second duration: the paper remains folded and continues burning at the final frame.

Verification and review

The generator verifies the tensor shape, type and range; decodes all 20 tensor slices and compares them exactly with the saved RGB PNGs; checks frame 00 against the source; and checks that every pixel outside x=73–154, y=0–95 is unchanged. The standalone player's embedded first frame and 19 delta packets are independently decoded and checked against the tensor. The original PNG and output frame 00 are also compared as files.

The actual image view tool was used to inspect the input photograph, enlarged details, the generated inspection.png, and all adjacent pairs in transition_check.png. Revisions corrected resampling of the static background, refined the source wisp masks, and prevented white-paper pixels from bleeding into the inpainted background. The final review shows a fixed camera and setup, fluctuating flame tongues, translucent rising smoke, and gradual localized paper darkening.
