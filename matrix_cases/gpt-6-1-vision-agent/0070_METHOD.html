This is a local photographic pixel animation made with Python, NumPy, SciPy and Pillow. It uses only first_frame.png and the instructions in prompt.txt. No external images, network access, generative model, 3D engine or other simulation engine was used.

The input is preserved exactly as frame 00. For frames 01–19, the original photograph supplies all textures. Bilinear sampling at four times the output resolution moves the glass's existing liquid surface upwards. A piecewise vertical map stretches the cloudy liquid beneath the surface while keeping the bottom anchored. Feathered interior masks preserve the glass rim, sides and base. A small color adjustment and smooth time-varying density patterns approximate grapefruit juice mixing into the already tinted water.

The receiving level rises at 3 source pixels per second, reaching 14.25 pixels above its initial position at frame 19. The source meniscus moves down by 3.5625 pixels. This uses an approximate four-to-one cross-sectional area ratio inferred from the containers' visible diameters. It is a visual volume-balance approximation rather than a calibrated measurement. The tap remains open throughout, consistent with the visible stream in the reference.

A narrow translucent shaded jet follows the existing outlet into the rising surface, with subpixel thickness and highlight variations. Analytic elliptical ripples, mild submerged variation and a few tiny bright bubbles represent the impact. These effects stay inside the glass. Subpixel changes are integrated as differences from the enlarged original, so pixels outside the fluid masks stay exactly equal to the photograph. The camera, stand, table, background and apparatus remain stationary.

The 20 RGB frames are 256 × 144 pixels, shown at 4 fps for 5 seconds. Their sample times are 0.00 through 4.75 seconds; each frame is held for 0.25 seconds. pixels.npz contains the key pixels, a uint32 tensor of shape (144, 256, 20), with each element encoded as 0xRRGGBB. frames/00.png through frames/19.png contain the corresponding RGB images. index.html is a standalone player with all frame data embedded.

Checks

The generator verifies the frame count, RGB dimensions, uint32 tensor shape, exact equality of frame 00 to the input, and exact equality of all 20 PNGs to the corresponding tensor planes. It also checks that all pixels outside the fluid edit regions remain unchanged. Numerical results are written to verification.json. inspection.png shows eight representative frames. transition_check.png shows selected adjacent transitions and 8× amplified absolute RGB differences.

Visual review completed with the image-view tool: the generated inspection sheet, transition diagnostic and enlarged glass crops were examined. The liquid rises progressively, the pour remains connected to the tap, and the background stays fixed. One revision adjusted the back edge of the meniscus and clamped its resampled texture below the original boundary to reduce displaced wicker highlights. The final inspection sheet, transition diagnostic and glass close-up were viewed again after regeneration.

Limitations

This is a local image-space continuation. Depth, flow and concentration are approximate; there is no full three-dimensional fluid solution. Glass reflections are preserved from the source and the liquid textures are resampled from the existing photograph. The stream and surface details are analytic pixel effects.
