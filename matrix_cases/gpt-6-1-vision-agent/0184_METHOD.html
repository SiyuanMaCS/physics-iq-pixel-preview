+    z = np.clip((x-a)/(b-a), 0, 1)

+def sample(src, xx, yy):
+    xx = np.clip(xx, 0, W-1.001)

+    return ((src[yi, xi]*(1-fx)+src[yi, xi+1]*fx)*(1-fy)

METHOD.md

Method and completed artifacts

This is a local photographic pixel continuation of first_frame.png, guided by prompt.txt. It uses Python, NumPy, and Pillow. No external images, trained models, network resources, 3D rendering engines, or subagents were used.

The first frame is copied byte-for-byte in decoded RGB pixel values. For subsequent frames, all pixels begin with the same source photograph. The juice meniscus and body are bilinearly resampled upward inside a soft, measured glass silhouette. A smooth vertical mapping translates the existing elliptical surface while leaving the rounded bottom anchored. This preserves the original orange texture, glass shading, and meniscus detail. The level increases evenly by 13.2 pixels across the sequence, with the surface center moving from y=88.45 to y=75.25. A restrained RGB adjustment approximates the lighter orange appearance as water mixes into the juice. The added water fraction is estimated from the increase relative to an initial 43-pixel effective liquid column.

The supplied photograph already contains a faint water stream. Its continuation combines that source detail with a narrow mathematical jet: gravity-dependent width, small changing centerline perturbations, local source-image resampling for refraction, and weak moving highlights. Elliptical ripple rings, an impact dimple, and several tiny transient bubbles are composited on the liquid surface. These elements are evaluated at four times the output resolution and area-averaged for subpixel coverage. The stream ends at the rising surface; all disturbances remain within the glass.

The inferred dispenser meniscus near y=31 is shifted down by up to 2.508 pixels. A localized difference of blurred source samples supplies the broad waterline adjustment while retaining most fixed glass rib detail. The receiver-to-reservoir volume relationship uses a constant approximate area ratio of 0.19. This is an image-based, analytic approximation of filling and mixing rather than a numerical fluid simulation.

generate.py deterministically creates all outputs using the provided output-only helper in artifact_io.py. pixels.npz contains pixels with shape (144, 256, 20), dtype uint32, and packed values 0xRRGGBB; axes are height, width, and time. frames/00.png through frames/19.png are RGB images at 256 × 144. Twenty frames displayed for 250 ms each give five seconds of playback at 4 fps; their sample times are 0.00 through 4.75 seconds.

inspection.png is the requested overview of eight representative frames. transition_check.png shows selected adjacent transitions and enlarged glass crops. index.html is a standalone interactive player with embedded pixel data, playback, seeking, and looping. verification.json records validation outcomes and adjacent-frame difference measurements.

The overview and transition sheets were opened with the image View tool and visually reviewed.
