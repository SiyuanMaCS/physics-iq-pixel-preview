OBSERVATION.md

Scene observation and event plan

Written before implementing the generator. The source image was inspected with the image view tool. Coordinates below are approximate, in source pixels, with the origin at the upper left.

Measurements

  • Source: first_frame.png, RGB, 256 × 144 pixels.
  • Yellow rubber duck: approximately x=105–150, y=38–87. The head is around (129, 52); the rounded body is around (128, 73). Two dark eyes and an orange bill face toward the camera, slightly to image right.
  • The bill occupies approximately x=124–144, y=58–66. The eyes are near (125, 56) and (138, 55).
  • The duck rests on a light brown wooden tabletop. Its existing contact shadow is beneath and slightly to the right of the body. The wood grain and all existing shadows must retain their source positions.
  • The plain white background appears gray/beige under the existing illumination. The rear table boundary runs approximately from (0, 56) to (192, 19), then toward (255, 38). The tabletop fills the remainder of the image.
  • There are no hands, apparatus, labels, overlays, or additional objects.
  • Camera and framing are fixed. The prompt explicitly states that the duck is stationary and describes no further action. Constant illumination and an unchanged image are therefore the intended continuation.

METHOD.md

Generation method

The prompt describes a stationary rubber duck in a fixed, locked-off shot and specifies no action. The continuation is a five-second hold of the supplied image: 20 RGB frames at 256 × 144, displayed at 4 fps.

generate.py reads first_frame.png with Pillow, then uses NumPy to repeat its RGB pixel array 20 times. No drawing, transformations, inpainting, synthetic noise, shading changes, external models, or rendering engines are used to generate the scene. All scene pixels, including the duck, table, background, and contact shadow, remain exactly as supplied.

The provided output helper artifact_io.py packs RGB values as uint32 0xRRGGBB and writes pixels.npz under the key pixels, with axes ordered height × width × time: (144, 256, 20). It also creates the PNG sequence, the eight-frame inspection sheet, and the standalone HTML player. The generator copies the source PNG bytes into all 20 frame files after exporting, preserving both pixel equality and identical file contents. The player embeds the initial pixel image and 19 empty change packets, and advances every 250 milliseconds. Frame 00 starts at 0.00 seconds and frame 19 starts at 4.75 seconds; each frame is held for 0.25 seconds.

transition_check.png shows frames 00/01, 09/10, and 18/19 alongside their absolute RGB differences. Its labels and inspection-sheet labels are outside the scene images and do not appear in the frame sequence.

Reproduce all generated artifacts with python generate.py from this workspace. The script verifies every PNG against its packed tensor slice and against the input pixels and file bytes, checks all 19 adjacent transitions, and checks the HTML player's embedded pixels and timing. The machine-readable results are saved in verification.json.

Validation

  • 20 RGB PNGs at 256 × 144.
  • 20/20 PNGs match their uint32 tensor slices exactly.
  • 20/20 PNGs match the source pixels and source file bytes exactly.
  • All 19 adjacent transitions have zero changed pixels.
  • Standalone player contains the verified initial pixels and 19 empty deltas.
  • Visually reviewed inspection.png and transition_check.png with the image view tool after generation. The sampled scene images retain the source composition, duck shape, table grain, background, and shadows. The displayed difference images are uniformly black, consistent with the numerical checks.
