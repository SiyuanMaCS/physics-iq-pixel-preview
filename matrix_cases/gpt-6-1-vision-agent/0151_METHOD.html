METHOD.md

Method

This is a local Python pixel compositor built with NumPy, Pillow, and SciPy. Its only visual source is first_frame.png; its action is based on prompt.txt. It uses no network, external references, learned models, rendering engine, or subagents. Run python generate.py from this workspace to regenerate the outputs.

The supplied image is retained exactly for frame 00. For later frames, the initial ball and its shadow are removed from a small elliptical region of the tabletop. A wood-texture donor comes from the same photograph, 31 pixels left and 26 pixels above the repair region. A discrete Poisson solve transfers that donor's image gradients while keeping the original pixels on the repair boundary. All unrelated photographed objects and the surrounding table remain fixed.

The ball is extracted with a soft elliptical silhouette centered at approximately (121.1, 62.8), with radii 8.6 and 9.65 pixels. Its photographic colors and smooth lighting are preserved. Small surface details are sampled with analytic rolling hemisphere coordinates, using traveled screen distance divided by apparent radius as the rolling angle. A weak curved seam modulation supplements the original blurred tennis-ball surface. This is an approximate surface animation of the photographed ball, rather than a reconstruction of unseen geometry or a full rigid-body simulation.

The photographed shadow is estimated as smooth log transmission from pixels outside the ball. Ball and shadow move together using subpixel bilinear sampling, with a feathered exterior that prevents rectangular patch boundaries. Shadow transmission multiplies the local table brightness. A locally drawn polygon follows the photographed foreground edge of the fabric box, preserving occlusion when the ball reaches it.

The prescribed trajectory advances from x=121.1 to x=190.35 while y decreases by 0.052 times the horizontal displacement. Contact occurs in frame 08 at t=2.00 seconds. The sphere radius is accounted for against the diagonal box edge. A small rebound then slows to a stop at x=180.4 by frame 16. The supported box is treated as stationary. The camera and illumination remain fixed.

Review and revisions

OBSERVATION.md was written before the generation code and contains the scene measurements and twenty-frame plan. The source image was viewed with the image tool, including an enlarged view. The initial generated contact sheet and transition comparison were also viewed. That review revealed a faint residual stain in the first tabletop repair. Several local repairs were compared visually, and the final Poisson repair removed the visible residual. The shadow extraction was then softened and its support widened to eliminate moving patch boundaries. The revised transition image and final inspection sheet were viewed with the image tool.
