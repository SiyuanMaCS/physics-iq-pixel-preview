METHOD.md

Actual generation method

The continuation uses only local Python pixel operations on first_frame.png, guided by prompt.txt. NumPy computes masks and analytic time-dependent fields, SciPy supplies bilinear image sampling, and Pillow writes and displays image artifacts. The supplied artifact_io.py serializes the results and fills the standalone HTML player template. No external AI model, rendering engine, network, reference image, or externally supplied mask was used.

The source photograph already contains a faint stream from the dispenser into the receiving glass. The animation continues that stream. The scene background and camera remain exactly fixed. Frame 00 bypasses all rendering and is copied directly from the RGB source array.

Liquid and optical motion

The receiving liquid surface is measured as a projected ellipse with center approximately (120.2, 97.2), horizontal radius 16.1 pixels, and vertical radius 5.3 pixels. Its center rises to y=80.4 over the 19 subsequent samples.
