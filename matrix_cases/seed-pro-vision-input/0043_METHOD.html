METHOD — case_0043

Two blue-black grabber tools pull a twisted green paper strip from opposite corners until it tears (20 frames, 256×144, 4 fps). Everything is local pixel math on first_frame.png: numpy, OpenCV and PIL only. No external images, models, engines, references or network access. 1. Scene decomposition (measured from frame 0)

Numeric measurements (colour masks, connected components, row-run geometry) are in OBSERVATION.md. Key anchors, all in frame-0 pixel coordinates:

  • left held corner A0 = (74,45) inside the left tool's jaw,
  • right held corner tip T0 = (207,2), right tool rotation pivot JR=(200,18),
  • tear location: the twist waist near (158,28), nominal ribbon parameter US = 0.52,
  • total tool travel: left D_A = (−26,+3) with +0.9° tilt, right D_T = (+18,−7) with −1.1° tilt.

The input frame is segmented into three layers:

  1. background (wood table + wall),
  2. paper — soft alpha paper_a from greenness z = G − 0.5(R+B), hard 1.0 inside the strict green silhouette, Gaussian-soft only on the 1–2 px anti-aliased rim,
  3. tools — the two large connected components of a black/blue/teal/dark colour key, morphological-close cleaned with only small (<40 px) enclosed holes filled so the blue jaw ring stays hollow.

2. Background plate

build_background() makes one static clean plate (the camera is locked off):

  • Telea inpainting (cv2.inpaint, radius 6) of the dilated paper+tool silhouette;
  • robust per-row table colour trend blended 45 % into the inpainted table zone and a 51×51 local wall mean blended 30 % into the wall zone;
  • wood-grain realism restored by horizontal row-wise interpolation of the high-frequency residual (large-scale blur subtracted), mixed 50/50 with a cyclic donor strip from a clean part of the same row;
  • soft low-frequency noise added in the wall zone;
  • feathered only at the fill boundary — every originally clean pixel is byte-identical to frame 0.

The plate is reused for all 19 generated frames, so nothing in the scene moves except paper, tools and their soft contact shadows.

3. Paper parameterisation (frame-0 source space)

  • Ribbon coordinate u along the held-corner diagonal: for every green pixel row, the green run(s) are mapped linearly between hand-fitted transverse edges of the twisted ribbon (T_TAIL / T_BRANCH / T_WAIST / T_FACE_UL/UR tables, see OBSERVATION.md). Above y=21 occluded bits of the narrow strip within 14 px are merged as one run. Pixels just outside the silhouette inherit nearest-neighbour u via a scipy EDT-index fill.
  • Crack centreline SEAM: the iso-u (|u−US|<0.025) curve through the waist, resampled to 6 polyline points. For every pixel, SMAP gives the arc position along this polyline and SDIST the perpendicular distance.
  • Raggedness jag(s): a smooth random knot curve (7 Gaussian knots, seed 43) offsetting the crack centreline; the open gap is |SDIST − jag(SMAP)| < GAPW[t] in source space.

4. Motion model

Pull progress is a piecewise smoothstep keyframe:

  • t0–9: tensioning to 55 % of total travel,
  • t9–11: fast extension while the crack runs (FRONT 0.08 → 0.25 → 0.55 → 1.1; it finishes through the last ligament during t11),
  • t12–19: final 15 % to full travel, then settle.

Per frame, every source pixel gets a dense destination map:

  • Taut sheet taut: chord interpolation between the rigidly moved grip points, with the transverse offset from the rest chord collapsing by a factor k = 1 − 0.25·p·(sin πu)^1.6 — the strip visibly straightens and the waist narishes while tension rises; a small 6 % axial stretch is included.
  • Left torn piece: rigid motion of the left tool about A0, plus a weighted pendulum swing PHI[t] (peaks +9° at t14, damps to 0°) and a gravity sag SAGL[t] weighted toward the torn edge.
  • Right torn piece: rigid motion pinned about JR/T0 plus a sag SAGR[t] weighted toward its torn edge; no flutter (an earlier flutter term intermittently severed the 1–2 px throat and was removed).
  • The relaxation weight E blends each side from taut-map to piece-map behind the running crack front (REL[t] ramped by distance behind FRONT[t]), so pixels relax smoothly as the crack passes.
  • Shading: brighter while under tension, relaxed tone after tear.

5. Rasterisation (render_paper)

The dense analytic map is rendered with a forward-warping triangle raster on a 3 px source lattice:

  • lattice vertices carry a motion-branch tag (0 taut, 1 torn-left, 2 torn-right); each triangle is assigned the majority tag of its vertices and warped with that branch's destination positions;
  • per-branch destination lattices are made continuous (_piece_lattice): non-owner lattice nodes inherit the nearest owner pixel's position via scipy EDT indices, so seam-adjacent triangles stay short instead of stretching across the open crack;
  • each triangle is supersampled 2×2; source texels are bilinear-sampled from the frame-0 paper (colour + soft alpha), and subpixels whose sampled tag disagrees with the triangle's branch are rejected (no bleed across motion boundaries);
  • the crack gap is sampled exactly in source space per subpixel (acc_gf > 0.5 cuts the assembled pixel) — an earlier vertex-interpolated and dilated band severed the narrow right-piece throat;
  • coverage > 0.5 snaps fully opaque so compression in the warp does not let background speckle through the paper interior.

Throat bridge (t ≥ 12)

Frame 0 has a genuine 1-row silhouette pinch at the twist waist (no green pixels across source row y=33 for a few columns), and the analytic ownership seam runs right through it. Strict ownership therefore split the narrow right piece into islands, and the rendered tail flickered between one and two connected components on alternating frames. Fix, in source space only:

  • each torn piece's solid footprint is morphological-closed with a 7×7 kernel (spans the 1-row pinch), keeping only real fragments (≥15 px, which drops the 4–6 px occlusion islands);
  • closure overlaps are assigned to the narrow right piece;
  • subpixels inside a piece's bridged footprint are allowed through and the crack-band cut is waived there; the pieces still separate on screen because their destination transforms diverge, and the visible crack between them stays open (0–2 green pixels per column in the gap zone).

The taut branch gets the same pinch-fill (gap cut retained) so the 1–2 px self-occlusion fold near the right jaw does not flicker before the tear; strict tag ownership applies everywhere else (t < 12).

Pinhole seal and torn-edge shading

After the gap cut, any remaining void with paper on ≥6 of 8 neighbours and outside the dilated crack band is filled from the mean colour of its solid neighbours, for two passes (closes only 1–2 px interior corridors; an exterior pixel beside an edge has at most ~4 such neighbours, and crack-band pixels are excluded, so the silhouette never fattens and the tear stays open). Rim pixels of the crack then receive deterministic per-pixel ragged shading (0.72× or 1.18× from a stable integer hash — no temporal flicker).
