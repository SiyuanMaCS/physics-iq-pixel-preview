  • mug opening ellipse measured to center (158, 53.5), rx ≈ 33, ry ≈ 16.5.

2. Clean background plate

All dynamic pixels (grabber + domino glow/halo) are removed and restored:

  • tabletop: horizontal mirror about x = 156.5, with shifted-column fallback;
  • opening/rim: mirror about the ellipse axis x = 158, 180° rotation fallback, then a normalised degree-3 polynomial surface fit to clean liquid/rim pixels for any residual site;
  • restored pixels get one horizontal 3-tap smoothing pass to suppress seams. The plate is identical to the source wherever nothing was removed (verified on static crops: table corners, handle, top-right dark object are byte-identical in every frame).

3. Sprites

  • Grabber: black and blue fingers are cut as RGBA sprites with a 1-px feather whose alpha comes from colour distance to the plate; feather pixels are restricted to their own finger's colour family so opening the jaws does not drag the other finger's colour. Frames 3–10 stamp the two sprites with an opening offset (2–3 px) and a rising offset (0 → 22 px, exiting the top edge by frame 10), plus a faint 1-px upward motion-blur ghost.
  • Domino: an analytic rounded white bar (soft edges, brighter centre band, bluish glow halo) for the part above the surface, and a depth-faded bluish column for the part seen through the translucent liquid (Gaussian core + in-scatter side lobes, smooth fade with depth, tip taper, clipped to the opening ellipse).
  • A photographic domino sprite is also cut from frame 0 (white/halo masks, gated to the tile column; rim pixels explicitly excluded). It is stamped shifted by the fall offset at frames 4 (70%), 5 (30%) and 6 (12%) so the real photographed texture — including the wide in-scatter halo — carries through the first moments after release instead of snapping to the analytic look. Frame 3 keeps original photographed pixels everywhere except where the fingers used to cover the tile's gripped top.

4. Motion and physics

  • Hold frames 00–02 are byte-identical copies of first_frame.png; frame 03 opens the fingers (release).
  • Domino vertical offsets (px) 04→14: 1, 3, 7, 12, 19, 24, 28, 32, 35, 37, 38 (accelerating free fall, then viscous drag in the liquid, tip reaching the mug floor). A slight rightward lean eases in over offsets 35→38.
  • Long-exposure motion blur: 7 weighted sub-step copies per frame for both the above-surface bar and the submerged column.
  • Splash (frames 8–10): expanding crown ring and dark entry depression at the back-centre of the surface (entry point ≈ (167, 42)), four ballistic 1–2 px droplets that rise and fall back, a few small bubbles at frames 11–13.
  • Ripples: five rings born on frames 9, 10, 12, 13, 15, drawn in surface coordinates (vertical compression 0.45), radiating to 10–28 px while fading over 8 frames; only centre/front-facing arcs are kept (back arcs meet the rim), with a paired dark trough for young rings. After frame 15 only faint remnants remain and the last transition mean abs-diff is 0.22/channel.

5. Output

artifact_io.save writes pixels.npz (uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, shape 144×256×20), frames/00..19.png, inspection.png, and the standalone index.html. transition_check.png (written separately) is a 2×10 contact sheet of ×4-amplified per-transition absolute differences with labels.

Verification (numeric, all passed)

  • frames/00.png is byte-identical to first_frame.png;
  • every PNG packs to exactly its pixels.npz column (all 20 frames);
  • the base64 delta stream embedded in index.html reconstructs all 20 frames byte-exactly (independent Python reimplementation of the player's patcher);
  • changed pixels never leave the local envelope x∈[120,220] y∈[0,120];
  • static crops (two table corners, top-right object, mug handle) identical in all 20 frames;
  • transition changed-pixel counts: 0 while held, peak 1022 at release/first fall step, then a monotone decay to ~130 as ripples settle.

Known simplifications

  • The liquid fill is mirror/model based; a handful of mirror-fallback pixels differ subtly from a true renderer but stay inside the opening and are soon covered by the splash/ripple sequence.
  • Through-liquid visibility is an empirical depth fade matched to frame 0's measured brightness (bright core to ≈ d 13, gone by d 20), not a volumetric simulation.
  • Ripples at this resolution are 1-px Gaussian arcs; late rings read as faint mottling rather than perfectly smooth wavefronts.
