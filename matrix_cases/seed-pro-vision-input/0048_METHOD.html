  • Mug portion (removal ∩ routed silhouette): horizontal mirror about x=119, offsets k ∈ {0,±2,±4,±6,±8,±12,±16,±22} and dy ∈ {0,±1,±2}, first source that is inside mugS, unremoved, and in the same rim-vs-liquid zone; analytic dark navy/tea fallback otherwise.

6. Outputs and verification

artifact_io.save writes pixels.npz (shape (144,256,20), uint32 0xRRGGBB), the 20 PNGs, inspection.png, and index.html; the script also writes transition_check.png (six frame-pair/diff triplets). The script then re-loads pixels.npz from disk and asserts: tensor shape and dtype, every PNG equals its frame, every packed uint32 equals the tensor, and frames[0] == first_frame.png; additionally cmp confirms frames/00.png is byte-identical to first_frame.png.

Numeric QA performed (probes in .tmp/, no image viewing):

  • f00 byte-exact; f1/f2 white mask is exactly f0 translated +2/+4 px.
  • Per-frame change masks vs f0 are confined to the tool corridor (x27–130, y≤63 while the tool is present) plus the mug silhouette and the block path; zero pixels change outside that union in every frame.
  • Temporal changes after f9 (ripple-only phase) are fully inside the mug silhouette (0 px outside), and pixel std over f12–f19 is 0 outside the analytic mug ellipse.
  • Block near-white counts shrink monotonously 541 (f0–f2) → 219 (f6) → ~0 (f8); no bright block pixels survive at the front lip in f7/f8 (residual values are tea-dark), and the far-wall sliver peaks around (42,41,57) at f7 before fading.
  • Droplets are only ever pasted inside the mug silhouette; crown/rings only inside the inset liquid disk.
  • Wedge/wood plate seam gradients checked row by row; the reconstructed corner is near-black wedge up to x≈41 with a smooth ramp to oak over ≈4 px, and late frames show no residual gray halo.

7. Known simplifications

  • The clean table is a per-row linear colour model with copied grain residuals, not a full texture model; it is only revealed under the small tool footprint and at the wedge boundary.
  • Plate rim/liquid under the occluded back strip comes from mirroring the visible half; real mugs are not perfectly symmetric.
  • The tile is treated as a rigid stamped sprite with global submersion darkening (no per-pixel refraction, caustics, or motion blur); motion blur is approximated only by the soft fringe alpha the sprite already has in frame 0.
  • The far-wall end-face treatment at f7/f8 (hard hide outside the disk, 82%/92% dimmed sliver inside) is a visual-physics judgment call.
