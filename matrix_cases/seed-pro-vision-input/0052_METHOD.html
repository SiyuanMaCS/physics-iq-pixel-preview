What happens

The blue/black grabber releases the two rightmost balls of the Newton's cradle. The clip plays idealised two-in/two-out momentum transfer over 5 s (20 frames, 4 fps): jaws open (frames 1–2) → the pair falls to the row and strikes (f3) → the two left balls swing out and back (f4–7) → the right pair swings out and back (f8–11), repeating with light damping (cycles f12–15 and f16–19 ending on the incoming impact). The centre ball (ball 3) is stationary.
