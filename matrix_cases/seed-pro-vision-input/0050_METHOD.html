Rendering method (preview; see METHOD.md after implementation)

METHOD.md

METHOD — case_0050 (PixelWorld-Vision-Input)

How generate.py turns first_frame.png + prompt.txt into 20 frames at 256×144, 4 fps. Everything is local Python pixel math (numpy, scipy.ndimage, PIL). No external models, engines, network access, masks or other cases are used. Generated frames were checked only with code and numeric diagnostics (never with an image viewing tool).

1. Physical reading

The prompt's action: the wooden stick fixed to the black rotating platform turns clockwise and pushes the first domino of the right-hand group; the group of three falls in a cascade that stops at the gap, leaving the left group of four standing. All measurements (pivot, arm length/angle, domino hinges and colours, platform ellipse, horizon) are documented in OBSERVATION.md; they were obtained numerically from frame 0.

Key constants used by the model:

  • arm pivot PIVOT = (215.0, 40.5), length L = 32.88 px, initial angle φ0 = 241.9° measured clockwise from 12 o'clock, angular speed +7.5°/frame (30°/s);
  • tip position tip(φ) = pivot + L·(sin φ, −cos φ) (screen coordinates, y down), so φ increasing is clockwise: the tip sweeps 8 o'clock → 9 o'clock → 12 o'clock and ends at ≈ (228.6, 10.6) in frame 19;
  • domino hinges/colours DOM and the frame-0 quads OBS_QUAD;
  • per-frame tilt keyframes ANGLES (degrees from vertical, + = falling left): B4 starts at −15°, crosses vertical in frame 02, W3 starts at frame 04, B3 starts at frame 07, and the wave is fully flat by frame 16 (full table in OBSERVATION.md and in generate.py).

The keyframes are hand-tuned kinematics (piecewise constant angular rates), not a rigid-body contact simulation: they encode the contact sequence (stick → B4 → W3 → B3 → gap) at the observed geometry.

2. Overall pipeline

For every frame t = 1…19 (frame 00 is first_frame.png, copied byte-for-byte):

  1. start from a static clean background plate (everything except the three animated dominoes and the arm);
  2. add analytic, tilt-dependent contact shadows for the dominoes and the moving arm shadow, gaussian-blurred and multiplied onto the plate;
  3. composite the three dominoes (B3, W3, B4) as homography-warped sprites with a thickness band;
  4. composite the arm by rigidly inverse-mapping the frame-0 arm pixels about the pivot;
  5. clip to 0…255 and cast to uint8.

3. Clean plate (build_erase, build_plate)

build_erase marks every frame-0 pixel that must disappear: the three domino body quads (dilated 1 px for AA edges), their contact-shadow colour zones, the B4 shadow wedge on the platform, the frame-0 arm strip/hub box, and the frame-0 arm shadow on the table. The left four dominoes and the entire left half of the scene are never in an erase mask.

build_plate fills erased pixels differently per surface:

  • table (outside the platform ellipse, below the measured horizon): the wood grain runs nearly vertically (−5°), so each erased pixel copies the nearest non-erased frame-0 pixel found by walking down (then up) along the grain direction, preserving the real grain texture;
  • platform ellipse: 1-D horizontal linear interpolation between the nearest non-erased ellipse pixels in the same row (edge pixels are extrapolated; a dark constant is the final fallback);
  • wall: horizontal neighbour interpolation (almost never needed).

A 1-px feather around each filled patch averages in free neighbours to hide seams. The same plate is reused for all 19 frames.

4. Dominoes (extract_sprite, model_corners, render_domino)

Each animated domino is an RGBA sprite cut from frame 0 at its observed quad, rectified into a canonical 6×25 upright rectangle by an 8-point homography (3× supersampled, then box-filtered; coverage comes from a clockwise inside-polygon test in y-down coordinates).

For tilt θ about the bottom-left hinge A = (ax, py) with width w, height h:

B = A + (w·cos θ,  −K·w·sin θ)          # bottom-right, with K = 0.45
D = A + (−h·sin θ, −h·cos θ)            # top-left
C = B + (D − A)                          # top-right

K adds a small 3D-lite foreshortening of the rising/sinking bottom edge.

Rendering each domino each frame:

  1. near-side thickness band — a small polygon along the hinge whose far corner E = B·cos²θ + D·sin²θ sweeps from the foot toward the fallen tip; painted with the measured dark side colour;
  2. face — the canonical sprite mapped to quad D-C-B-A with the inverse homography, bilinear sampling, 2× supersampling, and a tilt shading factor 0.85 + 0.15·cos θ;
  3. near-flat analytic face — above ≈ 66° the 25-px sprite is minified into a few rows and homography averaging turns to mud, so between 66° and 74° the warped sprite is cross-faded into an analytic flat quad filled with the measured face colour plus a 1.3-px lit "cap" along the far edge;
  4. layers are combined with standard source-over; the three dominoes are painted in cascade order B3, W3, B4 onto a shared layer.

5. Rotating arm (arm_alpha_at, render_arm)

The frame-0 arm pixels are classified in source coordinates:

  • on_strip: within the geometric strip from the pivot along the frame-0 arm direction (half-width 2.9 px, extended 1.5 px behind the pivot),
  • plus colour tests for the wood core/edges (r−b and brightness thresholds measured from frame 0), the green star (g dominant), and a fixed hub rectangle of non-blueish pixels (x 203–227, y 34–46).

Colours are bilinear samples of frame 0 at the same points.

For an output fine point p = pivot + d_out, the source point is

d_in = d_out · R,   R = [[cos δ, −sin δ], [sin δ, cos δ]],   δ = φ − φ0

i.e. the inverse of the forward screen-clockwise rigid rotation d_out = R d_in (row-vector form; in y-down screen coordinates this matrix is clockwise). Four quarter-pixel samples are box-averaged (2× AA). The arm is painted inside the bbox x 168–242, y 2–84 with source-over alpha. Because the mapping is rigid and pivots exactly at (215, 40.5), the wood strip and green star rotate without translating the hub.

The arm's table shadow is a separate analytic strip cast forward beyond the tip, darkened only outside the platform ellipse (soft elliptic clip), with a distance ramp; it naturally shrinks to nothing when the stick points up.

6. Shadows and compositing order

  • domino ground shadows: a stretching coverage quad from the hinge to the projected lead edge (ax − h·sin θ) plus a static contact blob, accumulated, gaussian-filtered (σ = 1) and limited to 0.6 opacity;
  • the blurred shadow field is hard-clipped at x = 128 so the gaussian tail cannot cross the gap onto the left-half table;
  • order per frame: plate → shadows (multiply) → domino layer → arm layer.

7. Outputs and packing

generate.py ends by calling artifact_io.save, which:

  • packs frames into pixels.npz as a uint32 (144, 256, 20) tensor of 0x00RRGGBB;
  • writes frames/00.png … 19.png;
  • builds inspection.png (8 key-frames with time labels) and index.html (self-contained delta-stream player);
  • save_transition_check then writes transition_check.png: a 5×4 montage of the 19 consecutive-frame absolute differences, amplified ×5 at half resolution, each tile labelled with the transition and its changed-pixel count. One tile slot is left empty.

8. Verification (all numeric; scripted in .tmp/)

  • frames/00.png is byte-identical to first_frame.png (total abs diff 0);
  • all 20 PNGs decode to the corresponding planes of the pixels.npz tensor;
  • left half invariance: zero changed pixels at x < 128 in every frame; the B1/W1/B2/W2 body bboxes contain 0 changed pixels in all 20 frames;
  • gap: the gap zone (x 121–131, y 50–74) contains 0 domino body pixels in every frame; final fallen slabs occupy x 132–180 (dark B3 reaches 132, white W3 band 147–152, dark B4 at the right) — 12 px short of W4;
  • cascade order: standing-face pixel counts show B4 moving from frame 01, W3 gone from frames 06–07, B3 gone by frame 09;
  • arm: fitting the observed warm strip pixels gives |Δφ| ≤ 1.3° against the commanded angle at tested frames, length 33.2–34.0 px (model 32.88), perpendicular RMS ≤ 1.5 px; ~100 changed pixels lie in the expected clockwise strip band each frame and ~0 in the opposite-rotation band;
  • transition changed-pixel counts decay monotonically in trend from 2114 (00→01) to 438 (18→19) as the motion settles.

9. Honest limitations

  • The physics is keyframed single-axis rotation/tilt, not contact dynamics; rates and contact frames are hand-chosen to match the geometry.
  • The plate is heuristic inpainting from frame 0 (grain walk + row interpolation); a few repaired tan pixels are reused statically every frame, and erased shadow zones are approximated rather than re-lit.
  • The arm sprite rotates a fixed hub rectangle; its beige pixels mostly re-cover the same platform region, but depth occlusion of stick/platform/ wall is handled only by painter's order, not a 3-D model.
  • Domino colour/shading near flat angles is an analytic approximation (flat face colour + lit cap) rather than reprojected texture.
  • There is no motion blur; anti-aliasing is limited to 2× (sprite cut 3×) box supersampling and σ = 1 shadow blur.
