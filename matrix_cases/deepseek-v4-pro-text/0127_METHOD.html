Inpainting Method

  • For pixels obscured by the paper towel, use horizontal scanline inpainting:
    • For each row in the paper towel area, fill from left-to-right using the boundary colors on either side of the paper towel mask.
    • Blend edge colors linearly across the gap.
  • For the dish/liquid region, preserve the original liquid color and pattern.

Ripple/Splash Method

  • Amplitude decays with distance from impact.
  • Ripple amplitude decays over subsequent frames.

METHOD.md

Method — Case 0127

Segmentation

Per-row channel-wise background comparison. For each row in the paper-towel ROI (y=20–90, x=92–142), the background is estimated as the per-channel median of pixels to the left (x=77–91) and right (x=143–157) of the ROI. Pixels whose average RGB exceeds the background median by more than max(5, channel-std-mean) — and that are not blue-tinted — are classified as paper towel. Morphological cleanup and largest-connected-component selection (requiring the component to touch y < 35, near the grabber) isolate the coherent object (~1000 px).

Inpainting

Horizontal scanline interpolation. For each row, contiguous masked runs are filled by linearly blending the nearest clean boundary colours. Subtle Gaussian noise restores image texture.

Physics — Sinking into liquid

The paper towel is already partially submerged in frame 0 (held by the grabber). When released, it sinks further into the dish.

  • Net acceleration: gravity (g = 60 px/s²) minus buoyancy proportional to submerged fraction (up to 85% buoyancy when fully submerged).
  • Drag: velocity-dependent damping increases with submerged fraction.
  • Integration: dt = 0.25 s (4 fps) with implicit drag damping per frame.
  • Hard limit: maximum sink depth capped at 75% of paper towel height below the liquid surface.
  • Lateral wobble & rotation: minor sinusoidal oscillation, damped.

Ripple

Circular displacement field centred on the impact point; pixels are pushed radially outward with exponential decay proportional to impact strength.

Compositing

  • Frame 00 is the unmodified first_frame.png (verified pixel-identical).
  • Frames 01–19 composite the RGBA paper-towel sprite onto the inpainted background, with per-row horizontal-shear rotation.
  • Submerged portions receive progressive blue tint and darkening proportional to depth.
  • Ripple distortion is applied before compositing.

Verification

  • Frame 00 == first_frame.png (max absolute diff = 0)
  • 20 × 256×144 uint8 RGB PNGs
  • pixels.npz: uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, shape (144, 256, 20)
