METHOD.md — Animation Technique

Approach

All frames are generated from first_frame.png using pure numpy pixel operations — no external renderers, physics engines, or AI models.

Scene Analysis

The base frame was analyzed numerically:

  • Dispenser nozzle: (142, 21) — red liquid exit point
  • Duck impact zone: (154, 44) — first contact on yellow rubber duck
  • Duck mask: Identified by color thresholds (R > 140, G > 100, B < 120, R dominant over B by 40+, G dominant over B by 30+) with morphological dilation to include edge pixels
  • Tray mask: Bright silver-gray pixels (R > 170, G > 160, B > 140, neutral tones)

Stream Rendering (all frames 01–19)

A red liquid column is drawn from the dispenser nozzle to the duck surface using a quadratic Bézier curve. The stream is rendered by stamping overlapping filled circles along the path, creating a continuous thick line with soft edges.

Parameters per frame:

Frame Thickness (px) Stream reach (fraction)
01 1.5 0.10 (droplet forms at nozzle)
02 2.5 0.40
03 3.5 0.82 (nearly reaches duck)
04–06 3.5 → 6.5 1.0 (full stream established)
07–19 6.5 → 8.5 1.0 (thick, sustained pour)

The stream tapers slightly toward the bottom (20% reduction) and includes ±10 random color variation per pixel for natural appearance.

Wetness Accumulation (frames 03–19)

A 2D accumulation map (wetness) tracks liquid on the duck surface. Each frame, wetness increases at an expanding elliptical region around the impact point, with:

  • Horizontal spread coefficient: 0.5
  • Downward (gravity) spread coefficient: 0.22
  • Upward spread coefficient: 0.85
  • Accumulation rate: 0.18 × (1 − normalized_distance)

This creates a realistic flow pattern where liquid pools at the impact point and spreads more easily downward due to gravity.

Duck Recoloring

Duck pixels with accumulated wetness are alpha-blended toward the red liquid color (RGB(210, 55, 28)). The wetness value directly controls blend weight, transitioning duck pixels from bright yellow to deep red progressively.

Splash Particles (frames 03–07)

At impact, small red pixel clusters are scattered in upward/sideways directions. Particles follow ballistic trajectories:

  • Random angle in [−0.5, 3.6] radians (mostly upward/sideways)
  • Random speed in [2.5, 8.0] px/frame
  • Positions computed as: pos = impact + direction × speed × (frame − 1.5)
  • Each particle stamps a 3×3 pixel cluster

Drips (frames 07–19)

Drips spawn at duck edges where wetness exceeds threshold (0.35) and the pixel below is non-duck. Drips are tracked with position and velocity:

  • Initial velocity: 0.8–2.3 px/frame (random)
  • Gravity acceleration: 0.35 px/frame²
  • Trail length: 6 pixels fading from red to dark red
  • Drips expire when they fall below y=120 or leave the frame

Tray Puddle (frames 12–19)

A circular red patch grows on the tray surface (y=85) near the duck base:

  • Radius: 3 + (frame − 12) × 4.0 px
  • Alpha: 0.55 at center, linear falloff to edge
  • Only drawn on tray-masked pixels

Dispenser Liquid Level (frames 06–19)

The top of the red liquid column in the dispenser fades slightly (50% blend with pixels below) to simulate the liquid level dropping as it pours out. Up to 10 rows are affected.

Color Palette

  • Liquid core: RGB(210, 55, 28) — bright red
