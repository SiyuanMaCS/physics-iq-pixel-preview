METHOD.md

METHOD.md

Overview

PixelWorld-Text animation: a lit match rotates clockwise on a black platform and touches a black balloon, causing it to burst.

Scene Elements (measured from first_frame.png)

  • Black balloon: left side, ~18 px radius, centred near (14,46)
  • Black platform: right side, left edge slopes from (54,55) to (84,85)
  • Lit match: flame at (62,40), stick at x≈54-56, ~23.4 px long, pivot at (54,62)
  • Wooden table: bottom portion, RGB ~(148,116,72)
  • Wall: top/right, light gray ~(193,198,204)

Method

1. Clean Background

The match+flame region (x:48-72, y:30-58) is inpainted by horizontal linear interpolation between left and right reference samples for each row.

2. Match Extraction & Rotation

  • Extract a patch containing the match+flame centred on the pivot point.
  • Create an alpha mask from the per-channel difference against the clean background, smoothed with a Gaussian (sigma=1.2).
  • Place the patch on a 140×140 canvas with the pivot at the canvas centre.
  • For each frame, rotate the canvas clockwise by t × 15.625° using scipy.ndimage.rotate (order=3 bicubic interpolation).
  • Alpha-composite the rotated match back onto the clean background.

3. Balloon Burst (frames 15-19, 4 phases)

  • Phase 1 (frame 15): Local deformation — rubber pushed outward radially from the flame contact point. Orange glow at contact.
  • Phase 2 (frame 16): Tearing — rubber peels from contact zone; wall/table background begins to show through the tear.
  • Phase 3 (frames 17-18): Collapsing — rubber progressively replaced by background colours. By frame 18, most of the balloon is gone.
  • Phase 4 (frame 19): Fully burst — wall and table visible with occasional dark rubber fragments scattered on the table surface.

4. Output

All 20 frames verified: frame 00 exactly matches first_frame.png. PNGs, packed uint32 tensor (pixels.npz), inspection sheet, transition check, and standalone HTML player generated via artifact_io.py.
