  • The platform top has a small gap/depression at x~109–112.

Black Balloon

  • Dark oval shape above the platform on the left.
  • Bounding box: rows 23–61, columns 56–130.
  • Centroid at approximately (93, 43).
  • Bottom edge rests near row 61, just above the platform top at row 63.

Lit Match

  • Match stick: brownish/wooden colored, rows 63–80, columns ~130–175.
  • Match pivot point (where attached to platform): approximately (132, 64) — the left end of the match body resting on the platform surface.
  • Match body extends rightward and slightly downward from the pivot.
  • Flame: warm yellows/oranges, rows ~72–96, columns ~140–175.
  • Match tip (flame center): approximately (160, 78).
  • Total match length from pivot to flame tip: ~36 pixels.
  • Initial orientation: pointing right-down, approximately 23° below horizontal right.

Animation Plan

Story: The lit match, attached to the black platform on the right, rotates clockwise and sweeps around to touch the black balloon on the left.

Technique

  1. Extract the match+flame as a foreground sprite from the first frame.
  2. Reconstruct the background (platform surface) behind the match using nearby texture samples.
  3. For each frame, rotate the match sprite clockwise around its pivot point, then composite onto the background.
  4. Frame 00 is exactly first_frame.png. Frames 01–19 show progressive rotation.

METHOD.md — PixelWorld-Text Animation Method

Task

Generate 20 RGB frames (256×144, 4 fps, 5 seconds) showing a lit match rotating clockwise on a black platform to touch a black balloon.

Approach

1. Match+Flame Segmentation

Scanned a tight region (y=55–104, x=120–187) of first_frame.png. Classified each pixel as match/flame by excluding:

  • Dark platform pixels: all RGB channels < 35
  • Gray wall background: channel span < 15 and r > 60

This maps to: the match stick (warm brown, r>>g>>b), the flame head (yellow/orange with r>>b), and the bright flame center (yellow-white, r≈g>>b). Yielded 2537 foreground pixels.

2. Background Reconstruction

For each segmented match pixel, inpainted the background by:

  • Wall area (y ≤ 62): horizontal mirror from the opposite side of the frame
  • Platform top (y ≤ 66): tiled nearby platform surface samples
  • Platform body (y > 66): copied from a fixed platform-interior reference column

3. Rotation and Frame Generation

  • Pivot: Global coordinate (y=64, x=131) — the match's attachment point on the platform surface

  • Rotation: Forward bilinear scatter. Each foreground pixel (relative to pivot) is rotated clockwise by the target angle, and its color is distributed to the four nearest integer pixel locations with bilinear weights. Accumulated contributions are averaged per destination pixel.

  • Frame 00: Unmodified first_frame.png

  • Frames 01–19: Progressive clockwise rotation at angles: [10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80, 90, 105, 120, 135, 150, 160, 170, 180, 190, 195, 200]°

    The match sweeps ~200° clockwise from its initial rightward orientation, passing through downward orientation (~90°) and reaching left-up orientation (~180–200°) where the flame tip contacts the black balloon.

4. Visual Effects

  • Flame flicker: Each frame adds random ±20 perturbation to red, ±12 to green, ±10 to blue for all flame-colored (r > 130, r > b + 20) painted pixels.
  • Balloon indentation: Frames 17–19 apply a slight darkening (up to 7 levels) in a 7×7 neighborhood around the match tip where it contacts the balloon surface.

5. Tools Used

  • Python 3 with NumPy and PIL (Pillow)
  • All pixel operations are local: segmentation, inpainting, forward-mapped bilinear rotation, alpha-free compositing (direct overwrite of inpainted background), and per-pixel noise for flicker
  • No external AI models, network requests, or non-local references

6. Output Verification

  • Frame 00 byte-identical to first_frame.png (assertion checked)
  • All 20 frames are uint8 RGB at 256×144
  • pixels.npz: packed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor
  • inspection.png: 1024×330 sheet with 8 key frames
  • transition_check.png: per-frame pixel-diff heatmap
  • index.html: standalone player with base64-encoded frame data
