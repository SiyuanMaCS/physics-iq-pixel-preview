METHOD.md

METHOD.md — PixelWorld-Text Case 0184

Scene

A static, locked-off shot of a glass beverage dispenser pouring water into a glass containing grapefruit juice. The dispenser is visible in the upper portion of the frame; the glass with amber grapefruit juice occupies the lower portion.

Approach

Direct pixel manipulation with NumPy. No external rendering engines, physics simulators, or AI models were used. All visual effects are implemented as analytic pixel operations.

Scene Analysis

The first frame (first_frame.png) was loaded and analysed programmatically to identify:

  • Gray background wall: x = 0–43, all y
  • Glass beverage dispenser body: x = 44–216, y = 0–54
  • Dispenser spout tip: x ≈ 123, y = 54
  • Glass interior: x ≈ 48–210
  • Initial grapefruit juice surface: y = 77
  • Dark right edge: x > 216

Frame Generation

Frame 00

Exact pixel copy of first_frame.png. Verified by zero-diff assertion.

Frames 01–19

Each frame is generated from scratch starting from first_frame.png and applying the following operations in order:

1. Water Stream (water_stream)

A translucent vertical column is drawn from the spout tip (x≈123, y=54) to the current juice surface. The stream has:

  • Width ≈ 5 pixels at the spout, narrowing slightly with distance (surface tension)
  • Sinusoidal lateral wobble (amplitude ±1.4 px) varying with time and depth
  • Slight parabolic arc (gravity)
  • Radial colour profile: darker blue-white at centre (~RGB 145,172,208), brighter at edges (~RGB 230,238,250) for surface reflection
  • Alpha blending at 0.42–0.70 opacity
  • Brightness enhancement near the surface (turbulence aeration)

2. Rising Juice Layer (fill_new_juice)

The region between the rising surface and the original surface (y=77) is filled with diluted grapefruit juice:

  • Dilution factor uses exponential decay with depth: strongest at the surface, decaying to original juice colour with depth
  • Two dilution targets: "very diluted" (~RGB 215,198,180) for the top 2.5 px, "light diluted" (~RGB 195,168,138) below that

3. Interface Blend (blend_interface)

A very subtle lightening band (3–6 px) applied below the original surface (y=77+) to create a smooth transition between old and new juice. The effect decays exponentially and is barely perceptible (<1 unit per channel).

4. Surface Effects (surface_fx)

  • Ripples: Sinusoidal height perturbation along the surface, decaying with distance from the impact point (x≈123)
  • Impact splash: Bright white/yellow highlight at the stream impact zone (4 px radius)

The surface rise uses power-law easing (exponent 0.80) to simulate the slightly slower initial rise as the flow settles, then steady filling.
