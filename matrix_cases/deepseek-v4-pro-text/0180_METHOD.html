METHOD.md

METHOD.md

Approach

This animation is generated entirely via local Python pixel operations.

Scene Understanding

The first frame (first_frame.png) was analyzed through numeric RGB inspection. The scene contains:

  • A light-colored wooden table occupying most of the frame.
  • A dark background above the table (wall/backdrop).
  • Two black pipes on opposite sides of the table, offset vertically:
    • Left pipe: dark region at x=0-59, y=0-69. Ball emergence near x=58, y=40.
    • Right pipe: dark region at x=240-255, y=47-76. Ball emergence near x=238, y=60.

Animation Method

  • Frame 00: Exact pixel copy of first_frame.png.
  • Frames 01-19: The background is copied from frame 00. Two small ball sprites (radius 5 px) are drawn at linearly interpolated positions using alpha compositing.
  • Ball rendering: Each ball is a pre-computed sprite with radial Lambertian shading (light from upper-left), rim darkening, and a small specular highlight. Alpha blending overlays the ball onto the background.
  • Ball trajectories:
    • Brown tennis ball: horizontal path at y≈40, velocity +10 px/frame (rightward). Emerges from left pipe at frame 01, reaches right edge around frame 19.
    • Grey tennis ball: horizontal path at y≈60, velocity -10 px/frame (leftward). Emerges from right pipe at frame 01, approaches left pipe around frame 19.
  • No external engines, AI models, or network access used.
  • All pixel math uses NumPy with standard PIL for I/O.

Output Verification

  • pixels.npz: 20-frame uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor (144×256×20).
  • Frame 00 verified byte-identical to first_frame.png.
  • inspection.png: 8-frame contact sheet.
  • transition_check.png: Side-by-side frame comparisons with amplified differences.
  • index.html: Standalone player with delta compression.
