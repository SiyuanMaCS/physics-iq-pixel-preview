METHOD.md

Approach

Sprite compositing with analytical sphere rendering.

  1. Background: Frame 0 (first_frame.png) is the master reference. A clean background is created by horizontally interpolating across the ball zone (rows 33–48, cols 164–205), replacing original brown/orange ball pixels with a smooth gradient from the wall/pipe area (left) to the table surface (right). The black pipe opening (x≈164–174, y≈34–44) is explicitly preserved. A wider path (x≈192–210) is also cleaned for the orange ball's rightward travel.

  2. Ball rendering: Each tennis ball is drawn as an anti-aliased Lambertian-shaded sphere with:

    • Diffuse + ambient shading (matching scene lighting from upper-left)
    • Blinn-Phong specular highlight
    • Subtle micro-texture (simulated tennis-ball fuzz via reproducible random noise)
    • Soft elliptical contact shadow beneath each ball
    • Smoothstep alpha blending at edges (~1 px transition)

  3. Animation: 20 keyframed positions drive the grey, brown, and orange ball centres. Frame 0 is overwritten with the exact first_frame.png pixels. Frames 1–19 composite rendered balls onto the cleaned background.

  4. Physics model: Newton's-cradle elastic momentum transfer.

    • Grey ball emerges from pipe at ~1.8 px/frame
    • Contacts brown ball at frame 5 (grey right edge meets brown left edge)
    • Momentum transfers through brown to orange
    • Orange rolls ~14 px right (from x≈185 to x≈199)
    • Grey ball stops near impact point
    • Brown oscillates slightly then returns near original position
