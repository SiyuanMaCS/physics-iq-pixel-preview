OBSERVATION.md — Scene Measurements & Event Plan

Scene Layout (256×144, RGB)

Static Background

  • Wall/Background: Gray tones (~RGB 140-165), rows 0–72, full width.
  • Wooden Table: Brown/wood tones (~RGB 80-140, 50-100, 25-65), rows 73–143, full width.
  • Bowl of Water: Dark interior on the table, roughly elliptical, visible from rows 78–143, spanning x≈75–180. Water surface visible as brighter reflections at perimeter (x≈78–95 and x≈160–175 on rows 110–125); center is darker (you see into the water).

Moving Objects

  • Orange-Black Grabber Tool:
    • Black body: x≈110–160, y≈0–131 (vertical strip).
    • Orange upper portion: x≈111–150, y≈23–52.
    • The tool extends from top of frame down into the bowl area.
  • Crumpled Piece of Paper: Small white cluster at rows 89–94, cols 144–156, centered at approximately (x=150, y=91). Pure white pixels (255,255,255) held at the grabber tip. Size: ~12×5 pixels.

Key Distances

  • Paper initial center: y≈91
  • Water surface (impact zone): y≈114
  • Fall distance: ~23 pixels

20-Frame Event Plan (4 fps, 5 seconds)

Phase 1: Release (Frames 0–2, 0.00–0.50 s)

  • Frame 00 (0.00 s): Exact copy of first_frame.png. Grabber holds paper above bowl.
  • Frame 01 (0.25 s): Grabber begins releasing. Paper detaches, moves down ~1 px. Grabber arms slightly open.
  • Frame 02 (0.50 s): Paper free-falling, at y≈95. Grabber returns to rest position.

Phase 2: Free Fall (Frames 3–5, 0.75–1.25 s)

  • Frame 03 (0.75 s): Paper at y≈99. Accelerating under gravity.
  • Frame 04 (1.00 s): Paper at y≈104.
  • Frame 05 (1.25 s): Paper at y≈110. Approaching water surface.

Phase 3: Water Impact (Frames 6–9, 1.50–2.25 s)

  • Frame 06 (1.50 s): Paper contacts water at y≈114. Small ripple ring begins.
  • Frame 07 (1.75 s): Paper partially submerged. Ripple expands outward (radius ≈15 px).
  • Frame 08 (2.00 s): Paper mostly underwater, visible through water. Ripple at radius ≈25 px.
  • Frame 09 (2.25 s): Paper fully in water. Ripple at radius ≈35 px, amplitude beginning to decay.

Phase 4: Settling (Frames 10–19, 2.50–4.75 s)

  • Frame 10 (2.50 s): Ripple expands to ≈40 px, amplitude decaying.
  • Frame 11 (2.75 s): Ripple at ≈45 px, decaying further.
  • Frame 12 (3.00 s): Ripple fading, paper rests just below surface.
  • Frame 13 (3.25 s): Ripple faint, ≈50 px radius.
  • Frame 14 (3.50 s): Ripple barely visible.
  • Frame 15 (3.75 s): Near-calm water.
  • Frame 16 (4.00 s): Water almost still, paper visible in bowl.
  • Frame 17 (4.25 s): Settled state.
  • Frame 18 (4.50 s): Settled state.
  • Frame 19 (4.75 s): Final state — calm water with paper resting at bottom/on surface.

Technical Approach

  • Inpainting: Paper removed from original position by sampling nearby background pixels (rows 88–89 at same columns) and blending into the paper region.
  • Paper tracking: Paper extracted as a mask at its original position; moved by offset each frame.
  • Grabber animation: Tip pixels slightly modified (1–2 px gap opening) at frame 1, restored at frame 2.
  • Water ripples: Concentric sinusoidal waves centered at impact point (x≈150, y≈114). Amplitude follows damped oscillation: A(t) = A₀ × exp(-t/τ) × |cos(ωt)|. Applied to water-region pixels as brightness modulation.
  • Paper in water: As paper submerges (frames 7+), blend paper color toward blue-tinted darker tone to simulate wetting/immersion.

METHOD.md

METHOD.md

Approach

Pure Python pixel operations using numpy, PIL, and scipy. No external AI, ML, physics engines, or network calls. All 20 frames generated procedurally from first_frame.png.

Scene Analysis

  • Paper: Bright-white pixel cluster (R>240, G>235, B>235) at rows 89–96, cols 144–157, centered at approximately (150, 92).
  • Water surface: Located at y≈114 via bowl-interior brightness analysis and water-reflection pixel properties.
  • Clean background plate: Built by four-directional nearest-neighbor inpainting — for each paper-region pixel, the nearest non-paper neighbor in each cardinal direction is sampled and averaged.

Animation Method

Paper Motion

  • Free fall (frames 0–5): y(t) = y₀ + ½·g·t² with g calibrated so paper reaches water at frame 6 (1.5 s).
  • Submersion (frames 6–19): Exponential decay toward max depth of ~4 px below water surface: depth(t) = 4 × (1 − exp(−(t−6)×0.4)).

Water Ripples

  • Two staggered thin Gaussian rings expanding from impact point (150, 114).
  • Ring 1: speed 7 px/frame, initial amplitude 45, decay 0.15/frame.
  • Ring 2: speed 5.5 px/frame, initial amplitude 28, lagged by 1 frame.
  • Modulation: A × sin(phase) × exp(−(d−r)²/σ²) applied as additive brightness to water-masked pixels.
  • Ripples terminate after frame 14.

Paper Wetting

  • When paper center is below water surface: linear color blend toward dark blue-tinted water tone (75, 90, 110), max 70% blend.
  • Alpha reduced slightly (max 25%) for partial sub-surface transparency.

Grabber Animation

  • Frame 1: grabber-tip dark pixels brightened slightly to simulate arms opening for release.

Verification

  • Frame 00: byte-exact match with first_frame.png (max diff = 0).
  • All frames: uint8 RGB, 256×144.
  • pixels.npz: packed uint32 0xRRGGBB tensor, shape (144, 256, 20).
  • transition_check.png: amplified (5×) frame-to-frame absolute differences.
