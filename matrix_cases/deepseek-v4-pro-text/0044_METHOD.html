METHOD.md

METHOD.md — Paper Tear Animation

Approach

Method: Pixel-level mask-based compositing with procedural tear propagation.

All generation is done via local Python pixel operations (NumPy, PIL, SciPy ndimage). No external models, engines, or networks are used.

Pipeline

1. Segmentation (from first_frame.png)

Three masks are extracted from the first frame:

  • Paper mask: Pixels where green channel exceeds red by >12 and blue by >8, with green >65. Captures the green paper piece (~5499 px).
  • Blue background mask: Pixels where blue dominates (blue > red+22, blue > green+12, blue >125). Captures the light blue tabletop (~3421 px).
  • Grabber masks: Spatially bounded zones around the tool bodies.
    • Left grabber: rows 16–55, cols 0–66, excluding paper and blue background (~2243 px).
    • Right grabber: rows 6–55, cols 200–256, excluding paper and blue background (~2277 px).

2. Background Plate

A clean background plate is created by filling paper and grabber areas with per-row average colors sampled from nearby background regions. Subtle noise (±5 per channel) is added to avoid flat-color artifacts. This plate represents what would be visible behind the paper and grabbers.

3. Tear Geometry

  • Tear path: Diagonal from the top-right grip point (row 9, col 201) to the bottom-left (row 95, col 70).
  • Tear line: Computed with slight random jaggedness (σ=2.5 px perpendicular perturbation, tapering at endpoints).
  • Piece assignment: Each paper pixel is assigned to the right piece or left piece based on which side of the tear line it falls on.

4. Frame Generation (20 frames, 4 fps)

For each frame t:

  1. Start with a copy of first_frame.png.
  2. Erase paper and grabber regions (fill with background plate).
  3. Determine animation phase:
    • Frame 1: Tension — paper stretches at grip points (displacement factor 0.05).
    • Frame 2: Tear initiates at right grip (displacement factor 0.10).
    • Frames 3–8: Tear propagates across the paper (ease-in-out progression, displacement factor grows to 0.35).
    • Frames 9–15: Pieces separate with slight overshoot (displacement factor peaks at 1.05, settles to 0.97).
    • Frames 16–19: Final settling.
  4. For each paper pixel, determine:
    • Which piece it belongs to (side of tear line).
    • Whether the tear front has reached it (distance along tear direction).
    • If torn: displace by the piece's motion vector.
    • If not yet torn: stays in place.
  5. Move grabber pixels by the same displacement as their respective paper pieces.
  6. The result: paper tears, pieces separate, grabbers move outward, background is revealed in the gap.

5. Output

  • frames/00.png–19.png: 20 RGB frames at 256×144.
  • pixels.npz: Packed uint32 tensor (0xRRGGBB format), shape (144, 256, 20).
  • inspection.png: 8-frame overview sheet.
  • transition_check.png: Per-pair difference visualization (10 frame pairs, 5× amplification).
  • index.html: Standalone player with delta compression.
