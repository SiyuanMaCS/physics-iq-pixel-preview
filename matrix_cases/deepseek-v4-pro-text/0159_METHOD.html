METHOD.md — Case 0159: Silk Fabric Lowered Over Teapot

Task

Static locked-off single-shot of a silk fabric piece being lowered over a teapot on a wooden table. Generate 20 RGB frames at 256×144, 4 fps, 5 seconds. Frame 00 must match first_frame.png exactly.

Method Summary

Scene Analysis

The first_frame.png shows:

  • A dark background/curtain on the left (x=0–155, y=30–120)
  • A bright window/light source in the upper-center (x=51–243, y=0–108)
  • A warm-brown teapot occupying the center (x=36–200, y=0–100)
  • A wooden table across the bottom (x=0–255, y=72–143)
  • A blue silk fabric partially covering the teapot (x≈105–165, y≈2–102 depending on column)

Fabric Detection

Per-pixel colour thresholding identifies the blue/purple silk fabric:

  • Condition 1: B > R and B > G and B > 25 (blue-dominant)
  • Condition 2: R < 55, G < 55, B ≥ R, B ≥ G, B > 15 (dark blue fabric)
  • Condition 3: B ≥ R − 10, B > G, B > 35, R < 190 (purplish transitions)

Binary morphological closing+opening (5×3 kernel) connects fragmented regions. Per-column fabric strips are extracted: minimum 6 contiguous pixels required. Single-column gaps are interpolated.

Fabric Texture Extension

For each fabric column x with original extent [top[x], bot[x]]:

  • The vertical colour strip from frame 0 is extracted
  • Upward (y < top[x]): mirrored from the top portion of the strip
  • Downward (y > bot[x]): cycled from the bottom 25% of the strip
  • A full-height fabric_tex[y, x] texture is precomputed for every column

Animation (Frames 1–19)

For each frame t:

  • Progress = t / 19 (eased with progress^0.7 for more noticeable early motion)
  • For each fabric column x:
    • Top edge stays fixed at top[x]
    • Bottom edge descends: new_bot[x] = bot[x] + eased * DESCENT_MAX (~42 px total)
    • Extended region [bot[x]+1 .. new_bot[x]] is filled by sampling fabric_tex
  • Subtle sinusoidal horizontal draping folds: amplitude up to 0.6 px
  • Leading edge alpha blend: cubic smoothstep with base 0.25 opacity at the very edge, ramping to 1.0 over 6 rows

Verification

  • Frame 0: pixel-perfect match with first_frame.png (verified via uint32 0xRRGGBB comparison)
  • pixels.npz tensor matches all 20 PNG frames exactly
  • Frame-to-frame diffs: 585–1435 changed pixels per transition
