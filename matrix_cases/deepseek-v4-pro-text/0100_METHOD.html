METHOD.md

METHOD.md — Generation Method

Approach

The wrench rotates clockwise 30° over 5 seconds (20 frames at 4 fps) on the black platform near the powerful magnet.

Wrench Mask

The wrench mask was defined as a polygon based on extensive pixel-level inspection of first_frame.png. The wrench occupies the region between the blue magnet (x~82–117) and the black platform (x~140–205):

  • Head: y~58–118, x~88–148
  • Handle: y~118–143, x~106–136
  • Jaw gap (magnet visible through): y~96–114, x~97–117

The polygon was drawn and filled using PIL ImageDraw, then softened with Gaussian blur (σ=0.8) for smooth compositing edges.

Rotation

  • Pivot point: (120, 137) — bottom of wrench handle at platform contact.
  • Total rotation: 30° clockwise (constant angular velocity ~6°/s).
  • scipy.ndimage.rotate with bicubic interpolation (order=3).
  • The sprite (RGB + mask) is rotated at the sprite's local coordinate origin, then composited onto the frame.

Background Inpainting

  • Iterative diffusion: each masked pixel is replaced with a weighted average of its 4-connected unmasked neighbors (80 iterations max, convergence when max change < 0.1).
  • Damping factor 0.6 (40% old, 60% new) for stability.
  • Feathered mask (σ=2.5) for smooth transition at mask boundaries.

Compositing

  • For each frame t > 0: start with inpainted background, overlay rotated sprite using rotated + feathered alpha mask.
  • Frame 00 is a pixel-identical copy of first_frame.png.

Verification

  • Frame 00 verified pixel-identical to first_frame.png.
  • All 20 frames saved as individual PNGs, compressed NPZ tensor, inspection sheet, transition check, and standalone HTML player.
