METHOD.md — case_0146

Overview

Generate 20 frames (256×144, 4 fps, 5 s) showing a grey tennis ball rolling left‑to‑right out of a black tube across a wooden table with a white lampshade above.

Scene Analysis

  • Source: first_frame.png (static background for all frames).
  • Black tube: Dark region at x=0–25, y=30–58; opening faces right.
  • Table: Warm grey‑brown surface visible from y≈60 downward (avg RGB ≈ [143, 139, 139]).
  • Lampshade: Bright white region at x=100–163, y=29–123, light source from upper‑right.
  • Wall: Beige background above the table and to the right of the tube (R≈95, G≈85, B≈70).

Generation Method

Frame 00

Exact pixel copy of first_frame.png. The tennis ball is still fully inside the dark tube interior and not visible.

Frames 01–19

Each frame starts from the background (first_frame.png), then a grey tennis ball is alpha‑blended at its computed position.

Ball Rendering

  • Sprite: Pre‑computed once as a (15×15) RGBA patch (radius=7 px).
  • 3D shading: Lambertian reflectance with a directional light matching the lampshade position (upper‑right). Ambient term = 0.35 ensures the dark side isn't pure black.
  • Anti‑aliasing: Soft 0.8 px edge transition using distance‑based alpha.
  • Shadow: Small elliptical dark patch offset below the ball (opacity 0.35, blended multiplicatively).
  • Blending: Standard alpha compositing onto the background; clipped to frame boundaries.

Physics / Motion

Constant deceleration (rolling friction) with constant horizontal drift:

x(t) = 25 + 53.7·t − 2.0·t²   [pixels]
y(t) = 52 +  7.4·t            [pixels]

where t = frame_index / 4 seconds.
