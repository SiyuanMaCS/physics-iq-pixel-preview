  2. Rising liquid: As the liquid level rises each frame, the empty area above the old level is filled with dark red liquid colors sampled/interpolated from the existing liquid. The glass interior width at each row determines the fill mask.

  3. Surface effects: Ripples/brightness variation at the liquid surface (top 1–2 rows of liquid).

  4. Spigot drip: After the flow stops (frame 09+), a small droplet hangs from the spigot then falls.

  5. Dispenser liquid level: The dispenser body is much wider than the glass, so the level change is negligible. Slightly darken the top 1–2 rows of the dispenser's visible liquid to suggest very minor level drop.

Approach

Pure local Python pixel operations. No external AI models, engines, or network access.

Technique

  1. Scene Analysis: Extracted precise measurements from first_frame.png using NumPy/PIL pixel inspection:

    • Spigot opening at (x=160, y≈84-87)
    • Glass interior shape from dark-red liquid region (y=93-134, x=139-181)
    • Two color palettes sampled: dark red (glass liquid body) and bright red (dispenser/stream)

  2. Glass Interior Mask: Defined per-row x-ranges for the glass interior based on the existing dark-red liquid distribution. Extrapolated upward from y=93 to y=86 (glass rim) using the meniscus curve.

  3. Liquid Level Animation: Linear rise schedule from y=93 (frame 0) to y=86 (frame 8), then stabilization with surface tension effects (frames 9-19).

  4. Liquid Fill: For each frame, the area between the previous liquid level and the new level is filled with procedurally-sampled dark red colors. Surface pixels (top 1-2 rows) are rendered brighter to simulate reflections. A subtle horizontal brightness gradient darkens liquid near the glass walls.

  5. Falling Stream: 1-2 px wide bright red column from spigot to liquid surface with sinusoidal horizontal wobble (amplitude ±1.5 px). Color varies along the stream path. Droplets separate near the stream bottom.

  6. Surface Effects: Sinusoidal brightness modulation at the liquid surface creates ripple highlights. The meniscus (topmost liquid row) receives extra brightening.

  7. Spigot Effects: Bright highlight at spigot opening during active flow. Post-flow drip animation (droplet forms, falls, hits surface).

  8. Dispenser Level: Subtle darkening of the top visible liquid edge in the dispenser body (frames 6+) suggests minor level decrease.
