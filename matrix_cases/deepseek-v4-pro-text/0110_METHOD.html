METHOD.md

Method

Approach

Sprite-based animation with clean-plate background inpainting. The match stick, hand, flame glow are extracted from frame 0 as a sprite with alpha mask (Gaussian-feathered difference from estimated background). Each subsequent frame composites the sprite at a progressively lower position while compositing against glass surfaces, handling water refraction, and simulating flame extinction with steam/ripple effects.

Technical Steps

  1. Clean plate: Background behind match estimated via wide-reference horizontal interpolation (x=38-48 left, x=112-122 right) blended with mirror symmetry inside the glass region.
  2. Alpha mask: Per-pixel difference from clean plate, thresholded and Gaussian-feathered (σ=0.5).
  3. Per-frame sprite compositing:
    • Match sprite translated downward (+ slight right drift)
    • Glass attenuation (82% brightness, blue shift) when inside glass
    • Water refraction (70% brightness, 2px right offset) when below surface
  4. Procedural warm flame glow: Teardrop-shaped warm orange glow (matching original RGB ~133,100,67 palette), sinusoidal flicker
  5. Extinction: Bright flash at frame 15 when flame contacts water, then steam plume rising + expanding water ripples in frames 16-19
  6. Glass front overlay: Rim band + edge specular highlights restored on top of composited sprite for depth illusion

Scene Geometry

  • Glass cylinder: rim y=56, base y=140, left 58→56, right 113→115
  • Water surface: y=109
  • Match: diagonal from ~(57,37) to ~(78,65) in frame 0
  • Flame glow: x≈75-95, y≈64-78 in frame 0

Tools

NumPy, PIL/Pillow, SciPy ndimage.gaussian_filter, artifact_io.py
