METHOD.md

METHOD.md

Approach

Sprite extraction and blitting — the marble is extracted from frame 0 using tight color-mask selection, the resulting hole is inpainted, and the marble sprite is blitted at keyframe positions along a zigzag path that follows the ramp structure.

Marble Detection (Frame 0)

The yellow glass marble is identified with two-tier color criteria:

  • Core: R > 110, G > 88, B < 70, (R − B) > 35 — bright yellow glass
  • Edge: R > 105, G > 85, B < 85, (R − B) > 28, y ≤ 15 — warm amber rim

Search restricted to y < 18 to avoid confusing the marble with a similarly-colored yellow rope visible at the bottom of the frame (y > 120). Result: 21 masked pixels in a 9×7 pixel patch centered at (115.0, 12.6).

Background Inpainting

The marble hole is filled using expanding-ring sampling. For each masked pixel, non-masked neighbors are collected from concentric rings (radius 1–14) until ≥4 samples are found. The hole pixel is replaced with the per-channel median of the collected samples. This produces a smooth, locally-consistent fill without edge artifacts.

Animation Path

Keyframe positions were chosen to follow the visible ramp structure identified in OBSERVATION.md:

Frames Phase Motion

1–3 Ramp 1 Slide right (x: 115→128, y≈13)

Gravity: ~10 px/frame², ramp acceleration: ~2–6 px/frame². Motion follows a zigzag pattern (right, left, right, right, left-down) matching the magnetic ramp arrangement on the whiteboard.

Frame Generation

For each frame t = 1…19:

  1. Start from the inpainted background (source with marble hole filled)
  2. Compute integer paste offset from keyframe position: dx = round(cx − offset_x), dy = round(cy − offset_y)
  3. Blit only masked marble pixels to preserve background in the patch's non-marble area

Frame 0 is the unmodified source — verified by np.array_equal.
