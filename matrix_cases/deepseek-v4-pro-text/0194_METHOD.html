Sprite Extraction

Objects extracted from first_frame.png with binary alpha masks:

  • Green paper: Sprite mask via G > R+20 and G > B+10. Content spans offsets 2-43 within the sprite. Full-bbox inpainting for zero ghosting.

Background Inpainting

Per-row wall color estimated from three safe regions (far-left, center gap between objects, far-right). Kettlebell inpainted via broad mask; paper region uses full-bbox fill to eliminate edge artifacts entirely.
