+    yy, xx = np.mgrid[0:H, 0:W]
+    dist = np.sqrt((xx - cx)**2 + (yy - cy)**2)

METHOD.md

Method

Approach

The task is to generate a 20-frame animation (4 fps, 5 seconds) of a brown tennis ball rolling out of a black tube and hitting an olive green fabric box, given only a static first frame and a text prompt.

Scene Analysis

The first frame was analyzed through numeric pixel inspection (no image viewing tools):

  • Black tube: Located on the left side at x≈0–11, y≈52–76, opening facing right at x≈11–12.
  • Olive green fabric box: Located on the right side at x≈204–255, y≈0–78.
  • Background: Neutral gray tones fill the middle of the frame.
  • Table surface: Warmer tones at the bottom (y≈120–143).
  • Ball: Not visible at frame 0 — assumed to be inside the black tube.

Ball Physics and Animation

Motion Profile

  • Frames 0–2: Ball hidden inside the tube (no visible change from first_frame.png).
  • Frame 3: Ball emerges from the tube mouth at x≈20, y=66.
  • Frames 4–16: Ball rolls right at constant speed (~12.6 px/frame) along y=66.
  • Frame 17: Ball contacts the box left edge, center at x=197 (radius 7, right edge touches x=204).
  • Frames 18–19: Ball compresses horizontally against the box (squash factors 0.78 and 0.65). The center shifts slightly but the visible right edge stays at x=204.

Rendering Method

  • Background: Each frame starts as a copy of first_frame.png. Only ball pixels are modified.
  • Ball shape: Soft circular mask with anti-aliased edges (radius = 7 pixels, diameter ≈ 15 pixels).
  • Ball shading: 3D sphere rendering using Lambertian diffuse shading with a directional light from the top-left, plus a specular highlight and a dark rim at the edge. Color palette: warm tennis-ball brown (center ~RGB(195,145,60), highlight ~RGB(230,185,105), shadow ~RGB(120,80,30)).
  • Squash and stretch: At contact frames, the ball's x-coordinate is compressed (multiplied by squash factor < 1) while the y-extent stays the same, creating the visual of a ball deforming against a surface.
  • Alpha blending: Ball pixels are alpha-composited onto the background using the anti-aliased mask.

Tools Used

  • Python 3 with NumPy for array operations and PIL/Pillow for image I/O.
  • No external models, engines, or network access.
  • All operations are local pixel manipulation: arithmetic, alpha blending, and mathematical sphere rendering.

Output Verification

  • Frame 0 pixel-exact match to first_frame.png verified.
  • All 20 PNG frames verified against the uint32-packed pixels.npz tensor.
  • pixels.npz shape: (144, 256, 20), dtype uint32, format 0xRRGGBB.
  • inspection.png: 8-frame overview grid.
  • transition_check.png: 8-pair frame difference visualization.
  • index.html: Standalone delta-encoded player.
